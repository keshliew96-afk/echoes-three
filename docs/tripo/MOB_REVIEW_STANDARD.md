# Mob review standard (Tripo foes and small mobs)

Started 2026-11-14 with the Thorn Boar. Same spirit as the hero and boss standards
(`tripo-pilot/CHARACTER_REVIEW_STANDARD.md`, `tripo-bosses/BOSS_REVIEW_STANDARD.md`); this file says what differs for mobs.
The game stays untouched (feature freeze). Kesh marks each mob passed on its page; until then it is not a game asset.

## The move list (MODEL_STANDARD foe list)
Idle, move (walk or run, labelled as the game's foe moves), turn left and right, every attack the game gives the foe,
hurt (upper body only, plays on top), spawn (the game's grow-in of 7 frames, about 10% over, then a short entrance), death
(falls and stays down; press again to spawn). No heavy hit or enrage unless the game has one for that foe.

## Numbers come from the game
- Speed, cooldowns, ranges, health and contact damage: `src/sim/enemies.js` (ENEMY_STATS) and the archetype tables.
- Attack timing: the foe's facet rig under `src/render/enemies/facet/` and the hit timeline in `src/render/facet/foe.js`
  (FRAME = 1/30 s; wind 1, strike 2.5, contact 2, snap 2.5, settle 5 frames; recover 0.35 s unless the rig says otherwise).
- Size: match the game's foot spacing or body length (the page's `CFG.unit`), and say on the page how it compares.
- Anything not in the game (idle extras, entrance flourishes, turn rate) is marked "mine" on the page.

## Budgets
Foe 4,000 triangles, small mob (brood, broodling, gravewisp) 4,000: Kesh raised it from 1,500 on 2026-11-15, when he passed the Brood (3,809) and the Gravewisp (4,028) as sent. Over budget: say so on the page, do not trim without his go.

## Checks before publishing (all from the model file)
- Planted feet move 0 mm; out-of-reach under 2 cm; loops pop 0; nothing under the floor but hoof or claw rims (say the worst).
- Lying dead: the body rests on the floor, not above it and not in it (`DBG=1 ONLY=death node build.mjs ...` prints the
  lowest point and the body's own lowest point per frame).
- Rigid parts (tusks, eyes, horns, shells, weapons) ride one bone; a crest or spines ride their own bones and nothing else.
- Humanoid mobs: no turned-out feet in walk and run (`CFG.toe`), trunk never bound to the arms (hero standard).
- Stills of every move from the side and three-quarter, then the page test in a browser with 0 console errors.
- Only stills are seen in the cloud (software GL about 1 fps): the page says motion and feel are his to judge.

## Kit
`/mnt/project-files/tripo-mobs/mob-kit/` (the boss kit plus `reach.mjs`, per-leg reach per frame, and `steps_boar.js`).
The kit's `build.mjs` floor check follows the exported hierarchy with bone scales (since the Quillback). Rig a mob with a `rig.py` like `boar/wip/rig.py`, moves in `clips.mjs`, page from `boar/wip/genpage.py` (built from the
Gloam Wolf page). Files per mob: `<mob>/<mob>-tripo-rigged.glb`, `<mob>/wip/`, `<mob>/review/`.
