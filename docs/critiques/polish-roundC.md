## Failures

### F1

C2 FAIL — reserved Ember band blows past the <500 bar on no-enemy gameplay frames whenever the leashed party stands in a brazier pool. Measured h5-25 / s>0.35 / L>40 counts: variant 1, player teleported to (6,-4) (allies leash in around the brazier at (3.2,-2.4)) = 969 px (captures/pq-sp-ne.png), 1408 px (pq-ne-rings.png), 659 px (pq-ne-rings2.png), 628 px (pq-lit-rings.png), 1216 px on the current v0.3.17 build (pq-final-ne.png); variant 3, player at (8.6,-3.8) = 1150 px (pq-brz-v3.png) and 1106 px (pq-final-v3.png). That is 1.3x-2.8x the bar with zero enemies on the field. The claim in docs/critiques/baseline-v030-advisories.md that the count is '43-75 px in all of them, far under the 500 bar, framing-independent' is falsified — the advisory only sampled framings where the player hugs a wall, which drags the allies AWAY from every pool. A/B on the identical frame with identity rings hidden drops it 1408 -> 350 px (captures/pq-ne-norings.png), so ~75% of the band is the party's own rings; the residual 322 px at (578,557) rgb 162,94,80 h10.2 is the Swordsman's wine tunic under the same light. Hiding only the additive pool decals still leaves 488 px (pq-ne-nopool.png), so the warm PointLight/key and the bloom spill are contributors too, not just the decal. Whole-frame warm-vs-cool also inverts there (warm 11.0% vs cool 28.2%) and stays inverted inside the advisory's own interior box 260,140,1080,620 (warm 10.8% vs cool 13.8%) — the pinned measurement basis does not rescue this framing. FIX DIRECTION: the identity ring band has to survive arena light unchanged (BUILD_BRIEF S17: rings are 'exempt from all palette/lighting shifts'). The precedent already exists in this repo — v0.3.10 moved the revive ring to a post-bloom, opaque-plate overlay (src/render/allies/index.js). Do the same for groundRing in src/render/critters/common.js: composite band + ink in a post-bloom overlay pass with NormalBlending so the pool decals (src/render/glow.js sprites placed at the emitters) and the bloom cannot add into it. A saturation-only stopgap — making ringBand's `near()` reserved-hue test cyclic so h348 counts as within 10 deg of the h5 Ember edge and takes the low sat clamp — would only hide the analyzer count, not stop the ring reading orange on screen.

### F2

C4 FAIL — the Swordsman's identity ring leaves its class accent and loses both legibility mechanisms on its pool-facing arc. Frozen frame captures/pq-lit-rings.png, ring centre (590.4,568.9), half 46.13 px: the brazier-facing (left) arc is a continuous 8 px band at rgb 208-211 / 125-131 / 118-120 = h 4.7-7.8, s 0.43-0.44, L 142-147 — 17-24 deg off #6B2E3A (h348.2), i.e. outside the ~12 deg criterion, and inside the reserved Ember band. The away-side arc of the SAME ring measures h 353.8 (delta 5.6), so the drift is purely the warm pool. On that arc neither fallback clears: band/adjacent-ground luma = 147/112 = 1.31 (< 1.6) and the outer ink stroke washes to L 95 against ground L 112 — 0.85x the ground, not a dark rim. Confirmed visually in captures/pq-swring.png and pq-brzv3.png, where the ring reads salmon-orange, not wine. Second instance in the same frame: the Tank's ring on its brazier-facing arc is blown out entirely — the whole scan from t 0.60 to 0.89 reads rgb 255,250,215 down to 255,235,160 (L 234-249) with no band and no stroke at all (captures/pq-tanklit.png shows the badger with no visible ring on that side). This is the direct cost of the C4 re-hue: the old pastel ring (#eec6cd, s~0.17) could never pass the analyzer's s>0.35 gate, so pushing the band back to real wine chroma (measured s 0.43-0.48) is exactly what let it enter the danger band under warm light. The two clauses of the claimed goal collide and were only verified where they do not meet. FIX DIRECTION: same as above — post-bloom, lighting-exempt ring compositing. Re-verify by re-running tools/actions/pq-lit-rings.json and tools/actions/pq-brz-v3.json and requiring band hue within 12 deg of the accent AND (ratio >= 1.6 OR >= 2 px dark rim) on BOTH the pool-facing and away-facing arcs, plus danger < 500 on those same frames.
