- No regression: LUMA gates hold on all 3 variants, heal effects deposit zero violet px, an Ember

## Advisories (non-blocking, with fix directions)

### A1 — Additive heal glow over the Swordsman's wine tunic manufactures Ember-band pixels (frame total up to 485 vs the 500 bar)

captures/xfx-sanct_00.png: whole-frame danger 485 (skillfx hidden: 33); xfx-sanct2 419/28; xfx-heal
217/27; xfx-wave 200/33; xfx-bond 190/28; in the pool xfx-poolsanct-v1 202/60, xfx-poolheal-v1 220/33.
Every attributed pixel sits on the fox's tunic (cells 720-800 x 480-520): e.g. (756,493) off
rgb(186,94,111) h349 -> on rgb(188,138,121) h15 s0.36. Pure green added to wine lands at h6-19 at
s0.35-0.37 — just over the analyzer's gate. It does not read as a threat cue on screen (a paler rose),
but a Sanctuary on a bunched party in a pool is the exact gameplay case and it is within 3% of the bar.
Fix direction: the heal-flash/zone glow layers that fall on BODIES should not be additive over albedo
(the bond core and wave crest already use NormalBlending for exactly this reason) — or give the
additive heal sprites a small blue component (Bright Heal has b=0x73) so the sum with wine leaves the
h5-25 window, or extend src/env/bandguard.js's clamp to the party's toon materials' emissive path.

### A2 — Restorative Wave crest is the faintest of the three lifted slices

Over cream fur only 339-382 "strong" changed px (Bond 625) and the crest reads as a ~1-2 px pale line at
1x (captures/xfx-crop-wave-1x.png). Fix direction: thicken the crest (0.06 -> ~0.10 u) and/or darken the
inkEdge under it for the first ~120 ms so the front has a real ink line against fur, matching the
bond's core+ink treatment.

### A3 — An Ember telegraph centred on a ring loses ~40% of its measured Ember over the band

captures/xfx-tele2: decal box 1254 px with the Healer's ring vs 2112 px with it hidden — the translucent
Ember over the bright mint band composites to a paler salmon (s<0.35) on the inner half of the hoop.
Still legible (hoop + chevron intact in xfx-crop-tele2-on.png), and on the Swordsman the hoop sits at
almost the same radius as his wine ring (xfx-crop-fight-fox.png), so the cue partly reads as "his ring
went red". Fix direction: while a telegraph is live on a party member, dim that ring's band (or draw
the decal's hoop slightly larger than the ring) so the Ember hoop is unambiguous.
