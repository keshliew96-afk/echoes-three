# Critic verdict (round 3) — Act-1 arena environment

VERDICT: REJECT

## Summary

REJECT. Verified all 6 criteria with 24 of my own captures (captures/kx-*.png) plus measured crops. Criteria 1, 3, 4 and 6 pass cleanly; criterion 2 passes with one broken emitter; criterion 5 FAILS outright — props have no contact shadow at all. Passing detail: ground flatness is 1.6-1.9% of 8x8 blocks (bar is <20%) with a dirt path, moss/leaf/crack decals, 520-720 instanced grass tufts and 46-82 flowers in every variant (captures/kx-v1.png, kx-v2.png, kx-v3.png). Walls measure one-to-two value steps darker than adjoining floor on all four edges — N floor 48 -> wall 30, E floor 88 -> wall 39.5, S floor 70 -> wall 42, W floor 55 -> wall 32. The corruption monolith is the only violet in frame (a 16x9 violet-pixel histogram puts 801+1038 px in the monolith cells and <=12 px in every other cell) and its veins peak at #aa89ce, correct hue for #B79CF0 though ~15% dark. 10-11 distinct prop types ring the edges (torch stake, lantern post, crate, barrel, fallen log, stump, fence post, stone slab, boulder cluster, monolith, bush) with the center ~85% open. Gameplay is intact: teleport/WASD/dodge/RMB-bolt all work, 3 hits + 1 kill + hitstop + damage numerals + splat decal in captures/kx-fight.png, and zero PAGEERROR/REQFAIL across all 24 captures. 161-164 fps sustained in all three variants, identical to graybox (163.9) on headless SwiftShader. Environment is alive: 8-13% of pixels change per 400ms from motes and flame flicker. The blocking problems are the missing prop shadows plus a crushed value range (0.008% of pixels above luma 200 vs 1.4% in docs/reference/pass-the-fear.png) and a warm:cool ratio measured at 22-37 warm : 62-77 cool, the inverse of the 70:30 Act-1 ratio the brief and the claimed goal both call for.

## Failures (each must be fixed and re-measured)

### F1

CRITERION 5 HARD FAIL — zero contact blob shadows under props (only the player has one). Measured grass luminance directly below each prop against grass flanking it at the same y: crates in captures/kx-v1-n.png (x570-700,y195-275) read under=77.0 vs left=95.8 / right=53.4, i.e. exactly on the ambient gradient, no darkening; barrels in captures/kx-v1-w.png (x395-520,y300-375) read under=56.9 vs right=58.9 — identical. By contrast the player capsule in the same frame reads under=42.7 vs 60.6/58.9 beside, a clear 29% drop. Visible unambiguously in the 6x crop of the crates (kx-v1-n.png region x540-760,y180-310): the crate cluster's base meets bright green grass with a hard black outline and no ground darkening whatsoever — the props read as stickers pasted on a lawn. Same for the stump at kx-v1-w.png x600-660,y50-95 and the whole SE cluster at kx-se-mark.png x1050-1300,y460-680. The debug API reports propShadows:46-49, so the shadow objects exist but render invisibly — they are almost certainly sized to the prop footprint and fully occluded by the prop, or at ~0 opacity. FIX: give every prop a blob shadow whose radius is 1.3-1.6x the prop's ground footprint so it reads outside the silhouette, opacity ~0.35-0.45, softened radial falloff, drawn on the ground plane with polygonOffset so it does not z-fight; match the falloff the player capsule already uses.

### F2

REFERENCE-BAR FAIL — the frame has no value range; it reads as uniform murk. Whole-image histograms: captures/kx-v1.png max luma 239 (that is the HUD fps text), 0.043% of pixels above luma 160 and 0.008% above 200; kx-v2.png 0.052%/0.008%; kx-v3.png 0.049%/0.008%. 99.8% of every frame sits below luma 144 — only 9 of 16 luminance buckets are used at all. The binding reference docs/reference/pass-the-fear.png measures 3.418% above 160 and 1.427% above 200 with all 16 buckets populated. Consequence: the warm 'light pools' are diffuse yellow-green smudges (peak floor luma ~110-120 inside a pool, e.g. kx-v1-n.png x300-420,y230-300) rather than readable pools, and nothing in the scene reads as an emissive core except a ~14px flame tip. FIX: raise the key/directional intensity so open lit floor lands near luma 130-150 while shadowed floor stays 45-60 (currently the whole floor lives in 45-90); push flame/lantern emissive material color past 1.0 so UnrealBloomPass (threshold 0.85) actually blooms them; and back off the vignette, which is crushing the bottom corners of kx-v1.png (x0-250,y700-900 measures luma 15-25) to near-black.

### F3

BRIEF VIOLATION — warm:cool light ratio is inverted. BUILD_BRIEF section 19.3 and the block's own claimed goal both specify ~70:30 warm:cool for Act-1 (warm hemisphere/directional key + cool fill). Hue-classified pixel counts: captures/kx-v1.png warm 28.8% / cool 70.5%; kx-v2.png warm 37.2% / cool 62.2%; kx-v3.png warm 22.3% / cool 77.1%. Every frame reads as a night forest under a blue-teal wash with small amber pools, not the 'uneasy pastoral woodland' the brief describes; the brief also asks for greens at 55-65% HSV saturation, and the open floor in kx-v3.png (x200-800,y400-800) is a desaturated blue-green. FIX: warm the hemisphere/key (amber-lifted, dominant) and reduce the cool fill to a rim/fill role so the base grass reads as saturated warm green, reserving the blue-teal for actual shadow pockets.

### F4

MODELING DEFECT — the stone-slab prop has a stray pale unlit quad detached from its body. Two instances confirmed: captures/kx-v1-s.png at x652-695,y608-642 and captures/kx-se-mark.png at x1213-1240,y471-487. At 9x (kx-v1-s.png region x630-750,y590-680) it is a flat mauve-grey rectangle with an outline on three sides, separated from the slab body by a 1-2px strip of visible grass — it reads as a broken face or a mis-sized outline hull floating on the ground. The slab body itself is also the single largest flat fill in any frame: one uniform #6a5c58-ish plane roughly 200x70px with a single thin dark line, no moss, no chipping, no bevel — it reads as an untextured placeholder box rather than a dry-stone slab. FIX: remove/reattach the stray quad, and give the slab a top-face value break plus edge chipping so it reads as stone.

### F5

COMPOSITION DEFECT — edge prop clusters interpenetrate instead of composing. In captures/kx-se-mark.png (region x950-1300,y420-700, seen at 3x) the crate's top-right corner is buried inside the stump, and the barrel at x1085-1130,y590-655 is buried inside the crate; in captures/kx-v1-e.png at x1190-1330,y490-650 the crate cluster clips through the wall base with a jagged seam where crate, grass and outside-rock all meet. In captures/kx-v1-se.png at x600-790,y540-670 a crate, a torch and four loose fence poles pile on top of each other on the wall line. The result reads as a dumped heap, not the composed edge dressing the reference camps show. FIX: add a footprint-radius rejection test when scattering edge props so silhouettes touch but do not intersect, and clamp props to the inside face of the wall collision line.

### F6

DECAL DEFECT — 'crack/vine' decals render as hair-thin uniform black curves that read as scratches on the lens. Instances: captures/kx-v1.png x830-960,y620-790 and x1380-1450,y400-450; captures/kx-v2.png x640-710,y565-650 and x500-590,y675-730; captures/kx-v3.png x1380-1500,y585-635. At 6x (kx-v1.png region x830-1000,y620-790) the mark is a constant ~2-3px width, near-pure black, with no taper, no light rim and no color — it does not read as a crack (needs a light rim on one side) or as a vine (needs green and leaf nodes). FIX: taper the stroke, tint it toward the ground hue rather than pure black, and either add a 1px light rim for cracks or leaf sprites for vines.

### F7

EMITTER DEFECT — the south-edge torch renders its flame occluded by its own post. captures/kx-v1-s.png at x425-465,y555-650 (7x crop of region x380-520,y540-660): the flame billboard is almost entirely hidden behind the leaning torch shaft and reads as a clipped cream sliver rather than a flame. The same prop type renders correctly on the N, E and W edges (white-hot core + amber body + ember specks, e.g. kx-v1-w.png x285-350,y655-735). The ground glow pool is still present so the criterion is not lost outright, but one of the 7 emitters reads broken. FIX: offset the flame billboard toward the camera along the view vector, or stand the torch upright so no camera side hides its own flame.

### F8

ART NOTE (not a hard fail, will bite at final polish) — the ground is per-pixel dithered micro-noise, which BUILD_BRIEF section 19.3 explicitly forbids ('Flat fills; gradients only for large light falloff. No micro-texture'). Visible at 5x in captures/kx-v1-e.png region x1020-1220,y350-510 and at 6x in kx-v1.png x830-1000,y620-790: chunky blue-green pepper noise plus soft navy blobs that read as mold on the grass rather than dappled shade, and the texture is visibly under-resolved and blurry next to the crisply shaded props. Also: variants 1 and 3 are only marginally distinguishable — both are a single diagonal dirt path across green grass with the monolith at top-right and a torch top-left; only variant 2 (crossroads) reads as a different room at a glance. Consider changing monolith placement, path width and edge-prop vocabulary per variant, not just path geometry.
