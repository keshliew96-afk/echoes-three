# Critic verdict (round 3) — Chibi party characters

VERDICT: REJECT

## Summary

REJECT. I re-captured the block myself (17 captures under captures/crit-*, all exit 0, v0.2.5, 164 fps, zero page errors) and judged pixels only. What genuinely lands: the four are separable in a 50%-scale greyscale strip (captures/crit-grey50.png) and at play distance (captures/crit-zc-grey.png) — bell+vertical staff / wide blob+square block / small wedge+diagonal line+pointed ears / tall body+long upright ears; head ratio holds (scene projection 42.0/43.0/42.0/42.5%, my own pixel measure of the healer in captures/crit-base.png: dome y≈362 → chin y≈440 = 78 px head over ear-top y≈349 → ground contact y≈538 = 189 px, 41.3%); ink is a consistent ~2 px Void Charcoal line (runs of 2-3 px measured at crit-cam-game.png y=420 x308-309, y=470 x305-306); all four rings hit their EXACT accent hex (#33513C @430,495 · #6B6157 @735,495 · #6B2E3A @891,501 · #6E7A3F @1181,495); idle animates 1.7-2.8% of frame with the diff mask (captures/crit-idle-mask-crop.png) lighting up ear tips, the fox tail and the staff; hurt flinches (archer centroid recoils 20 px, 1230→1210); downed collapses horizontal with the accent driven to #251D17 against Void Charcoal #221F1B — unmistakable beside the standing pair; and party warmth passes cleanly (fur sat 0.32-0.47 vs ground 0.10-0.11, no red-orange, no violet). But five criteria fail on rendered pixels. The Tank has NO readable identity ring at any camera — the single most important class signal in a 4-party top-down game, and the exact defect the previous round claimed to fix. The Swordsman's sword vanishes for most of its attack clip and the attack has less amplitude than its own idle. Glints are so oversized that the Tank and Archer read as glowing white slit-eyes — the treatment the brief reserves for ENEMIES. The walk/attack forward lean runs ~35-45° instead of the spec'd ~8°, folding the fox's ears into one dark spike and hiding every face at the real play camera. And the healer's staff gem — the party's only emitter — is a flat off-palette chip with zero glow halo, failing REFERENCE_BAR check 2. Seven further defects (downed rings staying full-chroma instead of Bone, props vanishing on downed, Hearth-Amber drift on the Tank, an outline gap along the healer's hem, the cone-shaped fox tail, the arm impaling the archer's bow, and critter ink not matching an environment that ships with no ink at all) are itemized below.

## Failures (each must be fixed and re-measured)

### F1

CRITERION 3 FAIL — Tank identity ring is invisible at every camera. captures/crit-bases3x.png (crop of crit-base.png x280-1290, y470-580): the Healer's green, Swordsman's maroon and Archer's olive ellipses all read instantly; the Tank (region x555-760, y495-560) shows only a grey blob indistinguishable from its own drop shadow. Confirmed at 4x contrast stretch in captures/crit-tankring-stretch.png (crit-base.png x540-780, y480-580 mapped 85→150): still no ellipse, just a soft halo merged with the shadow. Same failure at the real gameplay elevation (captures/crit-cam-game.png, x570-750 y480-560), from behind (captures/crit-back.png, x565-760 y480-560), when overlapping (captures/crit-overlap.png, x620-880 y430-540) and in greyscale at play distance (captures/crit-zc-grey.png, left figure). Measured: ring peak #6B6157 (lum 98) on ground #8F8780 (lum 136) — same hue family (30° vs 28°), sat 0.19 vs 0.11, and the contact shadow darkens the identical footprint. FIX: the Tank's accent IS a warm grey, so the raw hex can never carry identity here. Render the Tank ring as a legibility-matched derivative — lift value ~25% and push chroma to sat ≥0.35 (e.g. a #A79385 rim stroke ~2 px wide around a #6B6157 fill), and offset/shrink the contact shadow so it stops sitting on the ring. Match the other three rings' legibility, not their construction.

### F2

CRITERION 4 FAIL (cast) — the Swordsman's sword disappears for most of the attack clip and the swing has no amplitude. captures/crit-cast2_05.png, _06.png, _07.png (crops captures/crit-fox-c05.png, crit-fox-c06.png, crit-fox-c07.png and the wide captures/crit-fox-wide06.png covering x800-1120, y300-580): the blade is entirely absent — only a ~10 px tan grip nub survives at approximately (995,455). The blade is visible only in the rest-adjacent frames (crit-cast2_04.png), i.e. the class-defining diagonal line is gone through the beat that should read as the attack. Amplitude across all 12 frames of the clip: swordsman centroid x moves only 941.8→945.6 px and silhouette top 367→375 px — SMALLER than its own idle swing (idle top ranges 366→378 in captures/crit-idle_0*.png). The other three do have a wind-up/release (healer top 355→327 px at crit-cast2_05, archer 330→315, tank centroid x 657.8→663.1). FIX: constrain the swing so the blade axis never aligns with the view vector — keep it in the camera plane as a diagonal at all times; add a real anticipation (blade cocked back over the shoulder) and follow-through, and a smear/trail so the release reads as an event per REFERENCE_BAR check 10.

### F3

CRITERION 2 FAIL (eyes) — the white glints are so large the eyes read as glowing slits, which is the reserved ENEMY treatment. captures/crit-face1.png (Tank closeup): the glints at roughly (375-425, 368-395) and (498-545, 368-392) occupy ~45% of each visible eye, and the dark bean is completely swallowed by the badger head-stripe, so what reads is two glowing white crescents inside black bands — menacing, not friendly. captures/crit-face3.png (Archer closeup): glints at (378-408, 388-410) and (478-508, 388-410) are roughly half of each eye, giving a rolled-back stare. BUILD_BRIEF §19.2 specifies 'large dark bean/dot eyes... plus a TINY white glint — party/friendly-exclusive treatment' and reserves 'angular eye slits or faint glints' for enemies; the Tank currently wears the enemy read. FIX: shrink the glint to ≤20% of eye area and park it upper-left; on the Tank add a light fur/sclera ring around the eye (or narrow/lighten the stripe where it crosses the eye) so the dark bean separates from the head-stripe and the eye mass, not the glint, is what the viewer sees.

### F4

CRITERION 4 FAIL (walk) — the forward lean is over-cranked to ~35-45° instead of the spec'd ~8°, destroying the silhouette and face reads. captures/crit-walk_03.png, crop captures/crit-foxwalk3x.png (x880-1040, y330-530): the fox pitches until the camera sees its crown — the face is squashed to the bottom edge of the head and the two black-tipped pointed ears (the class's silhouette key per criterion 1) fold together into a single dark spike. captures/crit-w3.png (x280-1340, y310-580) shows all four bowing the same way; the archer's tall upright ears splay and merge. Worst at the real play camera: captures/crit-cam-game.png — every face is hidden behind the crown at rest, so any additional pitch is unrecoverable. The clip does bob and lean (healer centroid x oscillates 358.5↔365.2, swordsman centroid y 461.0→469.8), so the motion exists — it is simply tuned past the point where the characters stay storybook critters. FIX: clamp body pitch to ≤10° and carry the walk on hip bob + shoulder counter-rotation, with the head counter-rotated to stay near-vertical so ears and eyes survive the whole cycle.

### F5

CRITERION 2 FAIL (outline weight vs environment) — the critters carry a hard ~2 px Void Charcoal ink line while the shipped environment carries none, so the party will read as stickers pasted on a painting. Critter ink measured as dark runs of 2-3 px at silhouette edges (captures/crit-cam-game.png y=420 x308-309 and x397-398, y=470 x305-306; captures/crit-base.png y=400 x310 and x395-396). captures/crit-arena.png (?scene=arena&variant=1, full frame): grass tufts, stumps, boulders, the fence, the lantern post, the violet monolith and the walls all render as soft painted masses — scanning rows y=60, y=90 and y=700 across x600-900 returns only shadow/foliage masses, zero line runs at any prop edge. REFERENCE_BAR check 3 requires 'dark outline or strong rim separation' on the environment too. FIX: one side has to move before integration — add matching ~2 px hull ink to the env prop meshes (preferred, it is the reference-bar behaviour), or drop the critter ink to a rim-light separation. As shipped the two blocks do not match and this criterion cannot be claimed.

### F6

CRITERION 6 / REFERENCE-BAR CHECK 2 FAIL — the Healer's staff gem, the party's only light emitter, is off-palette and has no glow. Measured at captures/crit-base.png (404-409, 399-406): #C0EEA0 / hsv(95, 0.33, 0.93) — a pale chartreuse, not the spec'd Bright Heal #5FE873 / hsv(133, 0.59, 0.91). Visible at 3x in captures/crit-healer3x.png (crop x285-475, y330-570) as a flat outlined chip with a hard ink border and ZERO additive halo or bloom bleed into the surrounding pixels; identical in every cast frame (captures/crit-cast2_00..11.png), so it never brightens on cast either. BUILD_BRIEF §19.2 requires 'a glowing gem (Bright Heal glow sprite; the gem brightens on cast)' and REFERENCE_BAR check 2 requires a glow halo on EVERY emitter. It is also the one place a reserved hue lands on a critter, so it must at minimum be the exact reserved hex. FIX: retint the gem core to #5FE873, add an additive radial glow sprite sized ~3x the gem above the bloom threshold (0.85), and ramp its intensity on the cast release.

### F7

Downed rings keep full-chroma class accent instead of Bone, contradicting the downed read the body desaturation establishes. captures/crit-downed.png: the downed Healer's ring still measures #33513C at (300,575) and the downed Swordsman's still #6B2E3A at (838,520) and (850,545) — as saturated as the two STANDING critters' rings in the same frame, while their bodies have gone to #251D17. BUILD_BRIEF §19.1 assigns Bone #C9C2B3 to 'Common rarity, downed/neutral rings'. FIX: lerp the ring toward Bone on the same curve that lerps the body toward charcoal.

### F8

Props vanish on downed instead of being dropped. captures/crit-downed.png and the 2.2x crop captures/crit-downedhealer.png (x240-540, y420-610): the Healer's staff is completely absent — not in the paw, not on the ground, not behind the body — and the Swordsman's sword is likewise gone (x820-1080, y430-580). The mitten paw is still posed as if gripping. This pops the third of the three masses out of existence at the exact moment the player most needs to identify who went down. FIX: rotate the prop with the body and lay it on the ground beside the critter (or leave it in the paw), never hide it.

### F9

Palette drift — Hearth Amber lands on the Tank, where §19.1 reserves it for positive UI / Legendary / selection / Victory / camp fire. captures/crit-base.png (660,522): #DBAE5A, hsv(39, 0.59, 0.86) on the belly buckle, plus a second gold stud clipping out from behind the shoulder block at approximately (738,462) — visible at 5x in captures/crit-tankjoint4x.png (crop x680-790, y420-530), where it reads as a stray gold sliver half-buried in the body. The buckle is the single highest-value pixel on the character and pulls the eye to the belly instead of the face. FIX: retint both to Bone #C9C2B3 or a darker leather value, and either seat the stud fully in the block or delete it.

### F10

Outline gap + ground-plane clipping along the Healer's cloak hem. captures/crit-hem6x.png (crit-base.png x300-420, y520-565 at 6x) and captures/crit-hem-stretch.png (x305-395, y528-552 at 8x, mapped 10→90): the Void Charcoal ink runs along the hem only to about x=345, then STOPS — the front third of the hem (x≈348-395, y≈536-541) meets the identity ring with a bare colour boundary and no ink line, and a dashed remnant of hull pixels pokes intermittently through the ground/ring plane at approximately (333-370, 537-542). This is the inverted-hull outline being clipped where it expands below y=0. FIX: raise the hull's bottom ring above the ground plane or give the ink hull a depth bias/polygon offset so it can never be clipped by the floor or the ring quad.

### F11

Swordsman tail reads as a rigid traffic cone, not a white-tipped brush. captures/crit-back.png (region x955-1025, y485-535): the tail is a hard-edged cone sticking straight out horizontally from the hip with a pointed tip, no curve, no fluff, and the Bone white tip is not visible in the silhouette from behind; from the front (captures/crit-fox-wide06.png, left side x830-900) it reads as a pale flipper. BUILD_BRIEF §19.2 calls for a 'white-tipped tail' as the fox's species tell. FIX: curve the tail with a lathe/tapered spline, thicken the base, and place the Bone tip where the silhouette can see it from the play camera.

### F12

Archer's bow reads as a hoop/handbag and the arm impales it. captures/crit-face3.png (region x160-360, y410-620): the paw is rendered as a long horizontal tube that passes straight THROUGH the bow's opening and pokes out the far side at approximately x=180-200, so the arm appears skewered by the bow; the arc is held flat with the string chord inside it, giving a closed oval rather than the 'open negative-space arc' §19.2 asks for. Also at (555-590, 340-490) in the same capture the quiver clips into the head with a stray green nub emerging at approximately (575,350). FIX: grip the bow at the riser with the paw IN FRONT of the arc, rotate the bow plane so the arc + string read as an open C from the play camera, and reseat the quiver so it clears the skull.
