## Failures

### F1

BLOCKING — Ally-incoming damage numerals use the OUTGOING colour. §17 Zone 3: "incoming (party) = Bruise Umber with Parchment outline"; §19.1 reserves Bruise Umber #4E463F for "Party-damage numerals only". Measured directly off the live DOM (tools/actions/ck-t3-numerals.json, reproduced in 3 independent runs, captures/ck-t3-numerals, ck-t3b-numerals, ck-final-num): a boar's 8-damage contact hit on the PLAYER renders `rgb(78, 70, 63)` = #4E463F (correct), while three consecutive isolated 8-damage contact hits on an ALLY render `rgb(244, 239, 230)` = #F4EFE6 Parchment — identical to outgoing damage. Same damage value, so it is purely victim-kind driven. Visible in captures/ck-s3-hurt.png (the "8" over the badger at the revive ring is Parchment) and captures/ck-y-mantis.png (the mantis's "10" on a party member). Effect: for the whole of every fight this block creates, the player cannot tell "my badger took 8" from "the badger dealt 8". Root cause is the hit->numeral mapping in src/scenes/graybox.js (the handler the arena wraps): `kind: ev.kind === 'player' ? 'incoming' : 'damage'` — ally victims emit `kind:'ally'` and fall through to the outgoing style. FIX: treat ally victims as incoming, e.g. `kind: (ev.kind === 'player' || ev.kind === 'ally') ? 'incoming' : 'damage'`, then re-run tools/actions/ck-t3-numerals.json and assert the ally numeral reads rgb(78, 70, 63).

### F2

ADVISORY (not a reject; brief-literal reading is defensible) — damage-flinch revive breaks are OFF by default and only reachable via `cmd('reviveFlinchBreak', true)`. The stated criterion asks for a channel that resets when the reviver "is damaged into a flinch"; BUILD_BRIEF §10's break list is closed (nonzero move, dodge, skill cast, fresh attack press, reviver Downed) and damage is not on it, so the brief wins and I did not reject. If the intended contract really is "damage breaks", the flag needs to default on and §10 needs an amendment; leave it as-is otherwise.

### F3

ADVISORY (polish) — on an AI-owned channel the 2x reverse drain is effectively invisible. Measured in captures/ck-n-flinch: `revive_break reason:"flinch"` at tick 669 followed by `revive_start` at tick 670 — the §12 unconditional-eagerness AI re-claims the body on the very next tick, so the §10/§17 "reverse drain at 2x" reads for ~17 ms (1 tick, progress 73->71) before the ring snaps back to 0 and refills. Same shape after a move-break when the player keeps E held (drain visible for 8 ticks, ck-j). The drain only reads properly when the reviver actually leaves (rally: full 122->0 over 61 ticks, ck-o). Consider a short minimum drain-hold before a re-claim so the interrupt is legible.

### F4

OBSERVATION (no action needed, recorded so the next critic does not re-flag it) — `sundering_nova` (archer nova 20/8 s/1.1 u/cap 4) never fired in a 45 s sustained four-boar fight (11/12 kit skills did, tools/actions/ck-ac-kits.json). The archer's 3.6 u stand-off means a 1.1 u burst radius is almost never satisfied, which is consistent with §7's "AI casts a kit skill when: target in shape range". Also: the `rally` event payload lists downed allies in its `allies:[...]` array (captures/ck-o-rallybreak, allies:[1,2,3] with 3 downed) — cosmetic only, the downed body does not move.
