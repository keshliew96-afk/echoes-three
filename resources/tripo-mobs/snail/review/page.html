In the game
Lantern Snail 48 health, very slow (0.6 u/s). No attack of its own: it keeps 3.5 u back from the party and creeps to the middle of its pack. Every 240 ticks its lantern mends every other foe within 3.2 u (not bosses) for 12; the lantern swells for the 30 ticks before. The mend is not an attack, so it has no Ember warning. The Sunken Mill.

Open choices

  • Your lantern burns yellow; the game's is indigo. The game's lantern bulb is indigo, its one tell. Yours is a warm yellow lantern, so I kept its yellow and swell that, and drew the mend itself in the game's indigo. Say if you want the glass turned indigo.
  • The lantern is lashed to the shell, not on a stalk. The game's lantern hangs on a stalk that lifts and thrusts out. Yours is tied on top of the shell, so it tips back and forward on its lashings instead.
  • The head draws into the shell. Your snail can pull its head in, so it does when it is hurt, when it spawns and when it dies.
  • Mine. The look and the eye stalks in the idle, the turn rate, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The head draws in by shrinking toward the foot, as the model has no room inside the shell; the front of the shell stretches a little when it does.
  • The rope and moss are fixed to the shell; the rope does not swing.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the lantern's swell and flare, the indigo ring and sigil, the indigo streaks and motes on the foes it mends, and the lantern going out. They are kept dim on purpose.
