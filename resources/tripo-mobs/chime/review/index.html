Open choices

  • The bell. The game's bell is the wraith's body: big, hung below the shroud, with an indigo clapper as the tell. Your bell is small, in the chest. I swing it on the game's numbers and light it faintly on the wind-up as the tell. A bigger bell would read the toll better at game zoom.
  • The arms. The game's sleeves hold a yoke the bell hangs from. Yours hang free, so on the wind-up they lift and spread as the game's do, with nothing in their hands.
  • The choke. A stun mid-swing chokes the toll in the game; the game shows it only by the ring going away. I can add a muffled stop if you want one.
  • Mine. The turn rate, the idle's look round, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in and the death.

What is rough

  • The shroud is one welded piece with the arms, so it bends by height down three bones; its torn strips do not flutter on their own.
  • The bell's clapper is part of the bell, so it swings with it rather than striking the inside.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember ring, the bell's faint glow, the toll's sound rings, a pale tint on the hero while slowed, and dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
