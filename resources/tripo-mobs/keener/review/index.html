Open choices

  • The hood stays on. The game's keener throws its hood back to keen. Your hood is one piece with the head, so the head tips back inside it instead. If you want the hood to fall back, the model needs the hood as a separate piece, and I can redo it.
  • The arms spread less than the game's. The arms come out of the sleeves' rags, and spreading them as wide as the game's figure stretches the shoulders, so the spread is three quarters of the game's.
  • Mine. The turn rate, the idle's bow and looks, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in and the death's fall.

What is rough

  • The rags of the robe bend with the body in three bands; single rags do not flutter on their own.
  • The rags nearest the arms move with the arms, so the sleeves lift with them.
  • The eyes are painted, so they do not light; the mouth's indigo glow is a point drawn by the page.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the Ember cone, the mouth's swell and flare, the cone's Ember flash, the three sound-arcs, the ash and its stain, and a pale tint on the hero while slowed. They are kept dim on purpose.
