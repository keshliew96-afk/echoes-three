In the game
Barrow Ram 90 health. It walks at 1.1 u/s and turns at only 90° a second, walking where its horns point, so the party wins by flanking it. Its horn guard blocks every shot that arrives within 55° either side of its facing (arcs, novas, zones and heals still land). Horn Slam: a cone 1.8 u long at a half-angle of 50°, marked by a 60-tick Ember cone, 18 damage, cooldown 270 ticks. It takes only 0.3 of any knockback. Level III.

Open choices

  • The guard glow is on your horns. The game's ram carries its indigo tell on a bone shield disc where its horns meet. Yours has no disc, so the horns carry a faint indigo glow that flares on every blocked shot, with the game's soft indigo glow in front of them.
  • The body squash is softened. The game squashes its ram's body hard on each slam (19% wider). Here the legs hang from the body, so a full squash would push the hooves through the floor; I widen it by 40% of the game's amount and leave its height alone.
  • Mine. The idle on top of the breath, the step length, the hind step on the heave, the block's brace, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The jaw is part of the skull, so its mouth does not open.
  • The iron rings are fixed to the horns; they do not swing.
  • The eyes are painted in your texture, so they keep their glow and do not dim when it dies.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the guard's indigo glow and its flare, the Ember cone, the hero's shot, the slam on the hero, the dust and bone chips off the horns, and hoof dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
