The moves, and where their timing comes from

MoveWhat it doesTiming
IdleThe abdomen breathes, the fangs work twice, it sways a little, the head looks round to its left, and a front leg taps the floor.6 s loop (mine; the game's idle only breathes)
SkitterThe game's gait: legs 1 and 3 of one side step with legs 2 and 4 of the other, the body low and pitched a little forward, rocking side to side, the abdomen bobbing behind.Game: 1.7 u/s. Here a step every 0.1 s.
Turn left, turn rightThe same two sets of legs, stepping round on the spot, the head leading.225° a second (my number: the game turns it instantly).
BiteThree bites on a hero who stays in reach, the game's poses key for key. The right fang lunges with no wind-up, twisting the body across; then the left fang the other way; then the pounce: it rears onto its back legs with both front legs raised and the fangs spread wide, and drives down onto the hero, the abdomen kicking up behind. On each bite the front legs step in, the body stretches long and low on the contact, holds two frames and snaps back, and it holds the next wind-up until the bite is ready again.Game: a bite every second on each hero. Contacts at 2.5 frames, 1.0 s and 2.0 s; strike 2.5 frames, contact 2, snap 2.5, settle 5; the wind-up pulls back 4 frames before a strike. Recover 0.35 s. 3.5 s in all.
HurtThe body jolts back and squashes, the abdomen bounces and the fangs flare. Plays on top of any move, so planted feet stay planted.0.4 s
SpawnGrows in low, rises on its legs, flares its fangs and tests the floor with both front legs.Game: grow in 7 frames, about 10% over. The rest is mine.

Open choices

  • Triangles. It has 3,809, over the 1,500 small-mob budget (it is under the 4,000 foe budget). I did not trim it; say if you want it cut down or re-exported at a lower face limit.
  • The floating yellow piece. It hangs in the air above the body, not joined to anything. The game's spider carries an indigo hourglass on its back as its tell. I can keep it riding the body as it is, move it onto the abdomen as the mark, or remove it. On this page it glows faintly violet while a bite is wound up.
  • Death. The game bursts it into two Broodlings. Here it drops and curls up. When the Broodlings come in, the death could end in a burst instead.
  • Mine. The turn rate, the idle's extras, the spawn after the grow-in, the hurt and the death.

What is rough

  • The webbing on the abdomen is painted on and moves with it as one piece.
  • The mouth is closed; each bite is the fangs spreading and the body lunging.
  • Switching from Skitter into the Bite blends in 0.18 s, so a foot can slide a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: pale sparks at the fangs on each bite, dust under the feet, and the violet glow on the yellow piece. They are kept dim on purpose.
