Open choices

  • Both hands ring the bell. The game's acolyte rings with its right hand and hushes with a finger to its mouth with the left. Yours holds the bell in both hands, so both arms lift it and ring it, and there is no finger to the mouth.
  • The bell rises to its face, not over its head. The game lifts the bell overhead for the hush. Your sleeves are part of the robe, so lifting the hands higher tears the robe's shape; I lift it before its face.
  • The bell is cut free. In your model the bell was joined to the robe's front. I cut it free so it can swing out; the seam it leaves is behind the bell and only shows when the bell is lifted.
  • No indigo clasp. The game's sacristan has an indigo clasp on its breast as its tell; your model has none. Say if you want one added on the page.
  • Mine. The look in the idle, the stride, the turn rate, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The sleeves stretch when the hands lift (the rap's wind-up most), because the arms are carved into the robe.
  • No feet: the hem swings and the body sways, but the robe glides over the floor.
  • The eyes are painted in your texture, so they do not dim when it dies; the rope cord is fixed to the robe.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the pale ring, the bell's pale shimmer, the note's rings, the threads and veils on the hushed, the Ember cone, the rap's bronze burst and stone chips, and the death's dust and last ring. They are kept dim on purpose.
