Gravewisp from Tripo, rigged · version 1

The Ashen Barrow's warder: your export, with a skeleton and eight moves added by hand. It has no attack of its own. It hangs back behind another foe, gathers, and casts an indigo tether from its lantern; while the tether holds, that foe takes no damage. When the tether fades it snaps back and drifts until it can gather again. The hover, the lean, the tail's swish and the ward and snap poses use the game's numbers frame for frame. Pick a move, drag to turn it, and judge it. Nothing in the game has changed.

It came through whole, as one piece: a pale blue ghost with a flame-tipped head, two hollow white eyes, stubby arms, an iron collar with a lantern hanging on a short chain, and a tail that tapers in waves. The lantern was fused to the chest, so I cut it free and closed the chest behind it; it now swings on its own bone.

In the game
Gravewisp 12 health, no attack. It tethers the nearest other foe within 5.5 u (never a boss, a broodling or another wisp); while the tether holds, that foe takes no damage. The tether lasts 240 ticks and snaps if the two drift 6.5 u apart; then it needs 150 ticks to gather a new one. It drifts at 2.3 u/s, 1.6 u behind its ward on the side away from the party, and flees a hero inside 2.2 u. Level III, the Ashen Barrow.

Open choices

  • Over budget. It has 4,028 triangles (3,936 as you sent it, plus the caps where I cut the lantern free) against the 1,500 small-mob budget (the game counts it as a small flier). It could be decimated in Tripo to about 1,500, or kept, since it is a single foe and well under the 4,000 foe budget. Your call.
  • The lantern is the core. The game's wisp glows from an indigo core in its chest. Yours carries a lantern, so I light the lantern's glass in the game's indigo and drive it with the game's core numbers, about half as bright.
  • Mine. The turn rate, the idle's look, the lantern's swing, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in and the death.

What is rough

  • The arms are one bone each; the tail bends on three.
  • Where the lantern was cut free there is a faint mark on the chest, from the texture painted under it; you can see it when the lantern swings out.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the lantern's glow, its halo and floor light, the take's flare and closing ring on the ward, the thread with motes running down it, the ward's shimmer, and the snap's flash and sparks. They are kept dim on purpose.
