Open choices

  • The candle tip shows while it tunnels. The game's mole goes fully under with only the mound showing. Here it tunnels a little higher, so the top of the candle pokes out of the mound with its flame; through the ring it is fully under, as in the game. Say if you want it fully under while it tunnels too.
  • Surfaced for 1 s. The game keeps it up for 2 s; it is shortened here so the three bursts come round quickly.
  • The hero waits inside the ring. The game commits when the hero is within 0.3 u; the stand-in stands at the ring's edge so it does not sit inside the mole.
  • Emerge plays all three bursts in a row. The game picks one each time.
  • Spawn is on the floor. The game's mole spawns already burrowed; the page grows it in standing so you can see it.
  • Mine. The idle, the rake while tunnelling, the turn rate, the dig back in, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The planks and the candle are fixed to its back; they do not rock on their own.
  • The five claws on each paw move as one; they spread and curl together.
  • The mound is a plain earth hump with six clods, drawn by the page after the game's.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models. Your model has no glow of its own, so the indigo tell is lit on its claw tips.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the mound and its furrow, the Ember ring, the quake, earth and clods thrown by the burst, the hit on the hero, the earth raked back by the dig, and a small candle flame. They are kept dim on purpose.
