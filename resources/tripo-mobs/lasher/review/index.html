Open choices

  • The vine stretches. Its own length is 1.7 u; the lane is 5.4 u. Like the game's, it lays dead straight to the lane's end, so it stretches to about three times its length for the contact and the reel, its thorns drawn out with it. I can instead have it reach only its own length and say the lane is the danger.
  • Where the vine grows. The game's vine comes off the pod's face; this one grows from its right side. The coils keep the game's sides, but the backhand wraps round in front of the pod instead of starting from its left.
  • The jaws. The game's lid splits into two jaws over an indigo maw. Here the toothed rim's two halves are the jaws: they splay open on the coil and clap on the throw. The maw and eyes keep their yellow glow and brighten as the tell.
  • Death. It wilts and slumps where it stands (the standard falls and stays down; a plant on roots has nowhere to fall). The game pops it flat; I can build that instead.
  • Mine. The turn rate, the idle breath and look, the spawn and the death.

What is rough

  • Roots that touch are split between neighbouring root bones, so where two meet they bend together.
  • The leaves and flowers at the base ride the pod stiffly.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember lane, dust where the hook lands, the maw glow, and the hero dragged 1.8 u and walking back. They are kept dim on purpose.
