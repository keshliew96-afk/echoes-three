Open choices

  • The rear-ups are smaller than the game's. The game's toad rears on its hind legs; yours sits on its rump with a big belly, so I took the game's rear at 60% and let it rock back on its rump. On the heave its front feet still leave the floor.
  • No indigo glint in the eyes. The game's toad shows an indigo glint in its eyes as its one tell. Your eyes are orange with a cross slit, so I left them as painted. Say if you want the glint added.
  • The jaw opens to about half the game's gape. The lip is one surface with the throat, so a full gape tears it.
  • Mine. The throat flutter and the look in the idle, the turn rate, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The mouth opens only a little, and the corners of the mouth stretch when it does.
  • On the heave the hind heels push a little way into the floor as the body rocks up.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the spatter off the mouth, the glob and its Ember ring, the splash, and the slick. They are kept dim on purpose.
