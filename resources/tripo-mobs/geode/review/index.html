Open choices

  • The fists up. The game hauls a fist up beside the head. Your arms are huge and grown into the shoulders, so at the top of the coil the upper arm squeezes the shoulder's stone together. I can haul them less high if it reads better.
  • The glow. I light every violet crystal on the body as the game's tell, a low glow that brightens through the coil and flares on the slam. Your eyes are violet too, so they glow with it.
  • Mine. The stride, the turn rate, the idle's looks and fist lift, the spawn's floor beat, the hurt and the death.

What is rough

  • The model is one welded piece, so the crystals on the forearms and shoulders bend with the stone around them.
  • The jaw is cut on a line under the tusks, so it only opens a little.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember rings, the crystal glow, the shards, the patches, chips and dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
