Open choices

  • The core shows in its chest. The game's golem keeps its core in a crater in its back under five crust plates that are thrown back by the slam. Yours has its brightest seams across its chest and no plates, so the open core is the chest seams flaring white-gold for the 2 s, then cooling.
  • The fists rise beside its head, not overhead. The game rears with both fists overhead. Your arms are carved into huge shoulder boulders, and lifting them higher tears the shoulders, so the fists come up beside its head.
  • Upright, not a hunched heap. The game's golem is a low hunched heap on stubby legs. Yours stands tall, so I keep only a slight hunch.
  • No indigo shard. The game's golem has an indigo shard in its back as its tell; yours has a rake and bricks there instead. Say if you want a shard added on the page.
  • Mine. The shoulder roll in the idle, the stride, the turn rate, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The armpits and the undersides of the arms stretch when the fists go up, because the arms are carved into the shoulders.
  • The rake and bricks are fixed to its back; the rake does not rattle.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the Ember ring, the gold streaks of the fists coming down, the splash of molten slag, the chunks and smoke, the slag pool cooling from gold to dark, the slow on the hero, the core's flare and the heat drawn in with a puff of steam as the crust knits, the step dust, and the death's dust and chunks. They are kept dim on purpose.
