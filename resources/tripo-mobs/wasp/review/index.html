Open choices

  • Death. The standard says a foe falls and stays down, so it drops and lies on its side. The game pops every foe flat in the air instead; I can build that if you prefer.
  • The stab. The abdomen curls by the game's numbers. This abdomen is longer than the game's, so at the stab the stinger points back and a little down rather than at the floor. I can curl it deeper so the point hangs below the body.
  • The legs. The game folds six short legs under the thorax. These eight long legs hang under it, trail when it flies and tuck for the dive.
  • The tell. The game's stinger is indigo. Here the stinger glows a faint indigo that brightens through the coil; its own brown stays underneath.
  • Mine. The looks and antenna flicks in the hover, the turn rate, the tucked legs, the spawn flare and the death.

What is rough

  • Each wing is one stiff blade: it beats, rises and sweeps, but does not bend.
  • The wings beat 10 times a second; on a slow screen that can look like a flicker rather than a blur.
  • The stinger stretches along its length to show the game's thrust, so it thins a little as it grows.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember lane, the indigo glow, pale hit sparks and landing dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
