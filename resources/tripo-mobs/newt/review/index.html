Open choices

  • Its heat is in your cracks. The game's newt has a coal-bright belly, coals down its spine and a glowing tail tip. Yours glows in its cracks, spots and spines, so those carry the heat: steady, building through the crouch, brightest on the lunge, pulsing as it pants.
  • No indigo crown scale. The game's newt has a small indigo scale on its crown as its tell; your model has none. Say if you want one added on the page.
  • It idles in place. The game's newt never stands still; the idle is mine so you can see it.
  • Mine. The idle, the step, the turn rate, getting up after the rest, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The jaw is part of the head, so it does not open for the bite or pant at rest; the head nods instead.
  • The legs are short and nearly straight in your model, so the skitter stays low and quick with short steps.
  • The spines are fixed on the back; they do not flex on their own.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the heat in its cracks, the belly's glow on the floor, the Ember lane, the sparks lifting off it in the crouch, the gold streak behind the lunge, the bite on the hero, the skid's scuff, and the smoke as it pants. They are kept dim on purpose.
