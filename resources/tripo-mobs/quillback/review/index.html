Open choices

  • Size. I drew it 0.85 u long so the curled ball matches the game's rolling size. Standing, that makes it a little bigger than the game's (feet 0.29 u apart, the game's 0.24 u).
  • The crest. The game raises a separate crest of seven quills in the coil and flares its coat. This coat is one piece, so the whole coat swells (up to about 6%) instead. I can cut a crest of quills out onto their own bones if you want them to stand up.
  • The eyes. The game shuts its quillback's eyes as it tucks. These eyes are painted on the texture, so they stay open and show in the roll.
  • The tell. The game marks its quill tips indigo. Here the pale quill tips glow a faint indigo that brightens through the warning and fades in the daze.
  • Mine. The idle nosing and coat shake, the curled spawn, the turn rate and the death.

What is rough

  • Curled tight, the skin over the back of the neck stretches into a flat patch behind the head, which shows as it rolls.
  • The body is long, not a dome like the game's, so standing in the coil it rocks back and dips a third as far as the game's, to keep its rump off the floor. The full rock is kept once it is a ball.
  • Switching from Walk into a Charge blends in 0.18 s, so a foot can slide a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember warning lane on the floor, dust as it rolls, a pale hit spark and a plain stone wall for the crash. They are kept dim on purpose.
