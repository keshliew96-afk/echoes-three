Open choices

  • The petals. Your bud is one welded piece, so its five petals are bones laid round it by angle: they peel open from the top and stretch at the seams between them. I splay them half the game's angle so they do not tear. Separate petals in a new export could open as wide as the game's.
  • Size. Its height matches the game's at 1.1 u, but its roots spread half as far. Larger would match the roots and make it taller than the game's.
  • Mine. The turn rate, the spawn's unfurl, the creep's eight roots in two sets, the hurt's clench and the death.

What is rough

  • Opening, the bud shows no inside: the petals peel from a closed shell, so wide open it looks like a cup more than a flower.
  • The roots are modelled nearly straight, so they have little give: the creep's steps are short and quick.
  • The crystal leaves and shards ride the stem and roots and do not move on their own.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember ring, the core and petal glow, crystal shards and a violet ring on the snap, hit sparks and dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
