Gargoyle from Tripo, rigged · version 1

The Still Spire's sleeper: your export with the extra tail cut away, a skeleton and eleven moves added by hand. It waits as stone, perched on its haunches. When a hero comes near, the stone cracks behind an Ember ring and it bursts free. Awake, it rises and stalks, and it slams: up on its legs with both fists overhead while the ring fills in front of it, then down hard. Left alone, it settles back into stone. Pick a move, drag to turn it, and judge it. Nothing in the game has changed.

Open choices

  • The tail. I cut the one that curls round its right side and closed the cut. If you meant the other one, I can swap them.
  • Standing up. The game's gargoyle stands up on two legs when it wakes. Your model's legs are folded flat under it, so I raise it only part of the way, about 0.13 u, and lift its fists off the floor. I can raise it further, but the folded legs stretch past that.
  • The glow. The game lights indigo cracks across its stone. Your stone's cracks are painted into the texture, so I can't light them on their own, and the indigo glow is in the eyes instead.
  • Mine. The stride, the turn rate, the awake idle's looks, the hurt and the death.

What is rough

  • The model is one welded piece, so the arms pull the chest's stone with them when the fists go overhead.
  • Each wing is two stiff bones; the wing's ribs do not spread apart.
  • Where I cut the tail, the closed edge on the floor by its right foot is rough.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the two Ember rings, the eyes' glow, stone chips and dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
