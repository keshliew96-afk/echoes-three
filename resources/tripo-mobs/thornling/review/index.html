Open choices

  • Death. It topples onto its back and stays down (the standard). The game pops every foe flat; I can build that instead.
  • The crouch. The game drops the body 0.06 u on the stamp. These roots are half as long, so it drops half as far.
  • The bloom. The game's tiny buds grow 1.9 times as they bloom. This bud is already big, so it swells 1.5 times, and it glows a faint indigo, brightest through the root.
  • The patch. A dark bramble-green disc r 1.0 with pale thorn points, as the game colours it.
  • Mine. The idle looks and claw rub, the arm swings, the turn rate, the spawn pop and the death.

What is rough

  • The leaves on its head are stiff and ride the head; they do not flutter.
  • The sack rides the body and does not swing.
  • Each root foot moves as one stiff piece, with only the front toes bending.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the thorn patch, the indigo glow on the bud, pale hit sparks and step dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
