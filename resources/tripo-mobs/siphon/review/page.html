Vein Siphon from Tripo, rigged · version 1

The Kiln's leech: your export, with a skeleton and nine moves added by hand. A violet sac veined with brighter violet, warts on its crown, standing on six thin tendrils with soft pads. It hangs back from the hero, coils, and whips a tendril down an Ember lane. If it hits, it latches on and drinks: 2 from the hero every half second, healing itself as much, until it is full, the hero breaks away, or it is stunned. Open choices

  • Six tendrils, not four. The game's siphon hangs on four tendrils. Yours has six, so all six are rigged and move; the front pair does the whipping.
  • It hovers on its pads. The game's siphon is a flier with its tendrils dangling. Yours stands on pads, so I hold the pads a few centimetres off the floor and let them curl and paddle, which reads as floating rather than walking.
  • No mouth. The game's sac opens a mouth to drink. Yours has none, so the drink shows as the sac gulping and squeezing and the bead running up the tether.
  • The eyes are lit by the page. Your eyes are two dents, not painted; I light them a dim pale violet so it has a face to read, and they go out when it dies.
  • Mine. The paddle in the walk, the turn rate, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in, and the death.

What is rough

  • The tendrils are thin and bend on four bones each, so a tight curl shows corners.
  • The warts move with the crown as one piece; they do not jiggle on their own.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the Ember lane, the violet whip, the latch burst and tether, the violet beads and rings of each drink with the Ember flash on the hero, the let go's violet sparks, the veins brightening as its tell, and the death's violet drops and dark stain. The heal is violet, as in the game, not green. They are kept dim on purpose.
