Open choices

  • It flies flat, face up, like the game's kite. Your model is sculpted standing up, so I laid it flat with the head forward and the face up, which shows the face to the top-down camera. If you would rather it fly upright, tipped back like a real kite in the wind, it reads face-on from the side but thin from above; I can do that instead.
  • The eye glow sits on its face. The game's kite has an indigo eye painted on its back as its tell. Yours has its eyes on the face, so the indigo glow is on them.
  • The tail trails level. The game lifts each tail joint 0.16, which on this longer tail curls it up over the back, so it trails level with a slight droop.
  • The floor stops the sails. The game slaps the sails 0.2 down on the thump and lays them 0.12 under level, which would sink these into the floor, so they lie flat on it. It lies 0.1 u up instead of 0.07 for this model's back, and the low sail lifts by the game's tilt.
  • It flares flat at the bottom of the dive over the last third of the drop, so the beak does not hit the floor before the body.
  • Mine. The turn rate and bank, the soar's head turns, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in and the death's fall.

What is rough

  • Each sail bends on two bones; single torn rags do not flutter on their own.
  • The tail has four bones for the game's six, so the flutter runs down it in longer waves.
  • The eyes are painted; the indigo glow is drawn by the page.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page, after the game's own: the Ember lane, the eye glow, the snapped bridle string, wind streaks in the dive, the hit, the thump's dust, the climb's downdraught and the shadow. They are kept dim on purpose.
