In the game
Weir Crab 40 health. Scuttles sideways across its target's line at 1.9 u/s while edging in at 0.6, flipping side every 75 ticks, and turns at 300° a second. Its raised claws are a guard that blocks shots from within 60° of its front. Within 1.3 u it snaps: an Ember cone r 1.4, half-angle 55°, for 42 ticks, 10 damage. Open choices

  • The pincers. Each of your claws is one solid plate, so it cannot open and shut like the game's hinged finger. I tip the plate's top back for the game's gape and drive it down for the snap. I can cut a moving finger out of the top of each claw if you want the pincer to open.
  • The lunge. I lunge it about half the game's 0.14 u, so all eight planted feet keep their hold on the floor.
  • The guard down. The game lowers both claws to the floor in front after a snap. Your claws already reach the floor, so I tip them forward flat instead, which opens the face.
  • Mine. The scuttle's step rate, the idle's looks, the hurt, the spawn after the grow-in and the death.

What is rough

  • The arms are short and mostly hidden behind the claws, so the claws swing from just behind the plates.
  • The shell, the barnacles and the nail move as one piece.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page: the Ember cone, a faint indigo on the claws while it coils (the game's tell), pale sparks and dust. They are kept dim on purpose.
