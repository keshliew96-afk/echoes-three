## 1. The template

- **T1.** The shared rules are Mosslight's T1 to T15 (also in the repo's `docs/TALENTS.md`), with
  her closed choices: three paths; five tiers with one job each (I Style, II Power, III Trick, IV
  Reach, V Capstone); one pick per tier from any path, so 243 builds; every talent works alone;
  picked at camp, changing is free; Embers buy tiers (I free, then 20, 40, 60, 80); one build per
  hero; the Daily wears tier I only; a hero with no pick plays as today.

A mix reads like this: Stubborn, Bloodlust, Blood Price, Long Thorns, Deathless. He burns his own
health with every skill, the burn makes him harder to hurt, he swings faster, the foes round him
are pricked each time he is hit, and the hit that should down him does not.

### Shared rules the talents use

- **T2. Damage reduction adds and stops at 60%** (kit S6). Stubborn, Stone, Slabguard, Colossus and the
  rest add into one number. 1.7: Mountain makes each Stone worth 9% and lifts the stop to 80% while
  it lasts (it was a 75% that replaced the number).
  1.5: Stubborn lifts the stop as his health falls, to 70% at 20% health (T6).
- **T3. Health missing** is read at each hit: at 150 maximum and 60 health he is missing 60%.
- **T4. The thorn rule** (kit S8). A return is counted on the hit as thrown at him, before his
  reduction. Returns from talents and skills add together (Iron Hide 30%, Barbs 10% and Iron
  Bristles 60% make 100%), up to 150%. A return never crits and is not raised by his damage talents.
  1.5: Reckoning adds its 70% the same way. The flat part of a return ("+1% HP", "+3% HP") is paid
  at most once a second for each foe.
- **T5. The Iron rule** (Pent-up Iron turns it on).
  - Stored damage is Iron. It sits in the iron thorns on his back and shoulders, up to 40% of his
    maximum health (60 at 150). Ore veins in the thorns glow as it fills.
  - His basic hit 3, the overhead slam, lets it all out: every foe within 3 u takes the stored
    amount. A boss or champion takes double. Then the store is empty.
  - With nothing stored the slam is a plain slam.
  - Iron carries from room to room. It is lost when he is downed.
  - An Iron burst follows the thorn rule: no crit, not raised by his damage talents.

## 3. The fifteen talents

### Tier I, Style (free)

- **T6. Stubborn (Bedrock).** Changed in 1.5. For each 8% of health he is missing he takes 3% less
  damage, up to 30% at 20% health or below (it was 1% for each 2%, up to 40%). The stop on his
  reduction rises with it, 1% for each 8% missing, from 60% to 70%.
  - Worth nothing at full health and the most when it matters. On one full bar with no healing it
    is about 24% more damage soaked.
  - Why: at 40% it ran into the 60% stop. With full Stone 4% was lost, and Slabguard switched it
    off. Now Stone and Stubborn are 54% with nothing lost, and a skill on top still counts.
- **T7. Bare Claws (Feral).** He throws the shield away: the model has no shield, and he fights with
  claws and fists. He takes 20% more damage and all his own damage is 25% higher. His skills and his
  passive work the same; the moves have shieldless versions (kit, section 10).
- **T8. Iron Hide (Ironthorn).** Each hit he takes returns 30% of it, plus 1% of his maximum health,
  to the foe that dealt it (T4). A hit of 10 at 150 health returns 4.5.

### Tier II, Power (20 Embers)

- **T9. Thick Hide (Bedrock).** His maximum health is 20% higher, Hearty's growth included (150 to
  180; 225 to 270 at a run's end). Every "+% HP" on his skills grows with it.
- **T10. Bloodlust (Feral).** All his damage is 10% higher and his basic attacks are 20% faster.
  Alone that is all it does; with Bare Claws his damage is 37.5% higher.
- **T11. Barbs (Ironthorn).** He returns 10% more of each hit. Alone that is 10%; with Iron Hide 40%.

### Tier III, Trick (40 Embers)

- **T12. Take the Blow (Bedrock).** 25% of the damage an ally within 4 u would take is dealt to him
  instead, and his reduction shrinks it. It stops while he is below 20% health. The redirected part
  is a hit thrown at him: it returns to the foe under the thorn rule and fills the Iron store.
- **T13. Blood Price (Feral).** Changed in 1.5. Each skill of his that deals damage on its press
  burns 6% of his maximum health (kit S9), and that cast hits each foe for twice the health burned
  on top. At 150 maximum a Heavy Slam burns 9 and adds 18 to each foe, at any health above the
  floor. (It was 8% of the health he had, so it faded as he got low.)
  - The burn stops at 10% health: it takes only what is above that, and with nothing burned there
    is no bonus. A burn never downs him.
  - It waits for his first damage skill. 1.3: Grindstone has its own burn and takes no Blood Price.
  - Why: it now drives him low, where Stubborn, Savage Bite and Deathless want him.
  - 1.6: its burns also fill the Blood debt for his next Savage Bite (kit S29).
- **T14. Pent-up Iron (Ironthorn).** 25% of the damage thrown at him is stored as Iron (T5), counted
  before his reduction. His basic hit 3 bursts it out.

### Tier IV, Reach (60 Embers)

- **T15. Big Presence (Bedrock).** Every taunt of his reaches 25% farther: Bellow's ring, Rock Toss,
  Landslide, the Growlstone's roar, Slabguard's, Bodyguard's and Colossus's taunts, Slab Throw's
  throw and bounces, the Stone Ring's radius, the Stone Cubs' search, the growl, the
  slam's and the dash's taunts. His Stone lasts 9 s, not 6.
- **T16. Long Claws (Feral).** His basic attack and his melee skills (Heavy Slam, Maul, Savage Bite)
  reach 25% farther, and his dash goes 3.2 u, not 2.2. 1.2: it adds with Colossus's reach (50% in all).
  1.6: a Slab charge (kit S21) carries foes the longer dash.
- **T17. Long Thorns (Ironthorn).** Changed in 1.7 (K8). His thorn areas are 35% wider (Iron Briar,
  Iron Quills and their bursts, an Iron burst), and the Iron store holds half as much again (60% of
  maximum health). (It was: each hit on him also pricked every other foe within 2 u for 1% of his
  maximum health, and the areas were 25% wider. Counter Spin keeps the answer round him.)

### Tier V, Capstone (80 Embers)

- **T18. Mountain (Bedrock).** Changed in 1.7 (K4). When his health falls below 30%, for 5 s each
  Stone patch on him is worth 9% less damage instead of 3%, and the stop on his reduction rises to
  80%. Full Stone (8 patches) is then 72%. Once every 40 s. 1.5: a burn that takes him under 30%
  starts it too. (It was: living stone for 5 s, 75% less damage, cannot be moved, and every foe
  within 5 u taunted. Colossus keeps all three.)
- **T19. Deathless (Feral).** A hit that would down him leaves him at 1 health instead. For 4 s he
  cannot be downed and his basic attacks are 50% faster. Once a room. It heals nothing and absorbs
  nothing: after the 4 s he stands at 1 health.
- **T20. Reckoning (Ironthorn).** Changed in 1.5. Taunting a foe nails it for 4 s. A nailed foe
  takes back 70% more of each hit it lands on him. It adds to Iron Hide, Barbs and his thorn
  skills, up to 150%: 100% with Iron Hide, as before, and 70% alone. (It was 100% in place of Iron
  Hide's 30%, which switched tier I off.)
  - On a boss or champion the nail adds 35%. A boss is nailed even when it shrugs a skill taunt
    off. Bristle's free taunts leave a boss alone (kit S13), so they do not nail it.

## 5. Combos checked (T22)

**Clashes found and how the design settles them.**

1. *Reduction piling up.* Everything that can add comes to over 200% (Stone 24, Stubborn 30,
   Slabguard 40, Colossus 20, Rallying Cry 20, Grudge 20, Bodyguard 15, a node ward 20). They add
   and stop at 60%, or at up to 70% under Stubborn (T2, 1.5). 1.7: under Mountain the stop is 80%
   for its 5 s, and each Stone counts 9.
2. *Stubborn and the burns.* Blood Price, Grindstone and Bloodboil push him into Stubborn's
   reduction. Meant. 1.5: Blood Price and Grindstone burn a share of his maximum and stop at 10%
   health; Bloodboil burns a share of the health he has. None can down him.
3. *Bare Claws and reduction.* The 20% more is applied first, then his reduction: with full Stone
   he takes 1.2 × 0.76 = 91%. (1.5: the old example used Stubborn, which shares tier I with Bare
   Claws and can never be taken with it.)
4. *Mountain and Deathless.* Same tier, so never together. That is the tier rule doing its job.
5. *Take the Blow and Bodyguard on one ally.* 1.7: Bodyguard no longer takes a share, so they never
   overlap. Take the Blow keeps working on the ally he swapped with.
6. *Take the Blow and thorns.* A redirected hit is a hit thrown at him: it is returned and stored.
   Meant: that is the Martyr build.
7. *Bare Claws and thorns.* A return is counted before the 20% more and is not raised by his 25%.
8. *Reckoning, Iron Hide and Barbs.* 1.5: all three add (70, 30 and 10 make 110%); 150% is the
   ceiling.
9. *Pent-up Iron and Grudge.* 1.7: Grudge marks foes, it stores no damage. The same hits fill the
   Iron store as usual.
10. *Blood Price and repeats.* An Echo repeat is not a cast: no burn, no bonus.
11. *Deathless and burns.* A burn never downs him, so it never spends Deathless.
12. *Attack speed.* Bloodlust and Maul add, up to +50% (1.7: Bloodboil no longer speeds attacks).
13. *Hearty and Thick Hide.* Thick Hide is 20% of all his maximum health, growth included.
14. *Rallying Cry's ward on himself.* It is his own, so it adds into the 60%, it does not multiply.
15. *Colossus and Thick Hide (1.2).* Both are shares of all his maximum health and multiply: 150
    becomes 180, then 234 as a giant. His health keeps its share, so Stubborn reads the same before
    and after.
16. *Colossus and Mountain (1.2).* They can run together. 1.7: Colossus's 20% adds in as usual and
    the whole stops at Mountain's 80%.
17. *Colossus and the Iron store (1.2).* The store's ceiling grows with his maximum health while he
    is a giant. When he shrinks, Iron above the old ceiling is kept until the next burst.
18. *Slabguard and thorns (1.2).* A shot the slab stops never reached him: it is not returned and
    not stored. Hits that get through count as thrown, before the 40%.
19. *Fracture and Ironthorn (1.2).* A returned hit puts a Crack, so foes crack themselves. A stunned
    foe throws no hits for 1 s, which costs Ironthorn a little; the shatter damage pays for it.
    Reckoning's nail and a Crack are separate marks.
20. *Weight of stone and the 60% (1.3).* Heavy Slam adds 1 per 1% of reduction, up to +60. With
    Stubborn at low health that is +30 from the talent alone, and the count still stops at +60. 1.7: under Mountain the count still stops at +60. Bare
    Claws' 20% more taken does not lower the count.
21. *Grindstone and maximum health (1.3).* Thick Hide and Colossus raise both halves: at 234
    maximum health foes lose 14 a second and he loses 7. Its burn feeds Stubborn like any burn.
    Bare Claws and Bloodlust raise its damage; it is not a return.
22. *Grindstone and Deathless (1.3).* The burn stops at 10% health, so it never spends Deathless.
23. *Shots fly back and Ironthorn (1.3).* A returned shot is half the shot, whatever his return
    talents say; Iron Hide and Barbs do not add to it. It puts a Crack.
24. *The new taunts and Reckoning (1.3).* Taunts from the Stone Ring and Slab Throw nail foes like
    any taunt of his, and pay Stone. 1.7: the Stone Cubs hold, they do not taunt: no nail, no Stone.
25. *Hard knocks (1.3).* Stone from boss hits adds into the same 8 patches and the same 60%.
    1.5: each patch has its own 6 s (kit S5), so Big Presence's 9 s, the Brace node and Warden's
    Oath now each keep more patches on him.
26. *Stoneheart and reduction (1.4).* The hit is reduced first, then capped at 5% of his maximum
    health. Stubborn reads his health as usual. Thorns, Grudge and the Iron store count the hit as
    thrown, so a capped boss blow still returns and stores in full. Under Bare Claws the 20% more
    is applied before the cap.
27. *Counter Spin and the stores (1.4).* The same hits feed Counter Spin's count, Grudge's marks and
    the Iron store. The spin is a skill hit: Bare Claws and Bloodlust raise it, the thorn rule does not
    apply to it.
28. *Badger Toss (1.4).* Under Blood Price it burns and the thrown foe and the foes it lands on
    take the bonus. Long Claws lengthens the grab to 1.9 u.
29. *Blood debt and Blood Price (1.6).* A Blood Price burn pays twice at once and then fills the
    debt, so it pays three times in all. Savage Bite's own burn under Blood Price starts the next
    debt. The debt is not a return: Bare Claws and Bloodlust raise the bite that carries it.
30. *Spike and Crush (1.6).* A foe in the air is stunned, so Crush's ×1.5 and Spike's 50% both
    count: ×2.25 on that foe. A boss is never thrown up.
31. *Thorn slam, Grudge's burst and Long Thorns (1.6).* Long Thorns makes the briar wider, so it
    is easier to push foes into. 1.7: Grudge's spikes find quilled foes anywhere within 8 u. Under Reckoning
    a quilled foe is not nailed by the quill; only a taunt nails.
32. *Stone from more places (1.6).* Shatters, capped hits and boss hits all add into the same 8
    patches. A Stone chip is his hit, not a return: it never fills the Iron store.
33. *Slab charge (1.6).* It is a dash, so it taunts under Bristle and is a push for Wall slam. Under
    Bare Claws he carries the slab he ripped from the floor.
34. *Thrown Growlstone and Reckoning (1.6).* Its landing taunt nails like any taunt, and Big
    Presence widens it.

**Stacks that are strongest on paper.**

- Hardest to down: all Bedrock. 1.5: 180 health and 24% more soaked from Stubborn alone is about
  +48% on one unhealed bar, just under the +50% line for one path (it was +63% at Stubborn's old
  40%). The higher stop adds to that only while a reduction skill runs. The lever is Stubborn's
  ceiling (T28).
- Most damage: all Feral. Basic attacks ×1.65 and skills ×1.38 before Blood Price. It is the
  template's edge, but his damage starts low (basic about 14 a second against the fox's 31) and he
  pays in health. The lever is Bare Claws' 25%.
- Most returned: all Ironthorn in a pack of five, about 18 a second returned and a burst of up to
  90 every 12 s or so. Against one boss it is small: few hits come in. The boss's double burst and
  Reckoning's half are there for that.
- 1.6, to measure first: Blood debt on top of Blood Price (item 29), and Spike with Crush (item
  30).

No pair multiplies one lever twice: each tier holds one talent per lever.

## 6. AI rules (T23)

- Stubborn, Thick Hide, Bloodlust, Barbs, Iron Hide, Long Claws, Long Thorns, Big Presence,
  Deathless, Reckoning: no new rule.

  taunt.
- Blood Price: cast damage skills only above 35% health, unless Deathless is unspent.
- Pent-up Iron: with the store above half and two or more foes within 3 u, finish the basic combo
  rather than break it for a skill.
- Mountain: no rule; when it fires he walks into the largest pack.
