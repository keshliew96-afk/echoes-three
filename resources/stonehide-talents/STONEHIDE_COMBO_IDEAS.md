## Stone and earth

- **C1. Throw the stone. (my pick)** Rock Toss pressed with the Growlstone within 2 u of him
  throws the stone, not a rock. It lands beside the foe he aimed at (up to 7 u): that foe and every
  foe within 1.5 u take 16 and are taunted 3 s. The stone keeps the time it had left and roars at
  once from its new spot.
  - Links: Rock Toss, Growlstone. With Kick the stone he can now move it two ways.
  - Why: one press sends his taunt stone to the archer's side. Rock Toss is his cheapest skill.
  - Needs: the stone as a thrown thing (the roll in S34 already moves it).
- **C2. Slab pinball. (my pick)** Slab Throw also bounces off the Growlstone and the stones of his
  Stone Ring. A stone does not use up a bounce, and after the slab touches a stone it may hit a foe
  it already hit, once more. A Growlstone it touches roars at once.
  - Links: Slab Throw, Growlstone, Stone Ring.
  - Why: inside the ring the slab rattles between the stones and the pack. Easy to read, loud.
  - Needs: stones as bounce points.
- **C3. Rubble is ammo.** Burrow's exit and a giant's slams (Colossus) leave rubble as Fault Line
  does. Rock Toss pressed within 2 u of rubble throws three rocks at three foes and uses the rubble
  up.
  - Links: Fault Line, Burrow, Colossus, Rock Toss.
  - Not picked: three skills must leave rubble before it pays, and rubble is one more thing on a
    busy floor.
- **C4. The cubs copy him.** When he Burrows, his Stone Cubs dig to where he comes up and burst out
  with him (8 each). When he Bellows, each cub roars too (taunts within 2 u onto him).
  - Links: Stone Cubs, Burrow, Bellow.
  - Not picked: it needs two more cub moves before Kesh's cub model is in.

## Fury

- **C5. Spike. (my pick)** A foe in the air (thrown up by Fault Line, Burrow or Badger Toss) that
  he hits with Heavy Slam, basic hit 3 or Savage Bite is slammed down: that hit is 50% harder, and
  foes within 1.5 u of where it lands take 10.
  - Links: three throwers, three finishers. Nine pairs from one rule.
  - Why: a clear two-press combo with a loud pay-off. The air time is 0.6 to 1 s.
  - Needs: a check for "in the air" on the hit, a slam-down mark and sound.
- **C6. Blood debt. (my pick)** Health he burns (Bloodboil, Grindstone, Blood Price) is added to
  his next Savage Bite, up to 20% of his maximum health (30 at 150). The bite spends it.
  - Links: Bloodboil, Grindstone, Savage Bite, the Blood Price talent.
  - Why: his burns now build to one big bite, as Grudge builds to one big slam. It heals nothing.
  - Watch: under Blood Price a burn already pays twice at once; with the debt it pays three times.
    If that is too much, Blood Price's burns count half toward the debt.
  - Needs: a counter, and a rust glow on his jaws as it fills.
- **C7. Slab charge.** A dash out of Slabguard carries the foes in front of him the whole dash,
  where a plain dash knocks them aside. A wall at the end is a Wall slam.
  - Links: Slabguard, the dash, Wall slam.
  - Not picked: it changes the dash, which Kesh passed on the model page.

## Iron thorn

- **C8. Thorn slam. (my pick)** Iron Briar counts for Wall slam. A foe he pushes or throws into the
  briar (the dash, Slabguard's shove, Landslide, Badger Toss, the rolling Growlstone, an Anchor
  node) is impaled: 12 (+3% HP), and quilled 5 s as by Iron Quills.
  - Links: Iron Briar with five pushers, and with Iron Quills' mark.
  - Why: his lasting zone becomes a target to throw foes at.
  - Needs: the Wall slam check, pointed at the briar's circle.
- **C9. Grudge bursts the quills.** Grudge's slam makes every quilled foe in its reach burst, as a
  quilled foe does when it falls: its quills fly to up to 3 foes within 3 u for 10 each.
  - Links: Iron Quills, Grudge, and Iron Briar through C8.
  - Not picked: Ironthorn is already the strongest path on paper (audit N12).
- **C10. Stoneheart's chips.** Each hit Stoneheart caps puts a Crack on the attacker and gives him
  1 Stone.
  - Links: Stoneheart, Fracture, Stone.
  - Not picked: small, and Stone already comes from boss hits.

## Cracks and Stone

- **C11. Heavy hits crack twice. (my pick)** Heavy Slam, Fault Line, Burrow, Badger Toss and a Wall
  slam put 2 Cracks, not 1. Each shatter gives him 1 Stone.
  - Links: Fracture with his five big hits, and Fracture with Stone.
  - Why: Fracture now pays for his own big skills, not only for the party's hits, and feeds his
    reduction. Numbers only: nothing new to draw.
- **C12. Stone chips.** A Stone patch that crumbles (kit 1.5: each has its own 6 s) flies at the
  nearest foe within 4 u: 3 damage and a Crack.
  - Links: Stone, Fracture.
  - Not picked: up to eight small shots every few seconds is noise.
