## 2. Kit rules

- **S2. The pool.** 26 skills in four groups (1.4, inside Kesh's rule of 24 to 26 for every hero; it was 23 in 1.3, 19 in 1.2 and 18 in 1.0): 8 taunts and team play, 7 stone and earth, 6 fury, 5 iron thorn. He
  holds at most 4, all from wave rewards, as today. Any skill works with any talent; the groups only
  lean toward a path. 12 of the 26 taunt.
- **S3. No shields, no self heals.** No skill, node or talent of his gives a shield to anyone (S41
  asks about allies), and none heals him. The Bulwark node is never offered on his skills. He is
  healed by Mosslight, tonics and relics like everyone else.
- **S4. Taunt.** A taunted foe must attack him. Today's limits stay: a plain foe up to 4 s; a boss
  1 s, then it cannot be taunted for 5 s. A taunt mark shows over the foe's head.
  1.5: Bristle's free taunts leave a boss alone (S13), so his skill taunts decide when it turns.
- **S5. Stone.** Stone is his damage reduction. One Stone is one patch of his hide turned to stone,
  and each patch is 3% less damage taken. He holds up to 8 (24%).
  - Every foe he taunts gives 1 Stone. A boss or champion gives 3. A boss that shrugs the taunt off
    still gives 1.
  - The same foe gives Stone at most once every 3 s.
  - 1.3 (Hard knocks, from Timbersaw's Reactive Armor): each hit he takes from a boss or champion
    also gives 1 Stone, at most once a second. So he hardens in a boss fight, where taunts are few.
  - 1.5: each patch lasts 6 s by itself, then crumbles. (Before, one new patch renewed all eight,
    so a single foe kept him full.) A plain foe he holds keeps about 2 patches on him, so four
    foes fill him; a boss fills him through its hits.
  - So the more foes he holds, the harder he is: taunt first, then damage reduction.
  - 1.6 (Stone chips, C12): a patch that crumbles flies at the nearest foe within 4 u: 3 damage,
    and a Crack if he holds Fracture. A small grey chip and a soft tick.
  - 1.6: two more ways to gain a patch: each shatter (Fracture, S20) and each hit Stoneheart caps
    (S53).
- **S6. His damage reduction adds, and stops at 60%.** Stone, his skills and his talents add into
  one number. Wards from other heroes (Mosslight's Bark) multiply on top. Anything that makes him
  take more damage (the Feral talent Bare Claws) is applied first.
  1.5: with the Stubborn talent the stop rises as his health falls, to 70% at 20% health (T6).
  The Aegis and Rampart nodes are one entry in the sum: only the larger of the two counts.
- **S7. Weight.** Where a skill says "+6% HP" it adds that share of his maximum health as damage
  (9 at 150). More maximum health makes every big hit of his heavier.
- **S8. Returned damage (thorns).** Some skills and the Ironthorn talents return a share of each hit
  he takes to the foe that dealt it.
  - The share is counted on the hit as it was thrown at him, before his damage reduction. His
    reduction never shrinks his thorns.
  - It reaches any range: iron shards fly back at a foe that shot him.
  - A hit with no attacker (a hazard, a floor bed) returns nothing.
  - 1.5: the flat part of a return ("+1% HP", "+3% HP") is paid at most once a second for each
    foe, so a foe that hits in fast ticks does not multiply it. The share is paid on every hit.
  - It is his damage for kill credit. It never crits, no node or talent raises it except the ones
    that say so, and a returned hit is never returned again.
- **S9. Burning health.** A burn can never down him. It is not damage taken: his reduction does
  not shrink it, and it does not feed thorns.
  1.5: there are two kinds. Bloodboil burns a share of the health he has now. Grindstone and the
  Blood Price talent burn a share of his maximum health and stop at 10% health. A burn does count
  for anything that reads his health: it feeds Stubborn and it can start Mountain.
  1.6 (Blood debt, C6): while he holds Savage Bite, health he burns is kept as a debt for his next
  bite (S29).
- **S10. Casting.** Every skill fires on the press. He uses his five passed cast moves: Thrust,
  Roar, Plant, Sweep, Guard. Burrow needs one new move (dig). With the shield gone (Bare Claws) each
  move has a version with claws and fists.
  1.2: three more moves: a heavy hop (Bodyguard), a walk with the slab held up (Slabguard) and
  growing to giant size (Colossus). 1.3: a throw (Slab Throw). 1.4: a grab and throw (Badger Toss). 1.6: no new move of his: the
  thrown Growlstone uses Rock Toss's lob and the slam-down uses the moves he has. The cubs gain two
  (S50).
- **S11. Colours.** Stone and iron are Forge Steel `#9DB8CF` and dark iron; earth is Earth Ochre
  `#8F6B45` with debris `#5A4632`; these are his colours in the game today. Nothing of his wears
  Ember or Violet. Berserk needs a colour (S43). Glows stay dim.
- **S12. His helpers.** The Growlstone (S19) and, since 1.3, the Stone Cubs (S50) and the Stone Ring
  (S47) are the things he leaves working. Foes cannot hit any of them.
- **S51. Wall slam (new in 1.3, from Poppy's and Reinhardt's charges).** A foe that he pushes into a
  wall takes 10 (+3% HP) more and is stunned 0.5 s (a boss takes the damage, no stun).
  - What pushes: his dash, Slabguard's shove, Landslide's carry, the rolling Growlstone (S34) and
    the Anchor node's pull.
  - What counts as a wall: room walls, pillars, and the stones of his own Stone Ring.
  - 1.6 (Thorn slam, C8): his Iron Briar counts too. A foe pushed or thrown into the briar is
    impaled: 12 (+3% HP), and quilled 5 s (S32). No stun; the briar already slows.
  - Once per push. Look: a burst of dust, a crack mark on the wall and a heavy thud.
- **S56. Stuns (new in 1.5).** The game's rule stays: a stun lasts at most 1 s, then the foe cannot
  be stunned for 2 s, and a second stun never makes the first one longer.
  - "Thrown up" (Fault Line, Burrow, Badger Toss) is a stun under that rule.
  - The 0.3 s stun of his dash is the one exception: it does not start the 2 s. So a Wall slam at
    the end of a dash push still stuns, and a dash never spoils Fracture's shatter, Badger Toss or
    Fault Line.
  - A Tremor node's stun does start the 2 s, as today.
- **S58. Spike (new in 1.6, C5).** A foe in the air is one he threw up with Fault Line, Burrow or
  Badger Toss. If he hits it with Heavy Slam, basic hit 3 or Savage Bite before it lands, it is
  slammed down.
  - That hit is 50% harder on that foe, and foes within 1.5 u of where it lands take 10.
  - Once for each throw. What is left of its stun runs on the floor.
  - A boss cannot be thrown up, so it is never spiked.
  - Look: the foe is driven into the floor with a ring of dust and one hard knock.
- **S59. Rubble (new in 1.6, C3).** Rubble is a patch of broken floor, radius 1.2 u, that lasts 4 s
  and slows foes on it 35%.
  - What leaves it: Fault Line along its crack (as before), Burrow where he comes up, and, while
    he is a giant (Colossus), his Heavy Slam and his basic hit 3.
  - Rubble is ammo: Rock Toss pressed within 2 u of rubble throws three rocks (S17) and uses that
    rubble up.
  - Rubble is Earth Ochre with debris, never Ember.

## 3. Class passive, basic attack and dash

- **S13. Bristle, his class passive.** He has it from the first room, with all four skill slots
  empty. No button, no cooldown. It is one loop in four steps:
  1. **Free taunts.** Basic hit 1 (the shield bash) taunts every foe it hits for 2 s. Basic hit 3
     (the overhead slam) taunts every foe within 2 u for 3 s. His dash taunts every foe it knocks
     aside for 2 s.
     1.5: these free taunts and the growl do not taunt a boss and do not start its 5 s. Each
     still pays 1 Stone from a boss (S5). A champion is taunted as a plain foe.
  2. **The growl.** Every 8 s in a fight (first at 3 s) he growls by himself at the foe within 6 u
     that is attacking an ally, nearest to the most hurt ally first. It is taunted for 3 s. If no
     foe is on an ally, the nearest foe not on him. No random pick.
  3. **Taunts pay Stone now** (S5): every foe taunted is 3% less damage, up to 24%.
  4. **Taunts pay health later (Hearty).** Each foe that falls while taunted by him, or within 4 s
     after, gives +1 maximum health for the rest of the run (champion +5, boss +10). At most +15 in
     each level, so +75 over a full run (150 to 225). 1.5: his health keeps its share as the bar
     grows, as with Colossus, so nothing is healed (it said "the new point comes filled").
     1.3 (He grows, from Cho'Gath and Tiny): each gain also makes him a little bigger, 1% for
     every +5 maximum health, up to 15% taller at +75. Look only: the body that walks through doors
     and bumps into foes stays the same size. As a Colossus the two sizes multiply.
  - **It serves all three styles (Kesh's rule).**

    | | Free taunts | Stone | Maximum health |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Bedrock (pure tank) | the pack stays on him | adds to his own reduction | a bigger pool for "low health, more reduction" |
    | Feral (berserker) | foes walk into his claws | softens the extra damage he takes | more health to burn, and the burn pays by health burned |
    | Ironthorn (反甲) | every taunted foe hits him, so every taunt is returned damage | thorns count the hit before reduction, so Stone costs him nothing | returns and the store are sized by maximum health |

  - With the shield gone, hit 1 is a claw rake and the dash is a shoulder ram. Both taunt the same.
  - Why: his rule that taunts are cheap, easy and many. A player who drafts no taunt skill still
    taunts three ways.
- **S14. Basic attack.** The 3-hit combo passed on his model page: shield bash, fist hook, overhead
  slam. Its damage is set on his ability page (today's basic is 9 every 0.65 s). This doc adds only
  the taunts of S13 and "+4% HP" on the slam.
- **S15. Dash.** As passed: the two-paw shield charge, 2.2 u, knocks foes aside, stuns 0.3 s,
  untouchable while it runs. This doc adds only the taunt of S13.
  1.5: its 0.3 s stun does not start the foe's 2 s without stuns (S56).
  1.6: a dash out of Slabguard is a Slab charge (S21).
