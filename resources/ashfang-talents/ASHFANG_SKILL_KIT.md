## 1. The four wills (K1, K2, K28)

No fire, earth, water or wind in any name, line or look. The four families are wills. **A will is
only the mark it stores** (K28): no skill scars, daunts, stills, launches, pushes or pulls because
of its will. The words scar 剑痕, daunt 震慑, stillness 凝滞 and launch 挑飞 are out of the kit.

| Will | Was | A mark gives |
|---|---|---|
| 剑意 Intent | fire | 4% more damage |
| 不屈 Unbowed | earth | 4% less damage taken |
| 止水 Still Water | water | 1 health every 2 s in a fight |
| 狂热 Fervour | wind | basic attacks 5% faster |

**Colours (K35, K36).** Every skill, form, 剑气, 剑风 and combo is Fox Crimson #E8577A with a Moon
Silver #E7E3F0 edge. No colour tells a will. The ghost swords are Moon Silver with their character
(剑, 屈, 水, 热) glowing on the blade; the HUD shows the same characters, uncoloured. A special's
sword spirit is Moon Silver with a Fox Crimson edge. Sword Grove's swords are full-size, Fox Crimson
with a Moon Silver edge (K52). No Ember, no Violet.

## 2. Kit rules

- **The count is gone (K7).** Bladesong's count, the song and every line that read them are gone.
- **Six effects stay, because each is the skill itself (K29):** Moonfang's mark (4 s; his next
  hit on that foe spends it for 12 more, a sure crit; Kesh 18 Nov 20:27 SGT, every style), Blade Dance's speed, Riposte's parry, Mirror Image's taunt, the way Blade Wheel
  carries foes, and the gather of Radiant Cascade's swirl. The boss rule for shrugged effects holds
  only for these six (K32).
- **A lost damage over time (K30)** adds half of its total to the skill's own hit (Flurry, Crescent
  Finisher, Keen Crescent, Radiant Cascade's sweeps and dash, Triple Crescent).
- **Gone with the effects (K32):** the daunt's limits (S5), Spike, Smash down, Fervour spreads the
  scar, Scar spin, Settle, Coloured wheel.
- **Damage types (damage formula):** which lines are direct and which are bonus damage 附加伤害,
  the bonuses that share the 增伤 zone, and what is left of his control: talent doc T46. No DoT, no
  true damage.
- **Kept as they are (K9):** the dash cancel (S8), the six-hit combo (S12), the dash (S13).
- **Forms (K12, K34).** Each of the 23 pressed skills has a base form, a Swift form and a Crescent
  form: rows F1 to F23 of the talent doc, section 5. A form is part of the cast: no second mark,
  and it carries no effect. The passives have no form.

## 3. Class passive, basic attack and dash

- **S11. Sword Heart 剑心 (K18, K19, K21 to K24, K36, K38, K39).** Free from the first room, no button.
  - **No strong cut (K18).**
  - **Ghost swords (K19, K36).** Each will mark is a small Moon Silver ghost sword, about half the
    length of his own, floating point-down behind a shoulder, its character glowing on the blade;
    the newest is on the right. A new one draws itself out of his blade as the skill is pressed,
    with a soft chime; the oldest fades. He holds two; a third pushes out the oldest.
  - **No copies (K39).** The ghost swords make no copy after a skill. They are his marks, the sixth
    basic hit throws the newest one, and for a special the two fly into the sword spirit's paws.
  - **The sixth basic hit (K21, K34).** He keeps his sword in his right paw, flicks his open left
    paw forward and the newest ghost sword shoots out spinning like a disc: 4 u through every foe,
    12. It whirls 0.4 s, bursts for 6 within 1.5 u and flies back to his shoulder; the throw does
    not spend the mark. With no mark he throws a plain Moon Silver ghost sword that fades after
    the burst.
  - **Echo 残响 (K38).** Every fourth skill press is cast again by a Moon Silver ghost of him, for
    half damage. It steps out where he stood 0.4 s after his cast ends, aims as he aimed and does
    the whole skill in his style's form. An echo is not a cast: no mark, not counted toward the
    next echo. The HUD shows three pips beside the marks.
  - **Swift and Crescent (K22).** Under Swift a dash pressed while a thrown ghost sword is away
    takes him to it, cutting on the way. Under Crescent the basic attack is a charge shot (Kesh,
    18 Nov 14:54 Singapore time; the tiers are the page thread's draft, open): hold to charge, he
    sinks low with the sword drawn back and his open paw at the target; release to cut down or up
    (they alternate) and a 剑气 flies. Under 0.4 s: 8, 4 u, stops at the first foe. 0.4 to 1.2 s:
    14 to 26, 5 to 6 u, through every foe. At 1.2 s it fires by itself as the last hit: 30, 7 u,
    1.6 u wide, and the newest ghost sword flies with it (the sixth hit's throw: 12, burst 6).
    Held, full shots come back to back at about 31 a second on one foe (with the ghost sword);
    tapping gives about 15 and mid charges about 17 to 20, so holding is always the best play.
    A skill press and the dash keep the charge (it waits, then goes on; design thread, talent doc
    T14); the page clips do not show this. Open for Kesh: walking at half speed while charging,
    two crossing 剑气 at full (same 30), a bonus for a release timed at full.
  - **Specials (K23, K37).** On the cast that stores the second mark, the two ghost swords fly
    together, the sword spirit takes one in each paw and does the special; both are spent. No
    special leaves a scar, a daunt, stillness or a push (talent doc T12): One Thought 34 and
    nothing after; Unmoved 14, then 40% less damage and nothing stops or moves him for 3 s; Clear
    Mirror 12, he heals 12 and every harm on him ends; Still Mountain heals 4 a second and 30%
    less damage for 3 s, then a ring for 10; Last Stand 14, then 4 s that nothing stops him and
    20% faster attacks. The other five as T12.
  - **Spirit Swords (K24, the Crescent capstone)** are full-size Moon Silver swords circling over
    his head; they throw their 剑气 with each skill he casts and with the sixth basic hit.
- **S12. Basic attack.** The six-hit combo from his reel, as passed (10, 10, 12, 11, 14, the ghost
  sword throw 12).
- **S13. Dash.** A low thrust, 2.6 u, untouchable, 8 to each foe it passes. Look (M7b): flat and
  low, the blade leading like a spear; a thin Fox Crimson streak with a Moon Silver edge; a short
  spray of sparks where he stops.

## 4. The twenty-six skills (K10, K13)

Pool: 12 Blade, 5 Wave, 9 Art; 7 剑意, 6 不屈, 6 止水, 7 狂热. Each keeps its 1.7 cooldown; the
motion is the script named (M8 to M24), or, for the six ★ skills, the motion of his video under
its new words (M7d). Damage as written here (K30 sums included).

### 剑意 Intent

- **S14. Flurry** (Blade, 3 s, M8). Four cuts, 60° fan: 3.75 each to up to 6 foes.
- **S15. Crescent Finisher** (Blade, 6 s, M9). Rising cut, 70° fan, 1.0 u: 26 to up to 5 foes,
  50% more for each other skill landed in the last 2 s (up to 2), today's rule.
- **S16. Keen Crescent 锐月** (Wave, 4 s, M10). 6 u, 1.2 u wide, through every foe: 20.5.
- **S17. Radiant Cascade 耀舞连斩 ★** (Blade, 10 s). Two wide sweeps, an overhead slam with a star
  on the floor, a swirl of blades that gathers foes in, a dash through them. 7 + 7, 10, 3 × 3,
  dash 20.
- **S18. Crimson Edge 赤锋** (Passive, K31). 25% more crit chance. A crit calls a small ghost blade
  that cuts the foe again for 30% of that hit.
- **S19. Sword Bloom 剑绽** (Art, 7 s, M11, K31). A thin cut line opens on every foe within 4 u: 6.
  Then he catches the sword and cuts down: every line bursts for 10, and 4 to the foes within
  1.5 u of each.
- **S20. Triple Crescent** (Wave, big skill, 12 s, M12). Three great waves, 7 u, 2 u wide: 24 each;
  the 2nd and 3rd deal half to a foe already hit.

### 不屈 Unbowed

- **S21. Lunge Strike 贯刺** (Blade, 4 s, 1.0 s, M13, K42 to K46; not untouchable). A line 2 u long
  and 0.8 u wide, through every foe. The thrust 12; he drills round the blade, 3 turns of 2; a
  back somersault tears it out and a star opens behind the foes: 8 to the foes on the line, 4
  within 1.2 u of its far end. He ends where he began. Dash strike (C11): 1 u farther, 30% harder.
- **S22. Rift Cut 裂痕斩** (Wave, 6 s, M14). A rift runs 5 u: 14 to every foe on it.
- **S23. Titan Cleave 巨剑斩 ★** (Blade, charge, 6 s). Quick: 20 in 1.5 u. Full: 40 in 2.8 u.
- **S24. Ink Dive 墨坠 ★** (Blade, 9 s). 8, 6 + 6, 4 × 3, 6, landing 20 in 1.8 u. Ink strokes and
  ink spikes.
- **S25. Sword Grove 剑林** (Art, 8 s, M15, K47 to K52; in place of Caltrops). The plant (1.0 s):
  six ghost swords plant in a ring, 1.5 u, at a spot up to 4 u away: 8 to each foe there. They
  stand 5 s, a thread of light from each hilt to his paw. The call (0.3 s): a second press within
  5 s, or by itself at 5 s. Each sword flies straight to his paw through every foe on its way: 4 a
  sword, at most 3 on one foe. The call is not a cast (no mark, no Echo step). Closed: Kesh picked
  N1, Sword Grove, over Far Cut and Sword Cage (18 Nov, 16:25 Singapore time; K56, talent doc T48).
- **S26. Riposte** (Art, 8 s, M16). A 0.6 s guard; on a catch: 30 in a 90° arc. Sent back (C8) as in 1.7.

### 止水 Still Water

- **S27. Moonfang** (Blade, dash cut, 7 s, M17). 3.2 u, untouchable: 24 to up to 4 foes; marked
  4 s: his next hit on that foe spends the mark for 12 more, a sure crit; the mark adds no damage
  taken (Kesh 18 Nov 20:27 SGT: the base and Swift mark is the Crescent style's mark).
- **S28. Calm Forms 止水四式 ★** (Blade, 6 s). 10, 12, 8, 10.
- **S29. Calm Crescent 静月** (Wave, 5 s, M18). 2.5 u wide, 4 u: 14; he hops 1.5 u, untouchable.
- **S30. Mirror Ring 镜月剑阵 ★** (Art, big skill, 14 s). A ring of light, 2.5 u; seven mirror
  images thrust across it and he thrusts the eighth from where he stands (Kesh 16:18 SGT), 5 each;
  then 剑花 and four bursts, 4 each. All in 4 s.
- **S31. Mirror Image** (Art, 10 s, M19). Foes within 4 u turn on the image for 3 s (never a boss
  or the tank's foes); it bursts in mirror shards: 12 within 2 u. The image throws too (C6) and
  Burst the image (C7) as in 1.7.
- **S32. Calm Edge 静锋** (Passive, K31). While he has taken no hit for 3 s, his hits deal 15% more.
  A hit he takes ends it.

### 狂热 Fervour

- **S33. Fox Step** (Blade, dash cut, 5 s, M20; redrawn, Kesh 18 Nov 20:32 SGT). Three fox steps,
  about 3 u, untouchable: a blink forward-left with a rising cut (6), a blink across with a backhand
  (6), then a low corkscrew straight through (12); up to 3 foes each. Each step leaves a crimson
  shade that holds its cut and goes out as he lands.
- **S34. Blade Storm** (Blade, 7 s, M21). Five cuts of 4.
- **S35. Skyblade Waltz ★** (Blade, 12 s). As passed: 6, 8, 6, 8, 6, 10, then 6 per beam. A ring of
  light and blades of light.
- **S36. Blade Dance** (Art, 6 s, M22). 1.3 u: 10 to up to 6 foes; he runs 30% faster for 2 s.
- **S37. Blade Wheel 旋刃** (Wave, 7 s, M23). Travels 4 u in 1.6 s: 3 every quarter second; carries
  the foes and drops them together.
- **S38. Razor Wake** (Passive). Every second the 3 nearest foes within 0.9 u take 4.
- **S39. Thousand Cuts** (Blade, big skill, 12 s, M24). A blur for 1.2 s: eight cuts of 5, each on
  a foe within 3 u, the one nearest his aim first, then a different foe each time while there is
  one (40 on a lone foe). Untouchable; he ends beside the last foe cut. A8 holds: he never ends on
  a floor warning or a floor bed.
