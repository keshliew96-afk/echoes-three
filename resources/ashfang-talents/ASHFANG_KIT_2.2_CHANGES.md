## A will is only its mark

- **K28. No side effects from a will.** No skill scars, daunts, stills, launches, pushes or
  pulls because of its will. The words scar 剑痕, daunt 震慑, stillness 凝滞 and launch 挑飞 leave
  the kit. A will is the mark it stores (4% damage, 4% less damage taken, 1 health every 2 s,
  basic attacks 5% faster) and nothing else.
- **K29. Six effects stay, because each is the skill itself:** Moonfang's mark (20% more damage
  taken for 2 s, today's), Blade Dance's speed (today's), Riposte's parry (today's), Mirror
  Image's taunt, the way Blade Wheel carries foes along, and the gather of Radiant Cascade's
  swirl. Everything else on a skill line that is not damage goes. Examples: Lunge Strike and
  Rift Cut do not stun; Fox Step, Blade Dance, Razor Wake, Thousand Cuts and Skyblade Waltz
  throw nobody up and push nobody; Blade Storm does not pull; Moonfang, Calm Forms, Calm
  Crescent, Mirror Ring and Mirror Image's burst do not slow; Titan Cleave and Ink Dive do not
  stun or smash down; Riposte's answer does not stun; Caltrops are today's patch.
- **K30. A skill that lost a damage over time** gets half of that total added to its own hit
  (Flurry's four cuts, Crescent Finisher, Keen Crescent, Radiant Cascade's sweeps and dash,
  Triple Crescent).
- **K31. Three skills were only a side effect and are redrawn.**
  - **Sword Bloom 剑绽.** A thin cut line opens on every foe within 4 u: 6. Then he catches the
    sword and cuts down: every line bursts for 10, and 4 to the foes within 1.5 u of each.
    Cooldown 7 s. Motion M11.
  - **Crimson Edge 赤锋 (passive).** 25% more crit chance (today's). A crit calls a small ghost
    blade that cuts the foe again for 30% of that hit.
  - **Calm Edge 静锋 (passive).** While he has taken no hit for 3 s, his hits deal 15% more. A
    hit he takes ends it.
- **K32. Rules that go with the effects:** the boss rule for shrugged effects holds only for
  the six of K29; the limits of the daunt (S5) are not needed by his kit; Spike, Smash down,
  Fervour spreads the scar, Scar spin, Settle and Coloured wheel go.
- **K33. Combos that stay:** Wall slam for a foe that Blade Wheel carries into a wall (10, no
  stun), The image throws too, Burst the image, Sent back, Kick the caltrops (the patch is
  twice as wide; no stun), Dash strike.
- **K34. The forms and the ghost sword throw carry no effect:** drop "carries the skill's own
  effect at half length" (K14) and the four effects in the burst of the sixth hit's throw
  (K21): the burst is 6 within 1.5 u, nothing more.

## Normal colours

- **K35. Every skill, form, 剑气, 剑风 and combo is Fox Crimson with a Moon Silver edge.** No
  gold, bronze, pale aqua or will-silver sets. No colour tells a will.
- **K36. The ghost swords are Moon Silver**, each with its character (剑, 屈, 水, 热) glowing on
  the blade. The HUD shows the same characters, uncoloured. The sword spirit of a special is
  Moon Silver with a Fox Crimson edge.

## The specials (talent doc T12)

- **K37.** No special leaves a scar, a daunt, stillness or a push. One Thought is 34 and
  nothing after. Unmoved: 14, then 40% less damage and nothing stops or moves him for 3 s.
  Clear Mirror: 12, he heals 12 and every harm on him ends. Still Mountain: heals 4 a second
  and 30% less damage for 3 s, then a ring for 10. Last Stand: 14, then 4 s that nothing stops
  him and 20% faster attacks. The other five are unchanged.

## Echo is part of the passive (S11)

- **K38. Echo 残响.** Every fourth skill press is cast again by a ghost of him, for half
  damage. The ghost is a Moon Silver image that steps out where he stood 0.4 s after his cast
  ends, aims as he aimed, and does the whole skill in his style's form. An echo is not a cast:
  no mark, not counted toward the next echo. The HUD shows three pips beside the marks.
- **K39. The copies of K20 are dropped.** Kesh, 12:55 Singapore time: "E1". The ghost swords
  make no copy after a skill. They are his marks, the sixth basic hit throws the newest one,
  and for a special the two fly together into the sword spirit's paws. K20 leaves the kit.
