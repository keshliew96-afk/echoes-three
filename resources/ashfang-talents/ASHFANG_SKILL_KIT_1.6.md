## 1. Elements (S1)

Every skill has one element. The element is what the skill does to the foe on top of its damage,
and its colour.

| Element | What it does | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Fire 火 | burns: damage over time | gold flame along the blade, a burning foe smokes |
| Earth 土 | stuns | rock shards and dust, the blade lands heavy |
| Water 水 | slows | pale water trails, a slowed foe drips |
| Wind 风 | moves the foe: a cut throws it up or pulls it in, a wave pushes it back | silver streaks, leaves and dust lifted |

His plain cuts (basic attack, dash) have no element and stay Fox Crimson.

## 2. Kit rules

- **S2. The pool.** 26 skills: 7 fire, 6 earth, 6 water, 7 wind. Of the 26, 13 are Blade skills
  (close cuts, dash cuts and spins), 4 are Wave skills (剑气, ranged) and 9 are Arts (stances,
  zones and passives; 1.6: was 10, 7 and 9; 1.5: was 9, 9 and 8). Every element has Blade skills
  and Arts; fire, water and wind also have Wave skills, and earth (1.6) has none. So every path
  finds skills in every element. He holds at most 4, all from wave rewards, as today. Any skill works with any talent;
  the three kinds only lean toward a path. 3 of the 26 are passives. All 11 of today's skills stay
  by name.
- **S3. The count (连击).** His class passive (S11) keeps a combo count.
  - Each cut, thrust or wave of his adds 1 for each foe it hits, up to 3 from one cut or wave.
    A mix (T5) counts as one wave.
  - Burns, zones on the floor and his water image (Mirror Image) do not count. 1.5: Tide Ring's
    thrusts and bursts are his blade and count, up to 3 foes each.
  - The count falls to 0 when 3 s pass without a counted hit. It stops at 30.
  - A hit he takes does nothing to it.
  - It shows as a number by him and on the HUD. The sword rings one note higher at each 10.
- **S4. Sword waves (剑气).** A sword wave is a crescent of light thrown from his blade along his
  aim. Unless its line says otherwise it flies in a straight line, passes through every foe and
  stops at a wall. A wave is his blade for everything that reads a hit: it can crit, it counts
  (S3), and nodes and relics that read his hits read it. The four Wave skills (1.6), the waves of his
  passive (S11) and the waves of the Crescent talents are all sword waves.
- **S5. The four effects.**
  - **Burn.** Damage each half second for the time given ("3 a second for 3 s" is 9). One burn on
    a foe at a time: a new burn that is stronger takes its place, and any new burn starts the
    time again. A burn cannot crit and does not count. New machinery: the game has no burn today.
  - **Stun.** The game's rule stays: at most 1 s, then the foe cannot be stunned for 2 s, and a
    second stun never makes the first one longer.
    1.3 (A1): two small stuns do not start the 2 s, and do not land on a foe that is inside it:
    Razor Wake's throw (S38) and the earth stun of the talent Enchanted Edge. So they never
    spoil Hilt Bash, a throw up or a mix.
  - **Slow.** The game's rule stays: slows do not add, the deepest counts, and none is deeper
    than 60%.
  - **Wind.** A push moves the foe away along the wave. A pull moves it toward him or along with
    a whirl. Thrown up (浮空): the foe is lifted off the floor for the time given and can do
    nothing; it is a stun under the stun rule. A champion is pushed, pulled and thrown half as
    far and half as long.
- **S6. Bosses.** The game's rule stays: a boss cannot be stunned, slowed, pushed, pulled or
  thrown. So that three elements are not wasted in a boss fight, a hit of his whose stun, slow
  or wind the boss shrugs off deals 20% more to that boss. Fire burns a boss as it burns anything.
  (S48 is the choice.)
- **S7. In the air** (from Yasuo's Last Breath, in a small form). A cut of his on a foe that is
  thrown up adds 2 to the count, not 1. Juggling a pack is the fastest way to build the count.
- **S8. Casting.** Every skill fires on the press; none is held, except Earthsplitter's charge
  (1.5, S23). His passed moves are used as
  they are: the 4-hit combo, the dash thrust, the lunge, the edge draw, the caltrops throw, the
  crescent, the riposte and the Blade Storm spin. New moves this kit needs: a wave throw (one move
  for all crescents, drawn from the passed crescent cut), a pommel bash, a floor drag (Ground
  Wave), a flowing three-step (Flowing Blade), a hop with a throw
  (Tide Crescent), a three-wave draw (Triple Crescent) and a blur (Thousand Cuts).
  1.6: the floor drag and the flowing three-step go with Ground Wave and Flowing Blade. New: one
  long move for each of the four 1.6 skills, drawn from Kesh's videos (S17, S24, S28, S35).
  1.5: also a charge and overhead slam (Earthsplitter), the 剑花 flourish (Tide Ring) and the
  sword throw on the last basic hit. The basic combo itself is to be redrawn from Kesh's video
  ref (S58).
  1.3 (A9): his dash cancels a cast, as it cancels any wind-up. A cast cancelled before anything
  came out takes a 2 s cooldown, stores no orb and gives no strong cut.
- **S9. Colours.** Heroes never wear Ember or Violet, and green is kept for heals.
  - Plain cuts and the count: Fox Crimson `#E8577A` with Moon Silver `#E7E3F0`, as today.
  - Fire: blade gold `#FFB43C` with a Hearth Amber `#E8A23D` body. Never Ember `#FF5A36`; fire on
    the floor is a dark crust with gold cracks, kept dim.
  - Earth: Earth Ochre `#8F6B45` with debris `#5A4632` (the game's earth colours).
  - Water: a pale aqua `#7FE0F2` (the frost bomb's), not the Tide Caller's Deep Cobalt.
  - Wind: his own Moon Silver `#E7E3F0`, not Windbriar's Wind Jade.
  - 1.6: Inkstone Dive's brush strokes are Fox Crimson with a dark crimson ink (his plain-cut
    colour, as in its video); its stuns, spikes and landing are earth.
  - Colour is judged on the laptop. S49 is the choice.
- **S10. Orbs (1.1, Kesh's rule).** The orbs are part of his passive (S11), so every Ashfang has
  them, on any path.
  - Each skill he casts with a press stores one orb of that skill's element. He holds up to two.
    1.5: they float round his empty left paw in their colours (Kesh: "floating around the empty left
    hand side, not the holding sword right hand side"); the sword hand stays clear.
  - Each orb gives a very small bonus while he holds it. Two orbs of one element give it twice.

    | Orb | Bonus for each orb |
    |---|---|
    | Fire | 4% more damage |
    | Wind | basic attacks 5% faster |
    | Earth | 4% less damage taken |
    | Water | recovers 1 health every 2 s while a fight is on |

  - A new orb pushes out the oldest. So his two orbs are always his last two casts.
  - The basic attack, the dash and the three passive skills store nothing and change nothing
    (the attack button is held all fight). A cast repeated by a node stores no second orb.
  - Orbs stay from room to room. They are lost when he is downed.
  - The Spellblade talent Fusion spends the two orbs to cast a mix (talent doc, T4 and T5). The
    price is the two small bonuses, so it does not harm him. Without Fusion nothing spends them.
  - Water's recovery is a heal, so it shows in Bright Heal.

- **S55. Wall slam (new in 1.4, C2; Stonehide's rule, shared).** A foe that he pushes or drags
  into a wall takes 10 and is stunned 0.5 s.
  - What moves a foe: Skyblade Waltz's floor crescent (1.6, for Gale Crescent), Whirlwind Slash's drag, and the mixes that push or
    carry (Flood, Firestorm, Tornado). Enchanted Edge's small push does not.
  - What counts as a wall: room walls, pillars, and Stonehide's slab and the stones of his Stone
    Ring.
  - Once for each push. Look: a burst of dust, a crack mark on the wall and a heavy thud.
- **S56. Fire meets wind and water (new in 1.4, C1, C10, C12).**
  - **Wind fans fire.** A wind skill of his that hits a burning foe spreads that foe's burn to
    the foes within 1.5 u of it. Look: a lick of gold flame blown sideways.
  - **Fire spin.** Blade Storm does more: if a foe it cuts is burning, every foe the spin cuts
    catches the strongest of those burns, and the spinning blade trails flame.
  - **Quench.** Tide Crescent or Tide Ring (1.5) on a burning foe puts the fire out in a puff of
    steam: the foe takes what was left of its burn at once and is blinded 0.8 s (it starts no
    attack). A boss takes the rest of the burn and is not blinded. His other water skills leave
    a burn alone (Riptide Form too, 1.6).
  - So a fire build wants wind to spread it, and holds one water wave to cash it in.

## 3. Class passive, basic attack and dash

- **S11. Bladesong (剑鸣), his class passive.** He has it from the first room, with all four skill
  slots empty. No button, no cooldown. Five parts (the fifth is new in 1.1):
  1. **The count** (S3). Every hit of his blade builds it.
  2. **The song.** For each 10 count his skills cool down 10% faster (30% faster at 30).
  3. **The strong cut** (from 光信's passive). Hit 4 of his basic combo, the spin cut, and the
     first basic cut after each skill cast are strong cuts. A strong cut deals 50% more and
     throws a sword wave: 5 u, through every foe, for the cut's damage. A foe in reach is hit by
     the cut, not by both. So skill, cut, skill is how he is played, on any path.
     1.5 (Kesh: "the last hit throw the sword out, where we can throw a element bolt"): the last
     hit of his basic combo throws the sword itself instead of a wave (his video ref,
     4rvQNapDK). It spins out 4 u along his aim like a disc, through every foe, for the strong
     cut's damage; whirls on that spot for 0.4 s; then bursts, half the strong cut's damage to
     the foes within 1.5 u, and flies back to his paw in 0.2 s. The burst carries the element of
     his newest orb: fire a burn of 2 a second for 2 s, earth a 0.2 s
     stun (a small stun, S5), water a 20% slow for 1.5 s, wind a 0.5 u push. With no orb it is
     plain Fox Crimson and Moon Silver. It stores no orb. The strong cut after a cast keeps its
     wave (S58 b). While the sword is away (about 0.8 s) a skill press or a dash calls it back at
     once, with no burst, and his next basic cut waits for it (the design thread's gap 2).
  4. **The dash keeps the song.** Each foe his dash thrust cuts adds 2 to the count, and any dash
     starts the 3 s again, hit or not. So he can dodge without losing the count.
  5. **The orbs** (1.1, S10). Each skill cast stores an orb of its element; he holds two; each
     gives a very small bonus; a new one pushes out the oldest.
  - **It serves all three styles (Kesh's rule).**

    | | The count | Faster cooldowns | The strong cut's wave | The orbs |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Tempo (连击) | its talents make the count pay in speed and damage | more dash cuts and spins | reaches the next foe so the count does not drop | nothing spends them: wind orbs are attack speed, fire orbs are damage |
    | Crescent (剑气) | every wave that hits adds to it from a safe distance | more Wave skills | the seed its talents grow into a wave on every cut | nothing spends them: two fire orbs make every wave hit harder |
    | Spellblade (附魔) | mixes hit many foes at once, so they fill it | more casts are more orbs and more mixes | carries the sword's element with Enchanted Edge | Fusion spends them on a mix; one orb's bonus holds between casts |

  - Look and sound: the count's number grows a little at each 10 and the sword rings a higher
    note. At 30 the blade leaves a short crimson after-trail. A strong cut flashes Moon Silver
    along the edge before it lands. The two orbs circle his empty left paw (1.5); a new orb
    slides in with a soft chime as the oldest fades.
- **S12. Basic attack.** 1.5: the six-hit combo below (it replaced the 4-hit combo passed on his
  model page: cut, rising cut, thrust, spin cut). Today's basic is 11 every 0.35 s. This doc adds only
  the count and the strong cut.
  1.5 (Kesh: "this video from 0s to 13s make it as ashfang basic attack", Vimeo 497152947): six
  hits drawn from the reel's main swings, sped up for a basic combo: a big diagonal down-cut, a
  flat sweep, both paws over the head into a straight chop, a rising cut, a deep low lunge, and an
  overhand arc that throws the sword (S11). My numbers: 10, 10, 12, 11, 14, and the throw 12 as a
  strong cut (18). "Hit 4" in older lines now means this last hit.
  1.6 (the pace, for the talents): in the game the first five cuts land 0.35 s apart, as today,
  and the throw takes 0.6 s, so all six take about 2.35 s (2.6 cuts a second; today 2.9). The page
  clip runs slower, about 0.4 s a cut, so each swing can be read; "about 3.5 s" in 1.5 counted the
  sword's flight back. My guess, set on the laptop.
- **S13. Dash.** As passed: the low thrust (突刺) along the way, a slight cut on each foe passed
  (8, my guess), untouchable while it runs, top priority. This doc adds only part 4 of S11.
