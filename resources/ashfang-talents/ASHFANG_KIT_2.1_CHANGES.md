## The passive, Sword Heart 剑心 (kit S11, rewritten)

- **K18. No strong cut.** No stronger cut and no wave after a cast. Every line that names the
  strong cut goes.
- **K19. Will marks are ghost swords.** Each mark is a small ghost sword, about half the length
  of his own, floating point-down behind a shoulder in its will's colour (gold, bronze, pale
  aqua, silver). The newest is on the right. A new one draws itself out of his blade as the
  skill is pressed, with a soft chime; the oldest fades. Same rule and bonuses as before (two
  held, a new one pushes out the oldest). Nothing is shown in or over his left paw.
- **K20. They join every skill.** After the last hit of each skill, every ghost sword he holds
  copies that hit for 4 (my guess), with its own will's effect at half length.
  - Base form: each flies out, stabs the nearest foe the skill hit, and flies back.
  - Swift form: they fly beside him along his thrust and cut every foe on it.
  - Crescent form: each fires a 剑气 5 u along his aim from where it floats.
  - A copy is not a cast (no mark).
- **K21. The sixth basic hit throws a ghost sword, not his sword.** He keeps his sword in his
  right paw. He flicks his open left paw forward and the newest ghost sword shoots out,
  spinning like a disc: 4 u along his aim, through every foe, 12. It whirls there 0.4 s, bursts
  (6 within 1.5 u, with its will's effect: a scar of 2 a second for 2 s, a 0.2 s daunt, 20%
  stillness for 1.5 s, or a 0.5 u push) and flies back to his shoulder. The throw does not
  spend the mark. With no mark he throws a plain Moon Silver ghost sword that fades after the
  burst. The spin, whirl and burst are the ones from his video (4rvQNapDK), as now.
- **K22. Under Swift** a dash pressed while a thrown ghost sword is away takes him to it,
  cutting on the way. **Under Crescent** hits 1 to 5 each throw a 剑气, as now.
- **K23. The specials (Resolve).** On the cast that stores the second mark, the two ghost
  swords fly together, the sword spirit takes one in each paw and does the special. Both are
  spent.
- **K24. Spirit Swords (the Crescent capstone)** are full-size Moon Silver swords circling over
  his head, so they never read as the two small coloured swords at his shoulders. They throw
  their 剑气 with each skill he casts and with the sixth basic hit.
