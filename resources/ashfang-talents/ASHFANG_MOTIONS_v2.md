# Ashfang: skill motions (for the ability page)

**Version 2, 2026-11-18. Design only.** Kesh, 01:25 Singapore time: "the ashfang skills motion
need to be extremely fancy, i sent many videos before for reference, I want it at least that
level". 01:33 to 01:35: no element names or looks ("it can keep effect, but not the name"), and
a special is never "2 stack just for a dot, which not match fancy rule". This file is the motion
script for every skill that is not already one of his videos, and for the ten specials. It goes
with `ASHFANG_TALENT_PATHS.md` (the styles and the forms). Times are from the press.

## The bar (what his videos have in common)

- **M1.** Every skill is a small choreography: a ready pose, the cut, a flourish, an end pose.
  No skill is one plain swing.
- **M2.** Every beat has its own big shape: a crescent that skims the floor, a full ring, a streak,
  blades that rise from the floor, beams that fall, brush strokes that hang in the air, a
  starburst crack in the floor, a spinning sword.
- **M3.** The body leaves the floor often: leaps, spins, rolls, low crouches, a knee slide.
- **M4.** The end pose is held a moment (残心): blade out, back turned, or a slow sheathing.
- **M5. Length.** Fancy is not long. Quick skills run 0.6 to 1.1 s, the big ones 1.4 to 1.6 s.
  Only his six video skills run 2 s or more. The dash cancels any beat; hits already landed stay.
- **M6. Numbers.** Each skill keeps its total damage, effect and cooldown from kit 1.7. Where a
  skill now has more beats, the damage lands on the beat marked "hit".
- **M7.** The six skills from his videos and sketch keep their motion as written in the kit
  (under their new names, M7c). So do the six-hit basic combo and the thrown sword. Kesh gave
  leave to redo these too (01:26); they are his own refs and already at the bar, so they stay.
- **M7b. The dash** gets a look to match: he goes flat and low, the blade leading like a spear,
  a thin Fox Crimson streak with a Moon Silver edge; a short spray of sparks where he stops.

## No elements: the looks of the four wills

- **M7c.** Nothing looks like fire, rock, water or wind any more. Each will has one colour (the
  kit's four, unchanged) and its own shapes:

  | Will | Colour | Shapes | Its effect on a foe |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 剑意 Intent | gold | thin bright lines, brush strokes of light, stars | **the scar 剑痕**: a gold line on the foe; once a second a small ghost blade flashes in and cuts along it again (the old damage over time, now seen) |
  | 不屈 Unbowed | bronze (the kit's ochre) | thick heavy strokes, shock rings, cracks in the floor, the giant sword | **the daunt 震慑**: the foe cannot act (the old stun, same limits) |
  | 止水 Still Water | pale aqua | still rings, one thin ripple, moons, mirror images | **stillness 凝滞**: the foe moves slower (the old slow) |
  | 狂热 Fervour | silver | after-images, many fast strokes, spirals, wheels of blades | **the launch 挑飞**, the pull and the push: the force of the cut moves the foe |

- **M7d. The six ★ skills, same motion, new words.** Radiant Cascade (was Blazing Cascade): the
  swirl is a swirl of gold blades, the light gathers over his paw, its cuts scar. Titan Cleave
  (was Earthsplitter): bronze light runs up the growing blade; sparks and a shock ring, no
  rocks. Ink Dive (was Inkstone Dive): ink strokes and ink spikes. Calm Forms (was Riptide
  Form): pale arcs. Mirror Ring (was Tide Ring): a ring of pale light with seven mirror images
  of him. Skyblade Waltz: a ring of light and blades of light.

## 剑意 Intent (gold)

- **M8. Flurry** (0.7 s).
  - Base: a reverse-grip draw across the body (0 s). Four cuts in an X and an X (0.1 to 0.45 s,
    hits); each leaves a gold brush stroke hanging in the air. He flicks the blade clean and the
    four strokes flare into one small star (0.55 s). End: low, blade out to the side.
  - Swift: the four cuts are four stabbing steps; he ends behind the pack with his back to it.
  - Crescent: the four hanging strokes fly off one after another as thin 剑气.
- **M9. Crescent Finisher** (1.0 s).
  - Base: a deep crouch, the point dragging sparks on the floor behind him (0 to 0.25 s). A
    rising cut that lifts him off the floor in a back turn (0.3 s, hit); it draws a tall gold
    moon standing upright. He lands with his back to the foes (0.8 s) and the moon breaks into
    sparks.
  - Swift: the crouch is a low dash through the pack, and the rising cut comes out of it.
  - Crescent: the standing moon flies forward as a tall 剑气.
- **M10. Keen Crescent** (was Flame Crescent; 0.9 s).
  - Base: one full turn with the blade drawing a ring of gold light round him (0 to 0.3 s, a
    look). He plants and cuts flat out of the turn (0.35 s): the ring unrolls into the crescent
    and flies. A glowing cut line stays in the floor for a moment. End: arm fully out.
  - Swift: he does not let the ring go; he thrusts inside it, a gold streak.
  - Crescent: the crescent is twice as wide and opens into a flower of light at its end.
- **M11. Sword Bloom** (was Flashfire; 1.1 s).
  - Base: he tosses the sword up, spinning (0 to 0.3 s). He snaps his open left paw (0.4 s,
    hit): a thin gold scar opens on every foe in reach. He catches the sword over his head and
    cuts down (0.8 s): foes that were already scarred burst open as stars.
  - Swift: he throws the sword ahead and flashes to it; the scars open along his path.
  - Crescent: the catch-cut sends a ring of light rolling outward.
- **M12. Triple Crescent** (1.6 s, his big ranged skill).
  - Base: a salute, blade upright before his face; three ghost blades fan out behind him like
    wings (0 to 0.4 s). Three great cuts from three stances: flat, rising, overhead (0.4, 0.65,
    0.9 s, hits), so the three waves are a flat crescent, a slanted one and a tall one. He turns
    away and sheathes; the wings fold (1.3 s).
  - Swift: each ghost blade leads one thrust; he is the wave three times.
  - Crescent: each wave ends in a burst shaped like its cut.

## 不屈 Unbowed (bronze)

- **M13. Lunge Strike** (0.8 s).
  - Base: he coils low, blade beside his cheek, point forward, left paw sighting along it; a
    thin bronze line shows the aim (0 to 0.25 s). The thrust (0.3 s, hit): a shock cone bursts
    out behind the foe. He twists and rips the blade out (0.5 s): a second small burst, the
    daunt.
  - Swift: the thrust carries him through; the shock trails behind him the whole way.
  - Crescent: the point fires a bronze needle 剑气.
- **M14. Rift Cut** (was Ground Wave; 1.1 s).
  - Base: he flips to a reverse grip and stabs the floor (0.2 s). He drags the point in a short
    arc round his feet, carving a glowing line (0.2 to 0.5 s). He rips the blade up and forward
    (0.6 s, hit): the rift races out and blade-shaped fins of bronze light rise along it one
    after another, like a shark's back. The fins fade (1.0 s).
  - Swift: he runs with the blade in the floor; the fins rise behind him.
  - Crescent: three rifts fan out.
- **M15. Caltrops** (0.8 s).
  - Base: a spin on one foot with the blade low (0 to 0.3 s), then he cuts across the floor
    (0.35 s): a fan of iron spikes flies out and lands standing, like small blades planted in
    the floor (the patch).
  - Swift: he sows them behind him as he dashes.
  - Crescent: the fan flies farther and the patch is wider.
- **M16. Riposte** (0.6 s guard, then the answer).
  - Base: blade upright in a reverse grip before him, left paw open behind (0 to 0.6 s). On a
    catch: a bronze ring flashes, one slow frame, then he turns past the blow with a backhand
    cut that draws a full moon (hit), and flicks the blade. If nothing comes he lowers the
    guard with a slow turn of the sword in his paw.
  - Swift: on a catch he is gone and stands behind the attacker; the cut lands a beat later.
  - Crescent: the full moon flies off as a 剑气.

## 止水 Still Water (pale aqua)

- **M17. Moonfang** (1.0 s).
  - Base: he sinks low, blade at his hip as if sheathed; a thin pale line draws to the end of
    the dash (0 to 0.2 s). He is a streak (0.25 to 0.4 s). He stands at the far end, blade out,
    still. A beat later a pale crescent moon opens along the whole path (0.55 s, hit) and each
    foe on it wears a small moon (the mark).
  - Swift: he turns and streaks back along the same line; a second moon crosses the first.
  - Crescent: he stays in the low draw and the moon flies out without him.
- **M18. Calm Crescent** (was Tide Crescent; 0.9 s).
  - Base: a turning hop, body flat in the air like a barrel roll, the blade drawing a full pale
    circle (0 to 0.4 s). Out of the roll the circle unfolds into the wide wave (0.45 s, hit).
    It glides out without a sound, and one thin ripple spreads on the floor where he was. He
    lands low.
  - Swift: the roll is longer and cuts the foes he passes.
  - Crescent: no roll: a wide two-handed cut from a planted stance; the wave is 4 u wide.
- **M19. Mirror Image** (0.6 s).
  - Base: he draws a slow circle with the point in front of him, a mirror of pale light (0 to
    0.3 s), and steps back out of himself (0.4 s): the image stays in the pose. While it stands
    it turns its sword in slow wheels (剑花). It bursts as a ring of mirror shards.
  - Swift: he does not step back, he dashes out of the mirror.
  - Crescent: the image throws a small 剑气 each second.

## 狂热 Fervour (silver)

- **M20. Fox Step** (0.6 s).
  - Base: a low dash that corkscrews: his body turns once and the blade draws a spiral of
    silver strokes along the path (0 to 0.35 s, hit); the spiral launches the foes. He slides
    to a stop on one knee, blade behind him.
  - Swift: a second press corkscrews again, the other way.
  - Crescent: he stays; one rising cut sends the spiral along the floor by itself.
- **M21. Blade Storm** (1.4 s).
  - Base: a small leap, then three flat turns in the air; each turn draws a wider silver ring
    (0.3, 0.6, 0.9 s, hits) and pulls the foes in. He comes down with the point in the floor
    (1.1 s, hit) and a last ring bursts.
  - Swift: the three turns travel through the pack.
  - Crescent: the last ring rolls outward as 剑风.
- **M22. Blade Dance** (0.9 s).
  - Base: a no-hands cartwheel with the blade sweeping a tilted ring (0.2 s), then a second
    ring tilted the other way (0.45 s, hit), so the two rings cross like an X round him. The
    foes go up. He lands light, after-images trailing from his feet (the speed).
  - Swift: he cartwheels 2.5 u into the pack first.
  - Crescent: the two crossed rings widen to 3 u.
- **M23. Blade Wheel** (was Whirlwind Slash; 1.0 s).
  - Base: two big figure-eight wheels of the sword leave a wheel of ghost blades turning on
    the blade (0 to 0.5 s). An underhand throwing cut sends it off (0.6 s): the wheel travels,
    its blades circling, and carries the foes along.
  - Swift: he steps into the wheel and spins along with it.
  - Crescent: the wheel is wider and stands for a moment where it ends.
- **M24. Thousand Cuts** (1.4 s).
  - Base: he lowers the blade and breathes out; his outline splits into silver after-images
    (0 to 0.2 s). He is gone. Eight strokes appear on the foes, one every 0.12 s, each from a
    new side, each a long silver brush stroke that stays in the air (0.25 to 1.1 s, hits). He
    is back beside the last foe, slowly sheathing; as the sword clicks home all eight strokes
    flash at once and the last foe goes up (1.3 s).
  - Swift: he comes out of the blur where the player aims, with one more streak through the
    pack.
  - Crescent: he never leaves. He stands and sheathes slowly while eight images of him do the
    strokes; the click sends a ring of light out.

## The passives (looks only)

- **M25.** Crimson Edge: a crit leaves a gold scar stroke on the foe. Calm Edge (was Cold
  Edge): a pale sheen on the blade, one ripple on the fourth hit. Razor Wake: thin silver
  strokes circle his feet.

## The ten specials (Will)

- **M26.** A special is a cast of its own and as showy as a skill. It is done by a **sword
  spirit**, a large image of him in the colours of the two wills, so his own hands are never
  stopped. No special is only damage over time; where a scar is left, it is a side part.
- **M27. One Thought 一念** (ranged). A spirit sword three times his height rises behind him
  (0 to 0.3 s) and falls along his aim (0.45 s, hit): one white-gold line splits the floor,
  7 u long. The line glows on for 3 s.
- **M28. Unmoved 不动** (self buff). A great bronze sword drops from above and plants itself in
  front of him (0.3 s, hit): a shock ring. It stands for 3 s with slow rings rising round it,
  then breaks into slivers.
- **M29. Clear Mirror 明镜** (heal, and a debuff). The floor round him turns to a mirror for a
  breath (0 to 0.3 s). His reflection rises out of it, sheathes (0.5 s, hit), and one ripple
  crosses the mirror. The mirror fades from the edge in.
- **M30. Frenzy 狂澜** (melee, and a self buff). Four silver spirits burst out to four sides
  (0.1 s) and cut back in across him, a cross, then a second cross turned a little (0.2 to
  1.0 s, eight hits). His blade trails silver for the 4 s that follow.
- **M31. Counterblow 后发** (melee). A spirit stands before him with its blade crossed (1 s).
  A blow that lands on it rings like a bell; the spirit answers with a rising cut (hit). If no
  blow comes it bows and cuts once.
- **M32. See Through 看破** (debuff). Everything pauses for one slow frame. A thin gold line
  appears on every foe within reach, where the cut will come (0.2 s). Half a second later all
  the lines flash at once (0.7 s, hit).
- **M33. Wild Dance 乱舞** (melee, one foe). The spirit flickers round one foe; its ten strokes
  draw a five-point star and its inner lines (0.1 to 0.9 s, hits). The star folds in on the
  foe (1.0 s).
- **M34. Still Mountain 静岳** (self buff). A huge seated spirit appears behind him, sword
  across its knees, for 3 s; the floor under him is a still pale ring. At the end it rises,
  and a slow ring goes out (hit).
- **M35. Last Stand 破釜** (melee, and a self buff). The spirit raises the sword in both paws
  and drives the pommel into the floor (0.3 s, hit): a bronze and silver pillar and a shock
  ring. Thin silver strokes stream off him for 4 s.
- **M36. Flow 行云** (ranged). A ribbon of pale light with six small spirit swords along it
  flies out in an S, 6 u, through the foes, and comes back to his paw (0.2 to 0.9 s, hits).
