# Ashfang kit 2.0: what changes from kit 1.7

**Version 2, 2026-11-18, from the design thread to the ability page thread, which owns the kit
file.** Kesh's picks: R2 a and R3 a stand (01:30 Singapore time). R1 changed at 01:33 to 01:35:
"no keep"; "it can keep effect, but not the name"; a special is never "2 stack just for a dot";
a damage over time "can, but be a fancy way". Talents are 2.1 (`ASHFANG_TALENT_PATHS.md`).
Motions are version 2 (`ASHFANG_MOTIONS.md`). Lines K1 to K17.

## No elements

- **K1. The four families are wills, not elements (S1, S9, S10).** No fire, earth, water or
  wind in any name, line or look. Same skills in each family, same colours:

  | Will | Was | Colour | A mark gives | Effect on a foe (same numbers as before) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 剑意 Intent | fire | gold | 4% more damage | **the scar 剑痕** (was the burn) |
  | 不屈 Unbowed | earth | bronze (the ochre) | 4% less damage taken | **the daunt 震慑** (was the stun; same 1 s cap and 2 s rule, same small-stun rule) |
  | 止水 Still Water | water | pale aqua | 1 health every 2 s in a fight | **stillness 凝滞** (was the slow) |
  | 狂热 Fervour | wind | silver | basic attacks 5% faster | **the launch 挑飞** (was the throw up), the pull, the push |

- **K2. The scar is seen.** A gold line on the foe; at each tick a small ghost blade flashes in
  and cuts along it. No flames, no smoke.
- **K3. Will marks.** An orb is a will mark; same rule and sizes. On the HUD and over his empty
  left paw they show as 剑, 屈, 水, 热 in the will colours.
- **K4. Eleven renames.**

  | Was | Is |
  |---|---|
  | Flame Crescent | Keen Crescent 锐月 |
  | Blazing Cascade 炎舞连斩 ★ | Radiant Cascade 耀舞连斩 |
  | Flashfire | Sword Bloom 剑绽 |
  | Ground Wave | Rift Cut 裂痕斩 |
  | Earthsplitter 裂地斩 ★ | Titan Cleave 巨剑斩 |
  | Inkstone Dive 墨岩坠 ★ | Ink Dive 墨坠 |
  | Riptide Form 逆潮四式 ★ | Calm Forms 止水四式 |
  | Tide Crescent | Calm Crescent 静月 |
  | Tide Ring 水月剑阵 ★ | Mirror Ring 镜月剑阵 |
  | Cold Edge | Calm Edge 静锋 |
  | Whirlwind Slash | Blade Wheel 旋刃 |

- **K5. Looks.** MOTIONS M7c and M7d: 剑意 is thin gold lines, brush strokes and stars; 不屈 is
  heavy bronze strokes, shock rings, floor cracks and the giant sword; 止水 is still pale rings,
  one ripple, moons and mirror images; 狂热 is silver after-images, fast strokes, spirals and
  wheels of blades. In skill lines: flames and fire swirls are gold light and gold blades;
  rocks, stone spikes and shards are sparks, slivers and blade fins; water, foam and water
  images are pale light and mirror images; wind, gusts and whirls are strokes, rings and wheels
  of blades. Caltrops are iron.
- **K6. Rules and combos keep their effect and lose the element word.** "Fire meets wind and
  water" (S56) is "The scar meets fervour and calm": Wind fans fire (C1) is **Fervour spreads
  the scar**; Fire spin (C10) is **Scar spin**; Quench (C12) is **Settle 归静** (a 止水 skill
  closes a scar: the rest lands at once and the foe is blinded 0.8 s). Coloured whirl (C5) is
  **Coloured wheel**: the wheel takes the will of a crescent thrown through it. Light, then
  set off (S40) is scar, then Sword Bloom. Crimson Edge's crits leave a scar. Calm Edge reads
  stillness.

## The count, the passive, the pool (unchanged from version 1 of this list)

- **K7. The count is gone (S3, S7 and every line that reads them).** S11 parts 1, 2 and 4 go.
  S7 "counts double in the air" goes; launches stay, for Spike and Smash down. S14 Flurry:
  drop "the cheap way to build the count". S15 Crescent Finisher: back to today's rule (it
  reads the other skills landed in the last 2 s). S17: drop "All its cuts count". S30: drop the
  count line. S38 Razor Wake: drop "its cuts count"; the launch on the third cut stays. Also
  S40, S44, S53 (G1 keeps its strong-cut half, G2 goes), S54 (A4 and A5 fall away), and the
  page's count legend.
- **K8. The passive (S11) is Sword Heart 剑心.** Parts: the will marks (K3); the strong cut
  after each cast, with its wave; the sword thrown by the sixth basic hit (its burst carries
  the effect of his newest mark's will); the dash as passed.
- **K9. Kept as they are:** the daunt's limits (S5), the boss rule (S6), the dash cancel (S8),
  Wall slam (S55), the six-hit combo (S12), the dash (S13).
- **K10. Thousand Cuts is back; Gale Crescent is out** (R3 a). Thousand Cuts takes its kit 1.6
  text (old S39) without the count sentence; audit fix A8 holds again. Will 狂热. The pool is
  12 Blade, 5 Wave, 9 Art; 7, 6, 6, 7 by will.
- **K11. Combo lines:** Kick the caltrops (C9) is Rift Cut, Ink Dive and Skyblade Waltz's
  floor crescent. Wall slam (C2) names Blade Wheel and Skyblade Waltz's crescent. "Launch,
  then finish" may name Thousand Cuts again beside Ink Dive.

## Every skill entry

- **K12. Three parts for each of the 23 pressed skills:** the base form, the Swift form, the
  Crescent form. The forms are rows F1 to F23 of the table in the talent doc, section 5. The
  standard thrust, 剑气 and 剑风 are T6; safety and standing are T7 and T8.
- **K13. Base motions.** The 17 skills that are not Kesh's videos take the motion scripts M8 to
  M24. Each keeps its total damage, effect and cooldown; where a skill has more beats now, the
  damage lands on the beats marked "hit". The six ★ skills keep their motion; only their words
  and looks change (M7d).
- **K14. A form is part of the cast:** no second mark, no second strong cut. Its added part
  carries the skill's own effect at half length. The passives have no form (looks: M25).

## For the page

- **K15. Phase 1:** the 17 base motions (M8 to M24), the dash look (M7b), the new passive (two
  marks over the left paw; no count), the new names, and no element look on any clip.
- **K16. Phase 2:** the Swift and Crescent forms, with a Base, Swift, Crescent switch on each
  card. The basic combo: Swift changes only hit 5 (the low lunge carries him through) and adds
  the dash to the thrown sword; Crescent adds a 剑气 to hits 1 to 5.
- **K17. Phase 3:** the ten specials (talent doc T12), each a showy cast by a sword spirit (M26
  to M36). The ten Fusion mix clips go.
