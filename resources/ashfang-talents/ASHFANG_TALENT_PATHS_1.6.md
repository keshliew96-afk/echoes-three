  (kit S10): every Ashfang stores an orb with each skill cast, holds two, and gets a very small

  - Which mixes a run can make comes from the four skills the draft gave him. Fire, fire, wind
    and earth can make Blaze, Firestorm, Magma and Sandstorm, and the pure mix of wind or earth
    by casting that one skill twice.

## 3. The fifteen talents

### Tier I, Style (free)

- **T6. Crescendo (Tempo).** The count stops at 60, not 30. For each 10 count his basic attacks
  are 6% faster (36% at 60). The trade: each hit he takes knocks 10 off the count.
  - Why: the fantasy of the combo is "do not get hit". Without the talent the count is safe.
  - 1.3 (A4): only a foe's attack knocks 10 off, and at most once a second. Floor beds,
    hazards and damage that ticks do not.
- **T7. Sword Wave (Crescent).** Every cut of his basic combo throws a sword wave: 5 u, through
  every foe, for 70% of the cut to each foe beyond his reach. A foe in reach takes the cut as
  today, not both. Strong cuts keep their own full wave (kit S11). From 光信's 迅烈之华.
  - The trade is the 70%. What he gets is range and a line of foes hit by one cut.
  - 1.5: the last hit throws the sword itself (kit S11). Sword Wave leaves that hit as it is.
  - 1.6: the combo is six hits (kit S12), so one combo is five waves and the throw.
- **T8. Fusion (Spellblade).** 1.1 (it was Orbs). His two orbs are spent to cast a mix by
  themselves (T4, T5). He gives up the orbs' small bonuses for it.

### Tier II, Power (20 Embers)

- **T9. Keen Rhythm (Tempo).** All his damage is 4% higher for each 10 count: 12% at 30. With
  Crescendo 24% at 60.
- **T10. Honed Wave (Crescent).** Every sword wave deals 25% more (T3).
- **T11. Tempered (Spellblade).** The effects of his skills are stronger: burns deal 30% more,
  stuns last 30% longer (never past 1 s), slows are 10 points deeper (never past 60%) and last
  30% longer, and pushes, pulls and throws go 30% farther or longer. On a boss the 20% of kit S6
  is 30%.

### Tier III, Trick (40 Embers)

- **T12. Flash Step (Tempo).** His dash thrust hits twice as hard (16) and adds 3 count for each
  foe it cuts, not 2. A foe felled by the dash thrust readies the dash at once. From Irelia and
  Katarina. This is the "bit of dash cut": in a pack of weak foes he chains dash after dash.
- **T13. Returning Wave (Crescent).** A sword wave that reaches the end of its flight turns and
  flies back to him, cutting each foe again for half. The return carries damage only, no element
  effect.
  - 1.6: Flare Wave and Ground Wave left the kit (1.5: Stone Crescent and Blade Rain). The
    floor crescent of Skyblade Waltz comes back along the floor and pushes the foes 1 u toward
    him.
  - 1.5: the thrown sword already comes back to his paw; with this talent it cuts every foe on
    its way back for half.
  - Whirlwind Slash comes back along its path and brings the foes with it, to his feet.
- **T14. Enchanted Edge (Spellblade).** For 5 s after a skill cast his sword holds that skill's
  element, and each basic cut, and each wave a basic cut throws, carries a small effect. A new
  cast changes the element. From sword infusion in Genshin.
  - Fire: a burn of 2 a second for 2 s. Earth: every 4th cut stuns 0.3 s. Water: a 20% slow for
    1.5 s. Wind: a cut pulls the foe 0.3 u toward him, a wave pushes it 0.5 u.
  - It stores no orb. The boss rule (S6) does not apply to these small effects.
  - 1.5: the thrown sword's burst takes the element of his newest orb (kit S11). When he holds
    no orb, as right after a mix, it takes the element the sword holds from this talent.
  - 1.3 (A1): the earth stun does not start the foe's 2 s without stuns (kit S5).
  - 1.3 (A7): it moves the same foe at most once a second, so a wave on every cut cannot hold
    a foe away for ever.

### Tier IV, Reach (60 Embers)

- **T15. Wide Arc (Tempo).** Set in 1.6 against the six-hit combo (1.5 rewrote it; 1.0 made the
  old spin cut hit all round him).
  - Each of the first five basic cuts reaches 25% farther, and the two wide ones (the flat sweep
    and the diagonal down-cut) sweep a 25% wider arc.
  - The sword thrown by the sixth hit whirls and bursts at 2 u, not 1.5.
  - His Blade skills reach 25% farther: the fans, the slams and the leaps of all thirteen
    (Earthsplitter's slam is 1.9 u to 3.5 u, Thousand Cuts finds foes within 3.75 u).
  - His dash cuts go 25% farther: the dash, Fox Step 2.5 u, Moonfang 4 u, and the dashes inside
    Blazing Cascade (3.75 u) and Skyblade Waltz (3.1 u).
  - The count holds 5 s without a hit, not 3.
- **T16. Long Wave (Crescent).** Sword waves fly 40% farther and are 30% wider. For the waves
  that are not a line: Whirlwind Slash's whirl is 30% wider (1.6: Flare Wave left the kit). 1.5: the
  thrown sword flies 5.6 u and its burst is 30% wider; with Wide Arc the two add (55%).
- **T17. Wide Elements (Spellblade).** His element areas are 25% wider: every mix, Flashfire's
  ring and bursts, Caltrops, Mirror Image's burst, Blade Dance and Blade Storm's pull. Slows from
  his skills last 1 s longer. (Burns do not: a longer burn would be more damage, and this tier
  adds reach only.)

### Tier V, Capstone (80 Embers)

- **T18. Storm of Blades (Tempo).** When the count reaches 30, a storm of blades starts round him
  by itself: for 4 s, radius 2 u, moving with him, each foe inside takes 4 every quarter second
  (64 over the 4 s). Its cuts count. Once every 15 s. This is the "bit of bladestorm".
  1.3 (A5): when its 15 s are over it starts again by itself if the count is 30 or more.
- **T19. Spirit Swords (Crescent).** Each skill cast calls a spirit sword that floats at his
  shoulder for 8 s, up to 3. On each strong cut of his (kit S11: the last hit's sword throw, and the
  first cut after a cast) every spirit sword throws a sword wave too, for 4 each.
- **T20. Cataclysm (Spellblade).** Every 6th skill he casts, all four elements strike round him
  at once, radius 3 u: 24, thrown up 0.5 s, then burning 4 a second and slowed 40% for 3 s. Six
  small marks by the count fill as he casts. A mix is not a cast (T4).

## 4. What a build feels like (T21)

- **All Tempo.** He never stops cutting. The count climbs to 60, his cuts come a third faster and
  a quarter harder, he dashes from foe to foe, and every 15 s the storm whirls. One hit taken
  costs 10 count, so he dashes through what would hit him.
- **All Crescent.** He fights from 5 to 7 u. Every cut is a wave, every wave comes back, three
  spirit swords add theirs, and the long wide waves rake the whole room.
- **All Spellblade.** Every two skills something big happens: the room burns, freezes, sinks in
  mud or is thrown into the air. Between casts his cuts carry the last element. Every sixth cast
  all four strike at once.
- **Combo with waves:** Crescendo, Honed Wave, Returning Wave, Long Wave, Storm of Blades. Faster
  cuts are more strong cuts and more waves, and each wave feeds the count on the way out and on
  the way back.
- **Elements with the combo:** Fusion, Keen Rhythm, Flash Step, Wide Elements, Cataclysm. The mixes
  hit whole packs, so the count is full after one mix, and the count makes the next mix hit
  harder.

## 5. Combos checked (T22)

   cast. He is never without a mix.

- Crescendo: it dashes out of a wind-up aimed at him sooner (at 20 count or more).

- Fusion: it does not aim for a mix; it casts by the kit's rules (S44) and takes what comes. With
  an orb stored it does not hold a skill back. Without Fusion it does not steer its two orbs.
