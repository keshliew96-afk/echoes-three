# Ashfang: ways his skills play together

**CLOSED: all twelve taken.** Kesh, 2026-11-17 15:08 Singapore time: "the combo look good". Written into kit 1.4; kit S57 names the line for each.

**2026-11-17, on kit 1.2.** Design only. Twelve new combos, C1 to C12. Each is one small rule
between two skills or two elements; none needs a talent. The ones the kit already has are in kit
S40. Answer "all", or name the ones to drop: "drop C7, C11".

| # | Name | The rule | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Wind fans fire** | A wind skill that hits a burning foe spreads its burn to the foes within 1.5 u | any wind skill and any fire skill |
| C2 | **Wall slam** | A foe he pushes or drags into a wall takes 10 and is stunned 0.5 s. Stonehide's slab and Stone Ring count as walls | Gale Crescent, Whirlwind Slash, or a pushing mix |
| C3 | **Spike** | Crescent Finisher on a foe in the air slams it down: 50% more to it, and 10 to the foes within 1.5 u of where it lands | a throw up, then Crescent Finisher |
| C4 | **Bat it away** | Stone Crescent that hits a foe in the air sends it flying 3 u. The foes it lands on take 10 and are stunned 0.4 s | a throw up, then Stone Crescent |
| C5 | **Coloured whirl** | A crescent thrown through his Whirlwind Slash gives the whirl its element for the rest of its trip: Flame makes it burn, Stone makes it stun 0.3 s on touch, Tide makes it slow 40% | Whirlwind Slash and a crescent |
| C6 | **The image throws too** | While his Mirror Image stands, it throws a copy of each Wave skill he casts, at the same spot, for half | Mirror Image and a Wave skill |
| C7 | **Burst the image** | A dash cut of his that passes through the image bursts it at once | Mirror Image and the dash, Fox Step or Moonfang |
| C8 | **Sent back** | Riposte also catches a shot. A shot he parries flies back at the shooter as a sword wave for 30 | Riposte, against foes that shoot |
| C9 | **Kick the caltrops** | Ground Wave or Gale Crescent through his Caltrops scatters them: the patch is twice as wide for the time it has left, and each foe on it is stunned by its first step again | Caltrops and one of the two waves |
| C10 | **Fire spin** | If a foe that Blade Storm cuts is burning, every foe the spin cuts catches that burn | Blade Storm and any fire skill |
| C11 | **Dash strike** | Lunge Strike or Hilt Bash pressed during his dash, Fox Step or Moonfang comes out at the end of the dash, reaches 1 u farther and hits 30% harder | a dash and one of the two |
| C12 | **Quench** | Tide Crescent or Blade Rain on a burning foe puts the fire out in a puff of steam: the foe takes what was left of the burn at once and is blinded 0.8 s | a fire skill, then one of the two |

Notes.

- C1 and C10 make fire spread; C12 trades a burn for a blind. So a fire build wants wind, and a
  water skill is its safety valve.
- C3 and C4 give the throw ups a second use besides the double count (kit S7). C3 is Stonehide's
  Spike rule on Ashfang's finisher.
- C2 is Stonehide's Wall slam rule, shared. With A6 of the audit it turns a push into a choice:
  push toward a wall, not into the open.
- C5, C6 and C9 are for a Crescent build: waves that change other things he has put down.
- C8 gives Riposte a job against shooters, where a parry does little today.
- C11 is the dash attack from Hades. It needs the game to keep a pressed skill for the end of a
  dash, which is new machinery.
- The mixes of the Fusion talent are not changed by any of these.
- New looks: a burning, stony or wet whirl (C5); the image's own wave throw (C6); a foe batted
  through the air (C4).
