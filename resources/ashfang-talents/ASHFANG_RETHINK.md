## 1. The idea

- **N1.** One pool of 26 skills. Each skill has a **base form**: what is on his page today.
- **N2.** The tier I talent sets the style, and the style re-shapes every skill he holds:
  - **Swift 疾 (机动):** each skill gets a thrust, a dash or a cut through the foes.
  - **Crescent 剑气 (站桩):** each skill throws 剑气 (a line) or 剑风 (an area). He stands.
  - **Will 意志:** skills stay in their base form. Two will marks cast a special by themselves.
- **N3.** With no tier I talent he plays the base forms.
- **N4.** The four elements become the four wills (R1). The combo count goes (R2).
- **N4b.** Every skill's motion is redrawn to the level of his videos: a ready pose, the cut, a
  flourish and an end pose, each beat with its own big shape. Quick skills stay under 1.1 s.

## 2. Three picks

- **R1. What happens to fire, earth, water and wind.**
  - a) **My pick.** Skills keep their looks and what they do (burn, stun, slow, push). Only the
    name of the family changes: fire skills give 剑意, earth 不屈, water 止水, wind 狂热. This is
    the same pairing as his orbs (fire damage, earth damage reduction, water recovery, wind
    attack speed). No clip is thrown away.
  - b) The elements go fully. No fire or water looks, no burns or slows from a family; every
    skill is a plain sword art in its will's colour. Tide Ring and the fire skills are redrawn.
- **R2. The combo count (连击).**
  - a) **My pick.** Gone from the whole hero, the passive too. Nothing reads it any more.
  - b) Gone from path 1 only. It stays in the passive and still makes skills cool down faster.

- **R3. Thousand Cuts back, Gale Crescent out.** Gale Crescent came back yesterday only because
  path 2 had too few ranged skills. Now every skill has a ranged form, and Gale Crescent is a
  second straight wave beside Flame Crescent.
  - a) **My pick.** Thousand Cuts returns in its place: the blur that cuts from foe to foe, the
    one move of its kind. The pool is 12 close, 5 ranged, 9 arts.
  - b) Leave the pool as it is.

## 3. The passive: Sword Heart 剑心 (was Bladesong)

- **N5. Will marks** (the orbs, renamed). Each skill press stores the mark of its will. He holds
  two; a new one pushes out the oldest. Each mark is a very small bonus:

  | Will | Was | Bonus of one mark |
  |---|---|---|
  | 剑意 Intent | fire | 4% more damage |
  | 不屈 Unbowed | earth | 4% less damage taken |
  | 止水 Still Water | water | 1 health every 2 s in a fight |
  | 狂热 Fervour | wind | basic attacks 5% faster |

- **N6. The strong cut.** Kept: the first basic cut after each skill is 50% stronger and throws a
  wave. The sixth basic hit throws the sword, as on his page.
- **N7. The dash.** Kept: top priority, cancels anything, untouchable.
- **N8. Gone:** the count and the faster cooldowns that came from it (R2).
- **N9.** It serves all three styles: the marks are small bonuses for Swift and Crescent, and
  the fuel of Will.

One pick per tier from any path, as before. Every talent works alone.

- **N10.** Kept as passed: Sword Wave, Honed Wave, Returning Wave, Long Wave, Spirit Swords,
  Flash Step (without the count).
- **N11.** Renamed, same job: Fusion is Resolve, Tempered is Tempered Will, Enchanted Edge is

- **N12.** Gone with the count: Crescendo, Keen Rhythm, Wide Arc, Storm of Blades.
- **N13.** New: Piercing Step, Keen Point, Long Stride, Afterimage.
- **N14. Swift and safety.** A Swift thrust is untouchable only where the base skill already
  was. It stops at a wall and never ends on a floor warning: he lands on the nearest clear spot.
- **N15. Swift and the basic attack.** The fifth hit (the low lunge) carries him through the foes
  in front and stops behind them. With no foe in reach it does not move him. A dash while the
  thrown sword is away takes him to the sword, cutting on the way.
- **N16. Crescent and standing.** A Crescent form never moves him, except in the six skills made
  from Kesh's videos and sketch, which keep every move. The dash is his escape.
