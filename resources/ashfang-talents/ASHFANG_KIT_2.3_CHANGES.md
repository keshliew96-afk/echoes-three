## Sword Grove 剑林, in place of Caltrops

- **K47.** Art, 8 s, 不屈, motion M15. The plant 1.0 s, the call 0.3 s. The cooldown starts at
  the plant.
- **K48. Base.**
  - The plant: six ghost swords plant in a ring, radius 1.5 u, at a spot up to 4 u away: 8 to
    each foe there. They stand for 5 s, a thread of light from each hilt to his paw.
  - The call: a second press of the same key within 5 s; at 5 s they come back by themselves.
    Each sword flies straight to his paw through every foe on its way: 4 a sword, at most 3
    swords on one foe. So up to 20 on a foe that stands between the grove and him.
  - The call is not a cast: no mark, no step toward Echo or One Mind, no Spirit Sword. The
    dash cancels its 0.3 s.
  - An echo's grove is a short ghost ring for half that fades; it is never called back (the
    page thread's build, 16:08, taken).
- **K49. Swift (changes the call).** On the second press he thrusts to the middle of the grove
  from wherever he stands, through every foe: 10. The swords burst in a ring where he lands:
  12 within 1.8 u. At 5 s with no press, the swords come back as in the base. T7 holds.
- **K50. Crescent (adds).** Planted to 6 u. On the call each sword first spins a 剑风 ring,
  1.2 u for 4, at most 2 rings on one foe; then it flies back as in the base.
- **K51. Combo C9 changes.** Kick the caltrops becomes Ring the grove: Rift Cut, Ink Dive's
  landing or Skyblade Waltz's floor crescent that touches the grove makes each standing sword
  burst once, 4 within 1 u.
- **K52. Look.** The planted swords are full-size and stand in the floor, so they never read as
  the two small ghost swords at his shoulders or the Spirit Swords over his head. Fox Crimson
  with a Moon Silver edge, like everything of his.
