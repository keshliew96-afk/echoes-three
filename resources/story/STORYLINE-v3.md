# Echoes 回声 · World storyline, draft v3 (21 Nov)

v3 = after two Fable critic rounds (`CRITIC-1.md`, `CRITIC-2.md`; first draft kept as `STORYLINE-v1.md`).

Built on Kesh's 大方向: the 王庭 (royal court) holds a ritual to summon a god; no god answers; the
世界屏障 (world barrier) cracks open a 裂缝 (breach); an Old God 旧神 notices and looks through it;
creatures go corrupt and mad; the heroes fight their way to the 祭坛 (altar) to seal the breach.

Choices marked **[PICK n]** are Kesh's (all listed in §8). Design only: nothing in the game changes
until the freeze lifts. It keeps every passed model, all 5 levels plus the fork, both endings,
Endless, Daily, Boss Rush and the 40-room run.

---

## 1. The story

**Before.** Long ago the gods 众神 sang the world into being. Their song became the **Veil 天幕**, the
world barrier in the sky, and its warmth lit the **hearth-fires 炉火** of every land. Then the gods
went silent, and one by one the hearth-fires began to die.

**The court.** The **Bell Court 钟庭 [PICK 2]**, the ibex lords of the high Spire, ruled by the
**Ibex King 岩羊王 [PICK 3]**, would not let the fires go out. Generations ago they had taken a casting of
the oldest hearth bell, the one in the forest that still rings the gods' true note (Wick's bell; Wick
never knew). From it they made a bell **far bigger and louder**: a song that *commands* instead of
*asks*. Their founder cast it in the Ember Kiln: the **Summoning Bell 召神钟**. They hung it on the
**altar 祭坛** at the top of the Spire, and the High Cantor sang the **Calling 召神仪式**.

**The night it went wrong.** No god answered. The note struck the Veil and **tore it**: the
**Breach 裂缝** is a tear in the sky above the Spire. The ground split too, in a **Y**: its root is
deep under the Barrow (the Hollow Heart), one arm runs up to the altar, the other to the Kiln. The
High Cantor, at the altar's edge, fell into the ground-crack. The Summoning Bell fell down it to the
Kiln, where it still **drones** the Calling. **That drone widens the tear a little every night.**

**The Old God.** Outside the Veil, **the Watcher 凝视者 [PICK 1]** heard the bell and looked in. Its first
look landed on the altar: everyone inside the altar ring turned to stone, mid-song. Everyone outside
it went mad. Through the tear its look falls on every land as **violet light**, strongest along the Y.
Creatures in the light hear **the Hollow 虚声**, a hollow copy of the gods' song. They repeat it (they
*echo* it) and lose themselves. In each land, the light pushed one **verse** of the true song into the
strongest one standing there: the great beast of a wild land, or the court's own servant in a court place.
These are the **wardens 守护者**.

**The clock.** The verses only buy time. Each night the tear is a little wider, and when the Watcher's
eye fits through, it comes in.

**The heroes.** Wick's hearth is the last fire still burning. Its small band picks up the true bell
(which **hums**) and goes: free each land's warden, take back its verse, clear the light, and close
the tear at one end of the Y.

## 3. Chapters

Each chapter has the same five beats: **arrival**, **what went wrong in this land**, **the hero**,
**the verse**, **the clue**. They describe the land, never one boss, because a run rolls one of
three bosses. Lines about a single boss go in that boss's own voice line (the Hollow Voice).

**Unlock [PICK 5]:** default is Kesh's wording: **reaching a map unlocks its chapter and its hero.**
This is a rule change (today a hero unlocks on clearing the map before). It waits for the freeze
and replaces the settled rule in CLAUDE.md.

### Prologue · The Hearth Song 炉火之歌 (Wick, at camp)
"The gods sang the world, and their song kept every fire warm. Then they went quiet, and the fires
began to die. Ours still burns, because our bell still rings true. Last night a great bell rang far
away, and the sky tore open. Something is looking in. Take the bell. Take your friends."

### Chapter I · The Hollow Wood 苍林 · verse **Root 根**
- **Arrival:** the night the violet light came, the owls stopped calling.
- **This land:** the lord of the wood looked up at the light and could not look away.
- **Heroes:** the four of the hearth (§5).
- **Verse:** Root. The light over the wood thins; the freed warden's pale spirit comes to camp.

### Chapter II · The Drowned Bay 沉湾 · verse **Water 水**
- **Arrival:** the tide has stood at high water since that night; the mill turns backwards.
- **This land:** the warden of the bay holds the tide in.
- **Hero:** **Tide Caller (Rill)**, holding the last dry ledge with her shell. The light has half
  reached her (she hears the Hollow faintly); she joins at once, and the Water verse clears her head.
- **Verse:** Water. The tide finally goes out.

### Chapter III · The Ashen Barrow 灰冢 · verse **Stone 石**
- **Arrival:** the grave bells ring the hollow note with no one pulling the ropes.
- **This land:** the Y's root lies under the court's royal graves. The Hollow moves the old kings'
  bodies: their wyrm, their knights. (Bodies moved by the song, not the dead themselves.)
- **Hero:** **Bellwright (Tolly)**, the last sane grave-bell keeper. Her teacher went mad ringing the
  hollow note. She reads the bells: all cast from the same bronze as one great bell, with the court's crest.
- **Clue:** Tolly shows the crest to Quill. He goes pale and says nothing (§3b).
- **Verse:** Stone. The barrow floor splits open.

### Chapter IV · The Hollow Heart 空心 · verse **Heart 心**
- **Arrival:** under the Barrow, the caves glow violet and beat like a heart.
- **This land:** the root of the Y. The High Cantor, who sang the Calling, fell here. Down here the
  light is strongest; whoever holds the Heart verse sings the Hollow back up the crack.
- **Hero:** **Duskwing (Sable)**, of the bat colonies of these caves. His colony went mad; he flew free.
- **Verse:** Heart. The root is cut; the light everywhere dims, but the tear above the Spire is still open.
- **The fork (from the Heart verse itself, on the clear card, whichever boss fell):** the bell hums
  two ways at once: **up** to the altar under the tear, and **down** to a drone in the Kiln.
  - **Spire:** close the tear where the eye looks in.
  - **Kiln:** silence the bell that keeps tearing it.
  (Defaults to the Spire after 30 s, as now.)

### Chapter V-a · The Spire Court 王庭 · verse **Sky 天** · Ending **Seal 封印**
- **Arrival:** a spotless palace in silence. Inside the altar ring the court stands in stone,
  mid-song, facing up. Beside the altar, the High Cantor's plinth is empty.
- **This land:** the court's servants outside the ring went mad. On the altar sits **the Hush**: the
  hole in the song where the Watcher's look landed.
- **Ending Seal:** the party rings the true bell under the tear, with every verse they hold. The tear
  closes. **The eye closes slowly, and before it shuts it looks straight at the party: it will remember
  them.** The gods stay silent. The fires warm again. Far below, in the Kiln, the Summoning Bell still
  lies, quiet for now. (A threat left open.)

### Chapter V-b · The Ember Kiln 熔炉 · verse **Flame 火** · Ending **Recast 重铸**
- **Arrival:** the court's foundry; moulds of the Summoning Bell line the walls.
- **This land:** the founder who cast the bell is still casting copies while the fallen bell drones.
- **Ending Recast:** the party breaks the Summoning Bell and recasts it with their verses into a bell
  that *asks*, like Wick's. **It is raised to the altar and rung there** (an end-card line, not a new
  room). The tear closes. **The eye closes slowly and looks straight at the party before it shuts.**
  Far away, one hearth answers the new bell. The tear leaves a scar in the sky.

Seal: the door is shut, but the bad bell remains. Recast: the bad bell is gone, but the sky keeps a scar.

### Endless, Daily, Boss Rush
- **Endless Descent:** the tear is closed, but creatures still mad from the light roam the old lands.
- **Daily Descent:** one patrol a day.
- **Boss Rush:** Quill's chronicle of every warden, replayed.

## 3b. Why the party moves on 前进的理由

**The rule:** each verse **clears the light over its land** (and the land's fire warms again). Only
at an end of the Y can the tear be closed. Each verse freed makes the true bell **hum toward the next
land**, like a compass 罗盘. **The clock:** the tear widens every night, so they cannot wait.

**Quill's secret:** Quill was a court scribe. He knows the court sang the Calling, and he hides it
out of shame. Tolly's bells catch him in Chapter III (or the wind-tuners' log on the Highlands
road); he confesses everything after the Heart. This is the camp's own story arc.

| Step | Push 推 (why they can't stay) | Pull 拉 (why there) | What they learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Camp → Forest | The light reaches the hearth; the wood's beasts attack. | The bell hums toward the wood's warden. | A great bell rang far away and the sky tore. |
| Forest → Bay | The forest river stops flowing; its water backs up into the wood, turning violet. | The bell hums downstream. | Quill: "I saw a light at the top of the Spire." (He leaves out the rest.) |
| Bay → Barrow | With the tide out, the drowned bodies it held walk inland, toward the graves. | The ebb uncovers the court's sunken road 王道 to the Barrow; across the water the grave bells ring the hollow note. | A court barge in the mud holds a hymn sheet: "The Calling." Quill says it must be old. |
| Bay → Highlands | The mud road floods again at night; the only dry way is up. | The bell hums toward the storm on the peaks. | The same barge, the same hymn sheet. |
| Barrow → Heart | The barrow floor splits; violet light pours up from below. | Tolly: "Every bell here rings toward the deep." | Tolly's bells carry the court's crest. Quill is caught. |
| Highlands → Heart | The storm breaks the pass; the ice chasm is the only way on. | The chasm glows violet: it leads down to the root. | The wind-tuners' log: "the court's bell rang and the sky tore." Quill is caught. |
| Heart → fork | The root is cut, but the tear is still widening. | The Heart verse hums two ways: the altar and the Kiln. | Quill confesses: the court wanted to save the fires. |
| Spire / Kiln | The eye is almost through. | The last verse: Sky or Flame. | Spire: who froze and why. Kiln: the founder never stopped. |

In the game this is text only: a line on each level-clear card ("The bell hums downstream."),
a Quill line at camp per chapter, and the clue on the next chapter's arrival card.

## 4. The Old God 旧神: the Watcher 凝视者

- **What it is:** something outside the Veil, older than the gods. We see **an eye** behind the tear and
  **violet light**, and once, in the Heart, the shadow of something huge moving behind the eye.
  It does not hate the world. It noticed it, and it wants in. That is enough.
- **Cause and symptom:** the **light** is the cause; **the Hollow** is what creatures hear in it.
- **Its voice:** the Hollow Voice (the violet line under each boss's title, in the game now) is the
  Watcher speaking through each warden.
- **The ending look:** at either ending the eye closes slowly and looks straight at the party first.
  A true boss fight with the Eye is kept for later as a new model [PICK 7].
- **Seen in game (a visual, after the freeze) [PICK 8]:** the tear in each map's sky, a little wider each level.

## 12. The bard 吟游诗人 (hydra and gorgon blood 多首蛇 · 美杜莎血脉)

A wandering singer who fits every map, so no new land.

**Who (draft):** **Manyvoice 千声 [PICK 17]**, a serpent with **three heads** (hydra blood). **Why three
heads (Kesh, 21 Nov): a bard must play and sing, and with three heads she sings three different songs
at once.** Each head carries its own song, so three songs run side by side. For a top-down read: a bright frilled crest
on each head, gold eyes (gorgon blood, Medusa's line), and a rattle-drum on her tail tip (no hands: the
