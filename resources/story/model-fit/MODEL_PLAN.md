# Model plan for the new storyline · 21 Nov

**Kesh's picks (21 Nov, 2:35 PM):** 1a redo all 18 human figures as animals, in phases · 2 one shared
ibex base body for the court characters · 3 rename Bell Abbess → **Bell Mistress**, Bell Sacristan →
**Bell Page** · 4a Lich Ram redone as an old ibex king's body · 5 the **Summoning Bell is the Kiln's
third boss** (a gated row, like every land's third) · 6 make all six gap models.

Two Fable critics looked at the front concept image of every existing model (76 creatures and 6 NPCs)
against STORYLINE.md v5; updated to v7 after the round-4 critic. They judged concept sheets at about 512 px, not the rigged GLBs or textures in
the game's light. Design only: nothing goes in the game until the freeze lifts.

## What they found (three things run through everything)

1. **Human figures in an animal world.** 18 models are plain humans, human skeletons or human-like
   figures. That is the main 不合理 point for the new story ("an ibex court in an animal world").
2. **Real-religion look.** The Bell Abbess (a nun with mitre, crosses, crook), the Gale Herald (a church
   angel), the Bone Reeve (reaper plus bishop), the Violet Choir and the Censer (crosses), the Ash Keener
   (prayer beads), the First Bell (a cross on its bag). This is a risk for Steam.
3. **Almost no foe shows the violet light.** The bosses do; most foes have amber, green or cyan eyes.
   The colour rule already makes Violet the corrupted-foe colour, so one texture pass fixes it.

Also: the court is "ibex lords", but the only ibex model is the Skyhorn.
