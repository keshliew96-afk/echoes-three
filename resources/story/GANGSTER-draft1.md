# Gangster draft 1 (raccoon-era options, history)

## 1. Which animal (pick one)

Rules used: reads "gangster" from above at a glance; does not clash with the ten heroes (mouse, fox,
rabbit, badger, otter, snow leopard, alpaca, beetle, bat, serpent) or a big boss; has a natural "gang"
to summon.

| # | Animal | Why it fits | His gang (summons) | Home side map | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Raccoon 浣熊** | Built-in bandit mask; clever hands; a thief by nature | raccoon crooks, plus junk contraptions they build | Smugglers' Cove under the Bay | none big |
| 2 | **Octopus 章鱼** | Eight arms = eight jobs at once; ink cloud; a dock boss | eel runners, crab heavies, ink decoys | Smugglers' Cove (ties to the Sea DLC) | land movement looks odd; the bard already has "many heads" |

| 4 | **Crocodile 鳄鱼** | Old swamp godfather, slow and heavy, very "don" | baby crocs, hired toads | a drowned speakeasy in the Bay's marsh | the Mire King is already the swamp boss |
| 5 | **Alley cat 野猫** | Classic street boss, scarred ear, many lives | stray cats | the court city's back alleys under the Spire | two cats with the snow leopard |
| 6 | **Mandrill 山魈** | Loud colours, troop leader, a showman | baboon troop | Snowridge's old smuggling pass | primate looks close to the human figures we are removing |

**My pick: 1 Raccoon.** The mask says "gangster" with no costume, the hands make sense for a summoner
who calls and builds, and nothing in the game looks like him. Second choice: 2 Octopus, for the
best side map and the Sea DLC link.

## 2. Full details (written for the Raccoon; the other animals keep the same story beats)

**Name (pick):** (a) **Don Ringtail 环尾老大** (my pick); (b) **Velvet Mask 天鹅绒面具**; (c) **Big Tally 大账本**.
Gang name: **the Ringtail Crew 环尾帮**.

**Look:** a stout raccoon in a long coat with too many pockets, a striped tail, a gold-capped tooth, a
fat ledger on a chain at his hip and a big brass **whistle** he calls the crew with. Coat colour: deep
plum and gold (not Violet: plum, darker and warmer). Silhouette from above: the wide coat and the striped
tail.

**Story:**
- **Before the tear:** the Crew ran smuggling between the lands: salt from the Bay, bronze from the
  Kiln, court goods nobody should have.
- **Why he is sane:** the whole Crew stuffs **wax in their ears** (they always have: smugglers do not
  listen to anyone). The Hollow can't get in. It is the cheap, funny version of the bell's hum.
- **What he did:** while the court froze and the lands went mad, he **looted the court's sunken barges**
  (the looted crown on the Mire King came from his boats). He hoards court treasure in the Cove, and he
  is the one who owns the **court map** with the sky bridges whole (a DLC hook).
- **Why he fights the party:** they walk into his Cove with the true bell, the most valuable thing in
  the world. He wants it.
- **Why he joins:** beaten, he does the sums in his ledger: "If the sky falls in, nobody buys anything.
  No world, no business." He joins to protect his market. He never says it is for anyone else.
- **His arc:** at camp he argues with Bramble (he has been taxing her stall for years) and slowly pays
  her back; he and Quill trade secrets.

**How he appears (side map) [pick]:**
- (a) **Smugglers' Cove 走私洞** (my pick): after the first Water clear, a smugglers' door shows at low
  tide in one Bay room. Taking it is a side trip of about three rooms (Crew fights, a vault of loot),
  ending in his boss room. Bramble's rumour points to it ("the Ringtails took my best stock").
- (b) a one-room side quest: the Crew ambushes the party in the Bay; he appears as a champion-style
  fight in that room.
- (c) a full side map with its own entrance from camp, after Level II is reached.

**As a boss:** he stands back on a crate stack and blows the whistle: the Crew comes in waves
(Crooks that steal pick-ups, Heavies that block, a Lookout that marks the party), and he throws
smoke bombs and coin bags. At half health he calls his "big guy" (a summoned **Junk Golem** built from
barge wreck). When he drops, the Crew scatters and he waves a white handkerchief.

**As a hero (summoner kit direction, for his design thread later):**
- **Whistle calls:** each skill calls a different Crew member (Crook: fast stealer who grabs loot and
  steals a buff from a foe; Heavy: a blocker that taunts; Lookout: marks a target for bonus damage;
  Fixer: builds a junk turret).
- **The ledger (his resource):** kills and pick-ups add **coins** to the ledger; bigger calls cost
  coins. A "pay the crew" feel, plentiful so it stays 爽.
- **Three style paths (draft):** **Boss 老大** (fewer, stronger crew; he buffs them), **Swarm 人海** (many
  small crooks at once), **Hustle 诈术** (smoke, decoys, stealing from foes).
- Bramble's shop: he gets a small discount ("I know the owner").

**New models he needs:** Don Ringtail (hero, 15k), Crook, Heavy, Lookout, Fixer (summons, foe size),
Junk Golem (boss summon). For the side map: the Cove room set (layouts, dressing) and maybe a Crew foe
used in the side rooms (the same Crook and Heavy).
