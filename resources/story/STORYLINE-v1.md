# Echoes 回声 · World storyline, draft v1 (21 Nov)

Built on Kesh's 大方向: the 王庭 (royal court) holds a ritual to summon a god; no god answers; the
世界屏障 (world barrier) cracks open a 裂缝 (breach); an Old God 旧神 notices and gazes through it;
creatures go corrupt and mad; the heroes fight their way to the 祭坛 (altar) to seal the breach.

Everything below is filled in so it reads as one story. Names and twists marked **[PICK n]** are
choices for Kesh (list at the end). Design only: nothing in the game changes until the freeze lifts.
It keeps every model we already passed, all 5 levels plus the fork, both endings, and Endless,
Daily and Boss Rush.

---

## 1. The story in one breath

Long ago the gods 众神 sang the world into being, and their song became the **Veil 天幕**, the
world barrier that keeps the outside out. Then the gods fell silent.

The **Bell Court 钟庭 [PICK 2]**, who rule the lands from the high Still Spire, could not bear the
silence. They had the court founder cast a great **Summoning Bell 召神钟** in the Ember Kiln, hung it
on the **altar 祭坛** at the top of the Spire, and the High Cantor sang the **Calling 召神仪式** to
wake a god.

No god answered. The note struck the Veil instead, and the Veil cracked: **the Breach 裂缝**, a split
that runs from the altar straight down through the world to its roots under the Barrow.

Something outside heard the bell. **The Hollow 虚渊 [PICK 1]**, an Old God with no shape but an eye,
pressed its gaze to the crack. Its gaze falls as **violet light** (the game's corruption colour), and
it sings a hollow copy of the gods' song. Every creature that hears it repeats it back, and goes mad.
The great beasts, the **wardens 守护兽** of each land, fell first.

At the edge of the forest a small band keeps the **Hearth-Fire 炉火** with old Wick. They hold the
**last bell that still rings the true note**. They set out to walk the Breach from its root to its
top, free each land's warden (each one freed returns a **verse 诗节** of the true song to the bell),
and reach the altar to close the crack.

> This keeps the game's current lore (the gods' silence, the hollow song, the verses, the true bell)
> and makes it the *cause*: the court's ritual is why the hollow song began.

## 2. The road (5 levels plus the fork)

The party follows the Breach. Each map is a different land with its own look; the corruption is
weakest at the forest and strongest at the top and the bottom of the crack.

| III | The Ashen Barrow | 灰冢 **The Ashen Barrow** | Royal burial mounds, cold moon, grave bells, ash | The court's old kings lie here; the Breach runs under it and wakes the dead | Bellwright (beetle) **[PICK 4]** |

**Why the order works:** the forest is the edge, the bay is where the land meets the sea, the
Barrow is the court's graveyard on the way up, the Heart is the root of the crack, and after the

## 3. Chapters (one per map, unlocked by reaching it)

Each chapter has the same beats so it is easy to write all ten languages: **arrival** (prologue
card), **what went wrong here**, **the warden**, **the hero who joins**, **the verse freed**.

### Prologue · The Hearth Song 炉火之歌
Wick (owl) tells it at camp: the gods' song, their silence, the night the court rang the bell and
the sky cracked, the violet light. "Your bell still rings true. Take your friends. Bring the verses home."

### Chapter I · The Hollow Wood 苍林 · verse **Root 根**
- **Arrival:** the first night the violet light came, the owls stopped calling.
- **Wrong here:** the Hollow Stag, old king of the wood, looked up at the crack and could not look away.
- **Warden:** The Hollow Stag (or the Thornmother, or the Gloam Wolf once both are felled).
- **Heroes:** the four friends of the hearth already travel together (see §5).
- **Verse:** Root. The Stag's pale spirit returns to camp.

### Chapter II · The Drowned Bay 沉湾 · verse **Water 水**
- **Arrival:** the tide is stuck at high water; the tidal mill turns backwards.
- **Wrong here:** the bay's heron drowned looking at the violet reflection; the mill wheel turns on its own.
- **Warden:** The Drowned Heron (or the Millwheel, or the Mire King).
- **Hero:** **Tide Caller** (otter), of the otter fishers who call the tides. She has been holding
  the last dry ring of the bay alone and joins once the tide turns again **[PICK 6]**.
- **Verse:** Water. The tide goes out for the first time since the crack.

### Chapter III · The Ashen Barrow 灰冢 · verse **Stone 石**
- **Arrival:** the grave bells ring with no one pulling the ropes.
- **Wrong here:** the Breach runs under the court's royal graves; the dead kings' wyrm and their
  knights rise. The court's grave-bell keepers went mad one by one.
- **Warden:** The Barrow Wyrm (or the Lich Ram, or the Ash Raven).
- **Hero:** **Bellwright** (beetle), the last sane grave-bell keeper. Her teacher is the Gravebell
  Ringer (a champion here), now ringing the hollow note. She knows bells, so she is the first to
  say aloud that **the court's bell broke the sky**.
- **Verse:** Stone.

### Chapter IV · The Hollow Heart 虚心 · verse **Heart 心**
- **Arrival:** the floor of the Barrow falls away into caves that glow violet and beat like a heart.
- **Wrong here:** the root of the Breach. The gaze pooled down here and grew into crystal. The court's
  High Cantor, who sang the Calling, fell down the crack the night it opened and now sings the
  Hollow's song back up it.
- **Warden:** The Hollow Cantor (or the Geode Colossus, or the Vein Weaver).
- **Hero:** **Duskwing** (bat), from the cave colonies who lived in the dark under the Barrow. They saw
  the violet first; his colony went mad, he alone flew free.
- **Verse:** Heart. With the root cut, the party sees both ends of the crack: the altar above, and the
  Kiln where the bell was made. **The fork card: Spire or Kiln** (defaults to the Spire after 30 s).

### Chapter V-a · The Spire Court 王庭 · verse **Sky 天** · **Ending: Seal 封印**
- **Arrival:** the palace is spotless and silent. The court still sits at its table, turned to stone,
  facing the altar, waiting for the answer.
- **Wrong here:** where the god should have answered there is only a silence that eats sound: **the
  Hush**, the hole the Calling left.
- **Boss:** The Hush (or the Skyhorn, the court's royal ibex; or the Hourwarden, the court's clock that
  stopped at the hour of the ritual) **[PICK 7]**.
- **Ending Seal:** the party rings the true bell, all verses held, on the altar. The crack closes;
  the eye is shut out. The gods still do not speak, but the world sings its own song now. Dawn over
  the Spire.

### Chapter V-b · The Ember Kiln 熔炉 · verse **Fire 火** · **Ending: Recast 重铸**
- **Arrival:** the court's foundry, the fire that never went out. Moulds of the Summoning Bell line the walls.
- **Wrong here:** the Bellfounder cast the bell that broke the sky. The bell still hums the Calling
  note, and that hum keeps the crack open. The Hollow keeps the founder casting more.
- **Boss:** The Bellfounder (or the Cinderback, the kiln's fire salamander).
- **Ending Recast:** the party breaks the Summoning Bell and recasts it with the verses into a new bell.
  The crack closes from the bell's side. The new bell rings over the lands: a bell for the world, not
  for calling gods. The hearth burns at its brightest.

Both endings close the Breach and stop the corruption, so both stay true endings. Seal is the
"we close the door" ending; Recast is the "we fix what was broken" ending.

### Endless, Daily, Boss Rush (story frame)
- **Endless Descent 无尽深渊:** the crack is closed, but things already through it still wander. The
  party walks the old lands to clear them.
- **Daily Descent:** one patrol a day along the old Breach.
- **Boss Rush:** the court's records of every warden; Quill's chronicle replayed.

## 3b. Why the party moves on 前进的理由

**The spine (one rule for the whole road):** the true bell can only close the crack when it rings the
**whole song**, and the Hollow's gaze has **swallowed one verse into each land's warden**. So the
party cannot go straight to the altar: a bell with one verse would crack like the court's did. They
must free every warden on the way. Each verse freed also makes the bell **hum toward the next
broken verse**, like a compass 罗盘 (a beat on each level-clear card).

On top of the spine, each step has a concrete push (danger behind), a pull (a clue ahead), and a
question (what did the court do?). Quill reveals one more piece of the truth after each map.

| Step | Push 推 (why they can't stay) | Pull 拉 (why there) | Truth revealed (Quill / NPC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Camp → Forest | The violet light reaches the hearth; the Stag's beasts attack the village. Wick: "The fire will not hold them long." | The Stag holds the first verse. Wick gives them the true bell. | Prologue: the gods sang, then went silent. |
| Forest → Bay | The forest river now runs **backwards**, carrying violet water up from the sea; the woods will rot from the roots. | With Root freed, the bell hums **downstream**. The second verse lies where the water is wrong. | Quill: "The night the sky cracked, I saw a light at the top of the Spire. A bell, ringing." |
| Bay → Barrow | Tide Caller: the drowned dead walking out of the sea came **from inland**, down the old royal causeway. | When the tide finally goes out, it uncovers the court's sunken causeway 王道 leading to the Barrow, and across the water the Barrow's grave bells ring **the same note as the true bell**. | A court pilgrim ship in the mud carries a ritual hymn sheet: "The Calling, for the god's return." First proof the 王庭 did this. |
| Barrow → Heart | Felling the warden cracks the Barrow floor open; violet light pours **up** from below. | Bellwright: "The bells here were cast from the same bronze as the court's great bell. They are all ringing toward the deep." The crack has a root, and the gaze is pooling there. Seal the top first and the root keeps it open. | Bellwright reads the grave-bells: the court cast a Summoning Bell, and it broke the sky. |
| Heart → fork | The Heart's pulse is waking the whole land below; it must be cut now. Cutting it shows both ends of the crack in the sky. | The fallen Cantor's last words: "I sang it at the altar… and the bell still sings in the Kiln." Two ways to close it: at the altar where it was rung, or at the bell that rang it. | The Cantor was the court's High Cantor; he fell through the crack the night he sang the Calling. |
| Spire / Kiln | The crack is widest now; the eye is almost through. | Last verse: Sky at the altar, or Fire at the bell. | Spire: the court sits turned to stone, still waiting for the answer. Kiln: the founder never stopped casting. |

**How the player sees it (after the freeze, text only):** one line on each level-clear card
("The bell hums toward the sea."), one Quill line at camp per chapter, and the clue as the
next chapter's arrival card. No new rules.

## 4. The Old God 旧神

- **What it is:** a being from outside the Veil. No body we ever see, only **an eye** and a **violet
  light** through the crack. It does not hate the world; it has noticed it. That is enough.
- **How corruption works:** its gaze sings a hollow copy of the gods' song. Creatures that hear it
  repeat it (echo, hence *Echoes*) and lose themselves. Violet = touched by the gaze. Stronger
  near the crack: the Heart and the Spire are the worst.
- **The Hollow Voice** (already in the game, a violet line under each boss's title) becomes the
  Old God speaking through each warden.
- **Seen in game:** the crack in the sky over every map (a far violet line in the skybox, wider each
  level) **[PICK 8, a visual: after freeze]**.

## 10. 伏笔 Foreshadowing for future DLC (added 21 Nov)

**The one idea that ties all five together:** the Summoning Bell's note did not travel one way. It
had **five echoes 五道回声**, and each one cracked something else somewhere else. The main story
closes only the first crack. That fits the game's name (*Echoes*) and lets every DLC start from one
line on the ending card: *"The bell rang once. The world heard it five more times."*

Rules for every hook: a hint, never an answer; text, a prop or a far-off view only; no new rules.
Each DLC gets **three hooks**: something you **see**, something someone **says**, something you **find**.

| DLC | See 看见 | Say 听说 | Find 发现 | Question left open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Sea 深海** | In the Bay, a dark lighthouse far out past the reef; the tide is pulled *toward it*, not stuck. | Tide Caller: "The crack didn't stop the tide. Something out past the reef is holding it." Her elders sailed out the night of the ritual and never came back. | A pilgrim ship's log in the mud: "We sailed from the *outer* sea, not the court." | Who sails the outer sea, and why do they know the Calling? |
| **地心世界 The World Below** | In the Heart, the crystal veins keep going **past** the crack's root, down into warm light. A sealed stone door under the Heart breathes warm air. | Duskwing: "My colony came up from deeper. The old bats said there's a sun under the world." Stonehide: "The roots don't end. They go down to something." | The Geode Colossus's core holds a crystal with a tiny sky inside it. | What sun shines under the world? |
| **天空岛 Sky Islands** | From the Spire's top, floating islands drift above the clouds, with broken bridges hanging from them. | The Gale Herald (champion) was "the court's herald to the islands". ## 11. The mountain map 高山 (main story, new land)

### Where it goes in the road **[PICK 12]**
A run stays 40 rooms (settled), so a new land is a **road choice**, like the Spire and the Kiln:
- (a) **Level III fork: Barrow or Highlands** (my pick). After the Bay, the party picks the low road
  (the Barrow's graves) or the high road (the mountain pass). Both lead down into the Heart: the
  mountain's deep caves open into it. Also gives a reason: the court's pilgrims took the mountain road up to the Spire.
- (b) **Level II fork: Bay or Highlands**, right after the forest.
- (c) A whole new Level, making the run longer (would change the settled 40-room run).

### The land: 雷岭 **The Stormcrag** (name pick: 雷岭 Stormcrag, 雪脊 Snowridge, 云岭 Cloudhigh)
- **Style:** snow passes, prayer-flag bridges, a cliff monastery of the court's pilgrims, frozen
  waterfalls, a storm that never ends. Colours: slate, snow white, flag reds and yellows (not Ember).
- **What went wrong:** the Calling's note hit the peaks first and the storm froze in place: thunder
  rolls every few seconds and never moves on. The pilgrims' monastery went silent.
- **Verse:** **Storm 雷** (the game's 7th verse: Root, Water, Stone, Heart, Sky, Fire are taken, so this fills the last unwritten one).
- **Reason to go there (§3b style):** push: avalanches block the low road and the Bay floods the
  lowland; pull: the bell hums toward the thunder; truth: the monastery's prayer wheels record the
  night of the ritual ("the court's bell rang, and the sky answered with a crack, not a voice").
- **DLC hook:** from the highest pass you can see the sky islands for the first time.

## 12. The bard 吟游诗人 (hydra and medusa bloodline 九头蛇 · 美杜莎血脉)

A wandering singer who fits every map, so no new land is needed.

### Who she is (draft) **[PICK 14: name]**
Names: (a) **Coilsong 盘歌** (my pick), (b) **Manyvoice 千声**, (c) **Threnody 挽歌**.
- **Body:** a serpent with **three heads** (hydra blood) **[PICK 15: 3 or 5 heads]**, each head
  singing one voice (low, middle, high), so her songs are real chords. Snake-hair crests and gold
  eyes on each head (medusa blood). Carries a lyre on her tail.
- **Medusa blood:** her gaze can turn things to stone. **The Spire's court was found turned to
  stone, and people blame her bloodline.** She travels to find the truth and clear her family's name.
  (The truth: the court froze waiting for an answer, not from her gaze. She learns it at the Spire.)
- **Hydra blood:** when a head is hurt, it grows back. A kit idea: heads as a resource (lose one, it regrows stronger).
- **Why she fits every map:** she is a song-collector who has walked every land, the sea, and (she says) the sky islands. She knows the **whole** true song by heart, so she can tell which verse each warden holds.

### Her job in the story
1. **The narrator.** She is the one singing this tale; each chapter's arrival card is a verse of her song. (The game is called *Echoes*; she is the echo.)
2. **The DLC voice.** Most of the §10 hooks can come from her songs: she has been to the sea, seen the islands, heard of the dead land.
3. **Meets the party anywhere:** a wandering-bard event room that can appear on any map.

### How she unlocks **[PICK 16]** (a rule change: waits for the freeze lift)
- (a) Meet her in the bard event room 3 times, on any maps (my pick: fits "travelling around the world").
- (b) Clear any map with her met at least once.
- (c) A deed: hear all her songs.

### Kit direction (for the design thread, not now)
Bellwright already owns buffs and debuffs with bells in place. Keep the bard different:
**moving songs** (she plays while she walks), **petrify** (medusa: slow → stone → shatter),
**head chords** (two or three heads singing together makes a stronger song). Her style paths could be
**Hydra 九头** (regrowth, many heads), **Medusa 石瞳** (gaze, stone), **Ballad 吟游** (party songs).
