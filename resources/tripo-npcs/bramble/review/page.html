The moves, and where their timing comes from

MoveWhat it doesTiming
IdleThe game's idle: a slow breath the shell swells with; the neck slowly reaching out and drawing back in, and now and then a peek further out, the head turning to one side and tipping its chin up; blinks; the lantern and the herb bunches swaying. The game rolls its peeks and blinks; the page fixes four peeks (two to each side) and eight blinks in one loop.Game: breath 1 rad a second; the neck 0.8 rad a second; a peek every 5 to 9 s, eased over 0.5 s and held 2.8 s, 0.3 rad to the side; a blink every 3 to 6 s, 0.2 s; the lantern swaying 0.16 rad. The loop is 31 s so every wave closes; the lantern's sway is rounded to fit.
TalkThe game's talking: the jaw works and the head nods, eased in and out, the breath and the neck carrying on under it.Game: eased at 8 a second; the jaw 0.4 rad on an 11 rad/s beat; the nod on 6 rad/s. One line is 6.3 s (mine).

Open choices

  • The blink squashes the eye. The game hides a painted face for a blink; yours has painted eyes, so a blink squashes each eye shut.
  • The game tips her chin up at rest to show her face to the camera. Yours holds your pose. Say if you want the tilt.
  • The jars and pots stay still. The game swings its hanging pots; on yours the wares sit on shelves, so only the herb bunches and the lantern swing.
  • Mine. The length of one line of talk, and where the peeks and blinks fall in the loop.

What is rough

  • The neck slides out of the shell by stretching the skin between the head and the shell; the scarf stretches with it.
  • The jaw opens only a little, as the mouth is one surface with the face.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
