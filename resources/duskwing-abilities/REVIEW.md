### Pass 2 (page v6): 7.5 / 10
Built, on every card:
- Hit-stop on heavy hits, sure crits, kills and each card's top hit (the scene holds a beat, then catches up, so no card got longer); an impact burst (blood red spokes, Dusk Rose rim); a small camera punch; the foe squashes and flashes.
- Blood and dust on those hits; dust where he lands, where a foe is knocked back and where a launched foe hits the floor.
- A dark shade of him stays a moment where he blinks out; faint after-images trail his fast moves.
- A foe's back patch glows when he stands behind it, so every card shows where a backstab lands.
- A warm pulse runs through him when a heal lands.
- Claw blows answer through the body: wing tip whips past, ears flop, head snaps, small overshoot.
Built, on some cards:
- Phantom: a "?" over foes near him while he is Unseen, turning to "!" when he strikes from it. The player's pointer is drawn on the "nearest the pointer" cards (Phantom Wing Wheel, Bloodbite, Dusk Dive).
- Reworked: Phantom Wing Wheel (bats fly home to the spot behind the foe and spiral into him), Bloodbite (on its shoulders, blood spray, lick, drops off), Dusk Dive (the blow rolls out as a red wave that tears the others; he springs back off it).
- Blood Lance: thicker lance, a ring where it pierces each foe. Night Wings: a lash from a wing tip to each foe it hits.
Holding it back:
- Many cards still use the same three claw blows (now richer, but the same shapes).
- Several cards end with about a second of him standing.
- Seen only as software-GL stills in the cloud; motion and feel need his laptop.

### Pass 3 (page v7): 8 / 10
Built:
- Two new claw blows, a rising claw and a backhand. In any card with three or more one-sided claws, every third is a rising claw and every fourth a backhand, so no flurry repeats one blow.
- Blood lands on the floor past a foe after a heavy hit and stays a moment.
- Blood Moon: the moon has a face now (darker seas, three craters, a bright edge, a thin halo); a beam of red light falls on each foe a breath before it is hit; the floor under them reddens. All moon cards share this moon.
- Death Mark: he turns his back on the foe and walks off; cracks spread from the mark, the room dims with only his eyes lit, and it tears open behind him.
- Thicker lances on the combo Through the Cloud and on Rampage's three lances (still thinner than the base lance, as "thin" says).
Holding it back:
- The Rampage and Sanguine forms that only change a number look the same as their base card (that is the kit, not the page).
- Some cards end on a second of standing.
- Seen only as software-GL stills.
