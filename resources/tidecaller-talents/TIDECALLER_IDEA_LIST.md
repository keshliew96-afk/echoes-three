# Tide Caller: ideas from other games

**2026-11-19.** Design only. "Taken" ideas are in `TIDECALLER_KIT.md` at the line named. To
change one, answer with its number: "drop 7", "take 41".

**Kit 2.0, 21 Nov.** Jet is gone and Surge is Tsunami. No new web search was made for 2.0:
the Flood forms come from her own rules (her water, Call the Tide, Home Waters), not from
other games. Rows below still say "Jet" or "Surge" as they did when the idea was taken:
- A row that names a **Jet** form: that form is gone with the style. Two live on: the Hanging
  rain (now Flood's Rain Squall) and the Bubble flail (now Bubble's Breaker).
- A row that names a **Surge** form: read "Tsunami". Gone since: the whale (row 10, 17, 54:
  the Fall slam stands there), Overhead, the Sea fall, the Cradle (row 63), the Flail, the
  Swap; and row 23's Guard bubbles (the Bubble flail stands there).
- Row 39 (a stack for each cast) was left for Windbriar's Tailwind. Swell (T2) counts casts
  too; it pays in size on the fourth, not in a stacking buff.

How it was checked: web search only (20 searches). **checked** means a search result states it;
**memory** means I know the skill but the search did not confirm the wording.

## Ideas I left out, as spares

- **Ice.** Freeze soaked foes and shatter them. A fourth style if Kesh wants one; today a Frozen
  elite cannot be soaked, so it needs its own rules.
- **Flood 泛滥 style** (pick 1b): every skill leaves water; she dives from pool to pool.
- **A tide gauge**: high tide and low tide take turns every few seconds and change her skills.
- **A water mirror** that copies her casts from a second place (Duskwing's Roost shade does this).
- **A conch she sets down** that sings and soaks (Mosslight and Bellwright plant things already).
