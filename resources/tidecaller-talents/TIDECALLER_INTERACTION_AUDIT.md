# Tide Caller: interaction audit

**2026-11-21, on kit 2.0.** Design only. What happens when her rules meet each other, the other
heroes, bosses and the damage formula v1.2. Kesh closed all four picks on 19 Nov; the rest are closed here.
Rows G69 on are new in 2.0 (Flood, Tsunami); rows that were only about Jet are gone. My own read only.

## The four picks (closed)

- **A1. Soaked on a boss. Closed by Kesh, 19 Nov:** a boss carries the status for its full
  time and nothing else (no slow); an elite or champion is soaked at half value (kit R5).
- **A2. Holding a foe again and again.** Water Prison and her style's own holds (Flood: the
  Sink, the Pond clap's carry; Tsunami: the Abyss, the Tow, the Pick-up; Bubble: the Bubble wave,
  the Bubble wake, the Bubble flail) could pass one foe from
  hold to hold.
  **Picked a:** the cap of R10: no hold over 2 s (an elite 1 s), then 2 s free. Launches and
  Windbriar's kicks stay free, as he picked for her.
- **A3. Tsunami (then Surge) moves the pack away from her friends.** A melee friend's foes are suddenly 5 u away.
  **Picked a:** nothing: moving the pack is her job, and a taunted foe walks back to the tank
  (Windbriar's rule).
- **A4. Reflection on a friend's big skill** (River's Gift, Bubble form).
  **Picked b:** big skills too. The second cast is 40% of the first.

## How she meets the other heroes

- Windbriar: her arrows deal 30% more to foes Rill lifts or launches, and her Spike works on
  them. Windbriar's kicks can Slam foes into the Pinball dome's skin.
- Stonehide: his slab and Stone Ring are walls for her Slam. Bubbles can go on him; he has no
  shield of his own. The Decoy never takes his taunt (G36). He is the best friend for a Spill: the pack that hits him stands in its puddles.
- Mosslight: no shields of her own, so bubbles fill that gap. Rill's water can water her plants
  (combo C12); a Flood room waters all of them, once each, by Mosslight's rule.
- Ashfang: he fights in the pack she moves (A3). A pack spat out by the Abyss, swept in by
  the Round-up or heaped by the Whip wave is one place for his blade.
- Duskwing: Air bite works on foes she lifts; a foe afloat is a backstab from any side (G10).

## To measure first when it is built

- Her damage on a lone boss in each style, now that Jet is gone (notes N81, N114).
- Overflow with Deep Water on a pack in one piece; The Sea with Overflow for its 6 s.
- How much of a room is her water after 10 s under Flood, and whether the 12-part cap is felt.
- How often a full Swell lands on a skill worth doubling; Three Crests under a full Swell.
- The slide with Wide Water (90% farther); drenched foes under three Crash skills.
- Slam damage with Heavy Swell's double in a room with many pillars.
- How much of a fight a small foe spends held (A2).
- T3's 20% on afloat foes for a team of four; the Bubble flail's three rings in a pack.
- Reflection on each hero's big skill (his pick: allowed).
- Store on an elite with four heroes hitting it.
- Call the Tide every 6 s (Tide on tide) in a room full of her water, and how far its rim creeps in a long fight.
