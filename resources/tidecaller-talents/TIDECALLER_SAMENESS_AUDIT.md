# Tide Caller: sameness audit

**2026-11-21, on kit 2.2** (kit 2.0 after three critic rounds; sections 1b and 1c). Kesh's rule from Windbriar (18 Nov, 23:53 and 23:54 Singapore
time): every skill and every style form needs its own 特点; no idea may be used twice in one
style.

**What was checked:** the 24 pressed base skills, the 2 passives, all 72 style forms, the
passive's three shapes, the basic attack and dash forms, the 15 talents and the 12 combos.

**The rule used:** each form is boiled down to one core idea. A repeat is an idea used by two
forms of one style, or a form that does what a talent, a combo, the basic attack or the dash of
that style already does, or a form that only changes a number. The same idea in two styles, or
on two heroes, is allowed.

**How it was checked:** kits 1.0 and 1.1 were read by two fresh judges (their lists were
fixed then). **Kit 2.0's Flood column, its three new Tsunami forms and its ten new talents
have only my own read.** My own reads have missed repeats before, so this file does not claim
"0 left": section 5 lists the families that are close, and names two forms that only change a
number. His eye on the page is the check.

What Flood's 24 forms do now: lay water 8 (Pond-maker, Canal, Paint, Open the gate, Plunge
pool, Bow wave, Spill, his Hanging rain); reach through her water 5 (Dunk, Spreading ring,

## 5. Families that are still close

These share a goal, not a mechanic. They are the first ones to look at on the page.

- **Flood, a line of water:** F2 Canal (straight, and it runs: a current), F6 Paint
  (freehand, held) and F14 Bow wave (her own ride, foes drawn onto it). In 2.1 the dash no
  longer draws a fourth line.
- **Flood, a round pool is laid:** F1 Pond-maker (a shot), F13 Plunge pool (it grows under
  the fall), F18 Spill (where the bubble pops), F19 the rain's pool, F8 Open the gate (the
  big sheet). Laying water is the path, as giving a bubble is Bubble's; each lays it another
  way. In 2.1 the Spreading ring, the Paint's burst and the Lagoon left this family.
- **Flood, a hit reaches through her water:** F3 Dunk (a thrown foe's splash, 3 u), F9
  Spreading ring (a ring from her feet through the whole piece), F10 Roll-up (a wave that
  keeps rolling over it), F20 Blowout (a launch, 3 u), F4 Pond-hopper (a stone from foe to
  foe), beside the base Call the Tide and T1 Overflow's half. Each has its own picture: a
  splash, a ring, a wave, a sheet going up, a zigzag.
- **Flood, her water moves the foes:** F2 Canal (a current to its far end), F9 Spreading ring (carried out to the edge), F15 Pond clap (it folds in and claps), F5 Sweep-in
  (washed toward water), F16 Drain (dragged to her, and the water is spent). The Canal
  and the Spreading ring are the nearest pair: one is a straight line that sweeps to its end

- **Flood, a friend and her water:** F18 Spill (the bubble spills on whoever hits it) and

  Vice is one crush now, not a hold.

## 6. Base pool

- **B1. Undertow and Whirlpool** are both a pool that drags today. Undertow is dropped (his
  mark, 19 Nov). Call the Tide is the one skill that answers from all her water; the nearest
  to it are Geyser (one spot erupts) and C10 (a charge on her water).
- **B2. Whirlpool and Maelstrom** both pull (today's skills). Whirlpool is the lasting pool far
  away; Maelstrom is one pull round her and a burst. Clapping Waves does not gather: it is a
  far clap that stuns.
- **B3. Breaker and Crashing Wave** are both a Crash with a knock back (today's skills): the
  ring round her, and the front. If they read alike on the page, Breaker could launch instead.
- **B4. Spray** was a second Crashing Wave (a fan that pushes). It is now the cheap wide soak:
  no push, no Crash.
- **B5. Three skills move her:** Ripple Step (three short steps, spent one at a time), Otter Dive (a long swim
  under the floor), Wave Rider (a surf she steers). Wave Rider no longer carries foes: the
  tsunami and the dash do that.
- **B6. Lines from the staff:** Riverbolt (one bolt), Torrent (a jet through the line),
  Floodgate (a 2 s beam) and her stream. They differ by weight and time.
- **B7. Sky Fall and Tidal Dome.** Only the fall stops shots; the dome is the room where
  friends take less.
- **B8. Rain Squall and Whirlpool** (today's skills) are both a lasting circle that soaks and
  slows; the pool drags, the rain is wider and longer.
- **B9. Home Waters** was two numbers. In 1.1 it is the belly slide on her own water, and soaked
  foes leave wet footprints that are her water.

## 7. Talents, combos, basic attack and dash against forms

- T1 Overflow (half through the piece): the forms that reach through her water hit at full
  where they reach, and Overflow carries half to the rest of the piece (notes N24). No form
  copies the half.
- T4 Deep Water: no form only adds damage in her water.
- T7 Meeting Waters: the Canal, Paint, the Bow wave, Tide on tide's rim and the Lagoon's
  canals join pieces and so fire it. No form surges on a joining.
- T10 Floodplain: no form only widens water. Tide on tide's rim grows a piece 1 u a cast; Floodplain widens all of it.
- T13 The Sea: Open the gate is large water; The Sea is the room.
- T2 Swell: no form counts casts. T5 Heavy Swell: no form only adds damage for a move or a
  Slam. T8 Backwash: no form drags a thrown foe back.
- T11 Wide Water: no form only widens a wave or lengthens a push.
- T14 Great Wave crosses the room; in 2.1 Three Crests' third crest stops at 5 u, so only a
  full Swell and the Great Wave cross it.
- T9 Chain Pop: the Bubble wake also lifts foes, by her ride and not by a Crash or a pop.
- T15 Lifebubble: no form saves a falling friend.
- Flood basic attack (the stream pools) and F6 Paint both draw water by pointing: Paint is
  wider and bursts.
- Flood dash (a wide wet path) and F14 Bow wave both lay a road by moving: the nearest pair
  in the style.
- Tsunami dash (the tide rolls on and pushes) and F1 Wave head: both a plain push along a
  line from her.
- Bubble tsunami and dash (bubbles for friends and for herself): section 5.
- C1 (the orb parked in the pool), C3 and C8 (the prison shot up, or whipped away): no form
  does the same. C10 (a charge on her water launches) sits beside the Sleeper: under Flood
  the Sleeper's foes are launched too.
