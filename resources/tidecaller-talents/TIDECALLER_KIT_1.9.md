## 1. In one look

- **R1.** 26 skills: 8 Stream 流, 8 Wave 浪, 8 Spring 泉, 2 passives. She holds 4.
- **R2.** Three paths, one talent pick per tier from any: **Jet 水刀** (the stream never stops:
  one foe, bosses), **Surge 狂澜** (waves carry the pack and slam it), **Bubble 泡沫** (bubbles
  guard friends, lift foes, pop).
- **R3.** Her tier I talent sets her style and gives every skill that style's form (section 4).
- **R4.** 85 health, ranged. No heal. Her only shields are bubbles.

## 2. Passive, basic attack, dash, rules

- **R5. Ebb and Flow 潮起潮落. Soaked 浸湿**, 4 s. A small foe is 15% slower and slides 滑: her
  pushes and throws move it 50% farther. An elite or champion: half of both. A boss: the
  status only. Every skill uses it; the tag opens its cell:
  - **【浸】** soaks.
  - **【拍】** Crash 拍击: 60% more to a soaked foe, and spends the soak.
  - **【湿】** does more to a soaked foe, and the soak stays.
  - Jet: Pressure builds twice as fast on a soaked foe; a Crash gives 1 Pressure. Surge: a
    Crash's spent water soaks the foes within 1.5 u. Bubble: a Crash leaves an 8 bubble on the
    nearest friend.
- **R6. Basic attack** (as passed). Hold: a water stream, 4.5 u: 3 each 0.2 s. After 5 s, a
  tsunami: 【拍】28, 6 u, knocked back, then 【浸】. Jet: no tsunami; the stream builds Pressure
  水压 and at 5 is the water blade 水刀 for 3 s: through every foe, double. Surge: the tsunami
  comes after 3 s and carries small foes to its end. Bubble: 2 s of stream floats a small foe;
  the tsunami leaves a 10 bubble on friends.
- **R7. Dash** (as passed). A tide ride; its wet path slows and soaks. Jet: the stream does not
  break. Surge: the tide rolls on 3 u and pushes. Bubble: she steps off in a 10 bubble.
- **R8. Two paws.** With attack held she casts with the left paw and the stream goes on. Big
  skills and moves that carry her pause it (Jet's Full circle is the stream itself and does
  not).
- **R9. Slam 拍岸.** A foe she throws into a wall or pillar: 10, stun 0.5 s. Into another foe:
  6 to both. A foe is slammed once a second.
- **R10. Held 浮空.** A foe that is afloat, carried or swallowed cannot act; everyone can hit
  it. No hold lasts over 2 s (an elite 1 s); then that foe cannot be held for 2 s.
- **R11. Bubbles 水泡** are shields on friends. A soaked foe's hits take only half from a
  bubble. A bubble that breaks or ends pops: 【浸】 foes within 1 u.
- **R12. Her water 水域:** the wet path, puddles, pools, rain and the dome.
- **R13. Big foes.** A boss is never moved or held: that hit deals 20% more instead. A champion
  is pushed half as far and never held: 10% more instead.
- **R14. On a pad** the pointer is the aim stick's reticle.
