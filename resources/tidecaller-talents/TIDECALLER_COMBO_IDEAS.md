# Tide Caller: ways her skills play together

**2026-11-21, on kit 2.0.** Design only. Twelve combos, C1 to C12, all picked by Kesh
(19 Nov; C6 changed with Sky Fall on 20 Nov). Each is one small rule between two of her
skills, or a skill and her dash or her water; none needs a talent. The rules are unchanged in
2.0; only the table "Under each style" is new.

| # | Name | The rule | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Park the orb 珠落漩涡** | A Tide Orb let go over her Whirlpool does not burst: it circles in the pool, still ticking, and bursts when the pool ends | Whirlpool, then Tide Orb |

## Notes

- No combo is dead under any style (three were under Jet). Under Flood, C3 and C8 need the
  base Water Prison, which Flood keeps on dry floor.
- Flood gets the most from C2, C4 and C10: all three read water on the floor.
- C1 gives the Tide Orb a second way to end: parked. Her paw is free again while it circles.
- C2 and C10 make water on the floor worth stacking: rain over a whirlpool, then a charge.
- C3 and C8 give the Water Prison two follow-ups: up (onto the pack) or away (for a Slam).
  Neither makes the hold longer (R10).
- C4 pays her for keeping water on the floor: with the wet path a stone can skip eight times.
  The stone's own 湿 line (one more skip for each soaked foe) counts toward the same eight.
- C5 turns the dome into a ceiling for the Geyser.
- C6 is the only way the base fall moves.
- C7 needs the game to keep a pressed skill for the end of the dash, the same new machinery as
  Ashfang's Dash strike, Windbriar's Sky cast and Duskwing's Dash cast.
- C11 is her answer when a friend holds a boss: both skills on one friend. The bubble is not
  refilled (formula D33); the gift is what starts again, once for each bubble.
- C12 touches Mosslight's kit, so it also needs a line in Mosslight's design thread. Each plant
  and each Moss can still be watered only once (Mosslight's rule), so it is a help, not an engine.
- Sound: the two skills' own sounds and one bright chime when a combo fires. The AI seat uses
  C2, C4 and C10 when its water is already down, and never waits for a combo.
- New looks: the orb circling in the pool (C1), a tall whirlpool under rain (C2), a bubble
  shot up on a geyser (C3), a stone skipping eight times (C4), rain off the dome's roof (C5),
  a wave carrying the waterfall along (C6), a bolt riding the wet path (C9).
