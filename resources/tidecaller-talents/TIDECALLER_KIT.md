# Tide Caller: skills and talent paths

**Version 2.2, 2026-11-21. Design only** (the freeze holds). Kesh, 20 Nov: "as a mage and sea
and water element design character, i expecting tide caller is a massive aoe mage", and Bubble
"is good, can keep". His pick: **Flood 涨潮** and **Tsunami 海啸** stand beside Bubble; Jet and
Surge are gone (section 5). Kit 1.9 was the passed kit: `TIDECALLER_KIT_1.9.md`. Whole rules:
`TIDECALLER_NOTES.md` section 10. Numbers are guesses. Kit 2.2 is kit 2.0 after three rounds of a critic
loop (section 5).

## 1. In one look

- **R1.** 26 skills: 8 Stream 流, 8 Wave 浪, 8 Spring 泉, 2 passives. She holds 4.
- **R2.** Three paths, one talent pick per tier from any: **Flood 涨潮** (she fills the floor
  with her water, and her skills run through it), **Tsunami 海啸** (few casts, each one a wave
  that moves the pack; every fourth is double size), **Bubble 泡沫** (bubbles guard friends,
  lift foes, pop).
- **R3.** Her tier I talent sets her style and gives every skill that style's form (section 4).
- **R4.** 85 health, ranged. No heal. Her only shields are bubbles. No walls: water that stands
  tall is a waterfall from the sky.

## 2. Passive, basic attack, dash, rules

- **R5. Ebb and Flow 潮起潮落. Soaked 浸湿**, 4 s. A small foe is 15% slower and slides 滑: her
  pushes and throws move it 50% farther. An elite or champion: half of both. A boss: the
  status only. Every skill uses it; the tag opens its cell:
  - **【浸】** soaks.
  - **【拍】** Crash 拍击: 60% more to a soaked foe, and spends the soak.
  - **【湿】** does more to a soaked foe, and the soak stays.
  - Flood: a Crash leaves a puddle under each foe it crashes that stands on dry
    floor. Tsunami: a Crash's spent water soaks the other foes within 1.5 u. Bubble: a Crash leaves an 8 bubble on the nearest friend.
- **R6. Basic attack** (as passed). Hold: a water stream, 4.5 u: 3 each 0.2 s. After 5 s, a
  tsunami: 【拍】28, 6 u, knocked back, then 【浸】. Flood: where the stream stays 1 s it pools: one
  puddle that grows to 2 u (it follows a foe that moves less than 1 u); the tsunami leaves its
  whole path as water. Tsunami: the tsunami
  comes after 3 s, is twice as wide (2.6 u) and carries small foes to its end.
  Bubble: 2 s of stream floats a small foe; the tsunami leaves a 10 bubble on friends.
- **R7. Dash** (as passed). A tide ride; its wet path slows and soaks. Flood: where she steps
  off, the tide sinks into a pond, 2.6 u, 8 s. Tsunami: the tide rolls on 5 u, twice as wide, and pushes.
  Bubble: she steps off in a 10 bubble.
- **R8. Two paws.** With attack held she casts with the left paw and the stream goes on. Big
  skills and moves that carry her pause it.
- **R9. Slam 拍岸.** A foe she throws into a wall or pillar: 10, stun 0.5 s. Into another foe:
  6 to both. A foe is slammed once a second.
- **R10. Held 浮空.** A foe that is afloat, carried, sunk or swallowed cannot act; everyone can
  hit it. No hold lasts over 2 s (an elite 1 s); then that foe cannot be held for 2 s.
- **R11. Bubbles 水泡** are shields on friends. A soaked foe's hits take only half from a
  bubble. A bubble that breaks or ends pops: 【浸】 foes within 1 u.
- **R12. Her water 水域:** the wet path, puddles, ponds, pools, canals, rain and the dome's
  floor. It soaks the foes that stand in it. Water that touches is **one piece 一片**. Still
  water 静水 (what the Flood forms lay) soaks and does no damage.
- **R13. Big foes.** A boss is never moved or held: that hit deals 20% more instead. A champion
  is pushed half as far and never held: 10% more instead.
- **R14. On a pad** the pointer is the aim stick's reticle.

## 5. Picks

**Kit 2.0, Kesh, 20 and 21 Nov:**
- "for bubble style is good, can keep, but as a mage and sea and water element design
  character, i expecting tide caller is a massive aoe mage. you help think and list down the
  style and let me pick". Five styles were listed (Tsunami, Flood, Storm, Maelstrom, Tide).
- His pick: "1 and 3", then "wait, sorry i typo, i mean 1 and 2": **Tsunami and Flood**.
- "1a, 2a": his **Bubble flail 泡锤** (it was Jet's Breaker) moves to Bubble's Breaker in
  place of Guard bubbles; his **Hanging rain 悬雨** (it was Jet's Rain Squall) is Flood's Rain
  Squall, and the rain also leaves standing water.

**What kit 2.0 changed:**
- Jet 水刀 is gone with its 24 forms and five talents (Pressure, the water blade, Erosion,
  Fork, Long Stream, Sea Dragon). Flood stands in its column: 24 new forms, five new talents.
- Surge 狂澜 is now Tsunami. Its wave forms are kept as they stood on 20 Nov. Three forms that
  worked on one foe are new: Wave head (was Cradle), Whip wave (was Flail), Kick waves (was
  Swap). Three Crests now grows (2.2 u, 3.5 u, 5 u). Swell is the new style
  rule; Rolling Tide's two halves moved to Heavy Swell (Slam double) and Wide Water (40%
  farther).
- Bubble: only Breaker changed (the Bubble flail, pick 1a).
- The 24 base skills, the two passives and the soak rule are as passed.

**What kit 2.1 changed (the critic loop, 21 Nov; his ask: "run critic loop ... make sure it
fit the design element, and no repeating skills, fill in details insanely"):** two fresh critics
read kit 2.0, one for fit and one for repeats. Nothing he passed was changed.
- Flood forms no longer pay "for each separate piece", because the style joins her water into
  one. New rules for the Pond-hopper, Roll-up, Inflow (was the Drinker), Pond clap and Drain.
- Repeats taken out. The Sleeper is a torpedo, not a second Blowout. The Canal has a current.
  The Spreading ring runs through her whole water. Tide on tide spills the edge outward, not
  just bigger numbers. Water walk is free steps on water. The Porpoise is one great leap. Shared
  water makes a friend Crash. The Lagoon sends canals to all her water. The Spill (was the
  Paddling pool) no longer follows the friend. Paint's burst leaves no pool.
- Tsunami: the Wave head is the bolt and then a wide wave at its end; Kick waves go back the
  way she came; Three Crests stops at 5 u; the basic
  tsunami is back to the passed 3 s, twice as wide, not three times.
- Rules: Flood's dash leaves a pond where she steps off; the stream's puddle needs 1 s on one
  spot; a Crash's puddles are not laid on her water; Meeting Waters needs a cast of hers.

**What kit 2.2 changed (critic rounds 2 and 3, 21 Nov):** a fresh critic read kit 2.1 for fit
and repeats, and a detail critic read every motion. Nothing he passed was changed.
- Three rules were broken and are mended: Tide on tide's second step could never land (now
  within 9 s); the Pond-maker could keep water for ever (it lays a new pond each time; no
  water is ever topped up past the time it was laid with); the Blowout launched a soaked pack
  for 1.6 s (now 0.8 s for all).
- The Flood users had fallen into three sentences. Now each does its own thing: the Canal
  sweeps foes to its end; the Spreading ring carries them out of her water; the Stir (was the
  Inflow) runs the whirlpool's tick through the whole piece; the Lagoon's canals run to foes
  too; a Paint loop fills; Open the gate slaps back when it shuts; the Drain leaves one pond.
- Tsunami: Kick waves is one wave down her whole trail, sized by her steps (it repeated her
  dash). A full Swell pushes twice as far and no longer crosses the room: only the Great Wave
  does.
- Details: every Flood form and the four new Tsunami forms now carry wind-up, release and
  recovery times, how the water starts and ends, and a sound with an ending (notes N53a).
