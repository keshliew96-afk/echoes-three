The feetTripo set his boots toes-out, the left about 6° and the right about 12°. Every move turns them in so the toes point straight ahead, by your rule for humanoids. A planted boot keeps its heading when his hips turn.

The keyOne stiff piece on its own joint at the top of its ring. In the fist it hangs straight down whatever the arm does, and swings and settles after a drop or a blow. Out of the fist it flies where the move sends it.
The chained forearmThe chain wrapped round his left forearm is stiff with the forearm, so no link stretches. The wrist bends below it.

Open choices

  • Death. He falls on his back with the key held to his chest. Two other ways to play it: he sinks to his knees and the key drops from his hand, or he goes down through the floor as into water with the key hand last. Say which you prefer.
  • The whirl. The key circles beside him. Over his head like a lasso would read better from the game's camera, but the sleeve tears at the shoulder when the arm goes straight up. Say if you want it tried anyway.
  • Lock Turn. He stoops low to reach the floor, because his arms are short. Going down on one knee would look steadier, but the coat has no joints below the belt at the sides and would sink into the floor.
  • The walk. Boots straight ahead, short quick steps. Say if he counts as a heavy character and should waddle toes-out instead.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s roar. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the roar.
  • Size. Drawn 2.3 u tall.

What is rough

  • The chain between the fist and the flying key is drawn by this page, as plain links. The model has no spare chain: the chain on his forearm stays wrapped while the key is 5 u away.
  • While the key is away his left fist stays closed on nothing.
  • The padlock, the tags and the rings on his belt are one skin with the coat. They do not swing.
  • The coat has two joints, front and back. In the Lock Turn's stoop the back of the coat lifts in one stiff piece, and in the death it lies stiff along his legs.
  • The game's own Lockkeeper carries a lock-box lantern and wears the key on a chain over his shoulder. This model has no lantern and holds the key in his fist, so the moves are built for what he has.
  • The hero stand-in slides in a straight line when hooked, and the slow is only a Violet ring at its feet for 1.5 s.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a boot can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for his power. They are kept dim on purpose.
