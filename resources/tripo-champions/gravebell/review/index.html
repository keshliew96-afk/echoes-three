Open choices

  • How he tolls. He rings with his whole body: a bow for the Death Knell, a twist for the Grave Call, and the bell swings off the staff. He cannot swing the staff out in his arms, because the model has no arms inside the sleeves (see What is rough). If you want a big two-handed swing overhead, the model needs a new export with the arms and the staff held clear of the chest.
  • Death. He kneels, bows, and the bell tolls itself still. Two other ways to play it: he topples forward stiff as a post onto the bell, or he sinks into the floor as into a grave with the bell last. Say which you prefer.
  • The walk. Feet straight ahead, short slow steps, bowed under the staff. Say if you want him more hunched or shuffling.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s roar. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the roar.
  • Size. Drawn 2.5 u to the hood.

What is rough

  • Tripo welded his fists, the lower staff, the hair and the robe into one skin, and there are no arms inside the sleeves. So the fists stay where he carries them and the staff only rocks on his shoulder, under 30° either way. Where it rocks, the bandaged forearms and the cuffs stretch a little with it.
  • The staff came as two pieces that overlap at his shoulder. Where they meet, a small step can show when the staff tips.
  • The small bells and the rope on his belt are one skin with the robe. They do not swing.
  • The hair is one skin with the chest and does not swing.
  • The robe below the knee is two stiff halves. In the death they lie flat in front and behind, and his feet show behind him.
  • The game's own Gravebell Ringer is a hunched skeleton under a wooden yoke. This model is a pale old man with the bell on a staff over his shoulder, so the moves are built for what he has.
  • The ash patch on this page is only a grey disc. It shows the size and the 5 s, not the game's own look. The slow is only a Violet ring at the hero's feet for 2.5 s.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a foot can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for his power, grey for the ash. They are kept dim on purpose.
