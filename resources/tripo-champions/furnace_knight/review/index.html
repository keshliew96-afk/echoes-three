Idle or Walk sets what he does between attacks, and Going turns him on the spot. An Action plays over it and repeats until you press it again. Hurt plays on top of anything. Open choices

  • The grate. The game's grate is two barred doors that swing open for the blast. The model's grate is one panel, with its bars drawn into a few large faces, so here it only flares. A new export with the doors as separate pieces would let them open.
  • The sledge in the charge. He keeps it on his shoulder and charges helm-first. The game holds the sledge across his body. Across the chest, this short sledge's head sits in front of his visor, so I left it on the shoulder. Say if you want it across.
  • The sledge in the blast. It hangs from his right fist so the grate is bare. It could stay on his shoulder instead.
  • Death. He kneels and cools. Two other ways to play it: he topples forward onto his grate and the fire spills out, or he stays standing and cools on his feet, leaning on the sledge.
  • Rage. A new 1.75 s slam and roar. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the slam.
  • Size. Drawn 2.6 u to the top of the chimney.

What is rough

  • The upper arms are hidden under the shoulder plates, which are one skin with the chest. When an arm swings far out, the plate on that side stretches with it.
  • The chimney, the rings on the belt and the rivets are one skin with the body and do not swing.
  • The grate's bars are drawn into the texture on a few large faces, so they cannot open or glow one by one.
  • The glow of the cracks, the visor and the grate is the model's own texture, made brighter by this page. The cooling at death is drawn by this page too.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Kiln Gold for his heat and the slag, Violet only for the spawn's rune. They are kept dim on purpose.
