Open choices

  • Pole, or shield and maul. The game's Sluice Warden carries a sluice-gate shield and a millstone maul. Tripo gave him one long sluice pole with a paddle blade and built the gate into his chest. Both moves are built on the pole. Say if you want the shield and maul made for him instead.
  • Visor colour. His visor is cyan in the texture and flares in that colour here. The game's tell is an indigo visor. Say if it should be recoloured.
  • The wheel on his chest. His left fist cranks it in the Floodgate wind-up, and it glows Violet as the gate opens. The wheel itself is one piece with the chest and does not turn. I can cut it free and make it spin if you like the idea.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s roar. The game has no pause at half health, only the burning visor. The Enraged button shows the state without the roar.
  • Death. He kneels at his post and does not fall, like the knight. Say if he should topple instead.
  • Walk. Wide and heavy, with the pole as a third foot. Say if you would rather he carried the pole off the floor, or walked narrower.
  • Size. Drawn 2.25 u tall.

What is rough

  • The fist is fused to the pole, so the hand cannot slide along it. In the kneel his arm reaches straight out to the pole at shoulder height.
  • The left hand is modelled as a closed fist, so it presses on the wheel and does not grip it.
  • With the left arm thrown wide (the rage, the heavy hit) the cloth under the armpit stretches, and from below a hairline gap can show at the cut.
  • The moss and the barnacles bend with the armour like skin.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a foot can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for his power, a dull teal for the water. They are kept dim on purpose.
