Idle or Walk sets what he does between attacks, and Going turns him on the spot. An Action plays over it and repeats until you press it again. Hurt plays on top of anything. He blows three long breaths, his shoulders rising with each, and a bubble of molten glass swells on the gather. Then he flicks the pipe up and the bubble flies off in an arc. Open choices

  • How he blows. He holds the pipe slanting forward and down from his lips with both fists close to his mouth. It could point up at the sky instead, the way the bubble flies.
  • The bubble's flight. It leaves the pipe 0.45 s before the warning ends and lands on its last tick. It could instead grow and burst on the gather, with the patch dropping on the hero.
  • The Glass Gout. He holds the pipe level under his arm like a lance. It could be swung in a short arc instead, spraying the glass sideways across the lane.
  • Death. He kneels against the standing pipe and goes dark. Two other ways: he lets the pipe fall and the gather shatters on the floor, or he folds over and topples sideways.
  • Rage. A new 1.75 s strike and howl. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the strike.
  • Size. Drawn 2.4 u to the top of the pipe.

What is rough

  • His arms are short for his height, so a fist cannot slide far along the pipe; the moves are built round that.
  • The hair, the goggle straps, the tools and the bottles on his belt are one skin with the body and do not swing.
  • The glow of the gather, the goggle lens and the embers on his apron is the model's own texture, made brighter by this page. The dimming at death is drawn by this page too.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Kiln Gold for the molten glass, Violet only for the spawn's rune. They are kept dim on purpose.
