Open choices

  • One hand on the scythe. His right hand is fused to the staff and the left is free, so the sweep is swung with one arm, couched under it like a lance. A two-handed swing would need the left hand to hold the staff too, which the model's fixed grip makes hard. Say if you want me to try.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s roar. The game has no pause at half health, only the burning eyes. The Enraged button shows the state without the roar.
  • Death. He kneels and hangs from the standing scythe, and does not fall. Say if the scythe should drop, or if he should collapse into a heap of robe.
  • Bone spikes. The Grave Lance's spikes are plain bone-coloured cones made for this page. Say if you want a spike model from Tripo for the game.
  • Walk. A slow stalk with the scythe carried. Say if he should use it as a walking staff, striking the floor each step.
  • Size. Drawn 2.5 u tall to the hat.

What is rough

  • The right hand is fused to the staff, so it cannot slide along it. In the kneel his arm reaches up to the same spot on the staff.
  • The wide sleeves are one skin with the arms, so they spread like wings when an arm rises and do not hang or swing by themselves.
  • The legs are thin bone inside the robe. I saw no knee come through the cloth in the stills, but watch for it in the walk and the kneel.
  • The keys, the pouch and the tally tags are fixed to the belt and do not swing.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a foot can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for his power, bone white for the spikes. They are kept dim on purpose.
