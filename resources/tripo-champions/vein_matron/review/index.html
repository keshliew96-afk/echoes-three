Open choices

  • Death. She wilts standing, folded over her roots like a dead flower. Two other ways to play it: she sinks down into her roots until only the crown shows, or she topples sideways and the crown shatters on the floor. Say which you prefer.
  • The lash. Only the right vein lashes. It could be both hands at once, crossing, or left and right in turn each time she casts.
  • The glide. She slides smoothly. Say if you want her to bob more, or to lean further into it.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s roar. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the roar.
  • Size. Drawn 3.0 u to the tip of the crown.

What is rough

  • The roots on the floor are one skin with the gown and do not crawl. When she glides they slide on the floor.
  • The hair is one skin with her back and does not swing.
  • The veins are thin flat ribbons in the model. Seen edge on they almost vanish.
  • The long vein is about 1 u long, so the lash itself reaches only about 2 u. The crack down the rest of the lane is drawn by this page.
  • The crystal collar and crown are one skin with the shoulders and head. When she flings her arms up the shoulder crystals bend a little with them.
  • The drink is only violet motes running from the hero to her heart. The shards are plain violet crystal shapes made for this page.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet and magenta for her power. They are kept dim on purpose.
