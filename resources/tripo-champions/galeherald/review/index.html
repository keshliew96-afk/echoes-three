Idle or Walk sets what it does between attacks, and Going turns it on the spot. An Action plays over it and repeats until you press it again. Hurt plays on top of anything. Death leaves it lying where it fell: press it again and it spawns back in. The bar names the part of the move that is playing, in the game's own words. Enraged is its state under half health: it stays on over every move until you switch it off, and it walks faster. In the Stoop the floor moves under it, so it stays in the middle of the stage while it flies 4.5 u.

Open choices

  • Death. It topples forward like a statue pushed over and lies face down. The other way to play "back to stone" is that it freezes into its carved pose, goes grey and stays standing. Say which you prefer.
  • Walk. Heavy stone steps on the floor. It has wings, so it could instead move in a low glide with the wings half open and the feet just off the floor. Say if you want that tried.
  • The horn. It never goes to a mouth: the mask has none and the arm is stone. It is levelled at the hero like a cannon beside the head. Say if it should be played another way.
  • The weathervane. It spins on its rod in the Gale Call, the rage and the spawn, and swings in the idle. Say if it should stay fixed.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s call at the sky. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the call.
  • Size. Drawn 2.7 u tall to the vane.

What is rough

  • The right arm cannot lift. The horn turns in the fist, so in the Gale Call the fist stays at the shoulder while the horn swings round it.
  • The left arm moves only from the elbow, and the open hand is one piece.
  • The wings are stiff boards with one bend. Spread wide they show the plain back face of the feathers, and where a wing leaves the back there is a flat closed cut.
  • The robe is one skin over the legs. In the glide and the long step it stretches, and the folds of the stone lean with it.
  • In the Stoop the hero stand-in backs up so the Herald does not land inside it. In the game the hero would have to run.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a foot can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for its power, a pale grey for the wind. They are kept dim on purpose.
