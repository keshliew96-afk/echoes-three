Idle or Walk sets what it does between attacks, and Going turns it on the spot. An Action plays over it and repeats until you press it again. Hurt plays on top of anything. Death leaves it lying where it fell: press it again and it spawns back in. The bar names the part of the move that is playing, in the game's own words. Enraged is its state under half health: it stays on over every move until you switch it off, and it walks faster. Open choices

  • Death. It falls onto its side and the moss withers. The other way to play it, from its journal line, is that it folds its legs, lies down and goes back to sleep under its moss. Say which you prefer.
  • Antler Toss. The rack scoops from its right to its left and the forehooves hop. Say if it should be a straight lift, or stay on four hooves.
  • The leap. One high bound, 1.25 u at the top. Say if it should be flatter and faster looking.
  • Rage. A new 1.6 s bellow. The game has no pause at half health. The Enraged button shows the state without the bellow.
  • Size. Drawn 2.6 u tall to the antler tips.

What is rough

  • The moss coat, the mushrooms and the flowers are one skin with the body. Where the back bends they stretch with it, and the fringe over the thighs moves with the legs.
  • The hanging moss is one stiff bundle on each side, not separate strands. In the bellow it hangs into the moss on the back, and in the death it lies stiffly beside the antler.
  • The forelegs stand dead straight in the model, so the body rides a little low in the walk to give them room to step.
  • In the Hart's Leap the hero stand-in backs up so the Hart does not land inside it. In the game the hero would have to run.
  • The moss patch on this page is only a green disc. It shows the size and the 5 s, not the game's own look or the slow.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a hoof can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for its power, moss green for the patch and the leaves. They are kept dim on purpose.
