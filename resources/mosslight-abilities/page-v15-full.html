How foes read her

  • Every foe goes for the nearest standing hero. Healing makes no threat, so foes never turn on her for healing; only the Tank's taunts bend that rule.
  • Foes do not step out of her floor zones and do not dodge her bolts.
  • What answers her: the Famine curse (heals 60% weaker in its room), Withering (25% weaker for the rest of the run) and the Heart Censer, a foe healer that mends its kin 15% after a 0.8 s gather (a stun spills it).

How allies read her

  • Green is kept for heal output only: green numbers and rising motes on whoever she heals. Her damage uses the party's parchment-white core with an amber glow.
  • Heals pick the ally with the lowest share of health in reach, her included. F1 to F4 locks a heal target; the lock is hers alone and nobody else sees it (see choice 29).
  • As an AI seat she casts heals on the neediest member first and damage at the nearest foe, and keeps within 3.4 u of the party.

Kit rules (S1 to S7, S43, S44, S49)

S1

The pool.

16 skills: 5 quick heals, 5 lasting heals (one of them a passive), 6 damage skills. She holds at most 4, all from wave rewards, as today. Any skill works with any talent; the three groups only lean toward a path. 1.4: with the two candidates the page shows 18: 5 quick heals, 6 lasting heals, 7 damage skills.

S2

No shields.

No skill, node or talent of hers gives a shield. The Bulwark node is never offered for her skills. Kindred Shield is cut.

S3

Moss.

Moss is her heal over time on an ally. It ticks once a second. Each skill keeps its own Moss on each ally; casting the same skill on the same ally again restarts it and keeps what was still unpaid, up to one extra cast's worth. A crit is rolled once when Moss lands. Moss ends when its ally is downed. (This is the HoT rule of talent line T18. The Regrowth and Deep Roots talents make more Moss.)

S4

Bloom.

To bloom Moss is to pay out everything still unpaid on an ally at once. Heartbloom does it. It adds no healing; it turns slow healing into fast healing when a big hit lands.

S5

Weakened.

A foe that is slowed, stunned or exposed is weakened. Rootspear hits weakened foes harder. Rootsnare, Puffcap and Pollen Gust weaken; a boss cannot be slowed or stunned but can be exposed.

S6

Casting.

Every skill fires on the press, as today. Each uses one of her six passed cast moves: Point, Bloom, Plant, Sweep, Give, Channel. While the staff is planted she plays the same move with empty paws, and every skill still comes from her, not from the staff.

S7

Colours.

Heals and Moss are Bright Heal #5FE873. Her damage is the darker green Kesh picked for her bolts (head #8CC46E, trail #3E8240). Wispfall keeps Gem Flash's white-gold light. Nothing wears Ember or Violet. Glows stay dim.

S43

Seeds (new in 1.3, from Zyra).

A seed is a small glowing pod on the floor. It lies for up to 8 s.

  • A hurt ally who touches it is healed 11, and the seed is used up.
  • A seed that is at least 1 s old sprouts into a Spitter when a foe comes within 4 u of it.
  • A Spitter is a small plant that shoots the nearest foe within 4 u for 4, once a second, for 5 s. At most 4 Spitters stand at once; a fifth replaces the oldest.
  • Scatter Seeds (S20) throws seeds, Seed Bolt (S11) leaves one when it misses, and Rootspear (S21) sprouts every seed in its row at once.
S44

Her helpers.

Things she leaves working while she moves on: the staff turret, Mending Sapling, Spitters, Puffcaps, seeds, her wisps and the Firefly Swarm, and since 1.4 the Elder Oak and the Oakling. Foes cannot hit any of them. Each has its own cap, written on its skill. The wisp family is every small light that flies by itself: Glade Wisps (heal), the relic's wisp (stores overheal), Heartbloom's bloom wisps (heal) and Wispfall's wisps (damage).

S49

The garden (new in 1.4, R36 to R38).

Her plants are seeds, Spitters, Mending Sapling, Puffcaps and the Oakling. Three rules tie the skills together:

  • Water grows. Dew Wave and Dewfall water what they touch. A watered seed sprouts at once. A watered Spitter, sapling or Oakling lives 3 s longer and works 50% faster for 3 s. A watered Puffcap grows to double size and its cloud is 25% wider (2.75 u). Watered Moss lasts 2 s longer. Each plant and each Moss can be watered once.
  • Roots wake. Rootspear and Rootsnare sprout every seed in their area at once, and Rootspear bursts every Puffcap in its row.
  • Wind carries. If Pollen Gust crosses a waiting Puffcap or a spore cloud, the mushroom bursts and the wind carries its spores: every foe the gust pushes after that takes 20% more damage for 4 s.
  • Seeds now also come from Mossgrowth (S15) and Sapping Vine (S25).
  • Looks: bark and roots are wood brown, leaves her dark green, flowers small and pale. Bright Heal stays on the heal itself. Her plants must never look like the Thornling foes.

Her heals look like water and light.

Nothing guards one ally.

Wisps are well covered, so I add no wisp skill.

T34 · Combos checked

Clashes found and how the design settles them.

  1. Talents that needed their keystone. Version 1's Deep Roots, Bark, Patient Seasons, Overgrowth, Rich Sap, Rooted Staff, Deep Gem and Bounty did nothing without Regrowth or Reap. All are rewritten to work alone (T3). Patient Seasons is gone: Spring Rain now does that job when mixed with Regrowth, and Wide Canopy takes its place.
  2. Two cooldown talents. They add, they do not multiply (T9).
  3. Regrowth and the gem. A pour stays instant under Regrowth (T17). Regrowth only changes skill heals and the dash heal.
  4. Regrowth and Twin Buds. Two charges on one ally cannot stack past one extra cast's worth (T18), so the second charge is better spent on a second ally.
  5. Bark, Bounty and stronger statuses. The game refreshes a status to the larger size and the later end. Without a rule, Bark's 10% ward, refreshed every second, would keep Lantern Ward's 20% ward on forever, and Bounty would do the same to Rallying Cry's +20%. T9 forbids it. The build needs a separate record for talent statuses (section 10).
  6. Bark and her auras. An aura pulse lands every second, so allies inside an aura always have Bark. That is meant: it matches Quiet Hearth's ward in size and does not add to it.
  7. Perennial and her auras. An aura pulse lands every second, so allies inside an aura always regrow. That is meant, and it is bounded: 1% a second never stacks.
  8. Perennial and Regrowth. They add: a Regrowth HoT and Perennial's 1% run side by side. One vine ring shows each.
  9. Mercy Bell's wisp (her class relic). The wisp takes her overheal; no talent uses overheal any more, so nothing clashes with it. The wisp's heal is a heal: it refreshes Bark and Perennial.
  10. Second Flush and the Echo node. Both repeat the cast (two repeats at 50%). Neither repeat triggers the other.
  11. Bounty in a boss arena. A boss arena has one foe, so the 3 Sap per foe pays once. Her other Sap talents and the Inspired bonus still work.

Stacks that are strongest on paper.

  • Most healing per second: Quick Sprout, Deep Roots, Rooted Staff, any tier IV, Second Flush. That is ×1.25 × 1.2 × 1.1 = ×1.65, plus about 3 Sap a second from the staff. It beats all Spring (×1.57) by about a tenth and sits under the T8 ceiling of +70%. If play shows it is too strong, the lever is Second Flush (every 6th cast) or Deep Roots (15%).
  • Most protection: Regrowth, Deep Roots, Bark, Wide Canopy, Perennial, which is the all Evergreen build. No mix protects more.
  • Most damage help: all Harvest. No mix adds more Sap, because each tier has one Sap talent.

No pair of talents multiplies the same lever twice, because the two cooldown talents sit in tiers I and II and add together, and every other output talent sits alone in its tier.

T35 · AI rules

The AI seat and the autopilot follow one rule per talent that is on.

  • Quick Sprout, Spring Rain: no new rule; shorter cooldowns are used as they come.
  • Twin Buds: keep the second charge for an ally under 50% health.
  • Regrowth: cast a heal skill on an ally under 90% health who does not carry that skill's HoT; recast on an ally whose HoT has more than 2 s left only when that ally is under 40%.
  • Any Sap talent: with at least 10 Sap and an ally under 70% health, walk to stand within the pour's reach of that ally, inside her leash ring and off the beds she already avoids. Otherwise keep her usual distance.
  • Reap or Rich Sap: rank her damage skills higher in swap offers.
  • Rooted Staff: throw the staff (hit 3) toward the hurt ally when one is in throwing range.
  • Bark, Perennial, Deep Roots, Long Reach, Wide Canopy, Deep Gem, Second Flush, Bounty: no new rule.
