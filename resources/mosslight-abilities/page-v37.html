Roots wakeRootspear and Rootsnare sprout seeds. Rootspear bursts a Puffcap in its row.
Wind carriesPollen Gust bursts a Puffcap and carries its spores to the foes it pushes.
Light wakesWispfall's dives sprout seeds. Heartbloom opens them as healing flowers.
