## What the audit found

1. 26 skills shared six cast moves (Point, Bloom, Plant, Sweep, Give, Channel). Ten skills looked the same from her body alone.
2. Lure Lily, Snapjaw and Petal Twister had no move of their own and fell back to Point.
3. Wispfall is held for 15 s, but her channel pose played once (2.6 s) and she then stood idle.
4. Swiftleaf's kit line says "a hop and a turn"; she only held out a paw.
5. Plants appeared by growing at a steady rate, stood still, and shrank away at the end.
6. Nothing marked the frame her staff or paw met the floor.

## What changed

- Every skill has its own move. Each is a short wind-up (0.1 s), the blow on the frame the skill fires, then a follow-through that only that skill has.
- The moves are built with the same tools as her passed clips: eased keys, late chest, head and paws, ear and tail springs, feet kept on the floor, staff kept out of her body.
- The dash still cuts any of them off at once.
- Her passed basic attack (S8) and dash (S9) are untouched. The passed model file is untouched; the new moves load beside it.

## The effects

- Overnight (18 Nov): sparks spray and pop in, then shrink out; rings grow fast then slow and fade softly; heal trails ease out and swell as they land; the Leaf Gale wall springs up out of the floor and blows apart at the end.
- In 11 moves the staff now stays to her right or over her shoulder so the gem never crosses her face (front views).

- Every plant (seed, Spitter, Mending Sapling, Elder Oak, Oakling, Puffcap, Snapjaw, Lure Lily, thicket, rain cloud) now:
  1. is born with a springy overshoot (taller and thinner, then settles);
  2. breathes and sways while it stands (each one out of step with its neighbours; the oak barely, the seedlings more);
  3. squashes and bounces back when it acts (a spit, a bite, a slam, a pulse) and stretches up when it is watered;
  4. wilts over to one side as it ends, instead of only shrinking.
- Her staff and paws kick a dust ring off the floor on the frame they land: Elder Oak, Wild Thicket, Rootspear, both Oakling knocks, the Rootsnare slap, the Sapling touch, the Lily, the moss ball, and her feet after the Swiftleaf turn and the Puffcap hop.
- A small burst leaves the gem on Nectar Burst, Rain Cloud and Dewfall.

## Choices (Kesh 18 Nov 15:57 UTC: "Ok just make changes if I feel too long I will feedback again": all three kept as built)

1. Drowsy Spores: her yawn runs 1.2 s after the puff. It is only a look (the dash or any skill cuts it), but say if it is too long for a fight.
