## The rubric (out of 10)

The score is the lowest of three parts, so one weak part cannot hide behind two strong ones.

- **A. Kit sameness.** No two skills, combos or talent styles built on the same idea. Start at 10; a strong pair
  (same trigger, same shape, same payoff) costs 1, a partial pair costs 0.5.
- **B. Move sameness.** No two moves built on the same key pose or beat. Start at 10; a pair that shares its main
  beat costs 1, a pair that shares a smaller beat costs 0.5.
- **C. Move detail.** Each of the 30 moves scored out of 10, then averaged: anticipation 2, contact (arrives at full
  speed, weight drops) 2, follow-through and recovery 2, body mechanics (root and legs agree, feet locked, hips drive,
  no pops) 2, secondary motion and clean mesh (ears, tail, free paw and fingers, no clipping) 1, can be judged on the
  page (the move is big enough to see) 1.
