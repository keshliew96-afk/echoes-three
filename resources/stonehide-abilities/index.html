S13

鬃怒Bristle

职业被动Class passive

第一个房间就有，不用按键。Free from the first room, no button.

普攻第 1、3 击和冲刺免费嘲讽。每 8 秒，他对正在打队友的敌人低吼一次。对首领无效。Basic hits 1 and 3 and the dash taunt for free. Every 8 s he growls at a foe that is on an ally. Not on a boss.

每嘲讽一个敌人得 1 层石：受到伤害少 3%，最多 8 层。每层持续 6 秒，然后飞向 4 u 内的敌人，造成 3 点伤害。Each foe taunted is a Stone: 3% less damage, up to 8. Each lasts 6 s, then flies at a foe within 4 u for 3.

被嘲讽的敌人倒下时，最大生命 +1（每关最多 +15），每 +5 他长高 1%（最多高 15%）。A taunted foe that falls gives +1 max HP (up to +15 a level), and he grows 1% for every +5 (up to 15% taller).

被首领或勇士击中、碎裂、一次被封顶的伤害，也各给 1 层石。A hit from a boss or champion, a shatter and a capped hit also give 1 Stone.
