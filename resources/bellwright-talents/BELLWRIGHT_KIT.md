# Bellwright: skills and talent paths

**Version 1.2, 2026-11-20. Design only** (the freeze holds). All of Kesh's picks are in
(section 5); nothing is open. 1.2: his rule of 20 Nov, "no heal or shield, only buff or
debuff": every ward, every "cannot be hurt" and every blocked hit is gone; Solo replaces Golden
Bell (section 5 lists what changed). Whole rules, motions and boss lines: `BELLWRIGHT_NOTES.md`.
Numbers are guesses.

## 1. In one look

- **R1.** 26 skills: 8 Bell 钟, 8 Note 音, 8 Tune 律, 2 passives. She holds 4.
- **R2.** Three paths, one talent pick per tier from any: **Bell Array 钟阵** (many small bells
  round one giant bell, the Bourdon 洪钟, joined by chords 弦), **Reverb 回音** (sound that
  is heard again: it bounces, comes back, lingers, flies far), **Harmony 和鸣** (one sound for
  two sides: it lifts friends and spoils foes).
- **R3.** Her tier I talent sets her style and gives every skill that style's form (section 4).
- **R4.** 95 health, ranged, the slowest hero but Stonehide. No heal and no shield: she
  buffs friends and debuffs foes, and nothing else (R12).

## 2. Passive, basic attack, dash, rules

- **R5. Sympathy 共鸣.** Her bells toll together on **the beat 拍**, once a second. A foe a
  bell tolls on, or a bell's body strikes, is **Ringing 鸣震** for 3 s (a boss too). A
  **Note 音** deals 50% more to a Ringing foe, and a bell whose ring a Note passes
  **answers 应声**: it tolls at once (each bell once a second). The tag opens each cell:
  - **【挂】** hangs a bell. **【鸣】** rings her hung bells.
  - **【撞】** a Strike: a bell's body hits; foes are left Ringing.
  - **【音】** a Note. **【震】** makes foes Ringing by itself.
- **R6. Third stroke 三响** (after 赛乐芬妮) **and Chord 和弦** (after 琴女). Each skill cast
  lights a pip. Her third cast is heard twice: it sounds again 0.6 s later for half. It also
  readies a Chord: her next attack is the great stroke at once (R8, hit 3), coloured by the
  group of that third skill:
  - a Bell skill: **Forte 强音**, the stroke deals double;
  - a Note skill: **Vibrato 颤音**, foes it hits are dazed 0.5 s;
  - a Tune skill: **Diminuendo 弱音**, foes it hits deal 25% less for 4 s.
  - Bell Array: every hung bell plays the Chord too, round itself, for half. Reverb: two pips,
    not three. Harmony: each friend within 5 u joins in: the Chord deals 20% more for each, and
    each gets its gift (Forte: their next hit 30% more; Vibrato: 15% haste 3 s; Diminuendo: their
    next hit makes its foe deal 25% less, 4 s).
- **R7. Bells.** A bell: ring 1.3 u, toll 7, slows 20%, 8 s. A great bell: 1.9 u, toll 12,
  slows 35%. A chime 铃: 1.0 u, toll 5, and never counts to the cap. Three bells hang at most;
  a fourth takes the oldest one's place. Foes cannot hit a bell.
- **R8. Basic attack** (as passed). Hits 1 and 2: a bell shot, 4.2 u; at a hit or its end it
  rises, grows and falls: 【撞】8 within 1 u. Hit 3: she drums a great bell on its side: 【音】a
  fan, 3 u: three waves of 3 and a last great stroke of 8. Then the great bell stands up and
  【挂】hangs 5 s as a bell. Bell Array: each fallen shot bell stays 3 s as a chime.
  Reverb: the shots do not fall: they fly on through every foe as Notes, 6 u: 6. Harmony: the
  drummed waves go out all round her, 3 u.
- **R9. Dash** (as passed). A great bell drops over her: under it nothing hits her and she
  walks where steered, 1.5 times as fast, up to 2.5 s; an attack, a cast or a second dash
  lifts it. Bell Array: the bell stays hung where it lifts, 5 s. Reverb: it lifts with a ring:
  10 within 2.5 u, a Note. Harmony: it lifts on a cue 起拍: friends within 3 u have their dash
  back at once.
- **R10. Held 困.** A foe under a bell or on its back cannot act; everyone can hit it. No hold
  lasts over 2 s (an elite 1 s); then that foe cannot be held for 2 s. A daze 震晕 is a stun.
- **R11. Big foes.** A boss is never moved, trapped, flipped or held: that hit deals 20% more
  instead. A champion is moved half as far and never held: 10% more instead.
- **R12. Buffs and debuffs 增益 减益.** She never heals, shields or blocks a hit for a friend.
  The same buff or debuff again starts its time over and never adds. On an elite, champion or
  boss a debuff is at half value unless its line says more.
- **R13. On a pad** the pointer is the aim stick's reticle.

## 5. Picks

**Picked by Kesh, 19 Nov:** "1b, chimes and bourdon mix together, the empty slot pick harmony,
and harmony not only buff, it also give debuff to enemy": the styles are Bell Array (Chimes and
Bourdon in one), Reverb, and Harmony with both sides (In Tune, Off-key). "2a, refer to 琴女,
赛乐芬妮 passive": Sympathy, with the three-cast echo and the Chord (R6).

**Picked by Kesh, 19 Nov, second message ("all a"):**

1. The pool as listed: her eleven skills of today and fifteen new; Carillon is a tune of eight
   strokes.
2. The combo's great bell stands up and hangs 5 s after hit 3 (R8).
3. The dash: she cannot be hit from the first frame of the bell's drop (R9).
4. Discord (F13 Harmony) makes a small foe strike other foes.
5. Downbeat (T8) is the timing window: a press on the beat.
6. All twelve combos in `BELLWRIGHT_COMBO_IDEAS.md`.

**His rule, 20 Nov:** "bellwright, make it no heal or shield, only buff or debuff". I read
"shield" as anything that soaks, blocks or cancels damage for a friend. Kit 1.2:

- Gone: wards (R4, R12, the Diminuendo gift), Golden Bell and its three forms, Warding bell,
  Scapegoat, Vigil, the "take 25% less" of Safe ring, the shots stopped by Parade and Dead air.
- New or changed: **Solo** (F20) with Drummer, Your turn and Upstaged; **Vesper Chime** (F18) now
  lulls the foes that hunt a friend, with Friends' chord, Leftover and Common foe; **Tuning
  Fork** (F19) is haste alone; Harmony forms **Loud bell** (F1), **Catching** (F4),
  **Crescendo** (F16), **Clear the floor** (F22); Parade (F5) and Dead air (F22) re-worded.
- Kept, my call: the bell dash (his own dash rule and his passed design), and the moment she
  cannot be hit inside Wing Hop and Click. They guard her alone, as every hero's dash does.
  Bat back still returns shots: it is a swing, not a guard on a friend.
- Vesper Chime and Tuning Fork carry shields in the game today; taking them out waits for
  the build.
