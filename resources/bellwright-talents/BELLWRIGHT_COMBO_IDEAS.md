# Bellwright: ways her skills play together

**2026-11-19, on kit 1.2.** Design only. Twelve combos, C1 to C12, all picked by Kesh (19 Nov). Each is one small rule
between two of her skills, or a skill and her dash; none needs a talent.

| # | Name | The rule | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Strike the bell 当头一棒** | Bell Swing, or Click's landing, on a hung bell rings it like a Peal: 16 within 1.5 u, dazed 0.4 s | any hung bell, then Bell Swing or Click |
| C2 | **Bowling 连滚** | A Rolling Bell that meets a hung bell sends it rolling too, 3 u: 10. A Bell Jar it meets rolls on with its foe inside | Rolling Bell and a hung bell or a Bell Jar |
| C3 | **Dash cast 钟下施放** | Peal, Bell Swing, Boom or Call Chime pressed under the dash bell is kept, and comes out as the bell lifts, 30% wider | the dash and one of the four |
| C4 | **Jar and knell 瓮中丧钟** | Deathwatch's knell on a foe under a Bell Jar rings inside the jar: double on that foe, nothing round it | Deathwatch, then Bell Jar on the third knock |
| C5 | **Hurry the watch 催命** | A watched foe struck by Peal or Carillon takes its knell at once | Deathwatch, then Peal or Carillon |
| C6 | **Catchy 铃传绕耳** | Jingles thrown while an Earworm plays give each foe they hit 2 s of the tune | Earworm, then Jingles |
| C7 | **Curfew under a bell 钟下宵禁** | A Curfew laid round a hung bell lasts as long as the bell does | a hung bell, then Curfew |
| C8 | **Fork on the Anthem 定调** | Tuning Fork cast during the Anthem: its haste lasts the rest of the Anthem | Anthem, then Tuning Fork |
| C9 | **Bat the roller 击滚钟** | Bell Swing or Boom on her Rolling Bell sends it off afresh along her aim: 6 u more, 50% harder | Rolling Bell, then Bell Swing or Boom |
| C10 | **Every bell is a bell 一呼百应** | Peal and Carillon also ring a Bell Jar, the dash bell and the Anthem's bells (1.2: the Golden Bell is gone) | Peal or Carillon and one of the three |
| C11 | **Sound on water 水传声** | A Note that touches the Tide Caller's water hits every foe standing in that water, once | any Note, and the Tide Caller in the party |
| C12 | **Come in 请君入瓮** | Call Chime draws foes through a Curfew ring into it; once in they cannot get out | Curfew, then Call Chime inside it |

## Under each style

A combo reads the skill, whatever its form. Where a form changes what the combo can do:

| # | Bell Array 钟阵 | Reverb 回音 | Harmony 和鸣 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | a batted bell rings as it flies off; the Bourdon rings 2 u wide; a Meteor rings every bell within 2 u of its landing | Bat back has no bell: its clang rings a hung bell all the same; both landings of There and back ring | a bell rung this way tunes both sides (T3); Cut in rings a bell at the friend's feet |

## Checked against the forms

No combo repeats a form or a talent: C1 rings a bell by striking it (Bat the bells moves bells,
it does not ring them); C2 is bell on bell (no form rolls a hung bell); C9 is the only way to
re-send a roller. C3 follows the dash-cast rule the Tide Caller and Windbriar have.
