# Bellwright: interaction audit

**2026-11-20, on kit 1.2.** Design only. What happens when her rules meet each other, the other
heroes, bosses and the damage formula v1.2. Kesh closed the four picks on 19 Nov ("all a"; picks 2 to 5 in the
kit); the rest are closed here. Say so if a ruling should differ.

## The four picks (closed)

- **A1. The combo's great bell** (kit pick 2). Her bells are her whole kit, but a player may
  hold four skills and none of them a Bell skill. Then nothing answers her Notes.
  **Picked a:** the great bell she drums on hit 3 stands up and hangs 5 s. It tolls like a bell
  and never pushes a skill's bell out (G7). b) bells come from skills only.
- **A2. The dash's first 0.36 s** (kit pick 3). On the passed page the bell takes 0.36 s to
  come down, and she can be hit until it lands. His dash rule says a dash has i-frames.
  **Picked a:** nothing hits her from the first frame. b) as passed.
- **A3. Discord** (kit pick 4; F13 Harmony). A small foe turns on the foe nearest it for 5 s.
  No hero makes foes fight foes today; it needs a new piece in the foe AI.
  **Picked a:** yes: it is the clearest "debuff" a song can give, and it is funny. A kill it
  makes counts as hers. b) no: Off-key at double value only.
- **A4. Downbeat** (kit pick 5; T8). A talent that pays for pressing a skill on the beat. In
  co-op a guest's press arrives late, so the window must be judged on the guest's own screen.
  **Picked a:** the timing window (0.15 s either side; a soft pulse on her bell shows the beat).
  b) no timing: every fourth skill rings true.

## The damage formula on her kit (v1.2)

- A Strike, a toll, a Note, a second hearing: direct hits. A second hearing cannot crit.
- An answer, a chord's toll, Gift bells' ring, Many voices' echo: bonus 附加, not hits.
- Earworm, Standing wave, Rumble's bursts: Earworm is damage over time; the other two are zones.
- She has no shield and no heal: nothing of hers enters the shield layers (D33).
- "Deals less" debuffs (Diminuendo 25%, Off-key 15%, Upstaged 30%) multiply, as zones do:
  all three leave a small foe dealing 45% of its damage. Half values on big foes.
- Effect resistance as the formula has it: an elite 20%, a champion 30%, a boss 80% (never
  under 0.25 s). Kit R11 says what a boss gets in place of a move or a hold.

## Risks I see

- **Reading the screen.** Five bells, twelve chimes and chords is a lot of brass. Effects stay
  dim (the colour rule); chimes are small and thin; chords are hair lines that only flash on
  the beat.
- **Harmony with AI friends.** Most of its worth is on friends. With AI seats it still pays
  (they stand where she rings); alone it falls back to the base (G17).
- **Without shields she cannot save a friend in one press.** Her answer to a friend in
  danger is to weaken or slow what is hitting them (Vesper Chime, Diminuendo, Upstaged). The
  party needs the Tide Caller's bubbles or Mosslight's heals for the rest. That is his rule.
- **Holds.** Bell Array has many (jar, flip, knock down). The cap of R10 keeps one foe from
  being passed from hold to hold.
