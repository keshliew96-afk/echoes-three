change one, answer with its number: "drop 7", "take 41".

How it was checked: web search only (22 searches). **checked** means a search result states it;
**memory** means I know the skill but no search confirmed the wording.

## The count

75 ideas: 63 taken, 5 tried or taken and then cut (24, 28, 38, 50 by his rule of 20 Nov: no
heal or shield; 66 for sameness), 7 left (13, 30, 31, 32, 37, 51, 72). Kit 1.2's new pieces
(Solo, the lull of Vesper Chime, Loud bell, Catching, Crescendo) are my own; no search was run
for them. Searches that came back thin and are marked memory: Hi-Fi Rush, the FFXI Geomancer,
Sona's Power Chord, Seraphine's friend notes, 廉颇. Nothing here was read on a wiki page
itself: only search result summaries.
