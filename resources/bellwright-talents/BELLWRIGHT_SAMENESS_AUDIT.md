# Bellwright: sameness check

**2026-11-19, on kit 1.2.** Design only. My own pass over the 72 forms, the 15 talents, the 12
combos, the basic attack and the dash, looking for two that do the same thing. No fresh judge
read it (no helper was asked for), so this is one reader's view: Kesh's marks on the page are
the real check.

## What I tested each form against

1. The other two forms of its own skill, and its base.
2. Every other form in its own style column (families).
3. The forms of the other two columns.
4. The 15 talents, the 12 combos, the basic attack's and the dash's style shapes.
5. The names in the other six heroes' kits (a script; one clash found: "Split", now Two-tone).

## Families that still stand, and why I kept them

- **Bell Array, "the Bourdon comes"**: Bell hat, Drum the jar, Formation, Drummer, Meteor. One
  giant bell that she keeps moving is the style's promise. Each does a different thing with it:
  wears, traps, forms up, follows a friend, carries her.
- **Bell Array, "chimes appear"**: Handful, Belled, Clock face, and the talent Parting Chime.
  Where they hang differs: a fan at the pointer, on foes, round her, where a foe fell.
- **Reverb, "a Note meets a thing"**: Signal bell, Two-tone, Hung notes. Sent on, split, set
  off.
- **Reverb, "again"**: Three falls, Out and in, Your turn, There and back. The style is
  "heard again", so some of this is wanted: a landing, a ring, a buff and a leap.
- **Harmony, "friends deal more"**: Common foe, Crescendo, War song, and the talent
  Conductor. Who and when differ: the hunted pair against their hunters, one stored skill,
  a ramp over the Anthem, a standing aura.
- **"Foes deal less"**: Diminuendo (the Chord), Off-key (T3), Upstaged (Solo). Three
  sources by design: this is her answer to having no shield.
- **Harmony, foes that cannot fight well**: Rout (flee), Discord (turn on each other), Drag the
  beat (slow wind-ups), Strip (armour). Four different curses.

## The pairs I think are closest

If he marks any, these are my first guesses:

1. **Left bells** (Array Anthem) and the dash bell that stays hung (Array dash): both leave a
   bell where someone stood.
2. **Serve** (Harmony Bell Swing) and the base **Call Chime**: both bring foes to a place.
3. **Cut in** (Harmony Click) and **Bell hop** (Array Wing Hop): both hop to a set target.
4. **Standing wave** (Reverb Knell) and **Rumble** (Reverb Rolling Bell): both are sound that
   stays on a line and ticks.
5. **Solo** (base) is a number for 4 s. Its reset on a kill is what makes it a play; it may
   still read as flat on the page.
6. **Morning bell** and **Loud bell**: both are "stand in the ring and your skills get
   better". One is cooldowns, one is size.
7. **Upstaged** (Harmony Solo) and **Diminuendo**: both make foes deal less.

## Not alike by rule

- No form copies a talent, a combo, the basic attack or the dash.
- No form is a number alone: each changes a shape, a target, a place or a rule.
- Every form of a style uses that style's promise: bells and chords (Array), a second sound
  (Reverb), friends or a curse (Harmony).
