Open choices

  • Body length. 8 u is my pick: long enough to read as a wyrm at game zoom and to leave a tail down the hole. Say if it should be shorter or longer.
  • The slither's head. I carry it low and forward, like a snake travelling. The other way keeps it reared like the idle while the body moves under it.
  • Turning. It goes round a circle of 0.9 u at 72° a second. The game turns it instantly. A snake that spins on the spot looks wrong, so I would keep the circle.
  • After the burst. Only the neck comes out; the rest of the body stays down the hole and follows as it moves off. The other way brings the whole body out before it can move.
  • The dive. The game hides it the instant it burrows. The 1.5 s dive is mine, so the player sees where it went.
  • Enrage. The game has no pose for it. The coil and roar are mine: keep, shorten or drop.
  • The death. It turns to grey ash and lies there. Say if the ash should crumble away to nothing.
  • Where it comes up. 0.55 u short of the hero, so the two do not stand inside each other on this page. The game lets it come up within 0.4 u.

What is rough

  • The built body is plain: tiles, bands and spikes on a smooth tube. It has none of the chips and wear your sculpted head has, and the join sits under the second collar band.
  • Where the body goes through the floor it is cut off flat, and a dark hole with rubble is drawn round the cut. The floor itself does not break.
  • The tunnel is a mound and a line of cracks drawn for this page.
  • In a tight bend (the turn, the corner into a hole) the rings on the inside of the bend bunch and the spikes fan.
  • The neck's foot is steered to meet the trail, so in a hard turn the first rings of the neck shear a little rather than bend.
  • While the Burrow Strike is under the floor, and until its ring bursts, the other buttons wait.
  • Pressing another Action while the body is still half down its hole pulls the neck to its new pose quickly.
  • The inside of the mouth is a plain pocket of mine, violet at the lips and dark at the back.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for its power, grey for its ash. They are kept dim on purpose.
