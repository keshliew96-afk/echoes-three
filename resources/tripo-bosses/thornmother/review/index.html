Open choices

  • Call the wood. A new pose for the three add waves. The game has only the sound.
  • Which hoof paws. Built as the game has it: the hind-left hoof scrapes back twice. A forehoof pawing is the more usual boar warning and shows better from above. Say if you want to see it.
  • The stop. Built on locked hooves, as the game's rule: she lands, pitches over her forehooves and rocks back. A true skid, hooves sliding half a body with dust, would break that rule but could feel heavier.
  • Pods. The game throws three pods, so three of her four leave. The fourth, at the back, only swells.
  • Size. Drawn 2.2 u long. The game's own sow is longer and lower.

What is rough

  • Her mouth does not open. The jaw is one piece with the head, so the squeal is all head and neck.
  • Each blade is one stiff piece that swings at its root. It does not bend along its length.
  • The bark plates of the thicket bend with her back like skin, and the bristle crest moves with the head.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a hoof can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for her power, dark briar for the patches. They are kept dim on purpose.
  • In the death her legs stay stiff as she rolls over.
