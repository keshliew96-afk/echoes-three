Open choices

  • The halo, notes and motes. Your model has none, so they are mine: a violet ring with an inner ring and seven bone runes, seven violet notes on it and six crystal motes. A Tripo export with a halo would give it your own art.
  • The mouth. The mask's chin is cut free under the lips so it opens to sing, with a dark mouth that glows violet when it is wide.
  • Which notes light. The game lights a note per verse. Here they are the three at the top of the ring, so they show over the hood.
  • The Echo Step landing. Here it lands 4.5 u straight back; the game picks the clear fixed point farthest from the party.
  • The Verse pose. Arms up and head back is mine. The game's rig only flares the halo and the heart.
  • The turn. 120 degrees a second is mine; the game turns it at once.
  • The death. A heap of robe on the floor with the hood fallen forward. An empty robe that collapses with nothing inside is the other choice.

What is rough

  • The arms have no bones inside the sleeves on the model, so a sleeve bends as one piece at the elbow; raised high, the bell of the sleeve stretches a little at the shoulder.
  • The cut chin shows a thin seam under the lips close up when it sings.
  • The notes, runes and motes are flat shapes in the model's own violet, with no texture of their own.
  • When it crosses its hands for the Sung Lance, the wide sleeves press into the front of the robe.
  • The hero is a grey stand-in that does not dodge.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for its power. They are kept dim on purpose.
