Cinderback from Tripo, rigged · version 1

Your export of the Kiln's warden: the great fire salamander, long and low, with sooty hide, live coals down its back, gold-tipped gill stalks and a violet brow scale. It now has a skeleton, an opening mouth and 12 moves made by hand. It prowls with an S through its body. For the Magma Dive it noses into the floor and swims under it to the ring, then bursts up and lies spent and dull. It coils its tail and whips it round for the Tail Lash, and rears with its throat white-hot to spit three gobbets of fire. The timings come from the game. Pick a move and drag to turn the view, then judge the weight and the hit. Nothing in the game has changed.

Model as you sent it

four views

It came through in one piece with clean limbs: four splayed legs with five-toed feet clear of the body, a long tapering tail, three rows of glowing coals down its back, six gill stalks with gold tips behind the head, ember eyes, a violet brow scale and a pale chin and belly. Its head points straight down its length, so nothing was corrected. The mouth was closed, so I cut it open along the line of the lips, gave it a hinged jaw and painted the inside a dark red that heats up for the spit.

Open choices

  1. The coals. Your export has three rows of coals down its back. The game's stand-in has two. It could stay as it is.
  2. The mouth. It was closed, so I cut it open and made the inside. A re-export with the mouth a little open would give Tripo's own teeth and tongue.
  3. The spent pose. It lies sprawled flat with its chin down and goes grey. It could instead lie on its side or roll half over.
  4. The Ember Spit's rear. Mine: it stands up on its hind legs. It could instead lift only its head and chest.
  5. The gills. The three stalks on each side move together on one bone. Each stalk could get its own if you want them to flick one by one.
  6. The enrage, the death and the turn. Mine. The game turns it at once and has no pose for the enrage.

Rough so far

  • The gobbets, the glow under the floor and the slag pools are drawn by the page. They are not in the GLB.
  • The hero is a grey stand-in that takes the hit but does not dodge.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Kiln Gold for the heat and the fire, and Violet for its brow. They are kept dim on purpose.
