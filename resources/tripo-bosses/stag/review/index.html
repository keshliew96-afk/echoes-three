Open choices

  • Antler Quake ring. Now centred on the Stag, as you asked. The game still puts it on his target, so this is a rule change for after the freeze. At the game's 1.6 u the ring reaches about 1 u past his body. A wider ring, 2.2 u, would threaten more of the floor: say if you want to see it.
  • Trample with a warning. In the game the trample has no warning at all. The second button shows it with 0.46 s of head down and pawing, and an Ember lane on the floor. It reads better, but it changes a rule, so it stays a choice for after the freeze.
  • Call the herd. A new pose for the three add waves. The game has only the sound.
  • Walk. Built as the game has it, in diagonal pairs. A slower four-beat stalk, one hoof at a time, would look heavier. Say if you want to see it.
  • Face limit. 28,000 fits the boss budget with 2,380 to spare. The 15,000 export was also whole, if the min spec ever needs it.

What is rough

  • His jaw does not open. The skull and jaw are one piece in the model, so the bellow is all neck and head.
  • The ribs, the bone plates and the crystals bend with the body like skin. They do not slide as separate hard pieces.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a hoof can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warning, Violet for his power. They are kept dim on purpose.
  • In the death the legs go stiff rather than folding, and his far legs hang a little above the floor.
