Geode Colossus from Tripo, rigged · version 1

Your export of the Level IV boss that waits in the Heart Chamber on some seeds, with a skeleton and 12 moves added by hand. It walks on its fists like a gorilla: each fist is a foot as much as each leg is, pinned to the floor while it carries weight and stepping in diagonal pairs with the legs. The rock stays rock: the head, the fists, the feet, the crystal spires and the heart in the geode each move as one piece. Its timings are the game's. Pick a move, drag to turn it, and judge the weight and the hit. Nothing in the game has changed.

Open choices

  • The enrage. The chest beat and the slam are mine; the game only burns the cracks and quickens the heart.
  • The death. It goes down on its knees and rests on its belly, head bowed. It could crack apart instead, its plates and crystals falling away, which needs the model cut into pieces.
  • The mouth. The face is carved shut and has too few points round the mouth to cut a jaw, so it does not open to roar. A Tripo export with an open mouth, or a separate jaw, would give it one.
  • The turn. 70 degrees a second is mine; the game turns it at once.

Rough so far

  • The upper arm is short and the forearm huge, so when a fist goes high the skin at the elbow stretches a little.
  • The crystal spires do not move on their own.
  • The hero is a grey stand-in that does not dodge.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for its power. They are kept dim on purpose.
