Mire King from Tripo, rigged · version 3

Your export of the second Level II boss, with a toad's skeleton and 13 moves added by hand. The model came with its mouth sealed and its crown fused to its head, so I cut the mouth open along the lip, built the inside and a tongue that reaches the length of the lane, and cut the crown free so it sits loose and jumps when he lands. The throat sac and the belly swell, the eyes sink when he blinks or swallows, and his legs fold the way the sculpt folds them. His timings are the game's. Pick a move, drag to turn him, and judge the weight and the hit. Nothing in the game has changed.

Changed 13 Nov, your ask: the Swallow is gone. In its place is the Venom Shake: he charges while the warts on his back light up violet, shakes himself, and each wart throws a gob of venom that lands somewhere round him and leaves a small pool.

Changed 14 Nov, your ask: the Belly Flop no longer winds up like a dive. He sinks onto his haunches with his hands on the floor, his hind legs push twice (the hips bob up onto the toes and stamp back down), and on the third push he jumps, now 2.2 u high instead of 1.4 u.

Open choices

  • Venom Shake numbers. You gave the move; the numbers are mine: 11 gobs, each lands at a random spot 1.8 to 4.6 u from him, pools of 0.6 u that last 5 s, a 1.3 s charge. Say if there should be more or fewer, bigger pools, or a longer stay. It is a new mechanic, so it goes into the game only after the freeze.
  • Venom warning. I mark each landing spot in Ember from 0.6 s into the charge, so a player can step off it. Say if the spots should stay hidden until the shake.
  • Where the tongue stops. It stops on the hero and drags them in 2 u. The game hits everyone in the lane to its far end. Say if the tongue should always run the full lane and pull whoever it passes.
  • Call the brood. The game has no pose for the add calls. The three-beat croak is mine: keep it, shorten it, or drop it.
  • Enrage. The swell and the two-handed slam are mine. The game only changes the sac, the eyes and the gems.
  • The death. Belly up with the crown knocked off is my pick for a toad king. The quieter version slumps forward onto his chin with the crown still on.
  • The loose crown. It hops off his head on the Belly Flop landing, on a Heavy hit and when he is hurt. Say if it should stay fixed until the death.
  • Where the hero stands for the Belly Flop. The ring here is where he lands and the hero stands 1.7 u from its middle, so the hit shows without him landing on top of them. The game puts the ring on the hero.
  • Size. 3.0 u to the crown tips, which makes his body about 2.3 u tall, close to the game's toad (2.4 u), and a little narrower (2.35 u against 2.7 u). Say if he should be wider or bigger.
  • Turn speed. 120° a second is my number, the same as the wolf and the heron.

What is rough

  • The inside of the mouth is a plain pocket of mine, violet at the lips and dark at the back. It has no teeth, gums or throat detail.
  • The mouth is cut along the sculpted lip line. Closed, the cut shows as a thin dark seam that follows the lip's ticks.
  • The tongue is a plain tube with a pad on the end, in two tones.
  • He has no eyelids. A blink is the eye pulled down into the head, which smears the eye's paint for a frame or two.
  • The throat sac and the belly swell as balls, evenly. They do not wrinkle when empty.
  • Each hand and each foot is one piece: the fingers and toes curl together, not one by one.
  • The body cannot flatten on the Belly Flop landing. The page's squash does that; the model only spreads its limbs and drops.
  • The crown's underside is a flat rust lid, and the head under it is a plain teal patch. Both show when the crown is off.
  • The overlaps the audit still finds are in its table: limbs pressed into the body, 0.18 u at most.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a hand can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for his power and his venom, a dark slick for the mire. They are kept dim on purpose.
