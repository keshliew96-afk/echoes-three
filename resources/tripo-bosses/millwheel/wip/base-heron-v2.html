Drowned Heron from Tripo, rigged · version 2

Your export of the first Level II boss, with a bird's skeleton and 12 moves added by hand. The model came with its wings folded and fused to the body, so I cut each wing free and gave it twelve flight feathers that fan out when it opens. The bill gapes, the neck is four bones so the S can coil and strike, and the crest, breast plumes, tail and weed all swing. Its timings are the game's. Pick a move, drag to turn it, and judge the weight and the hit. Nothing in the game has changed.

New in version 2. The dive is rebuilt from how real diving birds go in. Bill Flurry is new: three rapid jabs at one target. And every move went through a frame-by-frame audit of every point of the model; what it found and what I fixed is in the table under the moves.

IdleA heron stands very still and moves in snaps. It breathes, the S of the neck eases, the head flicks left and then right with a cock of the head, the crest lifts each time it looks, the wings ruffle once, the bill clacks once, and the weed sways.6 s loop
WalkA wader's stalk: two slow high steps. Each foot comes up with its toes hanging and reaches. The head holds still in the air and then darts forward once a step, and the tail and the weed swing against the steps.2.0 u/s, as the game. 0.9 s a cycle.

Bill Flurry (new, your ask)It looks at the one it has picked, and a small mark closes under that hero. The neck coils into its S with the bill aimed down at them. Submerge and SurfaceThe dive is head first, the way herons and other diving birds go in. The bill stabs the water. The legs drive the hips up and over it, the wings snap shut against the body, and for a moment the whole bird is one upright line: bill, neck, body, tail and legs, toes pointed. It slides down the hole its bill made and the toes go under last. The violet ripple is all that shows. Then the bill breaks the water, the head and neck follow, and it bursts out with the wings flung wide, shakes, and folds.Game, two timelines played in a row. Submerge has no warning: gather 1.5 frames, plunge 2.5 frames (the bill is in the water at the end of it, 0.75 u ahead of its feet), splash 3 frames, under in 0.6 s (upright by 0.28 s, toes under at 0.55 s). The game keeps it under while its adds fight (up to 360 ticks); here 0.4 s. Surface: rise for the 48-tick ring (radius 1.8 u), burst 2.5 frames, wings wide 3 frames, settle 0.6 s.

Open choices

  • The wings. Built from the folded model, so an open wing is the folded wing's outer skin with my flat feathers fanned behind it. It reads as a wing from play distance. If you want painted, layered feathers up close, send one open wing as its own Tripo piece (about 3,000 triangles) and I will fit it to both shoulders.
  • Bill Flurry. The name, the 36-tick aim, the 10-frame rhythm and the small Ember mark under the target are mine. The third jab is the heavy one. Say if all three should hit alike, if it should be faster, or if it should step forward with each jab.
  • Where the dive goes in. It dives 0.75 u ahead of its feet and comes up where it stood. The game brings it up beside a hero; say if the dive should aim at the hero too.
  • Submerge and Surface as one button. The game plays them apart, with the add wave between. Say if you want two buttons.
  • The dash. The game's rig skims 0.42 u above its standing height, which carries the bill over a hero's head. I fly it low so the bill goes through the hero at chest height, and the hit lands when the bill gets there. The game counts the hit when the body arrives, about 0.1 s later.
  • Size. 3.0 u tall puts its hips where the game's heron has them. The game's notes call it 2.2 times a hero's height. Say if it should be smaller.
  • Turn speed. 120° a second is my number, the same as the wolf.

What is rough

  • The flight feathers are single flat blades in two slate tones. They have no barbs or paint, and where they meet the wing there is a visible step.
  • The skin I closed under each wing and on the body behind it is one flat colour.
  • The lower bill is cut out of a closed bill, so the inside of the mouth is a plain dark surface.
  • The breast plumes move at their free tips only. The white breast itself is part of the body.
  • The three crest plumes move together, not one by one.
  • The overlaps the audit still finds are listed in its table: soft feathers and weed against a leg, 0.11 u at most.
  • Going under, the body is cut off at the floor line. The pool hides the cut from most angles, not from all.
  • Switching from Walk into an attack blends in 0.03 s, so a foot can jump a little at that instant.
  • No ink outline and no flat game colours yet, the same as the hero models.
  • The effects are stand-ins drawn for this page in the game's colours: Ember for the floor warnings, Violet for its power, pale water and silt for the millrace. They are kept dim on purpose.
