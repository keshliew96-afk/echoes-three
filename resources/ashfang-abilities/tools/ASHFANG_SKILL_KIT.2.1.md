## 1. The four wills (K1, K2, K5)

No fire, earth, water or wind in any name, line or look. The four families are wills.

| Will | Was | Colour | A mark gives | Its effect on a foe (1.7 numbers) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 剑意 Intent | fire | gold #FFB43C | 4% more damage | **the scar 剑痕** (was the burn): a gold line on the foe; at each tick a small ghost blade flashes in and cuts along it. No flames, no smoke. |
| 不屈 Unbowed | earth | bronze #B08A5E | 4% less damage taken | **the daunt 震慑** (was the stun): same 1 s cap, same 2 s rule, same small-stun rule |
| 止水 Still Water | water | pale aqua #7FE0F2 | 1 health every 2 s in a fight | **stillness 凝滞** (was the slow) |
| 狂热 Fervour | wind | silver #E7E3F0 | basic attacks 5% faster | **the launch 挑飞** (was the throw up), the pull, the push |

Looks: 剑意 is thin gold lines, brush strokes of light and stars; 不屈 is heavy bronze strokes,
shock rings, floor cracks and the giant sword; 止水 is still pale rings, one ripple, moons and
mirror images; 狂热 is silver after-images, fast strokes, spirals and wheels of blades. Caltrops
are iron. His basic cuts stay Fox Crimson #E8577A. No Ember, no Violet.

## 2. Kit rules

- **The count is gone (K7).** Bladesong's count, the song and every line that read them are gone.
  "Counts double in the air" goes; launches stay, for Spike and Smash down.
- **Kept as they are (K9):** the daunt's limits (S5), the boss rule (S6), the dash cancel (S8),
  Wall slam (S55), the six-hit combo (S12), the dash (S13).
- **The scar meets fervour and calm (S56, K6).** Fervour spreads the scar (C1): a 狂热 hit on a
  scarred foe spreads its scar to foes within 1.5 u. Settle 归静 (C12): a 止水 skill closes a
  scar; the rest lands at once and the foe is blinded 0.8 s.
- **Forms (K12, K14).** Each of the 23 pressed skills has a base form, a Swift form and a Crescent
  form: rows F1 to F23 of the talent doc, section 5. A form is part of the cast: no second mark;
  its added part carries the skill's own effect at half length. The
  passives have no form.

## 3. Class passive, basic attack and dash

- **S11. Sword Heart 剑心 (K8, rewritten in 2.1 by K18 to K24).** Free from the first room, no button.
  - **No strong cut (K18).** No stronger cut and no wave after a cast.
  - **Ghost swords (K19).** Each will mark is a small ghost sword, about half the length of his
    own, floating point-down behind a shoulder in its will's colour (剑 gold, 屈 bronze, 水 pale
    aqua, 热 silver); the newest is on the right. A new one draws itself out of his blade as the
    skill is pressed, with a soft chime; the oldest fades. He holds two; a third pushes out the
    oldest. Same bonuses as before. Nothing is shown in or over his left paw.
  - **They join every skill (K20).** After the last hit of each skill, every ghost sword he held
    at the press copies that hit for 4, with its own will's effect at half length. Base form:
    each flies out, stabs the nearest foe the skill hit and flies back. Swift: they fly beside him
    along his thrust and cut every foe on it. Crescent: each fires a 剑气 5 u along his aim from
    where it floats. A copy is not a cast (no mark).
  - **The sixth basic hit (K21).** He keeps his sword in his right paw, flicks his open left paw
    forward and the newest ghost sword shoots out spinning like a disc: 4 u through every foe,
    12. It whirls 0.4 s, bursts (6 within 1.5 u, with its will: a scar of 2 a second for 2 s, a
    0.2 s daunt, 20% stillness for 1.5 s, or a 0.5 u push) and flies back to his shoulder; the
    throw does not spend the mark. With no mark he throws a plain Moon Silver ghost sword that
    fades after the burst.
  - **Swift and Crescent (K22).** Under Swift a dash pressed while a thrown ghost sword is away
    takes him to it, cutting on the way. Under Crescent hits 1 to 5 each throw a 剑气.
  - **Specials (K23).** On the cast that stores the second mark, the two ghost swords fly
    together, the sword spirit takes one in each paw and does the special; both are spent.
  - **Spirit Swords (K24, the Crescent capstone)** are full-size Moon Silver swords circling over
    his head; they throw their 剑气 with each skill he casts and with the sixth basic hit.
  - The dash as passed.
- **Echo 残响 (K26, trial, talent T44).** Every fourth skill is cast again by a silver ghost of
  him, for half. It steps out where he stood 0.4 s after his cast ends, aims as he aimed and does
  the whole skill in his style's form. On the page as a trial section only.
- **S12. Basic attack.** The six-hit combo from his reel, as passed (10, 10, 12, 11, 14, the ghost sword throw 12; 2.1, K21).
- **S13. Dash.** A low thrust, 2.6 u, untouchable, 8 to each foe it passes. Look (M7b): flat and
  low, the blade leading like a spear; a thin Fox Crimson streak with a Moon Silver edge; a short
  spray of sparks where he stops.

## 4. The twenty-six skills (K10, K13)

Pool: 12 Blade, 5 Wave, 9 Art; 7 剑意, 6 不屈, 6 止水, 7 狂热. Each keeps its 1.7 total damage,
effect and cooldown; the motion is the script named (M8 to M24), or, for the six ★ skills, the
motion of his video under its new words (M7d).

### 剑意 Intent (the scar)

- **S14. Flurry** (Blade, 3 s, M8). Four cuts, 60° fan: 3 each to up to 6 foes; the scar 2 a
  second for 3 s.
- **S15. Crescent Finisher** (Blade, 6 s, M9). Rising cut, 70° fan, 1.0 u: 20 to up to 5 foes,
  50% more for each other skill landed in the last 2 s (up to 2), today's rule. The scar 4 a
  second for 3 s. Spike (C3): on a foe in the air it slams it down, 50% more, 10 within 1.5 u.
- **S16. Keen Crescent 锐月** (Wave, 4 s, M10; was Flame Crescent). 6 u, 1.2 u wide, through every
  foe: 16, the scar 3 a second for 3 s.
- **S17. Radiant Cascade 耀舞连斩 ★** (Blade, 10 s; was Blazing Cascade). Two wide sweeps, an
  overhead slam with a star on the floor, a swirl of gold blades that pulls foes in, the light
  gathered over his paw, a dash through them. 5 + 5, 10, 3 × 3, dash 14; its cuts scar.
- **S18. Crimson Edge** (Passive). 25% more crit chance; his crits leave a scar, 3 a second for 3 s.
- **S19. Sword Bloom 剑绽** (Art, 7 s, M11; was Flashfire). A scar opens on every foe within 4 u,
  3 a second for 3 s; a foe already scarred bursts as a star: 12, and scars the foes within 1.5 u.
- **S20. Triple Crescent** (Wave, big skill, 12 s, M12). Three great waves, 7 u, 2 u wide: 18 each;
  the 2nd and 3rd deal half to a foe already hit; the scar 4 a second for 3 s.

### 不屈 Unbowed (the daunt)

- **S21. Lunge Strike 贯刺** (Blade, 4 s, M13). A thrust, 1.3 u: 26 to up to 2 foes in line, daunt 0.5 s.
  Dash strike (C11) as in 1.7.
- **S22. Rift Cut 裂痕斩** (Wave, 6 s, M14; was Ground Wave). A rift runs 5 u: 14 to every foe on
  it, daunt 0.4 s. Blade fins of bronze light, not stone.
- **S23. Titan Cleave 巨剑斩 ★** (Blade, charge, 6 s; was Earthsplitter). Quick: 20 in 1.5 u, daunt
  0.5 s. Full: 40 in 2.8 u, daunt 1 s. Bronze light runs up the growing blade; sparks and a
  shock ring, no rocks.
- **S24. Ink Dive 墨坠 ★** (Blade, 9 s; was Inkstone Dive). 8, 6 + 6, 4 × 3, 6, landing 20 in 1.8 u,
  daunt 0.6 s. Ink strokes and ink spikes.
- **S25. Caltrops** (Art, 6.5 s, M15). Iron caltrops, 0.7 u, for 5 s; a foe's first step on them:
  daunt 0.4 s.
- **S26. Riposte** (Art, 8 s, M16). A 0.6 s guard; on a catch: 30 in a 90° arc, daunt 0.5 s. Sent
  back (C8) as in 1.7.

### 止水 Still Water (stillness)

- **S27. Moonfang** (Blade, dash cut, 7 s, M17). 3.2 u, untouchable: 24 to up to 4 foes; marked
  2 s (20% more damage taken), stillness 30% for 2 s.
- **S28. Calm Forms 止水四式 ★** (Blade, 6 s; was Riptide Form). 10, 12, 8, 10; stillness 30 to
  40%. Pale arcs.
- **S29. Calm Crescent 静月** (Wave, 5 s, M18; was Tide Crescent). 2.5 u wide, 4 u: 14, stillness
  40% for 3 s; he hops 1.5 u, untouchable. It settles a scar (C12).
- **S30. Mirror Ring 镜月剑阵 ★** (Art, big skill, 14 s; was Tide Ring). A ring of pale light,
  2.5 u; seven mirror images thrust across it, 5 each, stillness 30%; then 剑花 and four bursts,
  4 each. All in 4 s. It settles a scar.
- **S31. Mirror Image** (Art, 10 s, M19). Foes within 4 u turn on the image for 3 s (never a boss
  or the tank's foes); it bursts in mirror shards: 12 within 2 u, stillness 40% for 3 s. The
  image throws too (C6) and Burst the image (C7) as in 1.7.
- **S32. Calm Edge 静锋** (Passive; was Cold Edge). His hits deal 15% more to a foe in stillness;
  every 4th hit on the same foe: stillness 30% for 1.5 s.

### 狂热 Fervour (the launch, the pull, the push)

- **S33. Fox Step** (Blade, dash cut, 5 s, M20). 2 u, untouchable: 18 to up to 3 foes, launched 0.5 s.
- **S34. Blade Storm** (Blade, 7 s, M21). Pulls foes within 2.5 u 1 u in; five cuts of 4.
- **S35. Skyblade Waltz ★** (Blade, 12 s). As passed: 6, 8, 6, 8, 6, 10, then 6 per beam. A ring of
  light and blades of light.
- **S36. Blade Dance** (Art, 6 s, M22). 1.3 u: 10 to up to 6 foes, launched 0.6 s; he runs 30%
  faster for 2 s.
- **S37. Blade Wheel 旋刃** (Wave, 7 s, M23; was Whirlwind Slash). Travels 4 u in 1.6 s: 3 every
  quarter second; carries the foes and drops them together. Coloured wheel (C5): a crescent
  thrown through it gives it its will.
- **S38. Razor Wake** (Passive). Every second the 3 nearest foes within 0.9 u take 4; the third
  cut in a row on the same foe launches it 0.4 s.
- **S39. Thousand Cuts** (Blade, big skill, 12 s, M24; back, K10). A blur for 1.2 s: eight cuts
  of 5, each on a foe within 3 u, the one nearest his aim first, then a different foe each time
  while there is one (40 on a lone foe). Untouchable; he ends beside the last foe cut, which is
  launched 0.6 s. A8 holds: he never ends on a floor warning or a floor bed.

**Gone:** Gale Crescent (R3 a).
