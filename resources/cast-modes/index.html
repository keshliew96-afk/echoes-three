Cast modes for every skill

Your ask: every skill of every hero aims with the pointer, in the three ways League of Legends casts. Mode 1 shows the skill's area on Q and casts on a left click. Mode 2 shows the area while you hold Q and casts when you let go. Mode 3 casts the moment you press, with no area shown. Mode 3 is how the game casts today.

The ask, as built here

The three modes

1

Normal cast

Press Q: the area shows and follows the pointer. Left click casts. Esc, Space (the dodge) or a right click cancels. Pressing another skill key swaps to that skill.

2

Quick cast with indicator

Hold Q: the area shows while held. Let go to cast where the pointer is. A quick tap still casts at once, so this mode never feels slower than mode 3. Space or Esc while holding cancels.

3

Quick cast

Press Q and the skill goes out at the pointer that same frame. No area. This is the game today, so nothing changes for a player who keeps it.

Indicator shapes

Fourteen shapes cover all 85 skills

Each skill's shape comes straight from its data (shape, range, area, count, dash, vault), so a node that widens or lengthens a skill also grows its indicator. The white area is what the skill hits; the dashed ring is how far it can reach; green rings mark the allies a heal will land on.

In the game

Seen in a real room

Stills from a review-only build: a local copy of the game that lays the indicator on the floor of a real Level I room, four seconds into the first wave, at normal play zoom, aimed at the nearest foes. Nothing is cast and the pointer is not drawn. Seen only in software GL in the cloud (1 to 30 fps), so colour and feel still need your laptop. Not pushed anywhere.

What the shots taught: the white reads apart from the foes' Ember telegraphs (shot 3 has both), but it catches the room's bloom and glows more than a guide should; the build would draw it outside the bloom pass. The first cooldown grey looked almost white under the room's bloom, so cooldown is now a dashed edge with no fill (shot 6). The first ally-pick ring hid inside the heroes' own foot rings, so it is now a wider green ring with a mark above (shot 2). Short cones such as Brutal Cleave (0.95 u) are small at play zoom; that is their true reach.

The rules

How it behaves

Settings

  • Settings ▸ Controls gets a Cast mode row (1, 2 or 3) for all skills.
  • Under it, one override per key (Q E C X): "Same as all", 1, 2 or 3, the way League sets it per key. Per key rather than per skill, because skills change every run and the key is what the hand learns.
  • Saved with the key binds, per browser. Ten languages for the new rows.

While a skill is armed

  • You keep moving and the basic attack keeps working.
  • The dodge always wins: Space dodges and drops the armed skill (your dash rule: top priority).
  • A skill on cooldown still shows, greyed, with its time left. Casting it is refused with today's "not ready" nudge. If it comes off cooldown while held, it turns white and casts on release.
  • A page opening (reward, doors, pause), being downed or stunned drops it.
  • Ground skills clamp to their reach the way the game already does: the circle stops at the edge of the dashed ring.

Gamepad

  • Its own Cast mode row. Default: hold a skill button to aim with the right stick, let go to cast; a tap casts at once with today's aim assist. That is mode 2 on a pad.
  • Ground skills: how far the right stick is pushed sets the distance (full tilt = full reach). With the stick at rest the aim goes to the nearest foe, as today.
  • Mode 1 on a pad: the same button again casts; LT (the dodge) cancels. RT stays the attack.

Co-op and AI

  • Only you see your indicator, as in League. Teammates see the cast when it goes out, as now.
  • Each player picks their own mode, like their keys. Host and guests can differ.
  • The cast press is sent on the frame you confirm, with that frame's aim, which every input frame already carries. The wire, the lag compensation and the replay stay the same.
  • AI seats and the autopilot have no indicator and cast as they do today.

Old profiles

  • A profile without the new setting plays exactly as today (mode 3) until the player changes it. New profiles get the default you pick (choice 1).
  • No deed, record or counter is touched.

How it would be built (after the freeze lifts)

  • One input change: src/core/input.js holds a skill press until it is confirmed, then queues the same skill_N press as today. The sim never learns which mode you use, so the nine goldens stay identical.
  • One indicator module in src/render/skillfx/, fed by a pure "which shape is this skill" function over the live skill numbers.
  • Settings rows in src/app/controls.js and the Controls tab; docs/CONTROLS.md; one probe (tools/cast-modes-probe.mjs); one draft PR under the slice rules.
For you

Open choices

Answer with the numbers and letters, for example "1a 2a 3a". Where I recommend one, it is the one the field above uses.

  1. Default for new profiles
    1. Mode 2, quick cast with indicatorrecommended. A tap casts at once, so it is never slower than today, and a new player who holds the key sees the area.
    2. Mode 1, normal cast. Every skill needs two presses; with cooldowns from 2.5 s that is a lot of clicking.
    3. Mode 3, quick cast, as today.
  2. Old profiles
    1. Keep mode 3, what they play todayrecommended. Your ask does not name old profiles, and the house rule is to leave them as they are.
    2. Move them to the new default too.
  3. Per-key override
    1. One row for all skills, plus an override per key (Q E C X)recommended.
    2. One row only.
    3. An override per skill (85 skills, most seen once a run).
  4. Mode 1: pressing the same key again
    1. Casts at the pointer (a double tap is a quick cast)recommended.
    2. Cancels.
  5. Mode 1: right mouse (your attack button)
    1. A fresh right click cancels the armed skill and attacks, the League habitrecommended.
    2. Right mouse only attacks; the skill stays armed.
  6. Ally heals and shields (9 skills: Swift Mend, Guardian Bond, Kindred Shield, Lantern Ward, Shield Wall, Rallying Cry, Bubble Ward, Vesper Chime, Tuning Fork)
    1. Keep the game's own pick (lowest health in reach, or the ally most foes chase; F1–F4 still forces one). The indicator rings who will get itrecommended. No rule changes.
    2. The ally under the pointer gets it. This changes a rule in the sim and the goldens, so it would be its own ask.
  7. Passive skills (10, such as Warding Aura, Iron Stance, Hum)
    1. Holding the key shows the passive's field; nothing castsrecommended.
    2. The key does nothing, as today.
  8. The tonic or bomb on G
    1. Bombs follow the cast mode too, with a ground circle where they landrecommended. Tonics drink at once in every mode.
    2. Skills only.
  9. Gamepad
    1. Its own row; default hold to aim, tap to cast; stick tilt sets a ground skill's distancerecommended.
    2. The pad always casts at once, as today.
  10. Indicator colour
    1. Parchment white for every shape, Bone grey while on cooldown, Bright Heal rings on the allies a heal will land onrecommended. Never Ember or Violet, which mean enemy danger and the gods.
    2. Each hero's own glow colour as a thin edge on the white.
  11. Teammates' indicators in co-op
    1. Only you see yoursrecommended.
    2. Teammates see a faint copy. That needs a change to the co-op wire.
  12. When to build it
    1. Wait for the freeze lift, then one draft PR (input, indicator, settings, ten languages, probe)recommended.
    2. Lift the freeze for this one slice now.
