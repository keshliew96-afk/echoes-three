# Mosslight's plants: turnaround sheet prompts for Tripo

**Version 1.3, 2026-11-18 (prompts 8 and 9 added for kit 2.1 and 2.2: the Snapjaw and the Lure Lily).**
Version 1.2, 2026-11-16. Kesh asked for prompts to generate the models the druid review needs
(`MOSSLIGHT_DRUID_REVIEW.md`, R35). One prompt per model, each complete, so paste one prompt into a
fresh chat. Each gives one sheet with four views in a row: front, left, back, right. Send the sheet
back in the chat and it is cut into the four view images for Tripo.

Design only. The models are for the review page first; nothing enters the game during the freeze.

**How her plants stay apart from the plant foes.** The Thornling, the Vine Lasher and the Rotcap are
dark, thorned, and have slanted glowing eyes. Every plant of Mosslight's is the opposite, and the
prompts say so:

- pale honey-brown wood and fresh spring-green leaves, round smooth shapes, nothing sharp;
- round dark friendly eyes or no face at all, never glowing eyes;
- one small pale green crystal bud, the same crystal as her staff gem;
- a thin terracotta ribbon, the same colour as the trim of her robe.

**Tripo settings, as for the other models:** HD Model H3.1, Ultra Mesh off, Triangle, 2K texture,
Remove Lighting on, PBR off, Generate in Parts off, no Tripo Rig.

## After the models are made

Each GLB and its four views go under `/mnt/project-files/tripo-summons/<name>/` (new folder, same
layout as `tripo-mobs/`). The ability thread then swaps the stand-in shape on the review page for the
model. The Oakling needs a walk, a slam, a wake-up out of the floor and a wither; the others only
need to grow in, sway and wither, and the Spitter to turn and spit.
