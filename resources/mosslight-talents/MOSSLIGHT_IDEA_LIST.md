## What her 18 skills lack

- Quick heals: nothing that makes other heals stronger, and no hold.
- Lasting heals: nothing that moves with the fight (Dewfall, the sapling and the oak all stand still),
  and no Moss that spreads. Only one passive.
- Damage: no big moment to match the Elder Oak, nothing that punishes foes for hitting an ally, and
  nothing that lets the party heal itself by fighting, which a boss fight needs.
- Water and wind: he likes both rules; only two skills water and one blows.
