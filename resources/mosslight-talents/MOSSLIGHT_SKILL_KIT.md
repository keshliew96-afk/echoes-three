**Kesh's rules, kept as written.**

- She is a druid style healer with three talent paths: Spring (spam), Evergreen (heal over time),
  Harvest (damage turned into healing).
- No shield skill at all. Shields go to another hero.
- Her class relic: overheal summons a nature wisp that circles the ally and stores the overheal;
  when that ally is hit, the wisp heals them.

  (3 damage bolts in darker green, 5 s or until her next basic attack). She casts her skills without
  the staff in hand.

  every 3 s.

## 1. Kit rules

- **S1. The pool.** 26 skills since 1.9 (18 by Kesh's R34 a, and the eight he picked on 2026-11-17): 6 quick
  heals, 10 lasting heals (two of them passives), 10 damage skills. His range for every hero is 24 to
  26. The Bramble Hedge (S53), Compost (S60) and Briar Coat (S63) are cut. She holds at most 4, all from wave rewards, as today. Any skill works with any talent; the
  three groups only lean toward a path.
- **S2. No shields.** No skill, node or talent of hers gives a shield. The Bulwark node is never
  offered for her skills. Kindred Shield is cut.
- **S3. Moss.** Moss is her heal over time on an ally. It ticks once a second. Each skill keeps its
  own Moss on each ally; casting the same skill on the same ally again restarts it and keeps what
  was still unpaid, up to one extra cast's worth. A crit is rolled once when Moss lands. Moss ends
  when its ally is downed. (This is the HoT rule of talent line T18. The Regrowth and Deep Roots
  talents make more Moss.)
- **S4. Bloom.** To bloom Moss is to pay out everything still unpaid on an ally at once. Heartbloom
  does it. It adds no healing; it turns slow healing into fast healing when a big hit lands.
- **S5. Weakened.** A foe that is slowed, stunned or exposed is weakened. Rootspear hits weakened
  foes harder. Rootsnare, Puffcap and Pollen Gust weaken; a boss cannot be slowed or stunned but can
  be exposed.
- **S6. Casting.** Every skill fires on the press, as today. Each uses one of her six passed cast
  moves: Point, Bloom, Plant, Sweep, Give, Channel. While the staff is planted she plays the same
  move with empty paws, and every skill still comes from her, not from the staff.
- **S7. Colours.** Heals and Moss are Bright Heal `#5FE873`. Her damage is the darker green Kesh
  picked for her bolts (head `#8CC46E`, trail `#3E8240`). Wispfall keeps Gem Flash's white-gold light.
  Nothing wears Ember or Violet. Glows stay dim.
- **S43. Seeds (new in 1.3, from Zyra).** A seed is a small glowing pod on the floor. It lies for up
  to 8 s.
  - **2.0 (Kesh, 2026-11-18): the times below are replaced.** A seed waits 5 s; a hurt ally who
    walks on it picks it up and heals 11. If nobody does, at 5 s it always becomes a Spitter (his
    "flower") for 5 s, foe near or not. Every source bursts its seed out on a low arc, never at a
    hero's feet. Skills still wake or use a seed at once.
  - A hurt ally who touches it is healed 11, and the seed is used up.
  - A seed that is at least 3 s old sprouts into a Spitter when a foe comes within 4 u of it. (1.7,
    U1, Kesh: it was 1 s. The wait gives the player time to use the seed. Every skill that wakes a
    seed still wakes it at once, whatever its age.)
  - A Spitter is a small plant that shoots the nearest foe within 4 u for 4, once a second, for 5 s.
    At most 4 Spitters stand at once; a fifth replaces the oldest.
  - Scatter Seeds (S20) throws seeds, Seed Bolt (S11) leaves one when it misses, and Rootspear (S21)
    sprouts every seed in its row at once.
- **S44. Her helpers.** Things she leaves working while she moves on: the staff turret, Mending
  Sapling, Spitters, Puffcaps, seeds, her wisps and the Firefly Swarm, and since 1.4 the Elder Oak and the Oakling, and since 1.8 the Rain Cloud and the Leaf Gale, and since 1.9 the Forest Spirit. Foes cannot hit any of them.
  Each has its own cap, written on its skill. The wisp family is every small light that flies by
  itself: Glade Wisps (heal), the relic's wisp (stores overheal), Heartbloom's bloom wisps (heal) and
  Wispfall's wisps (damage).
- **S49. The garden (new in 1.4, R36 to R38; 1.5 adds R25, R26, R39, R45, R46, R49, R50).** Her plants are
  seeds, Spitters, Mending Sapling, Puffcaps, the Oakling and, since 1.5, the planted staff. Four rules tie the skills together:
  - **Water grows.** Dew Wave and Dewfall water what they touch. A watered seed sprouts at once. A
    watered Spitter, sapling or Oakling lives 3 s longer and works 50% faster for 3 s. A watered
    Puffcap grows to double size and its cloud is 25% wider (2.75 u). Watered Moss lasts 2 s longer.
    Each plant and each Moss can be watered once.
    1.7 (U2): a seed woken by water comes up as a watered Spitter: it shoots 50% faster for its
    first 3 s and lives 8 s, not 5. That is its one watering.
    1.8: the Rain Cloud (S58) is her third water skill. It waters what it passes over.
    1.11 (A8): Creeping Moss (S59) creeps twice as fast under the Rain Cloud.
  - **Roots wake.** Rootspear and Rootsnare sprout every seed in their area at once, and Rootspear
    bursts every Puffcap in its row.
    1.7 (U6): Rootspear also bursts each seed in its row as a ring of roots before it sprouts: 12

  - **Wind carries.** If Pollen Gust crosses a waiting Puffcap or a spore cloud, the mushroom bursts

  - **The planted staff (1.5, R49).** While it stands it is a plant for watering only: Dew Wave or

### Quick heals (lean Spring)

- **S10. Swiftleaf (re-designed in 2.3; Kesh, 2026-11-18 14:28 Singapore time: "re-design, this
  skill is too simple, make it extremely fancy").** Three leaves, three curves, one meeting, and
  then one leaf for each style. The 1.11 text is in `cut/POLLEN_MARK_S64_AND_SWIFTLEAF_S10.md`.
  - She spins once and three leaves of light fly off the gem. One swings wide to the left, one
    wide to the right, one goes high and dives. All three reach the ally who needs it most
    within 5 u in the same moment, from three sides. Cooldown 2.5 s. Cast: Give. She may move as
    soon as they leave.
  - **The meeting (heal):** the three leaves cross on the ally and whirl round them as a
    pinwheel: 5 each, 15. On an ally who carries Moss, 7 each, 21 (S3).
  - Then the pinwheel springs apart, and each leaf has its own job:
    - **one flies on (heal):** to the next most hurt ally within 5 u of the first, for 7 (10 on
      Moss). It never heals her.
    - **one stays (lasts):** it circles the first ally as a sprig, Moss of 3 a second for 4 s.
    - **one cuts its way home (damage):** it flies back to her staff and hits every foe on its
      way for 6. She catches it on the staff tip.
  - **Damage on the way out:** each leaf hits the foes on its own curve for 3, and the pinwheel
    hits foes within 1.5 u of the ally for 5.
  - Combos:
    - F1, seeds ride the leaves (1.7, U5 a, kept): a leaf that flies over a seed lifts it; each
      seed adds 11 to the meeting. At most 2 seeds a cast.
    - F2, on Moss (kept): the bigger heal above.
    - F3, dew leaves (new): a leaf that crosses Dewfall, Dew Wave's wet lane or the Rain Cloud
      comes out wet, and the meeting waters the ally's Moss and every plant within 1.5 u (S49,
      once each as always).
    - F4, dusty leaves (new): a leaf that crosses the Petal Twister (S73) dusts the foes it cuts
      after that, for 3 s (S74).
  - The motion, about 1.2 s in all:
    1. Wind-up, 0.12 s: a small hop and one full turn, the staff sweeps a circle; three big
       leaves peel off the gem and hang at her left, her right and above her.
    2. Flight, 0.35 s: all three launch together. Each spins like a thrown blade and drags a
       ribbon of small leaves. The left and right ones bulge up to 1.5 u to the side; the high
       one grows, with its shadow on the floor, then dives. For half a second the three ribbons
       draw one big leaf on the floor: two edges and the midrib.

- What feeds it best: Moss ticking on a full-health ally, Glade Wisps landing on the same ally, and Dew Wave rolled out ahead of a fight.
  The gem's pour (Harvest) never overheals, so it never feeds a wisp.
- It stays a common relic offered only when Mosslight is in the party, and its heirloom unlock stays.
