## Clashes (two real ones)

- **A1. Forest Spirit gives free healing.** As written, an ally who steps into its 4 u gains 10% of
  their maximum health, and stepping out only trims health above the old maximum. Stepping in and
  out heals 10% each time, for ever.
  - a. ✔ Coming into reach raises the maximum only. Health stays as it is. Leaving trims health
    above the old maximum.
  - b. Health keeps the same share both ways (55 of 110 becomes 50 of 100). No free healing, but
    leaving costs health points.
- **A2. Leaf Gale against a barrage.** Every shot it stops heals 5 and bosses' shots count, so a boss
  that fires twenty shots at the wall heals the party 100.
  - a. As written, no limit.
  - b. ✔ Each wall makes at most 8 leaves (40 healing). It still stops every shot after that.
  - c. A boss's shots are stopped but make no leaf.

Checked and fine: Nectar Burst with the talents (it multiplies with Regrowth and Deep Roots, the one
place two healing bonuses multiply; it is flagged in the kit as the first number to watch); Rain Cloud
with the "watered once" rule (a plant Dew Wave already watered gets nothing from the cloud, which
keeps three water skills from stacking); Wild Thicket's 50% with watering (only one 50% counts);
Drowsy Spores with pushes and slows (they are not hits, so a gust moves sleepers without waking
them); Creeping Moss with Twin Buds (the second charge starts a second creep; an ally still carries
one Creeping Moss, restarted); Pollen Mark with Bark and Perennial (each heal renews them, so in a
boss fight every attacker keeps both, which is the same as standing in her garden).

## Combo ideas

For the three with none:

- **A3. ✔ Leaf Gale throws seeds (wind carries).** When the gale rises, every seed within 1.5 u of it
  is blown 3 u forward, away from her, and sprouts where it lands. A wall raised over Sower's seeds
  puts Spitters among the shooters.
- **A4. ✔ Pollen on the wind.** If Pollen Gust passes a dusted foe, it carries the pollen: every foe
  the gust pushes after that is dusted too, and the 6 s start again. One mark becomes a marked pack.
- **A5. ✔ The garden feeds on pollen.** A helper's hit on a dusted foe (a Spitter, the Oakling, the
  turret, the swarm) heals the most hurt ally 1, at most 3 a second in all. Spitters round a dusted
  boss become small healers.
- **A6. ✔ The spirit lives in the oak.** While an Elder Oak stands, every ally under its crown has the
  Forest Spirit's 10% too, wherever she is. She can leave the oak with the Tank and take the spirit
  to someone else.

For the two that are thin:

- **A7. ✔ Dream drain** (Pokémon's Dream Eater). Sapping Vine on a sleeping foe heals double (8 a tick),
  and its ticks do not count as hits, so it does not wake the foe. It is the pay-off for leaving a
  pack asleep.
- **A8. ✔ Moss loves rain.** Under the Rain Cloud, Creeping Moss creeps every 1 s, not every 2 s. Rain
  on a party standing together covers all four in 3 s.

More, not picked:

- **A9. Sticky nectar.** Foes inside Nectar Burst's splash are slowed 30% for 2 s, so they are
  weakened for Rootspear. It gives the skill a use when nobody is hurt.
- **A10. Wind carries rain.** If Pollen Gust crosses the Rain Cloud, the gust waters every plant and
  every Moss in the rest of its path. Water and wind, the two rules he likes, in one move.
- **A11. Pollinated.** A dusted foe that falls leaves a seed. (Close to Compost, which he dropped.)
- **A12. The spirit tends the garden.** Her plants within 4 u of the Forest Spirit live 2 s longer.
- **A13. Thicket under the oak.** Wild Thicket cast under the Elder Oak's crown tosses for 1.5 s, not
  1 s: the oak's roots join in.
