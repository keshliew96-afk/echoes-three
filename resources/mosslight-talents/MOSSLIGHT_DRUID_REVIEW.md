## 2. What is lacking

- **R2. Wood is almost missing.** There is no tree, no bark and no tree-folk, and the staff is not
  alive. This is the biggest gap. Answers: R31 Elder Oak, R32 Oakling, R17 the sapling grows, R20
  wooden root spears, R44 the staff takes root.
- **R3. Her heals look like water and light.** The damage half of the kit is the druid half. Of the
  nine heal skills, four show no plant, wood, root or wisp (Swiftleaf, Dew Wave, Pollen Gust,
  Dewfall). Answers: R9 to R16.
- **R4. Roots only hurt.** Both root skills are damage. No root shows on the healing side. Answers:
  the oak's roots (R31), the vine's root line (R23), the staff's roots (R44).
- **R5. No big skill.** Every skill is small or medium. She has nothing that changes the room for a
  few seconds. Answer: R31 Elder Oak.
- **R6. Nothing guards one ally.** With shields gone, her only way to get foes off a hurt ally is
  Pollen Gust. Answer: R32 Oakling, a helper that stands by that ally and knocks foes away.
- **R7. Her helpers do not work together.** She can leave up to eight things standing, but only five
  interactions exist (Moss with Swiftleaf, Moss with Heartbloom, weakened with Rootspear, seeds with
  Rootspear, Moss overheal with the relic). Answers: the garden rules, R36 to R39.
- **R8. Wisps are well covered, so I add no wisp skill.** There are five kinds of small light
  already. The risk is that they look alike. Each should keep its own shape: Glade Wisps small and
  pale green, the relic's wisp larger and circling an ally, Heartbloom's petals with a light inside,
  the Firefly Swarm many tiny dots, Wispfall white-gold and falling. The new wisp work is in the
  interactions (R31, R45, R46).

## 3. Enhance the skills she has

Looks change no numbers. A rule line names what it changes.

- **R9. Swiftleaf, look. [1.4]** A real spinning leaf with a trail of small leaves, not a leaf of
  light. When it heals, a sprig of leaves opens on the ally's shoulder for a moment.
- **R10. Dew Wave, look. [1.4]** Grass and small flowers spring up along the lane behind the wave and
  fade after 2 s.
- **R11. Dew Wave, rule. [1.4]** It waters every plant and every Moss in its lane (R36). This is what
  turns the plainest heal into the gardener's tool.
- **R12. Dewfall, look. [1.4]** The circle becomes a flower bed: grass and small flowers grow in it
  while the dew falls, with a ring of tiny mushrooms at its edge.
- **R13. Dewfall, rule. [1.4]** It waters every plant inside it and the Moss of every ally inside it
  (R36).
- **R14. Pollen Gust, look. [1.4]** The wind carries leaves, petals and yellow pollen. Allies it sped
  up trail petals while the haste lasts.
- **R15. Pollen Gust, rule. [1.4]** The wind carries spores (R38).
- **R16. Mossgrowth, look. [1.4]** Moss and small leaves creep up the ally from the feet, and a small
  white flower opens on each tick.
- **R17. Mending Sapling, look and one rule. [1.4]** It grows through three sizes in its 8 s, from
  sprout to a young tree as tall as the Tank, and its motes are blossoms. When its time ends it sheds
  its leaves: every ally within 4.5 u is healed 8. It is a plant, so it can be watered (R36).
- **R18. Mossgrowth, rule. [1.4]** Moss from this skill that runs its whole 6 s drops a seed at the
  ally's feet (the seed rule, S43). Evergreen players grow a garden by healing. This adds up to 11
  healing or one Spitter to each full cast, so the number needs a play test.
- **R19. Seed Bolt, look. [1.4]** The bolt is a seed pod with two leaf wings; it bursts into petals on
  the ally it heals.
- **R20. Rootspear, look and one rule. [1.4]** The spears are wooden roots with bark and a few leaves,
  not green light. A Puffcap in the row bursts at once (R37).
- **R21. Rootsnare, rule. [1.4]** Seeds inside the circle sprout the moment the roots break out (R37).
- **R22. Puffcap, rule. [1.4]** It is a plant: watered, it grows to double size and its cloud is 25%
  wider (2.75 u). Rootspear and Pollen Gust can burst it from far away (R37, R38).
- **R23. Sapping Vine, look and one rule. [1.4]** A thorned vine grows out of the floor at the foe's
  feet and coils up it; a root line runs along the floor from the foe to the ally it heals, and the
  heal travels along it. If the latched foe falls, the vine drops a seed where it stood.
- **R24. Firefly Swarm, Wispfall, Heartbloom, Scatter Seeds. No change.** They already read as wisp
  or plant. They join the interactions below without new rules of their own.
- **R25. Heartbloom also wakes seeds. [waits]** Its bloom sprouts every seed within 3 u of the
  flower. I left it out to keep "bloom" meaning one thing (paying out Moss).
- **R26. Wispfall's light wakes seeds. [waits]** A dive that lands within 0.9 u of a seed sprouts it.
- **R27. Swiftleaf comes back. [waits]** The leaf flies on past the ally and returns to her like a
  thrown leaf on the wind, healing a second ally on the way back for half.
- **R28. Dew Wave washes. [waits]** It also clears a slow from the allies it heals. Not a druid
  element, but she has no way to clear anything today.

## 4. Add: the wood family

Mending Sapling is the small one. These are the middle one and the big one. Both are written into
the kit as candidates so you can see them in clips before you decide the pool.

- **R31. Elder Oak (kit line S50). [1.4]** Her big skill, and the tree the kit is missing.
  - A great oak grows where she aims, up to 4 u away, in 0.6 s, and stands for 8 s. Its crown covers a
    circle of radius 3.5 u. The trunk blocks nobody.
  - Allies under the crown heal 6 a second (48 each).
  - Its roots spread across the floor under the crown: foes inside are slowed 25% (not bosses), so
    they are weakened for Rootspear.
  - Under the crown every seed sprouts at once and no plant withers: Spitters, the sapling, Puffcaps
    and the Oakling do not run out while the oak stands.
  - When its time ends, its leaves fly off as wisps: one flies to each ally under the crown and heals
    10.
  - One oak at a time. Cooldown 24 s, her longest. Cast: Plant. Group: lasting heals.
  - Nodes: Widen (crown 4.4 u), Linger (12 s), Reach (placed 5 u away), Quicken, Siphon, Detonate.
- **R32. Oakling (kit line S51). [1.4]** A knee-high tree-folk that guards the ally who needs it.
  - She wakes it from the floor up to 4 u away. It lives 10 s. Foes cannot hit it.
  - It walks, at 4 u a second, to stand beside the ally with the lowest share of health within 6 u
    of it (the other heroes first, Mosslight when she is alone).
  - Every 1.5 s it slams the foe nearest that ally within 1.6 u of itself: 9 damage, pushed 1.5 u
    away and slowed 30% for 1.5 s (a boss only takes the damage). Six slams, 54 damage.
  - One Oakling at a time. Cooldown 14 s. Cast: Plant. Group: damage; its slams count as her damage
    for Harvest.
  - It is a plant: it can be watered (R36) and it does not wither under the Elder Oak.
  - Nodes: Linger (lives 15 s), Multiply (a second Oakling guards the next most hurt ally), Widen
    (slam reach 2 u), Reach (guards within 7.5 u), Breeze (walks 40% faster), Quicken, Siphon,
    Detonate.
- **R33. Bramble Hedge. [waits]** A thorn hedge 4 u wide grows across her aim for 5 s. Foes cannot
  walk through it and take 6 a second while they touch it; heroes walk through. It is a wall, not a
  shield. I held it back because foes would need to path round a wall that appears mid-fight, which
  is new and risky machinery.
- **R34. Pool size with the two candidates (this replaces my pick on S34).**
  a) 18: keep all 16 and add both (**my pick for the review page**, then you cut after seeing clips).
  b) 17: add Elder Oak only.
  c) 16: Elder Oak and Oakling come in, Dewfall and Sapping Vine go out. Dew Wave would then be the
  only water, and the vine's Harvest job would rest on the talents.
- **R35. Models.** The oak, the Oakling, the sapling's three sizes, Spitters and Puffcaps are drawn
  with stand-in shapes in the review build. For the game each would need a model from you in Tripo,
  the Oakling with a walk and a slam. That is a cost of R31 and R32.

## 5. How the skills work together (the brainstorm)

### Three garden rules, each one sentence a player can learn

"Plants" are seeds, Spitters, Mending Sapling, Puffcaps and the Oakling.

- **R36. Water grows. [1.4]** Dew Wave and Dewfall water what they touch. A watered seed sprouts at
  once. A watered Spitter, sapling or Oakling lives 3 s longer and works 50% faster for 3 s. A watered
  Puffcap grows (R22). Watered Moss lasts 2 s longer. Each plant and each Moss can be watered once,
  so nothing lasts for ever.
- **R37. Roots wake. [1.4]** Rootspear and Rootsnare wake what sleeps in the floor: every seed in
  their area sprouts at once, and Rootspear bursts every Puffcap in its row.
- **R38. Wind carries. [1.4]** If Pollen Gust crosses a waiting Puffcap or a spore cloud, the mushroom
  bursts and the wind carries its spores: every foe the gust pushes after that takes 20% more damage
  for 4 s.
- **R39. Wind rolls seeds. [waits]** Seeds in the gust's path roll along with it and stop where it
  ends, so she can move a garden. Held back: it needs seeds that move.

### Combos these give, with no further rules

| # | Combo | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| R40 a | Scatter Seeds, then Dew Wave over them | Three Spitters at once, no 1 s wait and no foe needed, and the wave still heals the allies in the lane |
| R40 b | Scatter Seeds inside Dewfall | A flower bed that heals allies and grows three fast Spitters |
| R40 c | Mossgrowth, then Dew Wave | The Moss lasts 8 s (40 instead of 30) and the wave adds 12 |
| R40 d | Mending Sapling inside Dewfall | A blossom every 1.3 s instead of every 2 s, and 3 s more life |
| R40 e | Puffcap, then Rootspear through it | She bursts the trap from 7 u away; the cloud exposes the pack |
| R40 f | Puffcap, then Pollen Gust through it | The gust pushes the pack and leaves every foe it touched exposed |
| R40 g | Rootsnare over seeds or a Puffcap | The seeds sprout round the held pack; the drag pulls foes onto the mushroom |
| R40 h | Rootsnare, Puffcap, then Rootspear | Slowed and exposed: 28 plus 30% plus 20% is 44 to every foe in the row |
| R40 i | Pollen Gust into a garden | The push drops foes inside Spitter range, a Rootsnare or a Puffcap |
| R40 j | Elder Oak over a garden | Every seed sprouts and nothing withers for 8 s; foes under it are weakened for Rootspear |
| R40 k | Oakling beside the hurt ally, Dew Wave through both | The ally is healed and the Oakling slams once a second for 3 s |
| R40 l | Mossgrowth on a full-health ally | The overheal fills the relic's wisp, and the full run drops a seed |
| R40 m | Sapping Vine on a foe in the pack | Its heals run along the root line, and its seed lands in the pack when the foe falls |
| R40 n | Heartbloom on the ally the Oakling guards | The Oakling keeps the foes off while the bud grows, and the bloom heals everyone beside them |

### Three garden play styles the rules open, one per talent path

- **R41. Spring: the waterer.** Dew Wave on a 2.8 s cooldown (Quick Sprout and Spring Rain) waters
  seeds, Spitters and the sapling as fast as she can plant them.
- **R42. Evergreen: the grower.** Mossgrowth drops seeds, Dewfall and the Elder Oak keep everything
  alive, and Heartbloom pays the Moss out when a big hit lands.
- **R43. Harvest: the trapper.** Puffcap, Rootsnare, Rootspear, the Spitters, the Oakling and the
  staff turret all feed the gem with their damage while she walks among her allies.

## 6. Ideas for your own designs (none is written in)

- **R44. The planted staff takes root. [waits]** When hit 3 plants the staff, short roots spread from
  its foot and two leaves open under the gem; they pull back when she takes it up. Look only: the
  turret's numbers stay yours. This is the cheapest way to make her wood alive.
- **R45. An unused Glade wisp becomes a seed. [waits]** Today a wisp that waits 10 s fades. Instead it
  sinks into the floor where she stands as a seed (S43). Nothing is wasted, and wisps and plants meet.
- **R46. The relic's wisp becomes a seed. [waits]** A relic wisp that fades after its 8 s drops a seed
  at its ally's feet, not the stored heal.
- **R47. Thorn bolts. [waits]** Her basic bolts and the turret's bolts are drawn as thorns in the same
  dark green. Look only.
- **R48. The dash leaves flowers. [waits]** A short trail of small flowers opens along the dash and
  fades after 1 s. Look only.
- **R49. The planted staff is a plant. [waits]** It can be watered like one: Dew Wave or Dewfall on
  it makes the turret fire 50% faster for 3 s. This changes your turret, so it is last on my list.

## 7. Nodes

- **R50. A seventh druid node, Sprout (rare). [waits, with S39]** The skill leaves one seed where it
  lands: at the feet of the first ally it heals, or under the first foe it hits. It would let any
  skill of hers plant, which is the simplest way to put more plants on screen. It joins the six druid
  nodes of S39 if you pick S39 b.
- **R51. Breeze on the Oakling. [1.4]** Your speed node also fits the Oakling's walk.

## 8. What I am unsure of

- **R52.** All numbers are first guesses. The three I trust least: Mossgrowth's seed (R18), which adds
  healing to a strong skill; the watered Spitters (R36), which with Scatter Seeds and Dew Wave could
  out-damage her staff; and the Elder Oak's 24 s cooldown, since no skill of hers is that long today.
  Wood brown for the oak, the Oakling and the root spears is not one of the reserved colours, but the
  Thornling foes and the Thorned elite power already wear bramble and bark brown, so her plants need
  a shape and a tone that never read as a foe. That is a look to judge on your laptop. Bright Heal
  stays on heals only; leaves use her dark green.
