## The audit

What each skill does with a seed today:

| Skill | With a seed |
|---|---|
| Sower, Scatter Seeds, Seed Bolt, Mossgrowth, Glade Wisps, Sapping Vine, Mending Sapling | makes seeds |
| Dew Wave, Dewfall | water: the seed sprouts into a Spitter at once |
| Rootspear, Rootsnare | roots wake: the seed sprouts into a Spitter at once |
| Heartbloom, Wispfall | light wakes: the seed sprouts into a Spitter at once |
| Elder Oak | the seed sprouts into a Spitter at once, and plants do not wither |
| Pollen Gust | rolls the seed to where the gust ends |
| Oakling | the seed grows into another Oakling (Kesh's rule) |
| Swiftleaf, Firefly Swarm, Puffcap, Bramble Hedge | nothing |

**Finding 1. Seven skills "use" a seed, but all seven do the same thing: sprout it into a Spitter
now.** A seed already does that by itself after 1 s when a foe is within 4 u. So in a fight those
seven rules change almost nothing. Only two uses are really different: the Oakling's (the seed
becomes something else) and the gust's (the seed goes somewhere else). Those are the two that feel
good, and the Oakling's is Kesh's own.

**Finding 2. There is no time to use a seed.** With Sower, seeds now come for free, but near foes each
one turns into a Spitter after 1 s. The player has seeds for one second and Spitters after that, so
an Oakling cast almost never finds a seed to grow.

**Finding 3. A seed has one outcome.** More use of seeds means more things a seed can become, each
said in one sentence, not more ways to wake it.

## The picks

Each is one sentence a player can learn. My picks are marked.

- **U1. Give the player time: a seed waits 3 s before it sprouts by itself (now 1 s).** (**my pick**)
  Everything that wakes a seed still wakes it at once. This is what makes every line below, and his
  Oakling rule, usable. Cost: a seed left alone starts shooting 2 s later.
  Other way, b): keep 1 s, and let the Oakling and the lines below take Spitters as well as seeds.
- **U2. Water makes it strong.** (**my pick**; builds on the water rule he likes) A seed woken by Dew
  Wave or Dewfall comes up already watered: its Spitter shoots 50% faster for 3 s and lives 8 s, not 5.
  Today water on a seed only saves the 1 s wait.
- **U3. Wind spreads seeds.** (**my pick**; builds on the gust he likes) Every seed Pollen Gust carries
  lands as two, 0.8 u apart, where the gust ends. At most 6 seeds land from one gust. Like a
  dandelion: the wind does not only move the garden, it makes it bigger.
- **U4. Every plant skill copies itself onto seeds**, the way his Oakling does:
  - a. **Puffcap** (**my pick**): every seed within 3 u of where the mushroom is planted grows into a
    Puffcap too. Up to 4 may wait when they come from seeds.
  - b. **Bramble Hedge**: each seed within 1.5 u of the hedge's line makes the hedge 1 u wider, up to
    8 u.
  - c. **Mending Sapling**: each seed within 3 u is drawn into the sapling: one more blossom of 8 and
    2 s more life for each, up to 3 seeds.
- **U5. Heals pick seeds up:**
  - a. **Swiftleaf** (**my pick**): the leaf picks up every seed within 1 u of its flight, out and back,
    and adds each seed's 11 to the heal it carries, up to 2 seeds. Seeds that landed near foes become
    healing for the ally who needs it.
  - b. **Heartbloom**: seeds within 3 u of the bloom open as flowers, not Spitters: each heals every
    ally within 2 u of it for 8. This replaces "the bloom sprouts seeds" for this skill.
  - c. **Mossgrowth**: a seed within 1.5 u of the ally is used up and the Moss lasts 3 s longer.
- **U6. Roots burst seeds.** (**my pick**) Each seed in Rootspear's row bursts as a ring of roots: 12
  damage to foes within 1.5 u and a 30% slow for 2 s, and it still sprouts. Today the row only saves
  the wait.
- **U7. Firefly Swarm eats seeds.** A seed within 2 u of one of its stops is eaten and the swarm makes
  one more stop, up to 2 more.
- **U8. Puffcap spores on seeds.** A seed inside a spore cloud sprouts as a Spitter whose shots also
  make the foe take 20% more damage for 2 s. Skip this if U4 a is picked; one Puffcap rule is enough.
- **U9. Leave as they are:** Elder Oak (it already keeps the whole garden), Wispfall, Rootsnare, Dewfall
  beyond U2, Glade Wisps, Sapping Vine, Scatter Seeds, Seed Bolt, the basic attack and the dash.

## What I would build first

U1, U2, U3, U4 a, U5 a and U6. After them a seed can become six different things, each tied to one
kind of skill: water makes a strong Spitter, wind makes two seeds, an Oakling or a Puffcap makes a
copy, a leaf turns it into healing, roots make it burst. The rest can wait until those are seen on
the page.
