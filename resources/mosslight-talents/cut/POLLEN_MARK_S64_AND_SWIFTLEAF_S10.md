# Cut and replaced in kit 2.3: Pollen Mark (S64) and the first Swiftleaf (S10)

Kesh, 2026-11-18 14:28 Singapore time, in the page thread: "Pollen Mark: drop this and re-design a new one
with wind element, need same apply dusted" and "Swiftleaf: re-design, this skill is too simple, make
it extremely fancy". The text below is kit 1.11 as passed, kept for the record. Their kit 2.0 parts
(talent doc 3.5): Swiftleaf, a sprig and a razor leaf of 8 out and back; Pollen Mark, a pollen burst
of 12 in 2 u when the dust ends or the foe falls. The dusted status itself stays (kit S74).

- **S10. Swiftleaf.** A leaf of light crosses to the ally who needs it most within 3.2 u and heals 14.
  On an ally who carries Moss it heals 21. Cooldown 2.5 s. Cast: Give. Replaces Swift Mend.
  1.4 look (R9): a real spinning leaf with a trail of small leaves; a sprig of leaves opens on the
  ally's shoulder when it heals.
  1.5 (R27): the leaf comes back. After its heal it flies back to her, and on the way it heals the most
  hurt other ally within 1.5 u of its path for half (7, or 10 on an ally who carries Moss). It never
  heals her. With Bounce the leaf makes its hops first and then flies home.
  1.7 (U5 a): the leaf picks seeds up. Every seed within 1 u of its flight is used up and adds 11 to
  the heal the leaf is carrying: on the way out to the first heal, on the way back to the returning
  heal. At most 2 seeds a cast. The Moss bonus does not raise the 11. On the way back it takes a seed
  only if it has an ally to heal.

- **S64. Pollen Mark (new in 1.8; idea 7, from the Elder Scrolls Online warden's Living Vines).** The
  party heals itself by fighting.
  - Foes within 2.2 u of her aim point, up to 5 u away, are dusted with pollen for 6 s. Bosses can
    be dusted.
  - Every hit a hero lands on a dusted foe heals that hero 3, at most twice a second for each hero.
    Mosslight's own hits count for her.
  - 1.11 (A5), the garden feeds on pollen: a hit on a dusted foe by a Spitter, the Oakling or the
    staff turret heals the ally with the lowest share of health within 6 u of that foe for 1, at
    most 3 a second in all. (In 1.9 helpers' hits healed nobody.)
  - 1.11 (A4): Pollen Gust carries the pollen from a dusted foe to every foe it pushes after, with
    a fresh 6 s for all of them (S14).
  - 1.11 (A11), pollinated: a dusted foe that falls leaves a seed (S43) where it fell, at most one
    seed every 1.5 s, one limit for the whole room, as built on the page. (Close to Compost, S60, which Kesh cut. I flagged that, and he kept it: "Ok accept
    combo 11", 13:04 Singapore time, and "Compost drop is because it cost a skill slot". This
    combo costs no slot. It only works while the mark lasts.)
  - It deals no damage. Like Sapping Vine, it sits with the damage skills because it pays for
    fighting; its heals are instant and are not a heal skill for the talents.
  - Cooldown 12 s. Cast: Sweep.
  - Why: in a boss fight her allies are spread out and always attacking. This heals them where they
    stand.
  - Nodes: Widen (2.75 u), Reach (6.25 u), Linger (9 s), Quicken, Siphon.
