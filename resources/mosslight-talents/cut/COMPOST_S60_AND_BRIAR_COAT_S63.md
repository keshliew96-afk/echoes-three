# Compost (S60) and Briar Coat (S63): cut by Kesh, 2026-11-17

Kept for the record. Kesh's reason for Compost, 13:04 Singapore time the same day: "Compost drop is because it cost a skill slot". Its idea lives on as combo A11 on Pollen Mark (kit S64, S68), which costs no slot. Both were in kit 1.8 for about twenty minutes. Kesh, 2026-11-17 11:55 Singapore
time: "1 b" (drop Briar Coat for a pool of 26) and "3 drop compost, add 16" (Forest Spirit took its place).

- **S60. Compost (passive; new in 1.8; idea 4, from the Diablo 2 druid's Carrion Vine and Risk of Rain
  2 REX's DIRECTIVE: Harvest).** Her second passive. It takes a slot and has no press.
  - A foe that falls within 6 u of her leaves a seed (S43) where it fell, whoever felled it.
  - At most one seed every 1.5 s.
  - The seeds are ordinary seeds, so every seed use of S56 works on them.
  - In a boss arena there is one foe, so as written it gives nothing there (S66 iii offers a fix).
  - Why: the more the party kills, the more garden she has, with no press. With Sower she is never
    short of seeds.
  - Nodes, by type as on Glade Wisps: Reach (8 u), Quicken (one every 1.2 s), Multiply (two seeds,
    0.8 u apart), Linger (its seeds lie 12 s). No other node is offered on it.
- **S63. Briar Coat (new in 1.8; idea 6, from the WoW druid's Thorns and the Diablo 2 druid's Spirit of
  Barbs).** Briars that punish foes for hitting an ally.
  - Briars grow on the ally with the most foes within 2 u of them, among allies within 6 u of her
    (the other heroes first, Mosslight when she is alone). They last 6 s.
  - Each foe that hits that ally takes 8 and is slowed 20% for 1.5 s, at most once every 0.5 s for
    each foe. A boss takes the damage and is not slowed. This is her damage.
  - The hit lands in full. Nothing is absorbed or reduced, so it is not a shield.
  - One coat on each ally. Cooldown 10 s. Cast: Give.
  - Why: with shields gone, this is how she helps the ally who is being hit, and it feeds Harvest
    without aiming at a foe.
  - Nodes: Linger (9 s), Multiply (a second ally), Reach (7.5 u), Bounce (the thorn hops to another
    foe within 2.2 u), Quicken, Siphon, Detonate.
