- **S53. Bramble Hedge (new in 1.5, R33; a 19th skill for Kesh to judge).** A wall of living hedge.
  - A hedge grows across her aim, 4 u wide and 0.6 u thick, its middle up to 4 u away, in 0.3 s. It
    stands 5 s.
  - Foes that walk cannot pass through it. Heroes, helpers and every shot pass freely, both ways.
  - A foe touching it takes 6 a second. This is her damage.
  - A boss is not stopped: it tramples the hedge, takes one tick of 6, and the hedge ends.
  - A foe that flies crosses it and takes the damage while over it.
  - One hedge at a time. Cooldown 14 s. Cast: Plant. Group: damage.
  - It is a plant (S49): watered, it stands 3 s longer and hurts 50% more (9 a second) for 3 s; it
    does not wither under the Elder Oak.
  - It is a wall, not a shield: it absorbs nothing and no ally is protected except by where foes can
    walk.
  - 1.7 (U4 b): when it grows, each seed within 1.5 u of its line is used up and makes the hedge 1 u
    wider, half at each end. Up to 4 seeds, so 8 u at most (9 u with Widen).
  - Look: thick leaves in her dark green over wood-brown stems, pale blossoms, small thorns half
    hidden, a crystal bud at each end. Nothing like the Thornling.
  - Why: the kit had no way to close a doorway or split a pack. With Rootsnare, Puffcap and the
    Spitters behind it, it makes a defended garden.
  - Nodes: Widen (5 u wide), Linger (7.5 s), Reach (placed 5 u away), Multiply (a second hedge 1.5 u
    behind the first), Quicken, Siphon, Detonate.
  - New machinery, and the risk: foes must path round a wall that appears mid-fight. On the review
    page the dummies simply stop at it. In the game this needs the foe movement to treat it as a
    blocker for its 5 s, which is the costliest line of 1.5.
