# Cut by Kesh, 2026-11-18 11:30 Singapore time

"Forest Spirit: remove this and re-design one. Sapping Vine: remove this and re-design one."
The kit 1.11 text of both, with their 2.0 parts (talent doc 3.3: L15 and L22).

- **S67. Forest Spirit (passive; new in 1.9; idea 16, Kesh's own pick; from the Diablo 2 druid's Oak
  Sage).** Her second passive. It takes a slot and has no press.
  - A small oak spirit floats beside her, about 1 u behind her shoulder.
  - Every ally within 4 u of the spirit, Mosslight included, has 10% more maximum health (Tank +15,
    Mosslight +10, Swordsman +9.5, Archer +8 on today's numbers).
  - An ally who comes into reach gains the extra maximum only: their health stays as it is. An ally
    who leaves loses the extra maximum; health above the old maximum is trimmed, and leaving can
    never down anyone. (1.10, Kesh's pick A1 a: in 1.9 entering also gave the health, so stepping in
    and out healed 10% each time for nothing.)
  - Foes cannot hit the spirit. It fades when she is downed and comes back when she stands.
  - 1.11 (A6): while an Elder Oak (S50) stands, allies under its crown have the 10% too, wherever
    the spirit is. She can leave the Tank under the oak and take the spirit to someone else.
  - 1.11 (A12), the spirit tends the garden: a Spitter, a Mending Sapling, a Puffcap or an Oakling
    that grows within 4 u of the spirit lives 2 s longer. It is set once, when the plant grows, and
    adds to a watering. Not the Elder Oak and not the planted staff, whose times Kesh set.
  - It is not a shield: it absorbs nothing, and the health it gives is ordinary health that heals
    and overheals as usual.
  - Look: an acorn-sized spirit of pale green light with two oak leaves, larger than a Glade wisp
    and wood brown at its heart, so it reads apart from her healing wisps. A thin leaf ring on the
    floor shows its 4 u only while an ally is near its edge.
  - Why: a second passive, the party's one lasting buff, and a reason to fight near her. Stonehide,
    whose kit is built round maximum health, gains the most.
  - Nodes, by type as on Glade Wisps: Widen (5 u), Linger (an ally keeps it 3 s after leaving). No
    other node is offered on it.

- **S25. Sapping Vine.** A vine latches onto the foe nearest her aim within 4 u for 4 s. It deals 8 a
  second, and each tick heals the ally with the lowest share of health within 3 u of her for 4.
  It ends when the foe falls. Bosses can be latched. Cooldown 8 s. Cast: Point. New: damage over
  time on one foe. This is the Harvest idea as a skill, for a player on another path.
  1.4 (R23): a thorned vine grows out of the floor at the foe's feet and coils up it; a root line
  runs along the floor from the foe to the ally it heals, and the heal travels along it. If the
  latched foe falls, the vine drops a seed (S43) where it stood.
  1.11 (A7), dream drain (from Pokémon's Dream Eater): while the latched foe is asleep (Drowsy
  Spores, S65), each tick heals 8, not 4, and the vine's ticks are not hits, so the vine never wakes
  it. When the foe wakes, the heal is 4 again. A boss does not sleep, so nothing changes on a boss.

2.0 parts that went with them:

- Forest Spirit (L15): every 3 s the spirit heals the most hurt ally within 4 u for 4; allies keep
  the 10% for 2 s after leaving; the spirit throws an acorn every 2 s at the nearest foe, 6.
- Sapping Vine (L22): the latched foe is bound, 30% slow.

Combos that went: A6 (the Elder Oak carries the spirit's 10%), A12 (the spirit tends the garden),
A7 (Drowsy Spores, then Sapping Vine).


# Rootweb, cut by Kesh 2026-11-18 12:05 Singapore time ("no Rootweb, re-design one to replace it")

- **S70. Rootweb 根脉 (passive; new in 2.1, in the Forest Spirit's place; Kesh, 2026-11-18: "remove
  this and re-design one").** Her second passive. It takes a slot and has no press.
  - Roots run under the floor between her plants. Each plant links to the nearest other plant
    within 6 u by a glowing root line, 0.6 u wide. Plants: the planted staff, Spitters, the
    Mending Sapling, waiting Puffcaps, the Oakling, the Elder Oak, the Snapjaw. One line per
    plant; two plants that pick each other share one.
  - **Heal:** an ally standing on a line heals 3 a second.
  - **Lasts:** a foe that crosses a line is tripped: 30% slow for 2 s. A plant that gets linked
    lives 2 s longer, once (not the planted staff, whose 5 s is Kesh's number).
  - **Damage:** a foe that crosses a line takes 6, at most once a second for each foe.
  - Combos: water runs through the web (W1): watering one plant waters every plant linked to it
    in a chain, once each as always. The Elder Oak links to every plant under its crown (W2). The
    Oakling's line moves with it, a walking trip wire (W3).
  - It works with no other skill: the planted staff and Sower's Spitters link.
  - Foes cannot hit the lines. They fade when she is downed.
  - Look: a thin root ridge on the floor with a slow Bright Heal pulse running along it; a small
    burst of splinters where a foe trips.
  - Why: the kit pays for a garden with single plants; this pays for where she puts them.
  - Nodes: Reach (links 7.5 u), Widen (lines 0.9 u), Linger (linked plants live 3 s longer).
