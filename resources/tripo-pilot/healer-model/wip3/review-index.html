Eighth pass: the gem attacks on its own. On combo hit 3 she thrusts the fork forward and the gem drifts out gently, about 1.7 u ahead. It floats there for one second and then blinks back into the fork. From the moment it leaves it shoots a bolt forward about three times a second by itself. The gem runs on its own clock, so she is back to rest half a second after the release and can cast while it is out: press Staff combo, switch it off, then press any cast. For one second after the gem leaves, hit 1 cannot start: Tap attack and Staff combo grey out for that second. After that, starting hit 1 calls the gem back at once, before its float is over. The looping Staff combo starts hit 1 again 1.05 s after the gem leaves, so in a loop the gem always comes back early; to see the full one-second float, switch the combo off after hit 3. Casts made while the gem is away come from the empty fork.

Seventh pass: only the gem flies. On combo hit 3 the staff now stays in her hand. She thrusts the fork forward and the gem alone leaves it, flies out along her facing, hangs for a moment, and comes home into the fork. The mending motes and the soft catch are unchanged. The notes below that speak of the staff floating out describe the version before this one.

Sixth pass: made to fit a healer. Combo: hit 1 is now a sharp downward flick with a half step, not a heavy chop; the bolt does the work. Hit 3 is no longer a thrown blade: she pushes the staff out of an open hand, it floats away upright in its own light, and on the way back it sheds mending motes; the catch is soft and mends her. Freezes are shorter and the combo no longer shakes the camera. New cast, Give: she gathers light at her chest and offers it with an open hand; a light crosses to the ally ring in front of her and a shield shell and motes rise there. New cast, Channel: the staff comes upright in both hands, her head bows, and she holds it while a shell and slow motes surround her. Dew fan (was Sweep): no swing; she carries the gem slowly from her left to her right and dew falls from it. Effects: Point rings like a small bell and sends one bolt; Bloom lifts motes around her; Plant sends roots out across the floor. All effects are dim on purpose and switch off with Hit feel.

Fifth pass: your combo and your plant cast. Combo: hit 1 hauls the staff up over her shoulder and brings it down heavily with a step in, and a bolt leaves the gem as it comes level (0.24 s); the head carries on down to her shins. Hit 2 starts from there: she drives up off her front leg and whips the staff back up, shooting a second bolt (0.56 s); the swing carries it over her shoulder. Hit 3 starts from there: she sinks onto her back leg, points her free arm at the target, and throws (0.90 s). The staff turns flat, spins out along her aim line, comes back on the other side and is caught at 1.55 s, and the catch knocks her arm and chest back. Plant cast, from your sketch, with both hands gripping the staff from start to finish (her arms are now solved from the staff's path, so both hands stay on it): a small dip, both hands lift the staff high over her head on her toes, a stretch at the top, then she drives it straight down into the floor in front of her with both arms pushed forward and her feet wide (0.42 s), rebounds and leans on it. This replaces the combo and the plant cast described in the notes below.

Fourth pass: the four casts are rebuilt to the combo's level. The audit found the same faults in all four: one wind-up key and one release key, a frozen hold, no step or weight change under them, and the staff not aimed where the cast goes. Now: Point draws the staff back to her hip and thrusts it level at the target with a step in, and the tip kicks up on release (0.22 s). Bloom gathers the staff to her chest in a deep crouch, then she springs off the floor and thrusts it overhead with her free arm flung wide, and lands (0.32 s). Plant lifts the staff upright on her toes and drives its foot into the floor as she drops to a knee, then rebounds (0.26 s). Sweep winds low to her left and cuts a level arc to her right with a step through, finishing high (0.30 s). Each has its own hit feel: a small ring at the tip for Point, a wide ring around her for Bloom, a ring and shake at the staff's foot for Plant. The casts have no staff arc. Wave, Staff throw, Hurt, Dash and the downed moves were not redesigned in this pass.

Third pass: the 3-hit combo is redesigned. Hit 1 is a level sweep from her right to her left as she steps in. She does not return to standing: the wrap of hit 1 is the wind-up of hit 2, a rising backhand that carries the staff up over her shoulder. That in turn is the wind-up of hit 3, a leap and an overhead slam into the floor. Contacts land at 0.18, 0.52 and 0.88 s. With Hit feel on, the first two hits freeze briefly and the slam freezes longer, shakes the camera and throws a ring on the floor, and the staff head leaves a short arc so you can read each swing. The arc shows only during the 3-hit combo.

Second pass, same day. Her tail no longer curls to her left: it trails straight behind her in a soft S and sways both ways (use the Top view). Every move is bigger and has more going on. Walk and run have more hip sway, bounce, arm and wrist swing and ear flop. Idle shifts her weight, looks around and flicks her ears harder. The combo, casts, hurt and dash reach 12 to 35 percent further, and in the blows her hips turn first, her legs brace, her weight drops and her head stays on the target. The count of hard frames went from 72 to about 114 because the moves are larger and faster.

Also new: the arm, wrist and head now follow the chest a few hundredths of a second late instead of all moving at once, and a blow carries a little past its target before it settles. The 72 hard frames that remain are the blows landing and the dash launching.

What works

  • The model keeps the look of your sheet, and her face held up after trimming 1,900 triangles.
  • Ears, tail, arms, legs and head all bend cleanly. The staff is its own piece, so she can throw it.
  • The file loads straight into Three.js.

What is rough

  • The blows in the combo, the casts and the dash still start and stop hard on purpose, so a hit reads as a hit. Say if you want them softer.
  • The robe, cape and back cloak are one stiff piece. They don't sway or flap yet.
  • Her fingers don't close round the staff. It rests against her palm.
  • She has no ink outline and no flat game colours, so she won't match the other heroes yet.
  • Combining is a first test. While she walks, the upper-body move replaces her arm swing, and the hop on the third combo hit is dropped.
  • These moves play in place. In the game each one must follow the sim's timing. The four casts are stand-ins for her 19 skills, with no effects yet.
