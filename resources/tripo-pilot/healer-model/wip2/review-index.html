What works

  • The model keeps the look of your sheet, and her face held up after trimming 1,900 triangles.
  • Ears, tail, arms, legs and head all bend cleanly. The staff is its own piece, so she can throw it.
  • The file loads straight into Three.js.

What is rough

  • The moves are a quick first pass. The combo and dash need timing work before they match the game's feel.
  • The robe, cape and back cloak are one stiff piece. They don't sway or flap yet.
  • Her fingers don't close round the staff. It rests against her palm.
  • She has no ink outline and no flat game colours, so she won't match the other heroes yet.
  • Combining is a first test. While she walks, the upper-body move replaces her arm swing, and the hop on the third combo hit is dropped.
  • These moves play in place. In the game each one must follow the sim's timing. The four casts are stand-ins for her 19 skills, with no effects yet.
