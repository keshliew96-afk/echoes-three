Her moves

The rule for all of it: water, not steel. Nothing snaps: each move swells, breaks and washes back. Her weight sits low over her short legs, her trunk rolls into every swing, and the tail follows a beat late like a rudder.

Idle. She stands easy with the staff upright beside her, sways a little as if on a slow current, looks about, and the tail tip stirs on the floor.

Walk and run. The walk is a rolling waddle with the staff carried upright. In the run she leans in and levels the staff forward, and the tail streams behind.

Basic attack: the held stream. While attack is held she levels the staff, drops low behind it with her right shoulder forward and her free paw steadying it, and the water runs from the shell in one unbroken stream (2.6 m on this page; the game sets the range). She shivers a little with the recoil and her tail sways. A soft ball of water at the shell swells as the 5 seconds fill, so you can read how close the tsunami is. Over Walk or Run her legs keep going and only her upper body holds the stream. When she lets go, the tail of the stream flies on and the count empties.

The tsunami, my proposal for the motion: the pull and the heave (1.6 s, the wave leaves at 0.68 s). She cuts the stream and hauls the staff back over her right shoulder as if dragging the whole stream back to her, and the water balls up over the shell. She rises on her toes with the staff high and her tail lifted, hangs a beat, then heaves the staff over and down with her whole weight: her tail slaps the floor, her free paw shoves forward, and a low wave with a pale curling crest stands up in front of her and rolls 3 m straight ahead. It keeps one fixed width, about 1.3 m as in your two red lines, and does not fan out. If she is still holding, the stream starts again from zero.

Two kinds of cast. Not streaming: the full staff motions below. While streaming: the staff stays levelled and the stream keeps flowing, and only her empty left paw casts. Torrent: the paw is drawn up by her cheek and thrown forward. Call the water: the paw is flung up and out and stirs once. Whirlpool: the paw is lifted and slapped down ahead of her as she dips. Crashing wave: the paw is flung wide and swept across her front. The water of each cast leaves from the paw, a little smaller. The 5 seconds keep counting through a left-paw cast, because the stream is still flowing. Two things are still my guesses: the count empties when she lets go, and a started tsunami always finishes.

Dash: she rides a tide (13 November). She throws the staff up (0.09 s) and heaves the shell down ahead of her; a tide heaves up under her feet and lifts her (0.10 to 0.22 s). It is tsunami height: 0.95 u where she stands and 1.17 u at its curling crest, against 0.68 u for a practice post, so it rolls over the posts with her on top. Seen from the side it is a breaking wave: a long back slope, the flat top she rides, a crest that curls over a hollow face, and foam at its foot. She rides it side-on like a surfer for half a second, knees bent, the shell leading the wave, her free arm and her tail out behind for balance, leaning once each way. The tide breaks at 0.66 s and sinks under her; she hops off its face and lands low at 0.80 s. The ride covers 2.8 u. The water it leaves on the floor is 1 u wide, stays 3 s and slows any foe standing in it; on this page two practice posts sway slowly and turn blue while they stand in it. She cannot be hit until the tide breaks. This is new compared with the game today and needs your go and the freeze lifted before it enters it.

Hurt and wave, redone for her (13 November). Hurt: the blow rocks her back and round, away from the staff; her free paw flies up, her back foot catches her, the tail whips up and slaps down, and she sways forward again like water coming back. Wave: she goes up on her toes and waves the free paw at shoulder height, bouncing with it; her head tips from side to side, her hips sway, her tail wags, and she ends on a small nod.

Casts. Torrent (0.44 s): she winds the staff back and pushes the shell straight out, and a stream leaves it; this covers riverbolt, torrent and undertow. Call the water (0.60 s): the staff goes up high and stirs a circle overhead, and rain comes down around her; for rain squall, bubble ward and tidepool. Whirlpool (0.40 s): she plants the butt on the floor ahead and a pool stays there for a moment; for whirlpool and maelstrom. Crashing wave (0.46 s): a wide level sweep that sends a wave rolling out ahead; for breaker and crashing wave. Ripple step: she throws herself forward on her belly and slides, the staff held back along her side.

Effects. Deep Cobalt for the water and Sea Foam for its crest, the Tidecaller's two colours in the game, and River Pebble for the dust. The stream is two thin crossed ribbons with a pale core and a few drops where it ends; the tsunami is a see-through wave with a pale crest and a little foam left on the floor behind it. All of it is dim and switches off with Hit feel. The stream does not freeze or shake the camera; the tsunami, the whirlpool and the crashing wave do.

What works

  • The staff stays in her paw and clear of her head, belly, satchel and tail through every move.
  • The four casts and the ripple step cover her 11 skills in the game.
  • The tail drags behind every turn, lifts when she sinks so it never goes through the floor, and slaps down as the tsunami leaves.
  • Her feet stay on the floor when she drops her weight.

What is rough

  • Her arms are very short (about 19 cm of reach on a body 40 cm wide), so the staff cannot swing far across her front or behind her. The sweeps come mostly from turning her trunk.
  • Only her right paw holds the staff. Her left paw cannot reach it across her belly, so there are no two-handed moves.
  • She has no knees, so sinking low is a lean and a drop of the hips. Her fingers don't close round the staff, and the satchel and sash are stiff.
  • Her feet can slide while she walks, the moves play in place (the ripple step does not carry her forward), and she has no ink outline or flat game colours yet.
  • The page has no enemies, so the stream runs to a fixed range and the knock-back is not shown. Damage per second, range, the wave's size and the knock-back are yours to set, and all of this is a new mechanic that needs your go before it enters the game.
  • The Torrent cast now looks much like the basic stream (a stronger push of the same water). Say if it should become something else.
  • Her left arm is about 19 cm long, so the left-paw casts are small gestures; her head, chest and hips move with them so they read.
  • I only saw the stream, the wave and the left-paw casts as stills.
  • The downed moves use the other heroes' timing with her tail added; they were not designed for her from scratch.
  • The tide is one shaped sheet of water with painted foam, not a simulated wave, and the slow is shown only by the posts swaying slowly. How much it slows (I would start at 30%) and how long the water stays (3 s here) are first numbers for you to change.
