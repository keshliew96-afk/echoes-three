# Hero turnaround prompts for Tripo

All seven prompts share one style block, so the set matches the mouse sheet. Paste the style block, then one hero's line.

Each sheet gives three views: front, the character's right side (facing to the viewer's right), and back. Crop each view into its own square image with plain background around it, as in `healer-views/`. Put Front in Front, the side view in **Right**, and Back in Back. Leave Left empty.

The heroes stand in an A-pose with empty paws, because rigging works much better that way. Each weapon gets its own small prompt and becomes its own model. The game already treats the weapon as a separate part in the paw.

## Style block (start of every prompt)

> Character turnaround sheet for a 3D model, three views of the same character side by side: front view, right side view facing right, back view. Full body, standing in a relaxed A-pose, arms held slightly out from the body, paws open and empty, feet slightly apart, looking straight ahead, calm friendly face. Cute chibi storybook animal adventurer, head about one third of the body height. Soft cel shading, clean dark brown outlines, muted warm colours. Same size and height in every view, orthographic, no perspective. Plain light grey background, flat even light, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no glow or effects, nothing in front of or behind the body.

## Heroes

**1. Healer (mouse), redo in A-pose.** Use this one if the first Tripo try from the current sheet comes out messy.
> The character: a small grey-brown mouse with very large round ears lined in soft pink, big dark eyes, a pink nose, and a long thin pink tail curving out behind. She wears a loose tan linen robe to the shins with terracotta trim, a short terracotta capelet over the shoulders, and the hood DOWN behind the head in every view. A rope belt carries two small leather pouches. Her feet are wrapped in pale bandages.

> Weapon, separate prompt: a gnarled wooden walking staff, taller than the mouse, topped with a pale green crystal held in a small pewter claw setting. Front, side and back views, standing upright, plain light grey background, same style.

**2. Tank (badger)**
> The character: a stout badger, the widest and lowest of the party, with grey-brown fur and a bold black-and-white stripe from nose to crown, small round ears and a short snout. He wears a long warm-grey bell-shaped tabard of scale mail to the knees, a plated belt, and a stack of three ridged steel plates over the right shoulder with three short steel spikes on top. Sturdy short legs, broad paws.

> Weapon, separate prompt: a big rectangular steel slab shield, taller than it is wide, riveted edges and a round boss in the middle, a little dented and worn. Front, side and back views.

**3. Swordsman (fox)**
> The character: a slim fox leaning slightly forward, with russet fur, a long sharp snout, big pointed ears with dark tips, a cream chest, and a big bushy tail with a white tip that sweeps back and up. He wears a plum-wine tunic over a short skirt of ridged plates, a leather baldric across the chest with pewter buckles, a three-plate pauldron on the right shoulder, and a short scarf with notched tails.

> Weapon, separate prompt: a straight one-handed sword with a simple crossguard, a wrapped grip and a dark groove down each face of the blade. Front, side and back views.

**4. Archer (hare)**
> The character: a tall thin hare, the tallest of the party, with very long upright ears, a white blaze down the face, light brown fur and a round white puff tail. She wears an olive-green tunic with a bone-white bib laced down the front with leather cord, a mantle of layered feathers round the shoulders, and a quiver of five fletched arrows on her back.

> Weapon, separate prompt: a wooden recurve bow with a leather-wrapped grip and small horn tips on both ends, strung. Front, side and back views.

**5. Tidecaller (otter)**
> The character: a river otter with a long low body, sleek brown fur, a cream throat and muzzle, small low round ears, a ruff of fur at the throat, and a thick tapering tail resting on the ground behind. She wears a river-blue sash strung with small shells, a satchel at the hip, and a conch shell on a cord round her neck.

> Weapon, separate prompt: a tall reed staff, taller than the otter, topped with a spiral sea shell with short strands of kelp hanging under it, and a wrapped grip. Front, side and back views.

**6. Bellwright (beetle)**
> The character: a round bell beetle with a domed bronze-brown shell split down the back into two wing cases dotted with small studs, a segmented pale cream belly plate, two long curling antennae, and short sturdy legs. On her back stands a small wooden belfry with a shingled roof, and a bronze bell hangs in it above her head. A small tuning fork hangs at her belt.

> Weapon, separate prompt: a short bell mallet with a round bronze head and a wrapped wooden handle. Front, side and back views.

**7. Duskwing (fruit bat)**
> The character: a plump pear-shaped fruit bat with dusk-plum fur, a pale shaggy chest ruff, two big leaf-shaped ears splayed wide, a pug nose and two small fangs, a crest of fur on the crown, and a mane down the back. His leather wings are folded down at his sides like a cloak, held slightly away from the body, with small claws at each wing wrist.

> No weapon. He fights with his wings.
