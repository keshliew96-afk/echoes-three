His moves

The rule for all of it: a small hunter: still, then sudden. He folds in tight before every move and bursts out of it. The wings are his cloak when he waits and his blades when he strikes, and the ears tell his mood.

Idle. Alert and never quite still: he breathes, shrugs his wings up like a cloak and lets them settle, tilts his head to listen and flicks one ear, then the other.

Walk and run. The walk is a quick bouncing waddle on tiny feet with the wings swinging like a cloak. In the run he pitches forward with the wings half open and swept back, beating once a stride as if about to take off.

Claw combo, six hits in the same 1.35 seconds: rake, rake, bite, then three backstabs. Hit 1 (0.10 s): the right wing claw rakes down across his front and leaves three torn scratches. Hit 2 (0.24 s): the left claw rakes back the other way, so the two sets of scratches cross. Hit 3 (0.46 s): he rears up with both wings flared and snaps forward jaws first; two rows of torn tooth marks close on each other. Hits 4, 5 and 6 each have a blink of their own. He wraps himself in his wings, breaks into small bats (0.50 s) and comes out of the dark at the foe nearest the mouse; on this page the far post stands for that foe. Hit 4 (0.62 s) is the right claw. He blinks again (0.66 s) and hit 5 (0.78 s) is the left claw; he blinks a third time (0.83 s) and for hit 6 (0.96 s) he rears and drives both claws down together, which leaves two crossed sets of four cuts. Each blink goes to the foe nearest the mouse at that moment. When that is the same foe, as it is here, he comes out on a different side of it each time: behind it, then at one flank, then at the other, each a third of a turn apart and always facing it. He is gone for 0.06 seconds in each blink. After hit 6 he blinks home (1.04 s) and is back where he stood at 1.10 s. With Tap attack each press plays the next hit: hits 4, 5 and 6 each blink him out to their own side and bring him home if no press follows in time.

Casts. Screech (0.42 s): he draws his wings in and leans back to fill his chest, then throws his head forward with the wings flung back and his ears flat, and rings of sound leave his mouth; for echo screech and bat swarm. Nightfall (0.60 s): he wraps both wings across himself and curls small, then throws them wide and high and the dark opens round him; for nightfall, shadow veil and blood moon. Bloodbite (0.44 s): the deep bite: he rears, lunges further than in the combo, clamps and shakes his head, then tears away to one side; a bigger bite mark, and a thread of rose is drawn from it back to his mouth. Talon rake (0.42 s): both wings wound back to his right, then his whole body whips round with the wings out flat; four long scratches. Dusk dive (0.56 s): he crouches, springs up with his wings thrown high, hangs spread for a beat and drops with both wings slammed down; this also stands for flit.

Dash and downed. The dash is a blink, the fastest dash of all the heroes. He wraps himself in his wings and breaks into thirteen small bats that scatter (0.05 s); they stream to the spot and gather into him again (0.17 s), and he lands with his wings thrown open. The blink itself takes 0.12 s. When downed he sits down in a heap with his wings fallen round him, head hung and ears flat, and shuffles along on his seat.

The marks. Each scratch is a tapered, ragged strip drawn three times: a pale torn rim, the colour, and a darker core, like the cuts in your picture. It tears open along its length in under a tenth of a second, holds, and fades by 0.6 s. The marks always face the camera and hang where the target would be, since the page has no enemies. The combo rakes leave three scratches, the talon rake four longer ones. The bite is two rows of tooth cuts with longer fangs that snap shut on each other. They are flat colour with no glow. They are red as in your picture; the Dusk Rose marks button shows the same marks in his own game colours (Dusk Rose with a Dusk Plum core and a Pale Moon rim), because the game keeps crimson for enemy attacks and the fox. Both are there for you to choose. The casts use his game colours: Pale Moon and Dusk Rose rings for the screech, a Dusk Plum ring and motes for nightfall, Wing Leather dust.

What works

  • The wings spread, sweep and wrap from the folded shape they came in, and the membrane holds together.
  • The four casts and the dive cover his 11 skills in the game.
  • The ears and wing tips trail every move and settle after he stops.
  • The claw and bite marks read from the game camera and from close up.

What is rough

  • The wings cannot unfold. Spread wide they are a narrow fan, not a full bat wing. A model made with the wings open would spread properly but would not fold as neatly.
  • The combo's marks sit on two practice posts; the cast marks hang at a fixed reach because the page has no enemies. In the game they would sit on the enemy that was hit. They are a new effect compared with the game today and need your go before they enter it.
  • His legs are very short, so steps are small and the lunge comes from his body, not his feet.
  • His mouth does not open: the bite is a head snap and the mark does the rest.
  • The fur ruff, the pendant and the tail nub are stiff.
  • His feet can slide while he walks, the moves play in place (the dive does not carry him forward), and he has no ink outline or flat game colours yet.
  • The six-hit combo with its three blinks round the foe and the blink dash are new compared with the game today. They need your go and the freeze lifted before they enter it.
  • I only saw stills and per-frame numbers. Speed, weight and how the marks feel are yours to judge.
