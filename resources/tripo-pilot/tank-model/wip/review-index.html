What works

  • The model is clean: arms out, empty paws, same outfit from every side. As sent it was 16,249 triangles with the shield, so the body lost 1,276 to fit the budget. His face held up.
  • The shield sits in his left paw by its own grip, as its own piece. His combo follows the game's Tank: a backhand bash, a rising bash, then an overhead slam with both paws.
  • His feet stay on the floor when he crouches or lunges. The legs slide up under the tabard like a hidden knee bend.
  • Every move is now measured for the shield cutting into his body. Where it would, the build moves the shield clear.
  • The four casts are his own: a thrust, a roar, a shield plant and a wide sweep.

What is rough

  • The moves are a first pass, checked as still frames only. Timing and weight are yours to judge.
  • He has a spiked shoulder guard on both shoulders. The Tank in the game has one, on the shield side.
  • The tabard below the belt is one stiff piece, and the shoulder guards ride on the upper arms. Nothing sways yet.
  • His right paw reaches for the shield on the slam and the plant but does not grip it. His fingers don't close.
  • His feet can still slide while he walks, and he has no ink outline or flat game colours yet.
  • The four casts stand in for his 11 skills, with no effects.
