Second review, 12 November

Every move was looked at again from the front, the side and above. Six things did not hold up and were rebuilt.

1. Idle was still just standing. It is a guard now: sword side forward, feet staggered, weight low, the blade held out in front with its point up, the free arm back. 2. The combo had no footwork. The arms cut but the legs and trunk barely moved. Hit 1 now has a long step in with the trunk thrown down behind the cut, hit 2 comes up onto his toes behind the rising blade, and the spin tucks its legs. 3. The combo's finish was upright. He now lands in a deep, wide lunge with the blade swept round behind him and the free arm forward. 4. The crescent had no step. It has the same step and drop as hit 1. 5. The riposte's block crossed his face. The blade now sits across his chest with its point past his left ear. 6. Caltrops read as a wave and edge draw looked at the sky. The caltrops are scattered low along the floor from a deep lean with the blade tucked back, and in edge draw he keeps his eyes on the enemy.

Version 6: the free arm. You saw his free hand vanish in the spin. The arm was being turned into his body instead of out, in the spin and in most other moves, so it sank into his chest. It now swings out from his side everywhere: wide in the spin and Blade Storm, back for balance in the cuts and the lunge.

Looked at and left alone: walk, run, the lunge, Blade Storm, the strafes, dash, hurt, flourish and the downed moves.

What changed, move by move

The rule for all of it: a short load, a fast release, a quick recovery. The blade leads and the body follows it, he lands low and holds the finish, and the tail and the free arm throw the other way for balance.

Idle. An on-guard stance instead of standing straight: sword side forward, feet staggered, the blade out in front with its point drifting in a small circle, a quick breath, the tail swishing, ears turning one at a time.

Walk and run. The walk is light and up on his toes; the blade is carried still while the free arm and the tail swing. The run is a low sprint: far forward over his feet, ears flat, the tail streaming, and the blade now trails behind him pointing back instead of sticking out in front.

Sword combo, four hits in the same 1.6 seconds (Version 7). Cut, cut, thrust, spin, with no stop between them: each finish is the next hit's load. Hit 1 (0.14 s) is a forehand cut down across the front with a long step in. Hit 2 (0.32 s) whips the blade straight back up as a rising backhand, up on his toes. Hit 3 (0.50 s) is new: from the top of the backhand he snaps the grip back to his shoulder and stabs straight out in a short lunge. Hit 4 (0.82 s) is the spin cut: the thrust's recoil throws him into a full turn with the blade out level and the free arm flung wide, the blade cuts through the front on the way round, and he lands in a deep lunge and holds it before a small flick of the point. The last cut lands at the same moment as before (0.82 s, was 0.80) and the whole combo is still 1.6 s.

Casts. Lunge (0.22 s): the blade is drawn back to his hip and driven straight out, level, in a long low lunge; a thin streak runs down the thrust. Edge draw (0.36 s): the blade comes up before his face in a salute and is thrust to the sky, his free arm straight down at his side as if standing to attention, and a crimson edge sits on the blade for a moment. Caltrops (0.28 s): he drops low and flings them underhand off the free paw; six land ahead of him and stay a moment. Crescent (0.30 s): he winds the blade far back and cuts one wide level arc from right to left; a crimson crescent travels out along the floor. Riposte (block at 0.10 s, answer at 0.44 s): the blade snaps up across his body to catch a blow, he sways back under it, then answers standing tall with a whipping backhand slash at neck height from left to right. It no longer ends in a thrust, so the lunge is his only stab: the lunge is low and long, the riposte is upright and wide.

Blade Storm. The three-turn spin you asked for is unchanged, but it no longer needs Idle: it keeps spinning over walking or running legs.

Effects. Fox Crimson for the edge and Moon Silver for steel, the Swordsman's two colours in the game. A thin arc where each cut passes, silver sparks at a contact, a little dust under fast feet. All of them are dim and switch off with Hit feel. The trail behind the blade shows only in the combo, the lunge, the riposte, the crescent and Blade Storm. The two fast cuts barely freeze and do not shake the camera; the spin cut and the lunge do.

What works

  • The five casts cover his 11 skills in the game: lunge for the dashing strikes, edge draw for the stances and the crimson edge, caltrops, crescent for the arcs and wakes, riposte for the counter, plus Blade Storm.
  • His feet stay on the floor when he drops low or lunges. The legs slide up under the kilt like a hidden knee bend.
  • Every frame of every move is measured for the sword cutting into his body, and all 31 measure clear.

What is rough

  • He has no knees, so "low" is a lean and a drop of the hips, not a real crouch. The lunge and the landing after the spin would gain the most from a knee.
  • When the combo's spin plays over walking legs, the legs keep walking forward under a body that is turning. It reads at game zoom, but it is a shortcut.
  • The kilt is one stiff piece and the scarf does not sway. His fingers don't close round the grip.
  • His feet can still slide while he walks, the moves play in place (the lunge does not carry him forward), and he has no ink outline or flat game colours yet.
  • Dash, the downed moves, Hurt, Wave and Flourish were moved to the new build but not redesigned.
