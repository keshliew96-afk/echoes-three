# Windbriar: ideas from other games

**2026-11-18.** Kesh's refs: Dota's Windrunner (Windranger) and the Dragon Nest archer. Design
only. "Taken" ideas are in `WINDBRIAR_KIT.md` at the line named. To change one, answer with its
number: "drop 7", "take 31".

How it was checked: web search only (the wiki sites do not open from this session). **checked**
means a search result states it; **memory** means I know the skill but the search did not
confirm the wording.
