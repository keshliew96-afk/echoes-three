## Where this stands

Eight passes: 6.0, 5.25, 6.25, 6.75, 6.5, 6.75, 6.6, 6.65. From pass 3 on the score has stayed
between 6.25 and 6.75. Each round of redesigns fixed the pairs it was given and brought in about
three new ones, because 24 forms a style, 15 talents and 12 combos leave few cores unused,
above all in Gale, whose promise (gather, kick, move) pulls many forms toward the same results.
The bookkeeping fixes after pass 8 are worth about +0.8 by the judge's own estimate, so about
7.4. I stopped here rather than churn the page with another round that would likely regress.
