# Windbriar: skills and talent paths

**Version 2.4, 2026-11-19. Design only** (the freeze holds). Rules for every form are in
`WINDBRIAR_NOTES.md` section 10; old versions are `WINDBRIAR_KIT_x.x.md`. Numbers are guesses.

## 1. In one look

- **W1.** 26 skills: 8 Shot 狙, 8 Rain 雨, 8 Kata 斗, 2 passives. She holds 4.
- **W2.** Three paths, one talent pick per tier from any: **Deadeye 穿杨** (one foe, drawn
  shots), **Volley 万箭** (many foes, fans and rains), **Gale 弓斗** (within 3 u: kicks, wind).
- **W3.** Her tier I talent sets her style and gives every skill that style's form (section 4).
- **W4.** No heal, no shield, no damage over time. Bow Dance adds 风护 (T3).

## 2. Passive, basic attack, dash

- **W5. Tailwind 顺风.** Each cast gives a Tailwind (6 s, up to three, each 5% speed). At three,
  her next combo ends in the **Gale Arrow 风矢** (7 u, through all: 30). Deadeye: a full drawn
  shot, 42, ignores defence. Volley: breaks on the first foe into seven arrows of 10. Gale: a
  flip kick's 3 u wind ring, 24, pulls foes in and launches them.
- **W6. Basic attack.** 12, 12, then three arrows of 6, reach 4.5 u (as passed). Deadeye: hold
  to draw; a full draw is 26, 6.5 u, through one more foe. Volley: the shots split. Gale: a
  five-beat 弓斗术 within 3 u.
- **W7. Dash.** As passed: a somersault with three homing arrows, top priority, untouchable.
  Gale: it ends in a dive kick, 8.
- **W8. Held keys.** Power Shot, Skypiercer and the Deadeye basic: hold to draw, let go to
  shoot. Under Gale, Power Shot is a held kick.
- **W9. Kicks move everything** except bosses (they take 20% more instead). A foe can be
  juggled freely. Only Breakdance's kicked foes hurt others (附加).
