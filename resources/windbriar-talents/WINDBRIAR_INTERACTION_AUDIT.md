## Problems that need a pick

- **A1. Her pushes and the team.** Bow Sweep knocks a foe 2 u back, Cyclone Kick 1.5 u,
  Skypiercer carries small foes 10 u. That can drag foes off Stonehide's taunt and out of
  Ashfang's reach.
  - a) A foe that Stonehide holds taunted is not pushed, launched or carried by her; that hit
    deals 20% more instead, like on a boss. Pulls to a spot stay as they are: 聚怪 helps
    everyone. (**my pick**)
  - b) As written: she moves everything.
- **A2. Foes that never land.** A launch is movement, so the stun guard does not stop it. The
  Gale basic launches every 1.5 s, and Crescent Kick, Cyclone Kick, Bow Sweep and Eagle Dive
  launch too. A Gale hero can keep a small foe in the air for a whole fight.
  - a) A foe that lands cannot be launched again for 2 s; the kick still hits. (**my pick**)
  - b) Free juggling. Very 爽, but small foes stop being a threat.
- **A3. Volley's briars never run out.** Under Volley a torn briar flies to the next foe and
  stays there, so the same briars are torn again on every combo and their number only grows.
  - a) A briar hops once. The second time it is torn it just tears out. (**my pick**)
  - b) A hopped briar keeps only the time it had left and is not refreshed by new arrows.
- **A4. Through the Heart has no end.** A fused Deadeye arrow carries a whole skill (Power Shot:
  72). Each kill sends it on at full strength, so one shot can clear a pack of small foes, which
  is Volley's job.
  - a) Four flights at most for one arrow. (**my pick**)
  - b) No limit.
- **A5. Volley alone with a boss.** Hail needs three foes in one hit and the briars need a
  second foe to hop to. Against a lone boss a Volley hero has neither.
  - a) A champion or a boss counts as three foes for Hail, and with no other foe near, torn
    briars tear out of the boss in place. (**my pick**)
  - b) Leave it: Volley is the path for the rooms, Deadeye for the bosses.
- **A6. Gale's survival.** She has 80 health and no heal, shield or damage cut, and the Gale
  forms put her inside the pack (Windknot's Gale form pulls the pack onto her own feet).
  - a) Bow Dance also gives 风护: she takes 20% less while a foe is within 3 u of her. (**my
    pick**)
  - b) Bow Dance makes every flip and jump kick untouchable while she is in the air (about
    0.2 s each). Rewards timing, harder for the AI seat.
  - c) Nothing: the dash, the launches and Windrun are her safety.

## To measure first when it is built

- Eagle Stance under Full Draw with Hundred Paces: the highest single-target burst she has.
- Through the Heart with Kill Shot in a pack of weak foes.
- Falling Feathers with Ten Thousand Arrows: chain clears.
- Tailwind's share of her damage in each style (aim: about 15%).
- Free juggling under Bow Dance: how long a small foe can be kept in the air.
- Windrun's uptime with cooldown nodes (foes' shots miss her while it runs).
