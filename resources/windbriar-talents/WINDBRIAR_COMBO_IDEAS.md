# Windbriar: ways her skills play together

**CLOSED: all twelve taken.** Kesh, 2026-11-18 13:07 Singapore time: "All combo". Written into
the kit (section 5) and the notes (N91; how each works with every form: N96).

**2026-11-18, on kit 1.1** (C8, C10 and C12 re-fitted after Kesh's picks 1b and 2b).
Design only. Twelve combos, C1 to C12. Each is one small rule
between two skills, or a skill and her dash or basic attack; none needs a talent. Answer "all",
or name the ones to drop: "drop C6, C10".

| # | Name | The rule | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Thread the knot 一箭穿结** | Pinning Arrow shot into foes that Windknot or Tornado Shot has gathered threads its cord through up to three of them: all tied, all stunned 1 s | Pinning Arrow and one of the two winds |
| C2 | **Wall slam** | A foe she knocks or carries into a wall takes 10 and is stunned 0.5 s. Stonehide's slab and Stone Ring count as walls (the rule he and Ashfang already have) | Bow Sweep, Cyclone Kick, Skypiercer, or any form that moves a foe (notes N96) |
| C3 | **Air shot 空射** | Her arrows deal 30% more to a foe in the air, whoever launched it | any launch (hers or Ashfang's), then any arrow |
| C4 | **Spike** | Eagle Dive's heel or Kill Shot on a foe in the air slams it down: 50% more to it, and 10 to the foes within 1.5 u of where it lands (Ashfang's and Stonehide's rule) | a launch, then one of the two |
| C5 | **Through the twister 穿风** | An arrow of hers shot into her Tornado Shot whirlwind from outside comes out as three, each at a foe near the twister, for half | Tornado Shot and any arrow, basic shots too |
| C6 | **The rain rides the wind** | Windknot or Tornado Shot cast on her Rain of Arrows takes the rain with it: the rain moves to the knot, or drifts with the twister | Rain of Arrows and one of the two winds |
| C7 | **The kestrel hunts the mark** | While a foe carries Hunter's Mark, Kestrel Watch shoots only that foe, twice as often | Kestrel Watch and Hunter's Mark |
| C8 | **Kick them in 踢入陷阱** | A foe pushed, pulled or carried into her Briar Trap springs it harder: the stun is 1.4 s and its first second deals double (ruled in notes N96: the stun is capped at 1 s, then a 0.4 s slow) | Briar Trap and any push or pull |
| C9 | **Shoot the arrow 射爆** | Any arrow of hers that hits her standing Burst Arrow before it bursts sets it off at once, twice as wide | Burst Arrow and any arrow |
| C10 | **Dash draw 空中引弦** | A dash does not cancel a draw. While the key stays held she keeps drawing through the somersault, and can loose upside down in it | the dash and any drawn shot |
| C11 | **Sky cast 空中施放** | Volley, Feather Fan, Whirl Shot or Burst Arrow pressed during the dash is shot down from the top of the somersault and covers 30% more | the dash and one of the four |
| C12 | **Full sail 满帆** | Windrun fills Tailwind to three at once, and the Tailwinds do not run out while Windrun lasts | Windrun and the passive |

Notes.

- C3 and C4 are the 枪斗术 loop: kick it up, shoot it in the air, bring it down. They also work
  on foes Ashfang launches.
- C1, C5, C6 and C8 make the wind skills useful to all three styles: the wind sets up, the
  arrows cash in.
- C10 gives a Deadeye hero a way to move without losing a draw. It replaces the rule "the dash
  cancels a draw" (notes N10). It does not count as standing still for Hundred Paces.
- C11 is the dash attack from Hades on her own somersault. It needs the game to keep a pressed
  skill for the top of the dash, the same new machinery as Ashfang's Dash strike.
- C12 makes Windrun the opener: run in, and the first combo ends in a Gale Arrow.
- C2 with audit pick A1 b: she can slam a taunted foe into a wall too; it then walks back to
  the tank.
- New looks: a cord through three foes (C1), a foe hit in the air (C3), arrows spinning out of
  the twister (C5), a rain sliding across the floor (C6), the shot upside down in the dash
  (C10, C11).
