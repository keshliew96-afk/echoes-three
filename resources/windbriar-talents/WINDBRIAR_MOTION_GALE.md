# Windbriar: motion specs (batch 1 Gale, batch 2 Deadeye and Volley)

**2026-11-19. Design only; kit 2.6 is unchanged.** Kesh, on the ability page (19 Nov): Gale moves
are too simple; take them from street dance (街舞) and the Dragon Nest archer's combat poses.
Ride should really ride and steer the foe, not float on it. Eagle Stance should not be a 一字马,
and its Gale form should be fancy breaking rolls, not a sprawled spin. Head hop's jump looks
like a 僵尸 (zombie) hop. Written for the page thread to build.

## The rules every spec here follows

- Kesh's animation templates:
  - **Combo flow.** Each move inherits its momentum: the overshoot of one beat is the wind-up
    of the next. No return to idle between beats.
  - **Direction.** Beats inside a move change direction (low, high, round, down), and no two
    forms share the same signature move.
  - **Key poses.** Every beat has an anticipation, a strike, a contact and a follow-through.
  - **The self-check** (stated after each spec):
    1. Her root travels the way her legs push.
    2. A planted paw stays locked to the floor until her weight leaves it. She may turn on it,
       but it may not slide.
    3. Her hips and legs make the force her upper body shows.
- The page's limits:
  - 22 bones. The page adds a runtime knee that bends forward, with no splay (外八).
  - Root travel, flips and spins are page keys.
  - Ears and tail follow on springs, so the specs only mark where they should read.
- Her legs never open into a 一字马, and no move lies spread-eagled (四仰八叉). Legs move
  together, in a V of 90° at most, or one tucked and one straight. Toes are pointed in the air.
- Heavy moments get the page's long hit pause (0.085 s); light ones get the short one (0.055 s).
- **Frames are at 60 fps.** f0 is the press. "Bow" means the bow in her left forepaw unless
  a beat says otherwise.
- **Moves borrowed:**
  - Street dance:
    - toprock (the Indian step);
    - footwork (six-step and CC);
    - the coffee grinder (one straight leg sweeping a circle while she squats on the other);
    - the windmill (rolling across the upper back with the legs in a V);
    - the flare (the body held on the forepaws while straight legs circle under it);
    - the air flare, the 1990 (a one-paw spin in a handstand) and the swipe;
    - freezes (baby, chair, air chair) and the kip-up.
  - Martial arts and tricking: the tornado kick (a 360° jumping roundhouse), the butterfly
    twist, the scissor kick and the corkscrew.
  - Dragon Nest acrobat: shots loosed upside down at the top of a somersault, the moonsault
    kick, kick-then-shoot cancels, and the low, wide archer crouch with the bow held across.

## Audit of the current Gale motion lines

Too simple (one verb, no beats): F1, F3, F4, F8, F9, F10, F11, F12, F13, F14, F15, F16, F17,
F18, F19, F20, F21, F22, F23, F24. Kesh named three of those, F6, F7 and F13. Thin but workable:
F2 and F5. All 24 are respecified below, so the page has one consistent set.

## The Gale forms

### F1 追射 Chase (0.9 s: a mule kick, a forward roll, the chasing shot)
1. f0-8 Anticipation:
   - She plants her left hind paw.
   - She pivots 180° on it, turning on the ball of the paw. The paw stays put.
   - Her back faces the foe, and the bow drops low across her hips.
   - Ears flatten back.
2. f8-14 Strike: a mule kick.
   - She pitches forward 60° onto her forepaws, holding the bow flat under them.
   - Both hind legs snap from bent to straight, straight back at the foe's chest.
3. f14 Contact (long pause):
   - The foe folds at the middle and flies 3 u along a low arc lasting 0.35 s, toward the
     nearest pack.
   - Dust bursts at her forepaws.
4. f14-30 Follow-through:
   - The kick's push drives her body forward, so she rolls forward over her shoulder (a back
     rock) and comes up crouched.
   - A small hop turns her 180° to face the foe while it is still flying.
5. f30-46: She nocks and draws in the crouch, with her back leg extended behind like a
   sprinter. That leg is a knee-forward lunge, not a split.
6. f46-50: Release. The arrow bends after the flying foe.
7. f50-54: Settle. The bow lowers, and her tail flicks at the pin.

Self-check:
- The roll goes forward because the kick pushed backward.
- The pivot paw turns on the spot and does not slide.
- The kick comes from her hips with her forepaws planted.

### F2 蓄腿 Wound kick (held 0.3 to 1.1 s: a coffee-grinder coil, a tornado-kick release)
1. Press, f0-10:
   - She drops into a deep squat on her left hind paw, its knee bent 70°.
   - Her right leg stretches straight along the floor, pointing out to the side.
   - Her forepaws touch down behind her, with the bow flat under them.
2. While the key is held:
   - She turns slowly round the left paw, which pivots on its ball and stays in place.
   - The straight right leg sweeps the floor like a coffee grinder, 180° per 0.4 s and
     speeding up.
   - A Wind Jade coil winds up the right leg.
   - Her ears wrap round her neck by 0.5 s, and her tail spirals.
   - The dust circle grows with the wind-up.
3. Tap release (held under 0.2 s), f+0-12: The sweep simply speeds through 180° at ankle
   height. 1 u, a light pause.
4. Full release:
   - f+0-6: She springs up off the squat paw, with both forepaws throwing the bow up and over
     her head as a counterweight.
   - f+6-16: In the air she turns once fully (a tornado kick), the same way the coil turned.
     The right leg whips round at hip height, straight.
   - f+12: Contact with everything within 2.5 u (long pause). Foes skid 1 u outward.
   - f+16-26: She lands on the kicking leg first, then the other, and the dust ring pops.
     She ends in a low guard, bow across her chest.

Self-check:
- The coil and the release spin turn the same way, so the momentum carries over.
- The squat paw only turns in place.
- She lands on the leg that kicked.

### F3 连印 Beat mark (0.5 s: a toprock step, an axe kick, a freeze accent)
1. f0-8: A toprock cross-step in. The right paw crosses in front of the left, and the bow swings
   out to the side for balance.
2. f8-14:
   - She hops off the left paw.
   - Her right leg rises straight up past her ear, about 170°.
   - She leans back, holding the bow out behind her as a counterweight.
3. f14-20: The heel drops onto the foe's head. Contact at f18 (long pause).
   - The foe squashes 12% and its head dips.
   - Five pip rings flash out in turn round it, one every 2 frames.
4. f20-26:
   - The heel plants and stays locked.
   - She rolls her weight over it into a low crouch, her left forepaw on the floor and her
     right leg bent up (a baby freeze, held for 6 frames).
   - Her ears flop forward at the stop.
5. f26-30: She rises out of the freeze, tapping the bow on the foe's chest once. That tap starts
   the pip count.

Self-check:
- The hop comes off the planted left paw.
- The axe kick's drop drives into the planted heel.
- The freeze is held on a planted forepaw and paw.

### F4 弹弓 Slingshot (0.9 s: water-ski lean, double bounce, a corkscrew dropkick)
1. f0-12:
   - She shoots the cord arrow from the hip into the foe.
   - The cord whips taut, and she wraps it twice round her right wrist.
2. f12-30:
   - She sits back into a water-ski lean: both hind paws planted, knees bent forward, her body
     tilted 50° back, and the cord taut.
   - She bounces twice on the springy cord. Each bounce sinks her 0.15 u and lifts her
     0.1 u, and her paws stay locked.
   - Her ears stream back.
3. f30-36:
   - Release. She launches feet first, both legs together and straight, in a dropkick.
   - In flight she makes a half corkscrew twist, body horizontal, bow held tight to her chest.
   - Travel is up to 6 u in 0.25 s.
4. f36: Both soles hit the foe's chest (long pause).
   - The foe rocks back.
   - Foes within 1.5 u pop up 0.4 s with a shock ring.
5. f36-54: She drops beside the foe in a three-point landing (both hind paws and her right
   forepaw), the bow raised in her left. She holds it for 6 frames.

Self-check:
- The lean is backward and the launch is forward, the cord storing the force.
- Her paws stay locked through the bounces.
- The landing absorbs the fall into her bent knees.

### F5 影踢 Shadow kicks (Kesh's redesign, 19 Nov: three blink kicks, a launch, one arrow)
Each kick is a different one, and each sends the foe in a new direction, at least 90° from the
last. 1.9 s in all.
1. f0-12, kick 1, a hard spinning back kick:
   - She plants her left paw and turns on it (it stays put).
   - Her right leg drives straight out at chest height.
   - f12: contact (long pause). The foe flies back 2.5 u in 0.3 s along a low arc.
2. f12-30:
   - She holds the follow-through for a beat, then fades into a short jade blur (the blink,
     untouchable).
   - As the foe's knock-back ends (f30), she appears at its back, already crouched.
3. f30-44, kick 2, a side kick:
   - Her front paw plants and the side kick fires from her hip.
   - It sends the foe off at 90° to the first line (long pause).
4. f44-62: The blink again. She appears behind it at the end of its slide.
5. f62-76, kick 3, a jumping tornado kick:
   - A 360° turn in the air.
   - It sends the foe on at another 90° (long pause).
6. f76-90: The last blink. This time she appears under its front, low.
7. f90-106, kick 4, a backflip flash kick (Kesh: kick higher, kick to air):
   - She plants both paws, then throws herself back into a full backflip.
   - Her leading leg sweeps straight up through the foe's chin at the top of the flip (f96,
     long pause), the knee snapping from bent to straight.
   - A tall Blade arc traces the kick. The foe flies high, 1.0 s in the air.
   - She lands crouched at f106, both paws together, and they stay put.
8. f106-114: She draws as it nears the top, leaning back, her bow tilted up.
9. f116: The shot into the airborne foe at the peak of its arc (long pause).
10. She settles in a low guard under it as it falls.

Self-check:
- Each kick comes from a planted paw or a jump.
- The foe flies the way each kick pushed.
- She appears at rest after each blink, so no slide.
- The three directions differ, so the foe's path draws a Z.

### F6 骑射 Ride (0.9 s; the page is rebuilding this one: ideas to fold in)
1. f0-10: A leapfrog. Her forepaws land on the foe's shoulders as she vaults up.
2. f10-14: She sits astride its neck, her knees gripping (bent forward), and takes its ears in her
   right forepaw like reins.
3. f14-50: The steer, up to 2 u.
   - She leans hard toward where she aims, and its head turns that way.
   - It stomps after its head in heavy, stumbling steps (each step planted), arms flailing up
     at her.
   - Her body rocks with its steps like a rodeo rider, and her tail is up for balance.
4. f50-58:
   - She lets go of the ears and rises to stand on its shoulders.
   - The bow points straight down between her feet, and she draws.
5. f58-60: A point-blank shot down (long pause). The foe drops to its knees, with a dust burst.
6. f60-72:
   - The shot's recoil pops her up into a backflip.
   - She lands behind it, crouched, and turns her head back to look at it.
7. On a kill:
   - The foe flops flat.
   - She gives the head a little tap with her heel as she lands.

Self-check:
- The foe's steps carry the steer; she only leans.
- Its paws plant on each step.
- She stands up before shooting down.

### F7 风井 Wind well (Kesh's redesign, 19 Nov: a dive slam, a fixed wind well, a burst)
1. f0-10: A deep crouch, ears flat. Her forepaws swing back.
2. f10-30: The jump.
   - She springs up and forward toward the spot (within 3 u), forepaws swinging up.
   - She tucks into a front flip at the top.
3. f30-40: The dive.
   - She comes out of the flip heel first, both legs together and straight down.
   - The bow is held up behind her like a tail fin.
4. f40: The slam (long pause).
   - 10 to foes within 1.5 u, and a dust crown bursts up.
   - She lands in a deep crouch, one forepaw on the floor, knees bent forward.
5. f40-52: The well opens under the slam point.
   - A Wind Jade spiral, turning slowly inward.
   - She rises out of the crouch.
6. From f52: She is free, and the well stays where she slammed.
   - Foes within 4 u slide toward its centre at 1 u a second, leaning back against it, paws
     dragging (foes may slide here: the wind is moving them).
   - Dust streams into the centre.
7. At 4 s: The burst (long pause). A jade ring blows out to 3 u, and the foes in it are knocked
   flat for a beat. If she is near, her ears blow back.

Self-check:
- The jump goes forward the way she pushed.
- The landing absorbs the fall into her bent knees.
- She moves freely afterward, so her own clips (walk, dash, skills) play over the well.

### F8 矢枪 Arrow spear (1.6 s: a staff routine and a javelin throw)
1. f0-14:
   - She reaches into the wind drill and pulls the giant arrow out by its middle, both forepaws.
   - The weight of it dips her body 0.1 u.
2. f14-30: A figure eight round her body. The arrow passes behind her back and swaps paws.
   Her hips counter-turn to carry its weight.
3. f30-48: An overhead helicopter spin.
   - Arms are straight up, and the arrow turns twice flat over her head.
   - She pivots once on her left paw, which stays put.
   - Foes within 3 u are hit as the head passes (24, short pause each).
4. f48-60: A low sweep spin. She drops to a crouch, and the arrow passes at ankle height.
5. f60-84: The throw.
   - Two running steps back up.
   - The arrow is drawn back over her shoulder, her back arched, her left paw pointing ahead.
   - Her opposite leg kicks up behind for force, knee bent forward.
6. f84-90: Release. The drill flies 10 u.
7. f90-96:
   - She steps through onto the throwing side.
   - She ends in a low lunge, her throwing paw extended.

Self-check:
- The arrow is heavy, so her body turns against it.
- The pivot paw stays put.
- The throw steps forward with the forward force.

### F9 穿裆 Slide shot (0.6 s: a baseball slide, three shots up, a kip-up)
1. f0-8: Two quick bounding steps at the foe.
2. f8-14:
   - A drop: she throws herself onto her back and hip, feet first, in a baseball slide.
   - Her right leg is straight ahead and her left tucked.
   - Her ears pin flat, and the bow lies flat on her chest.
3. f14-22: She slides under the foe's legs. Dust streams along her path, and she slows with
   friction. This slide is meant: nothing is planted.
4. f18-24:
   - Lying on her back under its middle, she raises the bow upright.
   - Three snap shots go straight up, one every 2 frames (short pause each).
   - The foe pops up 0.5 s, legs flailing over her.
5. f24-36:
   - A kip-up behind it. Her legs whip up and over, her forepaws push, and she lands on both
     hind paws.
   - She turns her head up to the airborne foe, bow already raised.

Self-check:
- The slide spends the speed of the run.
- The kip-up's push is down and back, so she rises behind it.
- Nothing slides that is meant to be planted.

### F10 收雨 Closing rain (1.0 s plus the 2 s close: a coffee-grinder circle that closes)
1. f0-12: She leans far back, one hind leg kicked high, bent at the knee (not a split), and shoots
   one arrow straight up.
2. f12-18: The rain ring falls round the spot, 4 u wide.
3. f18-138: A coffee grinder. She squats on her right paw, and her left leg, straight, sweeps a
   circle on the floor. She hops over it with her forepaws each turn: one turn every 0.5 s,
   four turns in all. Each turn:
   - her circle tightens, the leg drawing in;
   - the ring of rain shrinks to match it, herding foes inward.
4. f138-150:
   - The leg tucks in fully.
   - She rises in one motion, both forepaws pulling inward and closing to a fist, as the ring
     closes on its centre.

Self-check:
- The squat paw pivots in place.
- The swept leg moves on the floor's surface and carries no weight.
- The ring is tied to the leg's circle.

### F11 爆步 Blast ride (0.9 s: sprinter's blocks, a flying side kick, a skid turn)
1. f0-10: She glances back and stabs the arrow into the floor behind her.
2. f10-20:
   - She crouches into sprinter's blocks, both hind paws braced on the blast side.
   - Her forepaws are on the floor and the bow is tucked against her side. Her ears lift.
3. f20-24: The blast. She launches, untouchable.
4. f24-36: A flying side kick. Her body is horizontal and her leading leg straight ahead. Her
   trailing leg is tucked and the bow is held back along her body. Each foe she passes takes a
   flick of the leading paw (short pause each) and spins aside 1 u.
5. f36-54:
   - A skid stop on both hind paws: a long dust skid, slowing. This skid is meant.
   - A 180° turn on the last frames of the skid, so she faces back down her path.
   - Her ears flop forward.

Self-check:
- The blast is behind her, so she flies forward.
- The skid slows her with friction.
- She turns only at low speed.

### F12 踢轮 Kicked saw (0.9 s: a gather spin, a keepy-up, a tornado-kick launch)
1. f0-14:
   - One spin on her left paw (the paw stays put) with the bow held out.
   - The twelve arrows orbit her at waist height and settle into a flat wheel.
2. f14-20: The wheel drops to her feet.
3. f20-28: She flicks it up with her right instep to knee height (a keepy-up, like a 毽子). It
   spins in place, and she watches it with her ears up.
4. f28-44: A tornado kick.
   - She jumps off her left paw and turns 360°.
   - Her right leg slaps the wheel flat forward like a frisbee at f38 (short pause).
5. f44-54: She lands on the kicking leg and settles crouched. The wheel skims 3 u and saws.

Self-check:
- The spin pivots in place.
- The jump comes off the planted paw.
- She lands on the leg that kicked.

### F13 踩头 Head hop (1.2 s: four springs, four kicks; Kesh: not a 僵尸 hop)
What makes a zombie hop: both feet together, straight legs, no crouch, arms stiff. Every spring
here has a crouch, an arm swing, a knee drive and a tuck, and each hop has its own kick.
1. f0-8, first spring:
   - She crouches 0.2 u deep, knees bent forward, and swings both forepaws back.
   - She drives up off both paws as her forepaws swing up past her head.
2. f8-20, head 1: a stomp.
   - She tucks both knees in the air, then extends one leg down onto the head.
   - The head squashes 15% (short pause).
   - That paw plants on the head for 4 frames: it bends at the knee to absorb the landing and
     to load the next spring.
3. f20-34, head 2: a scissor kick.
   - The spring rises off the head.
   - In the air her legs scissor: the left kicks up and the right drops. The right heel
     strikes head 2.
4. f34-48, head 3: a side flip.
   - She turns sideways, her body in a cartwheel arc, and both paws tap head 3 as she passes
     over it.
5. f48-62, head 4: a corkscrew.
   - She springs high and corkscrews once.
   - At the top she is upside down. Her heel strikes head 4 on the way over, and she draws
     the bow upside down (the Dragon Nest acrobat's air shot).
6. f62-66: The upside-down shot at the last foe (20, long pause).
7. f66-72:
   - She rights herself.
   - A three-point landing: both hind paws and a forepaw, with the bow out to the side.
   - Her ears flop at the stop.

Each head kick also turns her toward the next head, so her path weaves instead of going
straight.

Self-check:
- Every spring is a crouch and a push, with her forepaws swinging up.
- Each planted moment on a head is a real bend and push.
- She leaves each spring toward the next head.

### F14 回旋风 Homecoming twister (0.7 s plus the 2 s roll: a lasso, then a tug of war)
1. f0-18: She whirls the cord arrow overhead, three loops, faster each time, her hips swaying under
   it.
2. f18-24: She throws it 4 u, and the twister rises there.
3. f24-138: The haul back, hand over hand like a tug of war.
   - She leans back 40° with both hind paws planted and her knees bent.
   - Her forepaws pull in turn, one pull every 0.25 s.
   - The twister rolls back toward her, and foes tumble along inside it.
   - Between pulls she takes a small step back, each step planted.
4. f138-150:
   - The twister reaches her.
   - She pirouettes inside it, two turns on her left paw (in place), and it bursts outward.
   - She ends with the cord coiled on her arm.

Self-check:
- She leans back to pull.
- Every step back is planted.
- The pirouette turns in place.

### F15 荆鞭 Briar lash (0.6 s: an overhead circle, a low crack, a wrap-back)
1. f0-8: She draws the vine out of the arrowhead like a sword from a sheath, a pull across her
   body.
2. f8-18: The vine circles once overhead, a full loop, as she steps her right paw in.
3. f18-26:
   - A deep lunge, front knee bent forward.
   - The whip cracks across 120° in front at shin height, right to left. Contact at f22
     (short pause per foe).
   - Foes' feet are swept and they fall flat.
   - Her tail swings the opposite way for balance.
4. f26-36:
   - The vine wraps once round her body.
   - She snaps it back into the arrow with a flick of the wrist.

Self-check:
- The lunge steps into the crack.
- The planted front paw does not move.
- The tail counters the whip.

### F16 推浪 Bulldoze (1.8 s: three shoves with toprock between)
1. f0-12: She shoots the arrow up, leaning back. The curtain begins to fall 1 u ahead of her.
2. f12-40, shove 1:
   - A planted stamp.
   - The bow is held level across both forepaws, and she drives it forward from her hips
     (a two-paw push).
   - The curtain jumps forward 1 u.
3. f40-52: A toprock shuffle forward, two beats, the bow bouncing on her shoulders.
4. f52-80: Shove 2, the same, but from her other side: she leads with the left paw.
5. f80-92: Toprock again.
6. f92-108, shove 3:
   - A leaping two-paw push. She leaves the floor, her body straight from paws to bow.
   - The curtain drives foes to its far edge, and a dust wave rolls ahead of it.
7. f108: She lands with both paws planted, and her ears flop.

Self-check:
- Every push comes from a planted stamp.
- The leaping push carries her forward with it.

### F17 风车 Pinwheel (0.5 s: a finger spin, a cross punch, a back hop)
1. f0-10:
   - She tosses the five arrows up. They land point-up on her right fingertip and spin into a
     cone, like a basketball spun on a finger.
2. f10-18:
   - A cross punch with full hip turn.
   - Her rear paw pivots on its ball and stays planted.
   - The cone drills into the foe at f16 (long pause), and the foe shakes.
3. f18-30:
   - She hops back into a low crouch, one forepaw on the floor.
   - She watches near foes being pulled against the drill, and her ears point at it.

Self-check:
- The punch turns her hips, and her rear paw turns in place.
- The hop back comes off both paws.

### F18 换位 Switch (0.6 s: a handstand on its shoulders, an aerial swap)
1. f0-8: A bound in. Her forepaws plant on the foe's shoulders.
2. f8-18:
   - She rises into a handstand on its shoulders, legs together and straight up (a 1990 pose,
     held for 4 frames).
3. f18-28: The swap.
   - She pushes off. She and the foe turn round a shared centre like two ends of a seesaw.
   - She arcs down to its spot, and it tumbles over to hers.
4. f28-36:
   - She lands crouched where it stood.
   - It lands on its back where she stood, and dust puffs up.
   - She looks over her shoulder at it.

Self-check:
- The handstand is held on its planted shoulders.
- Their paths mirror each other.
- She lands with her knees bent.

### F19 空中连踢 Air juggle (0.9 s plus 0.6 s: a launch, a rebound jump, three air kicks)
1. f0-54: The base backflip kick launches the foe.
2. f54-56: She lands. Her landing squash is the crouch of the next jump: momentum carries over,
   with no idle.
3. f56-60: She jumps after the foe, her forepaws swinging up.
4. f60-66: Kick 1, a rising front kick, her leg straight up into its back.
5. f66-74: Kick 2, a butterfly twist. Her body is flat in the air and both legs whip round.
6. f74-82:
   - Kick 3, a bicycle kick over her head.
   - It sends the foe higher (long pause). She is upside down at the strike.
7. f82-90: She drops into a superhero landing, one knee and one forepaw down, her head up
   watching it fall.

Self-check:
- The second jump uses the landing.
- The kicks change direction: up, round, then over.
- She lands with her knees taking the fall.

### F20 蹴鞠 Cuju (1.0 s plus the 2 s dribble: a staff spin, then 毽子 tricks, then a volley)
1. f0-60: The base staff spin. The knot rolls the pack into a 1.5 u wind ball.
2. f60-160, the dribble, as she runs behind the ball:
   - Tap left and tap right with the insides of her paws, the ball rolling ahead.
   - A knee juggle: the ball bounces once off her raised knee.
   - A rainbow flick: she rolls it up her back leg with her heel, and it flies over her head.
   - Around the world: her leg circles the ball once while it is in the air.
   Every planted step is a real step, and her body keeps up with the ball.
3. f160-180:
   - The finish, a scorpion kick: she dives forward onto her forepaws, and her heel whips over
     her head into the ball (long pause).
   - The ball bursts 3 u ahead.
4. f180-190: She rolls out of the dive onto her feet.
