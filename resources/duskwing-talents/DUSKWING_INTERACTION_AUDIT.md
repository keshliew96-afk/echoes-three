the fifteen talents against each other and against the game's rules (blind, stuns, launches,
bosses, the other heroes, the damage formula v1.2). Two lists: five problems that need a pick

Answer like "A1 a, A2 a", or "your pick" for all.

## Problems that need a pick

- **A1. The Combo when he is hit.** Rampage has 80 health and no heal. Should a hit cost him
  the Combo too?
  - a) No. Only 3 s without hitting loses it. (**my pick**: it keeps the style 爽)
  - b) A hit that lands on him takes 5 Combo; a dodged one takes none.
- **A2. Night Hunt against a champion or a boss.** There are no kills in a boss fight, so the
  passive's kill part and its three shapes are dead there.
  - a) Each tenth of a champion's or boss's health that he takes counts as a kill. (**my pick**)
  - b) Kills only: he is the hero for rooms, not bosses.
- **A3. Unseen and his friends.** When he fades, the foes on him turn on the nearest hero at
  once, often the healer.
  - a) A foe that loses him looks round for 1 s (a "?" over it) before it picks someone else;
    it never picks the healer first. (**my pick**)
  - b) They pick a new target at once.
- **A4. Dodge and the big hits.** At full Combo he has a 30% chance to ignore anything, a
  boss's slam too.
  - a) Dodge works on every hit thrown at him; only floor beds and damage over time are sure.
    (**my pick**)
  - b) A hit with a floor warning is never dodged: those are for the dash.
- **A5. Leech from wide skills.** Blood Drinker returns 15% of everything. One Wing Wheel on
  five foes is 90 damage, 13 health; a pack heals him far more than a boss does.
  - a) Full leech from everything. (**my pick**: the packs are where he is in danger)
  - b) Area hits, zones and bleeds return a third.

## How he meets the other heroes

- Windbriar: her arrows deal 30% more to foes he launches (her C3) and her Spike works on
  them. Her Hunter's Mark and his Echo Screech are different 易伤 names: they add.
- Stonehide: a taunted foe faces the tank, so its back is open. A foe he pins or holds is still
  taunted when it is let go.
- Mosslight: he is often out of her reach. Blood Tithe stores her heals too.
- Ashfang: Mirror Image turns foes to the image, which opens their backs for him.

## To measure first when it is built

- Bloodrush at full Combo: skill output and how long he stays at 30 in a room and on a boss.
- Nightcloak with First Blood and Killing Hour: the highest burst he has.
- Blood Drinker with Blood Pact: how many extra casts the leech pays for.
- The kill's 1 s cut in a room of weak foes: how close his skills come to no cooldown.
- Dodge at 30% on a boss: downs with and without it.
- Unseen: how often the healer is downed while he waits (A3 b: foes retarget at once).
- The passive's 20% under Sanguine with Open Wounds: it is nearly always on.
