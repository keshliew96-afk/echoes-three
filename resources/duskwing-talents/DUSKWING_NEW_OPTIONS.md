Notes.

- R1 gives each "behind the nearest foe" form its own target: the bleeding foe (works with
  Sanguine picks), the foe that hit him, and a swap.
- R2b is Kesh's own line, "blink out to 脱离战场 or back to teammate".
- R2c is a counter, not a stealth: it needs a foe to swing at him.
- R3a makes Phantom useful to the team. An ally's hit gets the 40% only, never a sure crit.
- R5b, R6a and R7a make the Rampage form read the Combo, so the style shows on the card.
- R9a: a pool on the floor is a zone. The heal is his only; the pool never blocks allies' paths
  (AI steering rule, CLAUDE.md, applies only to slowing or burning beds).
- R11 changes three passed combos only by an added beat; the passed rule underneath stays.
- R12b: the threads are a zone drain, so Blood Drinker's 15% drops to a third on them (A5b). It
  is the only option that removes something passed (the bleed): pick it only if the bleed felt
  like a repeat.
- Page v11 (https://claude.ai/artifact/8Hk5PFCgGSqJZY4uhMMVSy) shows all 22 as "New, not passed"
  switches. Fixed after the page thread's check: R7a's growth is 0.2 u per 5 Combo; R11c's dive
  hits the foe he lands on too; R10a's plucks deal no damage. The ally in R2b and R3a is a slate
  blue dummy on the page only.
