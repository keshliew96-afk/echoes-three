# Duskwing: ways his skills play together

**CLOSED: all twelve taken.** Kesh, 2026-11-18 19:05 Singapore time: "all". Written into kit 1.2
(section 6) and the notes (N98).

**2026-11-18, on kit 1.0.** Design only. Twelve combos, C1 to C12. Each is one small rule
between two skills, or a skill and his dash; none needs a talent. Answer "all", or name the
ones to drop: "drop C6, C10".

| # | Name | The rule | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Pop the vein 血爆** | Last Bite or Blood Spikes on a bleeding foe brings the rest of its bleed out at once | Open Vein (or any bleed), then one of the two |
| C2 | **Air bite 空噬** | Bloodbite or Last Bite on a foe in the air slams it down: 50% more to it, and 10 to the foes within 1.5 u of where it lands (Windbriar's Spike rule) | a launch (Skyward Rip, Blood Spikes, or Windbriar's kicks), then a bite |
| C3 | **The swarm hunts the mark** | While a foe carries Death Mark, Bat Swarm and Night Wings bite only that foe, twice as often | Death Mark with Bat Swarm or Night Wings |
| C4 | **Screech in the dark 暗中回声** | Echo Screech cast from inside Nightfall's dark or Flit's puff rings all round him instead of a cone, and the blind on foes it hits starts again | Echo Screech with Nightfall or Flit |
| C5 | **The shade strikes too 影随** | While his Roost shade hangs, it repeats Crescent Wings, Blood Lance and Echo Screech from where it is, for half | Roost and one of the three |
| C6 | **Dash cast 化蝠施放** | Hawk Drag, Wing Wheel, Bloodbite or Crescent Wings pressed during the dash comes out as he gathers, at once, 30% wider | the dash and one of the four |
| C7 | **Through the cloud 穿雾** | A Blood Lance or a crescent of his that flies through his Bat Swarm or Blood Mist comes out as three, each at a foe near it, for half | Blood Lance or Crescent Wings with Bat Swarm or Blood Mist |
| C8 | **Feed the moon 血月饱饮** | Under his Blood Moon every bleed runs twice as fast, and what comes back to him is doubled | Blood Moon and any bleed or drain |
| C9 | **Push the bleeder** | A bleeding foe that is knocked back, launched or carried bleeds double for that second (it "moved") | Open Vein with Hawk Drag, Skyward Rip or Blood Spikes (Talon Rake before kit 1.4; Hawk Drag's skim comes out 30% longer under C6) |
| C10 | **Boil the swarm 血沸蝠群** | Boiling Blood cast while his Bat Swarm is out calls the swarm to him: it follows him for the rest of its time | Boiling Blood and Bat Swarm |
| C11 | **Kill in the dark 夜杀** | A foe that falls while blinded bursts into darkness: the foes within 1.4 u are blinded 1 s | any blind (Nightfall, Flit, Blood Moon) and a kill |
| C12 | **Out of the sky 天降** | Pounce or Last Bite pressed during Night Flight ends the flight on that foe at once: 50% more | Night Flight and one of the two |

Notes.

- C1 and C9 make the bleed a set-up for every style: bleed it, then push it or pop it.
- C2 is the same Spike rule Windbriar has, so the two heroes can juggle for each other.
- C4 and C11 chain blinds, and every hit on a blinded foe is a backstab (kit K5): the dark is
  where all three styles hit hardest.
- C5 turns Roost from a way out into a second firing point. The shade's copies are not casts
  and do not add to the Combo.
- C6 needs the game to keep a pressed skill for the end of the dash, the same new machinery as
  Ashfang's Dash strike and Windbriar's Sky cast.
- C7's three that come out are not shots through the cloud again.
- C11 is today's Eclipse node as a rule of the kit; if both are held they do not add: one
  burst, one blind.
- C12 gives Night Flight an early exit for a kill.
- New looks: a bleed bursting out (C1), a foe bitten in the air (C2), the swarm on one foe
  (C3), a ring screech (C4), the hanging shade firing (C5), the lance splitting in the cloud
  (C7), a dark burst on a kill (C11).
