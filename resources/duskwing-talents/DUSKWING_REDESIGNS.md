# 1. Talon Rake (F1), the whole card

## 1a) Talon Hook 钩爪 (6 s): the pull

- **Base.** He whips one wing forward and its finger talon shoots out on a dark sinew, 4.5 u. The
  first foe is hooked and yanked to his feet: 6. He spins with the pull into a two-wing rake,
  150°, 1.2 u: 14 to up to 5 foes. On a boss he is pulled to it instead.
- **Rampage.** The hook flies on through the first foe: up to three foes are hooked one after
  another and yanked in together (6 each), then two rakes of 14 and 6.
- **Sanguine.** The yank tears: the hooked foe bleeds 10 over 3 s, and the rake heals him 2 for
  each foe it hits.
- **Phantom.** The yank spins the foe round: it lands with its back to him and the rake is a
  backstab, 20 to it.
- **Motion** (0.8 s). A whip-crack of one wing, the talon line snaps taut, he plants both feet and
  heaves back; the foe skids in on a dust line; he turns with the momentum into a full-body rake,
  four torn marks; end in the cape pose.

## 1b) Hawk Drag 鹰掠 (7 s): the foot talons (**my pick**)

- **Base.** He skims low up to 4 u, seizes the first foe in his foot talons and drags it 3 u along
  the floor, carving a red groove; foes on the path take 8. Then one hard wing-beat flings it:
  16, knocked 2 u. Into a wall: 10 more, stun 0.5 s. On a boss he rakes along its body as he
  passes: 24.
- **Rampage.** After the fling he skims at the next foe within 3 u for a second drag: 10, and 8
  on its path.
- **Sanguine.** He bites while he drags it: 12 over the drag, half comes back; the groove stays
  2 s as a blood trail: 3 each half second, all of it comes back.
- **Phantom.** He drags it the other way: out of its pack toward the nearest ally (or straight
  back), alone. The fling is a backstab, 20, and it is blinded 1 s. The assassin's pick-off.
- **Motion** (0.9 s). Wings half folded like a falcon, a low skim a hand above the floor, feet
  thrown forward at the last moment, talons close; sparks and a red groove behind them; a hard
  beat throws the foe; he rolls once in the air and lands in the cape pose.
- Damage: the drag hits and the fling are 常规直伤, melee; the trail is 持续伤害.

## 1c) Shred 碎甲爪 (6 s): the armour tear

- **Base.** He hops onto one foe within 1.5 u, digs both wing claws into its chest and tears them
  apart: 18, and it loses 30% of its defence for 4 s (减防; the whole team gains). A foe with no
  defence (small foes) takes 30% more from this hit instead.
- **Rampage.** 10% more shred for each 10 Combo: 60% at 30.
- **Sanguine.** The torn wound bleeds 10 over 3 s and the tear heals him 6.
- **Phantom.** From behind he rips its back plate: a backstab, 22, and 50% 减防.
- **Motion** (0.7 s). A hop, feet hooked on the foe's shoulders, both wings sunk in at its middle,
  then ripped outward like opening a cloak; shards and a cross of torn marks; he kicks off
  backward in a flip.
- Why it fits: elites, champions and bosses have defence (formula D22); this is his team's answer
  to them, beside Phantom's own half-defence ignore.

# 2. Thousand Claws (F2), the whole card

## 2a) Claw Storm 爪暴 (6 s): hold to charge

- **Base.** Hold the key up to 1 s: he wraps his wings round himself and spins, slowly and then
  fast, walking at half speed. Let go: the wings fling open and a sphere of slashes flies out,
  4 slashes plus 1 for each 0.1 s held (up to 14), each 4 to a foe within 2.2 u; then a great X:
  12 within 1.5 u. A tap is 4 slashes and the X.
- **Rampage.** The Combo fills the charge: at 15 Combo it starts half full, at 30 full, so a tap
  is a full storm.
- **Sanguine.** Each slash that hits heals him 1; the X leaves the foes bleeding 8 over 3 s.
- **Phantom.** While he holds he is Unseen (it counts as waiting). The storm is one slash: all
  of the charge in a single huge claw at the foe nearest the pointer: 12, plus 4 for each 0.1 s
  held (up to 52).
- **Motion** (0.3 to 1.6 s). The wing cocoon turns faster as he holds, rose light leaking at the
  wing edges; the release throws the wings wide, a ball of torn marks, the X; the cape pose.

## 2b) Hanging Claws 悬爪 (6 s): slashes left in the air (**my pick**)

- **Base.** Eight fast slashes into the air in a 120° fan, 1.5 u: 3 to each foe a slash crosses.
  The slashes stay, hanging in the air as torn rose marks, for 3 s. Press again (or at 3 s by
  itself): all of them fly at the foe nearest the pointer within 5 u, 5 each (40 on one foe),
  and cut every foe on their way.
- **Rampage.** While they hang, each of his hits adds one more slash to the air, up to 16.
- **Sanguine.** The slashes fly back as blood: each that hits heals him 1 and adds 2 to a bleed
  over 3 s.
- **Phantom.** The slashes curve round and strike the foe's back: every one is a backstab.
- **Motion** (0.7 s, then 0.4 s). A blur of eight slashes in a fan; they freeze in the air like
  cracks in glass. The second press: a sharp clap of his wings, the slashes turn and stream at
  the foe in a spiral and land as one cross tear.
- Second press: add to notes N20 (Roost, Dusk Dive under Rampage, and this).

## 2c) Claw Climb 攀爪 (7 s): up a big foe, over small ones

- **Base.** On an elite, champion or boss within 2 u: he runs up its body clawing (six hits of
  4) and springs off its head in a down-slash: 14 within 1.5 u. On small foes: he hops from head
  to head over up to five foes within 3 u, a claw on each (8), and ends in the down-slash on the
  last: 10 within 1.5 u.
- **Rampage.** Up to eight heads, and each hop adds 1 more Combo.
- **Sanguine.** Every climb hit is a bite: each heals him 1; the down-slash heals 4.
- **Phantom.** He goes over the top and drops behind: the down-slash is a backstab, 20, and he is
  Unseen 1 s.
- **Motion** (1.0 s). Claws and feet in turn up the big foe like climbing a tree, ears flat;
  over small foes he springs head to head, wings half open; the down-slash from high with both
  claws, a red star on the floor.

## 2d) Cloak Turn 翻袍 (6 s): catch the shots, hunt the shooter (**my pick**)

- **Base.** He whirls his wings round him like a cape for 0.6 s. Every foe shot that touches the
  cape is caught (no damage to him) and flung back at its shooter as claw shards: that shot's
  damage + 8. Foes within 1.2 u are cut: 10. Then, if he caught a shot, he streaks down the line
  of the first one to its shooter (up to 6 u) and claws it: 12. If he caught nothing, half the
  cooldown comes back.
- **Rampage.** The whirl lasts 1.2 s, and each shot caught adds 3 Combo.
- **Sanguine.** Each shot caught heals him 4.
- **Phantom.** He arrives Unseen 1 s behind the shooter: the claw is a backstab, 18.
- **Motion** (0.6 s, then 0.3 s). The cape pose opens into a matador's swirl, both wings sweeping
  round; glowing shots sink into the folds; a snap of the wings flings them back as rose shards;
  bats streak down the line and he forms on the shooter, claws first.
- Why: his job is the 脆皮 back line, and shooters are most of it. No other card answers shots.
- Damage: the returned shards are his 常规直伤, ranged; the cut and the claw are melee.

## 2e) Triple Talon 三段爪 (5 s): three presses

- **Base.** Up to three presses, each within 1 s of the last. 1: a claw-step 1.5 u, 90°: 8. 2: a
  second step and a cross slash: 8. 3: a leap and a two-claw smash: 14 within 1.5 u. The
  cooldown starts after the third, or when a window runs out.
- **Rampage.** At 15 Combo or more a fourth press: a spinning slash, 10 within 1.6 u; the windows
  are 1.5 s.
- **Sanguine.** Each step heals him 1 for each foe hit; the third is a diving bite: 14, half
  comes back.
- **Phantom.** Each step is a short blink through the foe and a turn: every hit is a backstab;
  after the third he is Unseen 1 s.
- **Motion** (0.35 s a press). A clear one-two-three: a low step-slash to the left, a high
  step-slash to the right, a jump with both wings up and a slam; the cape pose.
- Second presses: add to notes N20.

## 2f) Shadow Rend 撕影 (7 s): claw their shadows

- **Base.** He rakes the floor toward himself: claw marks race out 4 u along the floor in a 60°
  cone to every foe's shadow in it. Each shadow is torn: 12, and its foe is rooted 1 s (it cannot
  walk; it can still attack).
- **Rampage.** Each torn shadow tears again 0.3 s later: 6.
- **Sanguine.** A torn shadow bleeds its foe: 8 over 3 s.
- **Phantom.** The root lasts 2 s and a rooted foe cannot turn, so its back stays where it was.
- **Motion** (0.7 s). He crouches and drags both claws back across the floor; dark marks shoot
  away from him; each foe's shadow rips with a cloth sound and a black claw holds it to the floor
  while the root lasts.
- The root is 定身 (formula D36): a fifth of its time on a boss.
