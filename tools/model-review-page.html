How to review

Each move has three clips. The first is a close-up in the model lab with no effects. The second and third come from a real room at play zoom, cropped round the hero at 1080p, first with every effect hidden and then with the effects on.

Write what you want changed in the box under a move, or in the thread. Notes save as you type. Each round brings back the same link with the changes and a line in the round log.

When the model is right, tick every line of the sign-off list and write "sign off" in the thread.
